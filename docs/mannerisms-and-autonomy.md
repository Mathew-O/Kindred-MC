# AI-player mannerisms
E-32/33/34 provide visible habits and brief attention without replacing the player's goals or action controller.

Movement habits such as sprint-jumping require world-tested space, safe landing, supported movement and a current lease. Clear transient controls on stop/cancellation. A notice-and-return pause must preserve the task checkpoint and return honestly after attention.

Human comments are social input; authorized settings explicitly change durable preferences. Temporary accommodations are bounded per speaker, cannot accumulate into control, and cannot change goals, access, activity or stop. Model/AI chatter never grants accommodation authority.

Qualify actual traversal, cramped/hazardous terrain, attention during container/work phases, cancellation and multiplayer feedback. Text or pose fixtures do not establish smooth native behavior. See [the player contract](../minecraft_player_spec.md).

## 3. Defects and risks

### Confirmed on this branch

- **D1. The edit switch gates survival, and the idle mode gates it entirely.**
  - The cause is `autonomyAdmitted` (`PlayerRuntime.java:4717-4727`). It requires `edits.autonomousEditsEnabled() && edits.protectionCompatibilityKnown()` and excludes `isIdleObserving()`, and every essential survival candidate sits behind it (`:4729`): eating, stepping out of fire or lava, swimming to shore, getting clear of a hissing creeper.
  - `IDLE_OBSERVING` is the default playstyle for a new player (`PlaystyleProfile.java:5`). A new player standing with a human at night does not eat when starving and does not step out of fire.
  - This is deliberate and tested: `NativeAutonomyGameTests.defaultPolicyKeepsAutonomyDisabled` asserts that with edits off no food is eaten. So changing it is a policy migration (decision D4 covers block edits, D5 unattended progression), not a bug fix. None of these reflexes edits a block, and every edit action already checks the edit policy itself.
  - **Decided.** The owner left open calls to whatever makes the players feel less like bots (2026-10-24). A person eats when hungry and steps out of fire whatever the server lets them build, so the reflexes no longer wait on the edit switch or idle (slice 2).
- **D2. The idle hurt remark accuses whoever is near** (`PlayerRuntime.java:7963-7978`).
  - Any damage at all (fall, fire, drowning, a skeleton) sends "Hey! Why are you hitting me?!" or "Careful there, we're on the same team!" to every player within 16 blocks.
  - It repeats every two seconds while the damage lasts, and bypasses SocialBrain's consent, audience and cooldown.
- **D3. Native players take no part in SocialBrain.**
  - `socialParticipant` needs `entry.body`, the legacy mob body (`PlayerRuntime.java:6203-6211`). For a native player it returns null, so `SocialBrain`'s profile, `ambientEnabled`, `publicConversation` and listener limits never apply to it.
  - What a native player says goes out as eight direct broadcasts to the whole server (`:3516, :3525, :3530, :3600, :3630, :3659, :3877`, and `:7976` above).
  - Two of them give coordinates ("I built and lit a Nether portal near x y z", "I found the End portal near ..."). In a manhunt that tells the hunters where the runner is; in Factions it tells the other factions.
- **D4. Shared projects (E-11, marked complete) are not what the tracker says.**
  - Nothing in production creates a project: `registerProject` is called only on load (`PlayerRuntime.java:516`) and by `NativeTeamGameTests`.
  - Nothing saves a claim, start, completion or revocation: `saveProject` and `saveReservation` have no callers outside the store.
  - Startup recovery releases every ACTIVE reservation and reopens every CLAIMED or IN_PROGRESS task (`SqliteCompanionStore.java:1452-1477`), whatever became of the actions behind them, even those it has just marked UNCERTAIN.
  - `completeTask` credits the declared `produces_item` and `produces_count` to stock, not what was observed (`TeamCoordinator.java:210-214`).
  - `claimTask` skips the project-state check that `findEligibleTasks` makes (`:124-167`): a task of a cancelled project can be claimed directly.
  - No items are made by any of this; the risk is accounting and reassignment once projects are live. Completion is already idempotent: a completed task is refused by its state check, so the brief's "credit twice" does not happen.
- **D5. `main`'s fix is missing here, and resume says what is no longer true.**
  - `4cb2b33` (resume connects a saved body first; the roster reconnects saved players rather than skipping them; the default player is the one connected) is not on this branch.
  - Our resume message says "Automatic progression remains disabled" (`PlayerRuntime.java:667`) while autonomy runs.
- **D6. Idle presentation outlives stop.** `applyIdleObservation` runs whenever the body is usable and idle (`:4654`), with no stop check. It sets the look and pulses sneaking on a stopped player. It is presentation only, but it is input after a stop.

### True of `55eea9c`, not of this branch

- **Idle wandering and jumping.** On this branch `applyIdleObservation` never moves the body (`:7948`: gaze, glances, a remark). On the handoff branch it still calls `bot.jump()`.
- **The world-design prototype.** Already reviewed and replaced in core ([building plan](building-plan.md)). Its separate ledger, two-material world and approval-before-ability are not used; the ideas kept are listed there.
- **Schema disagreement.** Code and manifest agree at schema 8 here. The prototype's draft migrations V9–V11 exist only on the handoff branch.

### Suspected risks

- **R1. `PlayerRuntime` keeps growing.** 8,470 lines; each kind of work (build, ranch, field, moat) adds a nullable field, a candidates method and branches in several places, and its own copy of the authority checks in its `start…` method. One work-runner interface has begun this (the field and the moat share it, in the stage-5 change).
- **R2. Work is not restart-safe.** Builds read their progress from the world, but the runner, its scaffold list and the field, ranch and moat runners live in memory only; after a restart the player does not resume them (E-08b's "not yet kept across a restart", F-13).
- **R3. Fixtures are wall-clock sensitive.** Claims expire after 15 s and skills have wall-clock limits, so a loaded machine cancels steps; suite flakes have come from this. Not a gameplay defect, but it makes evidence noisy.
- **R4. Planning cost is bounded by size, not by work.** The build planner looks over the whole box each step; the pure simulation copies the site every step. Five towers planned as one build took a box covering the whole compound; splitting them was the fix (M-06a).

## 5. Making the players more believable

| Behaviour | Change | Measure | Target |

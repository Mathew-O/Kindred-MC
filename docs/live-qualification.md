# AI-player live qualification
Completion remains in [the tracker](feature-spec.md). Record exact build/hash, pinned environment, seed/difficulty and paired native/metadata snapshots.

1. Join with vanilla, Fabric without Kindred and optional enhanced clients. Check identity, rendering, equipment, tracking/reconnect and commands.
2. Test administrative access with two humans and three AI identities; reject unauthorized controls, stock access and stale requests.
3. Measure 100 stop samples during actual navigation/mining/use and delayed callbacks; verify settlement before replacement.
4. Restart, unload, die/respawn and change dimensions. Verify one identity/body, preserved stop/activity and exact item conservation.
5. Disconnect every human. Qualified local work continues under server policy, including provider outage and contention.
6. Earn first-night survival and iron with one AI, then three sharing supplies. Fixture grants and repositioning do not count.
7. E-40: three previously untested recorded seeds on normal survival, empty inventory through Dragon, all three contributing, no human gameplay repairs/grants/teleports. Include restart/API outage and report failed runs/costs.

F-01 covers live control; F-04/10 execution; F-13/15 recovery/unattended; F-25b player mechanics; E-11/17/39/40 cooperation/progression. Partial evidence does not pass a larger gate.

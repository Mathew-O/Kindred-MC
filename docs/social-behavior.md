# AI-player social behavior
Use [the product contract](../minecraft_player_spec.md), existing SocialBrain and F-18/20.

Ground speech in observations, preferences and accepted commitments. E-11 assignments create shared plans; dialogue cannot assign work, grant authority or claim unverified completion.

Require visibility/proximity and audience privacy. Public speech is opt-in. Permitted direct chat need not wake a stopped body. Bound player/listener/global chatter; AI exchanges terminate and cannot re-enter as new inputs. Invalidate stale facts/privacy-dependent speech.

Attention during work uses the existing arbiter/checkpoints. Qualify real multiplayer transport and native attention; text fixtures alone do not establish either.

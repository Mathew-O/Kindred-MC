# AI-player relationships
E-24/25/26 record individual friendship/trust toward humans and AIs. Use attributed, deduplicated evidence; permit corrections and invalidate derived summaries. Missing items, proximity or accusations are not proof of theft. Generic profanity, short commands, disagreement or declined gifts do not automatically mean hostility.

Relationships influence tone and discretionary social choices, never authority, survival competence, access or resource budgets. No revenge, pet/property harm or refusal of administrator stop. Generated conversation cannot farm rapport.

## Tone drift versus relationship
E-35 lets temporary pleased/sour expression ease toward the player's baseline while durable friendship/trust and its behavior band remain unchanged. Persist separate values and timestamps. Corrections affect the relevant evidence, not an invented forgiveness event.

Qualify peer separation, limits, correction/deletion, restart, tone decay and absence of gameplay/control escalation. See [the player contract](../minecraft_player_spec.md).

# AI-player outings and interests
E-27/28 enrich [autonomous players](../minecraft_player_spec.md) with optional shared outings and individual hobbies.

An outing uses an observed destination/map and a bounded plan: participants, objective, route, supplies, time and return conditions. Participants accept or have standing team policy; each keeps its own authority and limits. Invitations never unlock skills or assign work by dialogue alone. Dragon expeditions use E-39, not an informal invitation shortcut.

Hobbies/collections are small editable interests with provenance. Meaningful verified experiences can change unlocked preferences; raw message volume cannot. Preserve survival/project reserves and physical item identity. Qualify cancellation, participant loss, failed travel, accepted commitments and truthful outcomes. Declining is not a rapport penalty.

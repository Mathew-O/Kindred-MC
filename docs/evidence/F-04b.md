# F-04b — Mid-skill danger interruption and cancellation settlement barrier
Date: 2026-10-17 EDT; report 2026-10-17.
Antigravity / uncommitted main, no commits. Complete for this child slice; parent F-04 remains open.

Mid-skill danger observation and cancellation settlement barrier ensure that autonomous AI players do not remain locked in non-urgent work while facing immediate environmental or hostile threats:
1. **Bounded Danger Observation While Busy:**
   - In `PlayerRuntime.beforeNativeTick`, when a native player is busy executing an active skill (`entry.runner.isRunning()`), a bounded danger scan (`isHazardousSituation(player)`) inspects immediate standing/contact hazards (fire, lava, magma, cactus, sweet berry bushes, powder snow) and lethal falls or imminent threats.
2. **Synchronous Interruption Through Arbiter and SkillRunner Barrier:**
   - Upon danger detection, `entry.hazardInterrupted` latches true, immediately invoking `entry.arbiter.cancel(lease, "Hazard detected")` and `entry.runner.abort("Hazard detected")`.
   - Native body physical inputs (movement, sneaking, jumping, hand swinging) are immediately zeroed via `entry.body.stopMotion()` and `entry.body.resetActionInputs()`.
   - Prevents overlapping controllers: cancellation sets the arbiter into pending cancellation state; `autonomyAdmitted` explicitly verifies `!entry.arbiter.cancellationPending()`, guaranteeing no new lease or skill dispatch can occur before full settlement of the aborted lease.
3. **Goal and Checkpoint Preservation:**
   - In `observeRunner`, when a skill completes with `entry.hazardInterrupted == true`, `entry.currentTaskId` is preserved rather than cleared, and the task checkpoint is persisted to the store so high-level tasks can resume once safety is restored.
   - Normal completion clears `currentTaskId`, distinguishing hazard interruptions from finished work.
4. **Delayed Callback and Replay Protection:**
   - Late callbacks or callbacks from delayed/orphaned tasks cannot restart stopped or replaced work: `ActionArbiter.isLeaseCurrent()` verifies exact epoch and token matching, discarding any late mutation attempts.
   - Emergency actions cannot bypass pending reconciliation, stop latches, server edit policies, or activity limits.

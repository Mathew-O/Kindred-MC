## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a builder that has slid off its own scaffold column onto something beside it (the build itself, or a
ledge), above the column's remaining top, steps down onto that top when it is a drop of at most three, and takes the column
down from on top of it, as a player would. When the top is further below than it can drop and come back from, it is refused
once, and the second time the build ends, built, saying the scaffolding at that cell was out of reach and is left standing.
Before, it waited on "Scaffolding at … is out of reach to take down" until the test's ticks ran out.

Found: in a local class run on F-22c46's branch, `NativeHouseGameTests`' cabin builder slid off its own column after its last
slab, onto the cabin. It ended diagonal to the column, four above the ground, with the column's top out of sight below, and
waited on "out of reach to take down" until its ticks ran out (F-22c46's evidence, Remaining). #113's base cabin (run
42756884925) ended the same way. A staging aimed at #113's
"Down the scaffold: Nothing solid to break" form put the player on a staged perch beside its column the same way.
- From the perch no stance beside the top was in reach and walkable back from, so the take-down had nothing to offer.
- F-22c40's "Scaffold N up to take down" offered a scaffold from the ground, a walk the navigator could not take from the perch.
- The lead's choice was to step back onto its own column. Digging down beside it (off-plan digging) stays out.
- A first plan put a block on top of the column to stand on. It was dropped: the placement is out of reach (4.62 against 4.5).

Changed files:
- `companion-core` `ConstructionPlanner`: in the take-down, after the existing stances and before F-22c40's scaffold, a
  column's own top is offered as a stance:
  - the cell above the top is reachable on foot from where it stands (the walk's drop of at most three);
  - it can come back from there by `returnable`, with the blocks it can spare (F-22c45);
  - it is standable, not shunned, and neither it nor the cell above is a block of the build.

  The break is "Scaffolding down, from on top of it". Never into liquid, and never from where it could not come back,
  both hold as before.
- `companion-server-adapter` `BuildRunner`:
  - the same top refused as out of reach at two plans running, on the ground, ends the build ("built, X/Y blocks;
    scaffolding at P out of reach to take down, left standing");
  - a plan made off the ground (mid-fall) does not count;
  - a completed break, place or pillar resets the count. Without the last two, a seed cabin coming down its column was
    ended mid-descent in the class runs below, so they were added before these results.
- Tests: F-22c45's planner case accepts the break from the trench's floor or from on top of the column; its water case
  asserts no stance under the water and nothing broken into it.

Nothing is granted: it walks down onto its own blocks.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: when the player is up its own tower by a caged crystal and sees the dragon coming, it walls itself in before the wings arrive. "Coming" means the dragon, in its line of sight, will be within its wings' reach within three seconds if it keeps its present flight. Each wing's box sits about 4.5 blocks out from the body, and vanilla widens it by four on each side, so the reach is about 11 blocks. That fling would take it off a tower one block wide. Vanilla 1.21.1's `EnderDragonEntity.launchLivingEntities` pushes a player in reach every tick: away from the body by four over the distance, and up by a fifth of a block. The upward push builds up over a few ticks and would carry the player over a wall. So the guard is walls at the feet and a block over the head, built in this order:
- the wall on the side the push goes toward: a block beside the tower's top, set against its side, then one at the feet on top of that;
- the block over the head, set against whatever stands beside that space (the pillar's obsidian or the cage's bars), or on a short column built up on that wall;
- the walls on the other open sides.

Each block is a recorded placement of its own blocks under the edit policy. Walled in, it waits while the dragon goes by and breaks nothing underfoot, then carries on. The block over its head is taken down before it pillars higher. Nothing is built unless the dragon is seen coming.

## Scope and implementation

**Found:** `NativeObsidianPickupGameTests.anUntakenStepToTakeTheWaterBackLeavesNoObsidianBehind` (F-22c94's and F-25c4cs's test) failed on #465 (docs only), run 45074150100, attempt 1, with "Went exploring with its obsidian left in the water".

From the log and the artifact, Smith did everything right:
- His mine of the obsidian was cancelled ("In deep water; up for air first").
- He climbed out and picked the obsidian up ("picked up 1 minecraft:obsidian").
- He took the water back, then went on at 1 of 10.

**Cause, in the check:** `exploredFirst` was set by any goal line with "Explore" while no obsidian was in hand. Smith's first step that run, with the lava not yet in sight, was a walk to look for it, "Obsidian: Make and mine obsidian (0/10) - Explore the cave toward lava", before any obsidian existed. In six other runs of this test (#451 ×2, #457 ×2, #454, #465's 45056595996/2) the first step was "Obsidian: stand beside lava".

The trail's "Climb toward the surface for logs: back the way it came" lines are the message's last 700 characters. They come later, at the sticks stage, after the obsidian was in hand. So it is not #469's hold. Nor is it F-22c94's shape, a drop set aside "no way into its cell" and left: here the drop was picked up.

**The change (`NativeObsidianPickupGameTests`):**
- The check is armed only once the obsidian is mined, read as the staging reads it: neither obsidian nor lava where the lava was. It keeps its meaning, exploring while its obsidian lies in the water.
- Two new tests share the scene:
  - `aWalkToLookForLavaBeforeAnyObsidianIsMadeIsNotExploringWithItsObsidianLeft` (batch `obsidianPickupUnseen`): a block over the lava at the start, taken away once the player looks for lava. That is CI's shape.
  - `obsidianLeftInTheWaterAndAWalkToExploreIsStillCaught` (batch `obsidianPickupLeft`), the negative control: the mined obsidian made one no player can pick up where it lies in the water. Passes only once the check catches the player exploring.

No product code changes, nothing is granted, and no check, bound or timeout is loosened.

## Design changes

- The contradiction check reads stated counts, owners and prices from the words against the ledgers' facts for the span; anything the ledgers do not hold is not checked rather than refused.
- A summary failing either check is replaced by the plain list, so nothing unchecked is ever kept.
- Order is not checked by `Chronicle.check`; the prompt carries it (lines given in order, never said otherwise), measured live above.
- Pages are written one request at a time on the planning lane for now; the provider's batch interface (half the price) is F-33b's remaining step.
- Pages sometimes run under the 60 words asked (55 to 63 here); kept, as the design's 150 to 300 tokens is an upper guide and short pages are cheaper.
- The strategist's history keeps the last 4 events since the newest day summary.

## Design changes

- The contradiction check reads stated counts, owners and prices from the words against the ledgers' facts for the span; anything the ledgers do not hold is not checked rather than refused.
- A summary failing either check is replaced by the plain list, so nothing unchecked is ever kept.
- Order is the transport's, not the model's: the model writes sentences with their line numbers, put in line order (the Record's ids are its sequence); words that order events (earlier, before, between, meanwhile, previously) or speak of the page are refused by `Chronicle.check`, measured live above.
- Pages are written one request at a time on the planning lane for now; the provider's batch interface (half the price) is F-33b's remaining step.
- Pages are plain and terse (44 to 74 words); the design's 150 to 300 tokens is read as an upper guide.
- The first engine run's lesson: a day is ~1,900 events, nearly all journal lines, so a page is written from a condensed day, never from every event; its citations are the condensed lines' ids, which are real events.
- The strategist's history keeps the last 4 events since the newest day summary.

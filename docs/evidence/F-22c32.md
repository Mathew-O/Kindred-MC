## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. The source is #72's CI (`a2c7baa`), in the lake fixture,
`NativeBringGameTests.aLibrarianAcrossALakeIsRowedOverAndBroughtHome`:
- Hal put a boat down beside the strolling librarian. It waited for the librarian to step in, judged the boat not taken,
  and started a break of the boat so it could pick it up and put it down again.
- The librarian stepped into the boat while that break was still running.
- The bring's `HOLD` keeps a boat step that is under way until it ends, so the break went on. It broke the boat that now
  held the librarian, and the librarian was let go on the lake.

Changed files: `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime` (the bring's `HOLD`), `NativeBoatAction`, `NativePlayerSkills`.
- **The running boat step is no longer kept once the villager sits in a boat.** `HOLD` checks the villager's vehicle
  first, and drops the step.
- **The break of a boat taken up for a villager breaks only an empty boat.** The `HOLD` break passes `empty=true`
  (a new optional argument of `native_boat`; its descriptor lists it). `NativeBoatAction.onlyEmpty()` then settles the
  break as blocked, "The boat is no longer empty: … sits in it", if anything sits in the boat when the strike would land.
  Breaking a boat in any other case is unchanged.

New fixture: `NativeBringGameTests.aLibrarianThatStepsIntoTheBoatLateIsHeldInIt` (batch `bringSeated`).
- It is the lake scene with the strolling librarian.
- Fixture staging: once, the librarian is seated in an empty boat near it that has stood for at least 12 ticks, after
  Hal's wait for it. That is the late step-in from CI. Nothing else in the scene is staged.
- It checks the bring as the lake fixture does, and that the boat the librarian was seated in is not broken in the
  100 ticks after.

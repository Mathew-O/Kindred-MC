## The diagnostic

`audit/ItemLeaveMixin` injects at the head of `Entity.setRemoved`. For every item entity leaving inside a live bring
test's plot or kept ground, `ItemLeaves` logs:
- its stack and position;
- the removal reason;
- its age and pickup delay (`audit/ItemEntityAccess`);
- whether its chunk ticks entities, is loaded but not ticking, or is not loaded;
- the player acting, if any;
- the frames that removed it.

A pickup (`onPlayerCollision`), a merge (`ItemEntity.merge`), a despawn (`ItemEntity.tick`), an unload and a fixture's
restore each show in the frames. A pickup or a merge empties the stack before it is removed, so those log
`0 minecraft:air`. Any other way out keeps the stack, so a lost item is named.

## The lectern and chunk hooks (this change)

- **`audit/LecternGoneMixin`:** a HEAD inject on `LecternBlock.onStateReplaced`. For a lectern in a live bring test's
  ground replaced by any other block, `ItemLeaves.lecternGone` logs the test, the position, the new state, the player
  acting and 12 frames. Frames from `net.minecraft.world.chunk` and `net.minecraft.block` are skipped, so the step that
  called a shared helper such as `breakAt` shows. A chunk reload logs no line, since unloading replaces no block.
- **Chunk lines:** `ServerChunkEvents.CHUNK_LOAD` and `CHUNK_UNLOAD` log "Chunk loads/unloads under {test}" for a chunk
  under a live bring test's ground.

### Cause #3's fix (CI GREEN, 2026-11-16)

**The change:** the door is put back without spending a block the bring owes.
- `BuildRunner.keep(items)` names the items its owner has to keep. `backUp`'s way back up to the site never spends
  them.
- `PortalRunCampaign`'s way-block pick skips any item a `spendNever` rule names. The rule is set only while
  `backUp` plans its route or climb, through new `buildRoute` and `climbOut` overloads, and is none otherwise.
- DOOR_BACK keeps the bring's rails, cart and dirt.

**Why the first version failed:** it only put kept items last in `backUp`'s spend-first preference. That still
failed 0 of 3, each run "stairs up 1". The way-block pick takes a preferred block if it holds one, and otherwise
falls back to any sealing block, dirt included. Hal held no other.

**Nothing granted.** The door is still built from Hal's own blocks. A climb that needs a block only the bring owes

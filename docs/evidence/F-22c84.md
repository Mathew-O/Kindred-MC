## The diagnostic

`audit/ItemLeaveMixin` injects at the head of `Entity.setRemoved`. For every item entity leaving inside a live bring
test's plot or kept ground, `ItemLeaves` logs:
- its stack and position;
- the removal reason;
- its age and pickup delay (`audit/ItemEntityAccess`);
- whether its chunk ticks entities, is loaded but not ticking, or is not loaded;
- the player acting, if any;
- the frames that removed it.

A pickup (`onPlayerCollision`), a merge (`ItemEntity.merge`), a despawn (`ItemEntity.tick`), an unload and a fixture's
restore each show in the frames. A pickup or a merge empties the stack before it is removed, so those log
`0 minecraft:air`. Any other way out keeps the stack, so a lost item is named.

## Cause #2, caught by the hook: the cart and boat items left in the cell while a walk to the stand repeats

#354's CI on `c3d0cc4`, which carries #318's F-22c85 fix (run 43719957093, job 132829572163).
`aLibrarianHeldBehindItsLecternIsRowedRoundIt` failed "Hal still in play", with "63 rails, 0 carts … carts and dirt
about: []".

**What the log shows:**
- **14:40:53.** Under the shut trapdoor, Hal broke the boat (`native_boat`, `bringCandidates:12245`) and the cart
  (`native_cart`, `:12257`) in the cell. It took up the front rail and picked it up. It then walked out of the front
  (`native_walk`, `:12326`).
- **Then 1,990 lines `native_goto COMPLETED [from standAt:13025]` over 31 s,** about 6,000 ticks at the test pace,
  with nothing else from this bring.
- **14:41:24.** The oak boat at `-10225821, -57, 9411074` and the minecart on the cell floor at `…, 9411075` left by
  `ItemEntity.tick:210`, DISCARDED, age 6000, acting nobody: a despawn. In the same tick the bring went on: "the cart
  taken back: rails lying by the cell []", the rest of the line taken up, the door built, "Brought home".
- "Lectern gone" logged 0 times, and no chunk line.

**Not F-22c85's path.** F-22c85 is two walks ending without the item, then "let be". Here every walk completes, so no
give-up fires.

**The reading, from the code (KEEP, under the trapdoor, `PlayerRuntime` near 12282–12294; to be confirmed by a
fixture):**
- With a rail or minecart lying in the cell and the player outside it, the branch first stands the player on the
  stand before the front, to within 0.25 of its middle (`squaredDistanceTo > 0.0625`).
- `native_goto` with `centre` completes within `CENTRE_WITHIN` = 0.4. A player stopped between 0.25 and 0.4 off the
  middle would have each walk complete without moving, and the branch issue it again.
- The 100-tick window under the trapdoor (`UNDER_TRAPDOOR_TICKS`, from `b.underFrom`) is started only after that check.
  So the window never starts, and only the item going ends the loop. That fits the loop ending on the despawn tick.
- The items it fetches there are rails and minecarts only, and the "left lying in the cell" note for a boat comes after
  this block. So the boat item was neither fetched nor said.

**Routing.** Bring behaviour, so CI GREEN's. During the freeze, only once a fixture fails before the fix and passes
after: the player stood 0.3 off the stand, a minecart item on the cell floor, the trapdoor shut.

## The lectern and chunk hooks (this change)

- **`audit/LecternGoneMixin`:** a HEAD inject on `LecternBlock.onStateReplaced`. For a lectern in a live bring test's
  ground replaced by any other block, `ItemLeaves.lecternGone` logs the test, the position, the new state, the player
  acting and 12 frames. Frames from `net.minecraft.world.chunk` and `net.minecraft.block` are skipped, so the step that
  called a shared helper such as `breakAt` shows. A chunk reload logs no line, since unloading replaces no block.
- **Chunk lines:** `ServerChunkEvents.CHUNK_LOAD` and `CHUNK_UNLOAD` log "Chunk loads/unloads under {test}" for a chunk
  under a live bring test's ground.

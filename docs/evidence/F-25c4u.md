## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a block placed against another that is gone by the time the placement acts, dug out meanwhile say, is not placed, and the player goes on. Before, vanilla put the block in the gone block's cell instead of the one planned. The record could not verify that and stopped the player for an operator to reconcile, taking its body out of the world.

- The record: `native_place`, "Native placement effect cannot be verified; reconcile", outcome unknown, no after-inventory kept.
- Sol was blocked ("Native action outcome requires reconciliation") and its body was out of the world for the run's last half hour.
- Which way it went in play is not certain, because the record kept no after-inventory. Taking away the block clicked against before the placement acts gives the same record and the same stop.

Changed modules/files:
- `NativePlaceAction`:
  - its preconditions: the block it clicks against must still be one vanilla places against, not air, a fluid or a plant it would replace instead (a replaceable block with nothing to collide with);
  - an outcome it cannot verify now records whether the block was placed, what is at the target and which slots changed.

## Scope and implementation

M-06k lists four first-day live checks for the owner's server, taken from M-06j1's walk because a console walk could
not show them. M-06k5 rehearses them natively, so that the first day only confirms them. Every check goes through
`/faction`'s own method, the one a person's request and an AI player's request both use. These are Factions-only paths,
and no rule was found broken. One line of code changed, for the tests: online time is measured on the factions clock
(below).

**What was already native on main** (from the M-06c, M-06e and M-06f evidence, and M-06j1's list):

**New:** `NativeFirstDayGameTests`, with one test for each gap. Each test says in its comments what it stages.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `FactionsService`. Online time is now measured on its own clock, `now()`, at a connect,
  a disconnect, the once-a-second step and the stop. That clock is the wall clock plus an offset only a test sets; it
  already ran the shields. In production the offset is 0, so nothing changes.
- gametest: `NativeFirstDayGameTests` (new) and its entrypoint.
- docs: this file and the tracker row.

Schema/configuration changes: none.

Runs (narrowed to `NativeFirstDayGameTests`, `NativeCaptureGameTests`, `NativeFactionPowerGameTests`,
`NativeSafeZoneGameTests` and `NativeTerritoryGameTests`):
- The first run: the cap and shield tests passed. The power test timed out: the GameTest server runs its ticks faster
  than real time, so 800 ticks were about 5 s, shorter than its 8-second wall-clock wait. The fix was a 5-second wait
  and a 6,000-tick limit. No rule changed.
- After a 5-second wall wait: 13 of 13, three runs. The lead then ruled it out: whether a tick limit outlasts a wall
  wait depends on the runner's speed, a new intermittent. Online time is now measured on the factions clock, and the
  test moves that clock instead of waiting; the tick limit is back to 600.
- With the clock change, narrowed to every class that moves the factions clock or counts power
  (`NativeFirstDayGameTests`, `NativeCaptureGameTests`, `NativeFactionPowerGameTests`, `NativeSafeZoneGameTests`,
  `NativeTerritoryGameTests`, `NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests`, `NativeFactionBackOffGameTests`,
  `NativeFactionPlayGameTests`, `NativeFactionNoticesGameTests`, `NativeFactionShelterGameTests`,
  `NativeFactionBasinGameTests`): 32 of 32 in runs 1 and 3. Run 2 failed one test, not this slice's:
  `NativeFactionBackOffGameTests.aFactionWalkRefusedBetweenItsGoalsTasksIsBackedOffEachTime`, "9 refused, 8 counted",
  on a claim move. That count is faction play's bookkeeping. `blockedWalks` counts every blocked faction walk, and
  `refusalsCounted` only one whose move is still current. It reads no online time, and the factions clock's offset is 0
  for all of that test's ticks, so the change cannot reach it. It was reported to the lead and not fixed here (body
  behaviour, held for the corpus gate). All three new tests passed in all three runs.

Environment: Linux, JDK 21, the manifest's Minecraft 1.21.1 and Fabric; `:companion-server-adapter:runGametest`, with
the narrowing in `fabric.mod.json` restored before the commit.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- **Staged, not earned.** Claims, power and the clock are staged. A ten-member faction with eighty chunks, and an hour
  online, are the owner's first day to confirm, together with the checks already native.
- **No rule was found broken**, so there was nothing to raise with the lead or Session A.
- **The portal test** calls the portal block's teleport target directly; no player walks through a portal. The first
  day's walk-through on the owner's server stays the confirmation.

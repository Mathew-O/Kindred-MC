# M-06k evidence: the month run's load

M-06k is the month run on the owner's server (the tracker's row). Its hitches are not yet attributed; this file holds
what has been measured toward that, one slice at a time.

## The gather's best-tool question asked once per kind of block (2026-11-11, claude/m06k-gather-tool-cache-1111)

After the safe zones' fix (M-06f, #185), a JFR profile of seed 110 Factions (`115c960`, alone, 288,000 ticks) still had
`WorldKnowledge.nearest` at 9.42% of the server thread's samples against 2.85% in a quiet stretch, and under it
`NativeBreakAction.bestTool` at 2.81% against 0.30%. The goal's gather and its walk-out stop
(`PortalRunCampaign.gather`, through `gatherFrom`, and `stopFor`) search every remembered block of a kind with one predicate,
`gatherable`, and that predicate asked `bestTool` (a pass over the 36 inventory slots) for every block that passed its
cheap filters. The answer depends on the block state and the inventory alone, and a search does not change the
inventory.

Change, no decision changed: `gatherable` keeps a map from block state to the answer, made with the predicate, so it
lives for one gather's or one stop's search within a tick and is never kept across ticks. `mayWork` (the world's rule
and the territory guard) now runs before it. The two checks are pure predicates, with no side effects and no counters
(`mayWork` is `canPlayerModifyAt` and `TerritoryGuard.mayChange`; the tool check reads the inventory), so changing
their order cannot change any answer.

**The same answer, pinned first.** `NativeGatherTargetGameTests` (GameTest, a bare native body and one campaign): rock
in cobblestone, deepslate and stone (with the block it stands on remembered, never its own support), iron and diamond
each in deepslate and stone, and three logs of which only one is a tree's (one a post against planks, one held up by
leaves). The cell gathered first is asserted for rock, iron, diamond and logs across seven inventories: a bare hand, a
wooden pickaxe off the hotbar, a golden, a stone and an iron pickaxe, a stone pickaxe with the caller's rule excluding
the nearest rock and iron, and a bare hand again on the same campaign (nothing found with a pickaxe kept for a hand).

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/aiplayer native break <x y z> [bot]`, and the Nether-portal goal, break one block the way a survival player does: the fastest owned tool that can harvest it is moved into the hotbar, vanilla mining time and tool wear apply, and the break's own drops are picked up by walking over them (or left for the player to walk over, for passage cells). When no owned tool helps, it digs with an empty hand or an item that takes no wear, so pickaxes are not worn down on dirt.

Policy (decision D4, recorded server-wide in `edits.properties`, off when the file is missing): with no assigned work area, `RoamingEdits` allows a break only where the player itself may edit (`canPlayerModifyAt`, so spawn protection applies), inside the loaded world and border, never a container or block entity other than an empty furnace, never spawners, portals or end portal frames, never the block under the player's feet, and never a break that would let lava out. Obsidian with water above it or a still water source beside it is the one lava exception, because the water refills the hole. An assigned work area keeps the older area policy.

Changed modules/files: `NativeBreakAction` (phases PREPARING, SWAPPING, MINING, COLLECTING; durable admission settles VERIFIED, no-effect or UNCERTAIN), `RoamingEdits`, `NativeEdits`, `NativePlayerBody.miningActive`, `InventoryEvidence.itemTotal/sameContents`, `NativePlayerSkills` (`native_break`: x, y, z, optional collect and guarded; 30 s), `PlayerRuntime`, `PlayerCommands`.
Dependencies satisfied: F-05a admission, F-06 claims, F-08a mining receipts.
Schema/configuration changes: none. `edits.properties` absent now means edits off.

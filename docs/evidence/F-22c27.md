## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product:
- `PortalRunCampaign.hunt` looks for game only within 24 blocks of the player. With none there, the life-first food
  need fishes, then explores.
- A cow that fled drew the chase out of those 24 blocks, about 60 blocks north of the herd. With no animal about it,
  the player explored north, east and south, found no way off the field, and began its base short: 31 food against 32
  (`sim-full-2`; PR #42's CI, run 42498227499, the same fixture). Main's CI failed it once before F-22c26
  (run 42455310230).

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign`:
- `gameSeen`: where and when it last saw each animal it could hunt; only animals in sight, nothing hidden.
- `backToGame`: with none in sight, it goes to the nearest place it saw game in the last minute, before it explores.
  - The way there is the route builder's (`buildRoute`): it may dig, stair and pillar under the server's edit policy,
    as a player gets back up a ledge. The first version walked only: in the hunting classes' third run the new fixture
    chased a cow off the field's south edge, a drop of three, found "No walkable route" back up to each place it had
    seen game, explored below and began its base at 31 against 32.
  - A place is dropped once it stands within 4 blocks of it, once no way there is found, or a minute on.
  - Mid-jump it waits a second and plans on landing.

New fixture: `NativeLifeFirstGameTests.ledOffFromTheHerdItGoesBackToWhereItSawItAndHuntsFirst`.
- It is the hunt fixture, with the player moved once, after its first kill, to the field's north edge, o+(16, 7, -8),
  about 36 blocks from the herd.
- Fixture staging: that move only.

New fixture: `NativeLifeFirstGameTests.offTheFieldsEdgeItGetsBackUpToWhereItSawTheHerd`.
- The same, moved once after its first kill to the foot of the field's south edge, o+(16, 4, 46), three blocks below
  the field, where the edge hides the herd.
- Fixture staging: that move only.

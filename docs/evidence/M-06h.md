## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/ah` (open listings and orders), `/ah mine`, `/ah sell <start> [buyNow] [hours]` (the stack in
hand), `/ah bid <id> <amount>`, `/ah buy <id>`, `/ah cancel <id>` (no bids yet; the fee is kept), `/ah order <item>
<count> <each>`, `/ah unorder <id>`; prices in coins; AI players through `/faction as <player> ah ...`.
Rules: 25 coins a listing and 3% of a winning bid, both destroyed; five listings a player; 1 to 48 hours; a bid at least
the start, then a coin or a twentieth over the best; a bid's coins held until outbid (handed back) or the close; buy now
at once; a buy order holds its coins and a later listing of the same key it would pay at least the start for is sold to
it at once; unsold or withdrawn, the item comes back. Nothing between players makes coins. The wash-trading filter: a
trade counts toward the market's demand only if not one account with itself and not the same item back the other way
between the same two accounts within a week; a counted trade draws the market's stock of what it is made of down by a
tenth of the units (a `market.signal` event).
Changed modules/files: companion-core `market/AuctionBook`, `market/Auctions` (rules and ledger), `market/MarketLedger`
(`market.signal`), `record/Ledgers` (auctions); companion-server-adapter `AuctionHouse` (escrow, closes and deliveries
once a second, from the ledger), `PlayerRuntime.auctionRequest`, `FactionCommands` (`/ah`), `reportOpenSales`
(unfinished deliveries); with M-06g's follow-up in this branch: a sale's seller file is written before its coins, a
purchase is receipted (`market.received`), by the two-phase save below (`PlayerSaves`, `PlayerSaveHandlerMixin`,
`MarketDesk.Holds`).
Escrow: the listed stack serialised (SNBT) into its `auction.list` event; taken from the hand, the player's file
written, then the listing written (a failed listing write gives it back). Delivery: `auction.delivering`, the stack
handed over, the player's file written, `auction.delivered`; a crash in between is reported for an operator, never
handed over twice.
The two-phase save (the lead's design, 2026-11-08): (a) on the server thread the change is made and the player's state
snapshotted (`writeNbt` into a fresh tree); (b) one ordered writer thread writes it to a temporary file, copies the
current file to `<uuid>.dat_old` and moves the temporary file over `<uuid>.dat` atomically; (c) back on the server
thread, the coins, listing or receipt is written; (d) until then the player's further market and auction requests are
refused with "Your last trade is still being saved; try again in a moment" and no delivery is begun for them. A failed
save is a `market.uncertain` event carrying the items (SNBT) for an operator, nothing paid; a crash in between leaves an
unfinished intent, purchase or delivery that the next start reports as `market.uncertain`. While a write is pending,
vanilla's own save of that player and a native body's own save are queued behind it, never under it, and reads wait for
it (a native body's background read chains after it; vanilla's load at a join within those milliseconds waits on the
server thread, the one place a tick can wait on this write). The server's stop drains the writer.

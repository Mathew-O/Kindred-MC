## Remaining limitations and handoff

- The stuck buckets read 60 console lines before the watchdog's line, or before the spell's tick placed between the run's tick marks. With the failure rules alone, 42% of e40c2-d640518's 154 spells fell to "other". With the quiet-work tier and the busy-work loop, none do: hole or pit 31%, busy-work loop 26%, mining for a find 12%, water loop 9%, hemmed in 9%, no walkable route 8%, waiting 4%, refused 1%. The busy-work loop is a fallback (one step six times in sixty lines), so it is broad; a person should read one build's consoles against the buckets before a fix is judged by them.
- "Not reached" counts as the run's length in the paired times, which understates a seed that never got there.
- Ten seeds give a sign test p of 0.002 at best (10 of 10) and 0.021 at 9 of 10; read p together with the interval.

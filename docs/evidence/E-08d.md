# E-08d — A log built into something is never a tree

Date: 2026-10-29
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/loose-ends-1029
Build: `2b1f325`
Status: complete (this slice); E-08 and E-08c stay open

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player short of logs cuts trees, never the corner posts of a hut or a cabin, its own or anyone's. Before this, a column of logs on solid ground counted as a tree wherever it stood. A player whose build came up short of logs went for the nearest such column, which was often its own cabin's corner posts.

This was one of the two causes of the night-and-bed fixture's intermittent failure (loose end E21, `NativeLifeFirstGameTests.atNightItLeavesItsBuildForItsOwnBedThenBuildsOnInTheMorning`, "Its body"); the other is [F-25c9a](F-25c9a.md). This one, traced in this session's runs:
1. Its first cabin went slowly: a roof slab it could not reach (the review's item #82).
2. Night came early, and its second go at the cabin used more logs.
3. The hut came up twelve logs short, and the log search took its own cabin's corner posts for trees.
4. With its cabin broken open, it had no bed of its own to go to at the fixture's night.
5. The fixture ran on to the runtime's scheduled stop (tick 29,500), so its last check found no body ("Its body"): a timeout, not a death.

The loose-ends page measured it failing about one run in two alone on `385a6c2`.

The same chain shows in this branch's own full suite runs. The night fixture passed in six of the seven suite runs logged on 28 and 29 October (s52 to s56, v2), and failed in v1 (2026-10-29, 12:21). In v1, after "building hut 5x5 … short of {minecraft:oak_log=12}", the player's first twelve log breaks were its own cabin's twelve corner logs: x −13517154 and −13517148, z −2284642 and −2284636, three high (y −52 to −50) at each corner, the cabin built at −13517154, −53, −2284642.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign`: `trunkFoot`, the one test of "a tree the player would chop", which planning and gathering both use, now rejects a column with any log in it touching a building block (`builtIn`).
  - The building blocks are planks, stairs, slabs, walls, doors, trapdoors, fences, fence gates, glass, panes, wool and beds. A hut's and a cabin's corner posts stand on their plank floor, between walls, under the roof.
  - Cobblestone and dirt are left out, because the player's own scaffolding beside a trunk is made of them. Stone is left out, because trees grow against it.
  - A neighbour in a chunk that isn't loaded counts as nothing built, so the check never loads a chunk.
  - `PortalRunCampaign` is also changed on `modest-faraday`, but in other places (around lines 951 and 3,791); this change is around line 1,872.
- `NativeBuildNeedsGameTests`: one fixture, `aBuilderShortOfLogsCutsTreesNotTheCornerPostsOfAHutStandingNearby`.
- `NativeCompoundGameTests`: its `state` helper is now package-private, so the new fixture can use it.

Requirements covered: the needs queue's "logs cut from trees in sight, never from the build itself" (E-08c), extended to every build. The contract's "earn all progress": a build's blocks are never mined back out as material, as obsidian already wasn't (`madeFromLava`).

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- A log column built into nothing a tree never touches (a bare post of logs on stone, say) still counts as a tree.
- A tree a player builds right against (planks nailed to a trunk) is no longer a tree to it.
- E-08c's remaining work is unchanged: a build ended short in play, smelted blocks, the build and its trip kept across a restart, a team's stock.
- Updated documentation: the operator guide ("One trip for the rest").

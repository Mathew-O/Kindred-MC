## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player with nothing feasible to do, who would take the shortest jump off its ledge, no longer counts wading into water level with its own floor as a jump. Out of the water, it would only swim straight back to where it stood, as it did again and again. A jump into deeper water, or onto ground it can afford to fall to, is taken as before.

Found in the live pair on `458d192` (seed 8675309, strategist off):
- From 19:05 Echo stood in a pocket it had dug at the foot of its staircase down to iron, beside an underground lake at Y=40. It had 12 of its 18 iron. The world save shows water on every side and below the pocket.
- While tunnelling past the water it had dug out the floor of its staircase's lowest step, at 91, 41, 61, so the way back up was a two-block step.
- Its goal had nothing feasible. Each time, its last resort was "Jump down from the ledge" into the lake, one block down. The swim out of the water then took it straight back to the pocket ("Get out of the water: to dry ground").
- It did that 107 times in twelve minutes. Its watchdog gave up on the spot sixteen times, and every move elsewhere ended at once, the walks into the water and nothing to dig. Drowned killed it there at 19:17.

## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (found by Session B's audit of limited World Record reads; filed by the lead). `/market history <item>` read the first 10,000 sale and buy events from the Record's start (`worldEvents(…, 0, 10_000)`) and showed that item's last 10 of them. Past 10,000 trades it showed old trades as the latest. The month run on the owner's server at 8 players passes that.

**The change (Factions-only, `PlayerRuntime.marketHistory`).**
- It reads every sale and buy in the Record, a page of 5,000 at a time on the ordered writer (`HISTORY_PAGE`), each page handed back to the server thread.
- It keeps only the item's last 10 trades (`HISTORY_SHOWN`), so no more than one page is held at once.
- What it shows is as before: each trade's kind, count, price and time, oldest first, or "No trades of … yet".

## Acceptance evidence

`NativeMarketHistoryGameTests.theHistoryShowsTheLastTradesPastTenThousand` (new), staged:
- **In a faction world:** 10,020 sales of cobblestone written to the Record, one coin each, then a last one of 7 for 42.42 coins.
- **Asked:** `/market history minecraft:cobblestone`, its lines captured.
- **Asserted:** 10 lines, the last that last sale.

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The new fixture | 0 of 3 (10 lines, the last "sold 1 for 1.00": the first 10,000's last) | 3 of 3 (the last "sold 7 for 42.42") |
| The fixture, `NativeMarketScreenGameTests` and `NativeFactionTradeGameTests` | — | 9 of 9, three runs |

No gameplay path reads the history; the corpus gate is not run for this change.

## Not claimed

- The time a month's history takes to read: every trade is read a page at a time off the tick, and its cost grows with the Record.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: asked about someone it knows ("what does Sol need?", "what is Sol doing?", "where is Sol?"), a
player with its voice on (E-20a) answers in the voice's words, told the ledger's lines by the same tiers as before and
costed as any reply. When the voice gives no line (its budget spent, a refused or failed reply, or no answer in time) the
player says the ledger's own words, as it does with no voice at all. An unknown name is ordinary talk.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `CompanionSocials`: `Hearing.about` (the ledger's own answer, apart from E-52's asks); with a voice available, a
    question about someone goes to the voice; the ledger's words when the voice gives none, also for a message that waited.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: hook lines (`about` reads `PeerRadio.answer`; `answer` is E-52's alone).
- gametest: `NativePeerLedgerGameTests.aTeammatesNeedsReachItsLedgerWithNothingSaidAndItAnswersFromThem`, to the
  owner's decision: it had checked that the question was answered without the model.

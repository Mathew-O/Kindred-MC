## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (MODES's trace, on main `99063e9`, in `NativeFactionShelterGameTests`' dug-in fixture). A SCOUT whose target was the chunk Ada stood in was cleared as arrived 583 times in 13 seconds:
- `FactionsPlayer.arrived` is true at once, since she is in the chunk;
- the arrival sets `decidedAt` to `Long.MIN_VALUE / 2`, so faction play weighs again on the next call;
- `FactionPlay.raid` chooses the same scout again.

**Why the same scout.** `raid` first asks to capture the chunk it stands in. When that capture is refused (its cost above its coins, or its reserve), the scout targets are the exposed enemy chunks it saw, nearest first, and the nearest is the one it stands in.

**The change (Factions-only, `companion-core`).** In `FactionPlay.raid`, the chunk it stands in is never a scout target; another exposed border it saw still is. An arrival still weighs again at once, so a claim or capture where it now stands is asked without waiting.

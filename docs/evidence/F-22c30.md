## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. The source is #70's CI, run 42533785406, attempt 1,
`NativeBringGameTests.aLoadedCartThatLosesItsWayIsSentOnFromNearWhereItRolledBack`:
- The loaded cart ran out of way short of the climb into the hall and rolled back fast.
- Hal was waiting on the rails behind it, as F-22c25 has it, and the cart went through Hal's body.
- It rolled on past the line's first rail into the cell Hal pushes from, off the rails (y -58.00, not -57.94).
- Pushed from there it went off sideways over the field, and the bring gave up: "stopped short of its cell", rail -1 of 25.
- Nothing stopped the line's open end. A player's body does not hold a fast cart.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime`, the bring's roll.
- **The stop goes down (`lineStop`).** As the loaded cart rolls away from the line's start, Hal lays two items of its own:
  - a rail in the cell it pushed from, joined to the line;
  - a block just beyond it on the line's axis, one of `SHUTTERS` (dirt, cobblestone and the like).
- **When there is no stop.** With no rail or block to spare, no room, or a line's start that is not straight, the line is
  left open as before, and the log says why. A derailment then still ends in the give-up.
- **A cart that rolls back** stops on that rail, still on its rails, against the block.
- **Pushing it on from the stop.**
  - Hal breaks the block. The break is an ordinary break, so the server's policy and any claim are judged at that moment.
  - Hal stands where the block was and pushes the cart from straight behind.
  - It puts the block back as the cart rolls away.
- **At the end of the bring:**
  - the rail is lifted with the line's own (`laid`);
  - the block is taken back first in `UNBRIDGE`, and its drop is picked up with the rest;
  - a break of the block refused 400 times over leaves it, and the log says so.

New fixture: `NativeBringGameTests.aLoadedCartRollingBackAtTheLinesStartIsMetOnItsFirstRail`.
- It is the wandered bring scene, with the loaded cart's first roll home turned back once. The turn comes short of the
  climb into the hall, at half a block a tick down the line.
- Fixture staging: that one change to the cart's velocity.
- It checks the bring, and that the cart never stood past the line's first rail off the rails.
- It logs when the cart comes back past the first rail and what it stands on, so a pass shows whether the stop was met.

## Scope and implementation

**Found:** on #359's CI (run 43850682824, attempt 2, job 133281193187),
`NativeOwnRanchGameTests.withItsCowsPennedItKeepsAPairOfSheepInAPenOfTheirOwn` failed "Its cows still penned: 3". The
log, with the sheep pen's post at (…598, −53, −4826108) and so its gate at z −4826102:
- The sheep run: "Shut the sheep in", "Feed a sheep wheat", "Put the food for the sheep away", then "Open the gate for
  the sheep" (one of the pair fetched again).
- At "Ranch leaving" (13:21:13) the lamb just born stood at z −4826101, outside the gate. At "Ranch done" it was still
  outside.
- The run ended "The sheep are not ready to breed again yet (2 in the pen, 0 ready, 0 in love)", with no lamb counted.
- The test's sheep check then read "2 sheep and 0 lambs" and was retried. Meanwhile the player bred its ready cows
  again, as it is meant to, and at 13:21:39 a calf made the cow check 3.

This is not F-22c91 (#414, the crafting table in the pen): no animal stood on a table.

**Cause, in core `Ranch`:** its breeding counts only young inside the pen. A young one born while the gate stood open
for a re-fetch, and out of it before the count, was never counted. The pair, both cooling, were taken for "not ready to
breed again". The lamb was never added to `penned`, so the look back on leaving (`escaped`) did not bring it in either.

**The change:** once its breeding has begun, the ranch remembers the young of its kind it saw then (`youngAtBreed`).
Any other young of its kind within reach of the gate is born of it:
- it is counted bred;
- it is penned, with the animals inside;
- the run leaves "A young … born in the pen".

The look back as it leaves then brings it in, as it does any penned one that slipped out.

**Nothing granted.** Changed files: `companion-core`: `Ranch`. Test: `RanchTest`.

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the fixture: `NativeAccountingGameTests.aRunningSkillStopsWhenItsHoursEnd` stops a break at the end
of its activity hours. Once the player reads idle, it asks for the break once more and expects it turned away for the
hours. But the stopped break's slice is settled to the store after the player goes idle, and until it is, all new work
is turned away for that ("Connect and resume a reconciled, idle native player; activity settlement must finish first").
That is the runtime's rule, and it is kept. The fixture asked once only, the tick after idle. On a slow disk the
settlement was still being written then, the one answer it saw was the settlement's, and it failed.

- **CI on PR #25** (`b2fe399`, run 42065936843): 560 of 561, this fixture the one failure, "Connect and resume … settlement
  must finish first" in the log right after the break was stopped.
- **Reproduced locally** on a 25 ms-per-operation store ([F-22c5](F-22c5.md)): 6 of 6 failed with the same words. On the
  ordinary store, 6 of 6 passed.

Changed file: gametest `NativeAccountingGameTests`. While the refusal it sees is the settlement's, the fixture asks
again. Its check is unchanged: the request must be turned away for the hours.

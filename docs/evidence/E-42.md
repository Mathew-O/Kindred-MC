## Scope and implementation

The owner's direction (2026-10-25): a player never walks past valuables. "It should dynamically understand value, what you should and shouldn't pick up and save for later": something of no use now may still be picked up and kept for later.

**What an item is worth** (`Worth`, core, pure). Reckoned as players do: what it would cost the player to get otherwise, in minutes of play, and what the player would do with it as it stands.
- **Kit better than its own** in that slot (`ShareOut.gain`, enchantments breaking a tie) is taken and put on: "it has no chestplate", "better than its leather boots".
- **What its run is short of** is carried: "its run is short of 2 iron ingots", "its run is short of 4 food". A run's wants are by item, or by food, blocks or a piece of gold armour for the piglins (E-07e).
- **What it has no use for yet but will** is taken and kept for later: gold before the Nether, an enchanted book, a saddle, a name tag, a run of rails, a diamond more. A spare iron pickaxe is kept; leather boots when it wears iron, or a gold sword, are left.
- **What is not worth a slot** is left: a rail or two, rotten flesh, cobwebs, blocks it has no want of, things it has no use for (a music disc).
- Worth falls off past what a player could use (the thirtieth diamond is worth a tenth of the first); blocks of a material are worth what they are made of (a gold block, nine ingots), raw ore what it smelts to.
- **How far out of its way** it goes: six blocks and twelve more for each minute a thing is worth, up to 64; nothing for what it would leave. A container it cannot see into is worth what one of its kind usually holds: a mineshaft's cart 42 blocks, a ruined portal's chest 54.
- **With no room**, what is worth least to it goes for something worth plainly more; never what its run is short of, nor kit better than its own.

**On the goal** (`PortalRunCampaign`, `Valuables`, `NativeLootAction`, new). After the drops it walks back for, and before the route's next step, by day:
- **A drop worth the way to it**, up to sixteen blocks off, is picked up; never one somebody threw, which was thrown to someone or thrown away by the player itself.
- **A structure's container nobody has opened** (a chest, trapped chest or barrel it has seen, or a chest minecart in a mineshaft's tunnels) is walked to and opened with a use click, which rolls its loot as for any player; what the value model would not leave is shift-clicked out, best first, as far as there is room, and the rest left in it. Each is gone to once. What kind of container a chest is comes from what stands round it: netherrack, magma, gold or crying obsidian, a ruined portal; a spawner, a dungeon.
- **Gold blocks** in sight, as ruined portals have, are mined with an iron pickaxe, up to 32 blocks off.

**Whose it is.** Only a container still holding its loot table is looted: one nobody has opened since the world made it. Storage anyone has opened, the player's own or another's, never has one and is never taken from (`RoamingEdits.lootDenial`, under the server's roaming policy: AI world edits on, protection compatibility known, the block loaded and not spawn-protected). Reading whether a chest has been opened does not roll it; nothing in it is looked at before the player opens it.

**Conservation.** Each loot is one recorded action: exactly what left the container arrives in the player's inventory, item by item, and nothing else of either moves, or the action is held for reconciliation.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- Outside the goal: a player building, farming or idle does not yet go for valuables in sight.
- The optional strategist is not yet told what the player holds and has stored by worth, nor asked what is worth fetching. Since (`fd789ed`, E-43): its report says what it has stored, as it last counted, with what it holds; by worth and what is worth fetching it is still not asked.
- What is stored for later stays in the inventory until room is short; storing it at home is E-43.
- Containers are known only when seen in the knowledge sweep; a structure's chest out of sight is not inferred.
- Only ruined portals and dungeons are told apart from any chest; mineshafts by their carts. Temples, shipwrecks and buried treasure are any chest for now.
- With no room it takes what fits, best first; giving up what is worth least for what is worth more (`Worth.makeRoom`) is not yet used in play.
- A live run reporting what was looted and what came of it.

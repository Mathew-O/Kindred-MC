In #442's log, when the ranch began "leading cows into the pen", both cows were already down in the sunken patch, at y −3 from the pen's post. Every walk up to a cow was refused ("No walkable route"), and the ranch ended "No cows in sight to bring in". Nothing it did was wrong: with both cows down there, there was rightly nothing to lead, as the test's own note on #73 says.

**Cause, in the test's staging:**
1. The test holds Daisy still (AI off) "until the pen stands", so that one cow is on the field and one in the patch when the leading begins.
2. It let her go as soon as `ranchStatus` read anything but "Crafting…" or "Building…".
3. `RanchRunner`'s progress reads "Starting" until the runtime takes the ranch's first step. That step comes through `ranchCandidates`, which is evaluated with the player's routine candidates, behind its survival needs and other routine work, under the attention scheduler.
4. Let go on "Starting", Daisy grazes through the whole craft and build, and can walk off the field's edge into the patch.

Locally, logged at the release on main's test:
- in the class, 10 of 10 runs let Daisy go on "Starting", at ticks 1453 to 1964, before any block of the pen stood;
- run alone, the first step came before the test's next tick, and she was let go only once the pen stood.

**The change (test only):**
- Daisy is let go only once the pen stands, read from the world as `RanchRunner.built` reads it: its gate where the gate goes, a fence everywhere else, 24 blocks in all. No status of the ranch's can let her go early.
- As she is let go, the test asserts that the pen stands.
- A pen that never stands fails with its own message, "The pen stood: N of its 24 blocks", rather than as "penned alone".

**Not a loosening:** this tightens the staging, so that it holds as its javadoc says. The test's checks are unchanged. Nothing is granted, and no check, bound or timeout is loosened.

Changed files: test only, `NativeRanchGameTests` (the release, its two checks, and the pen read).

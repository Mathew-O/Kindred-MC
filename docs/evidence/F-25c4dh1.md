# F-25c4dh1 — No rest to heal in the open with a mob on foot after it

Date: 2026-11-22
Agent / branch: Claude (MODES on MOVEMENT) / `claude/movement-1122-healrest`, from main `6749ed7e`; merged with main as `cadf3dcb` (main `8d4d30c2`)
Status: in progress (the PR's CI)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a hurt player in the open, with a zombie or another mob on foot coming after it within 16 blocks, no longer stands still for a four-second rest that the mob's arrival cuts short. It goes on with its work, and its fight-back and back-off reflexes still answer the mob when it comes. With nothing after it, or walled in, it rests to heal as before.

Found in F-25c4dh: "heal before going on" is a 4 s `native_wait`, taken when health is under 10 (under 18 while recovering) with food to heal on. Its "nothing after it" reads `threats()`, which is empty below six health by design. So the rest was taken while a mob on foot was after the player, and the "fight back first" interrupt cut it short. F-22c104a's second-zombie deaths come in this pattern.

Changed modules and files:
- companion-server-adapter `PortalRunCampaign`, at the heal-before-going-on point: if it would rest, but a mob on foot is after it within 16 (`huntedOnFootWithin(player, 16)`) and it is in the open (not `underground`, or more than 4 `openings`), it does not rest. One line per spell: `<name> hurt with a mob on foot after it in the open: no rest to heal, on with its work (health N)`. The field `healSkipped` keeps it to one line per spell.
- gametest `NativeBackOffFlipGameTests`: `hurtInTheOpenWithAZombieAfterItItDoesNotStopToRest` and its control `hurtInTheOpenWithNothingAfterItItRestsToHeal`, through `healRest`.
  - The stage: open stone with a wall 5 off, health 7, food 20.
  - The zombie is held 10 off the player each tick (STAGED), because one left where it stood would be outwalked past 16 by the player's work.
  - The staging is checked on the first tick held: at most 12 off.

Unchanged: the rest everywhere else, `threats()`, the fight and back-off reflexes, and every other campaign step.

### The cost, matched

Main's consoles do not log a mob's distance, its target, or whether the ground is open, the parts of the condition that decide a skip. So the comparison uses a stand-in: **a rest taken within 5 s of a fight or back-off goal against a melee mob on foot** (zombie, husk, drowned, spider, cave spider and the like).
- The stand-in covers all 21 of the branch's skips.
- It is unmatched on distance, target and open ground, so it is broader than the condition and takes in enclosed places underground too.
- Script: `heal-matched.py`, over the same three gate runs a side.

The whole situation, a rest or a skip after a melee fight:

| Deaths within 30 s | Branch | Main |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rest or skip after a melee fight | 6 of 85 (7%) | 7 of 67 (10%) |

Not worse on the branch: P(branch ≥ 6) = 0.85.

The mechanism: the skips are taken out of the riskiest rests.

| Deaths within 30 s | Branch | Main |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skips | 5 of 21 (24%) | — |
| Rests after a melee fight, matched | 1 of 64 (2%) | 7 of 67 (10%) |
| Other rests | 32 of 145 | 28 of 129 |

The skips against main's matched rests: 24% against 10%, one-sided p = 0.12. That is not clearly below, at the lead's bar of 0.10.

So the change is not worse on deaths. **The gain is fixture-only**: main 0 of 3, the branch 5 of 5, the control 5 of 5. The corpus shows the change neither worse nor better, and metric 4 did not fall.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player building comes all the way down a scaffold column before it walks off to the next
spot, and is never left with no way to the block it wants because of its own scaffolding:
- **Down a column:** it digs the block underfoot, lands on the next, and digs again, to the ground. It no longer plans
  the walk to the next spot while it is still dropping onto the next block, which set off from that block's top and left
  the rest of the column standing.
- **Hemmed in:** when nothing it can walk to reaches the block it wants, it first takes down what of its own scaffolding
  is in reach (a column's top at a time, the plan looked at again after each), and only then calls the block out of reach.
- **From a block's edge:** a walk starts on the block its body rests on, not on whatever stands beside it on the open
  side its middle is over.

Found: the roof-reach stops (#82's family), the most common red in CI. A build stopped "8 steps failed one after another",
each a walk that found "no walkable route". The navigator's own notes (`No route from … (expanded N, standing S)`) over
the 17 stops in the saved CI logs (runs 223, 298, 305, 309, 313 and 9):
- **Hemmed in (8 stops):** the navigator could reach only the one cell it stood in ("expanded 1", a seed cabin's last
  slab in run 9, a spruce cabin's stairs in run 305), or 27 to 36 cells (the keep, runs 9 and 223).
  1. Coming down a column, the runner plans again after each block dug from underfoot. Planned while the player was still
     dropping onto the next block, the step was a walk to the next column's foot, and it set off from that block's top.
  2. Each such walk left the rest of the column standing: 169 of the 726 walks to a column's foot in run 9 set off from
     above the build's floor, and 132 of 674 in run 305.
  3. Two or three such stubs round a cell beside a wall shut it in. The planner had no stance, scaffold or prop for the
     block and called it out of reach; the runner's walks round to each side found no route; the build stopped.
- **Started from the wrong cell (4 stops, "standing false"):** stood at a column's edge with its middle over the open
  side (x 0.87 or 0.95 into the cell beside, runs 298 and 9; the keep's planks, runs 305 and 313), the search began from
  the top of whatever stood next on that side, from which there was no way back up. The walk was to the very block it
  stood on.
- **Not covered here (3 stops):** below the floor in a hollow beside the build ("expanded 6000, standing true", runs 9,
  223 and 309).

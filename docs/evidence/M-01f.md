## Scope and implementation

Found in the Factions runs (factions2b, seeds 109 and 110, goal none). Each player logged about 1,000
`native_goto BLOCKED: No walkable route toward …`, the planner giving up at its node limit. The same target was tried
40 to 60 times: seed 109 Kindred toward 52 83 -60 45 times, seed 110 Echo toward 45 65 -50 59 times. The lines carried
no task label, so the routine that issued them could not be read from the log.

**The trace (2026-11-09).** Every ended task's outcome line now says where it came from. That is its campaign or build
step's label, or its life task's; or else `from <method>:<line>`, the routine that proposed it.
`NativeTaskCandidate` records its proposer when it is made (a `StackWalker` frame, no call sites changed). The
autonomous choice hands it to the dispatch, and a task asked for by a command carries none. The same label goes to the
run observer (`RunObserver.ended`), so an unattended run's record has it too. Behaviour is unchanged.

Changed: `PlayerRuntime` (`NativeTaskCandidate.source`, `dispatchSource`, `outcomeLabel`); test
`NativeOwnRanchGameTests.withItsCabinStandingItKeepsAPairOfCowsItHasSeen` (an observer: every ended task labelled).

**The set-aside (2026-11-10, LOOPS cause 1).** The routine was found from the trace. Seed 109 ran one game hour, no goal
set, on main `b01cac7` merged into #72's `dbd6eaa`, an eval-only local merge (`479c0d6`). It refused 494 faction walks
(`[from factionCandidates:9138]`) and 99 "Walk to gravel at …". Each target stayed within its own routine's back-off,
three refusals at most per gravel block or faction key. But neighbouring targets of one place were asked for in turn:
one block refused 20 times in two minutes over several faction keys, and one patch's gravel blocks were refused 3 each.
So the shared back-off is global and counts by cell:
- `companion-core`: `WalkSetAside` (new, pure). A target is set aside for ten minutes (12,000 ticks) after 3 refusals
  from within 8 blocks of the first, and only for a player still within 8 blocks of there. An arrival, or the player's
  own break or placement, forgets it. At most 256 are kept. `WalkSetAsideTest` has 5 tests.
- `PlayerRuntime`:
  - `walkOutcome` counts a `native_goto` BLOCKED "No walkable route" or "… is blocked" against the four-block cell of
    its target, and logs the set-aside once with the routine's label.
  - `withoutWalksSetAside` drops a walk to a set-aside cell from the routine candidates, whichever routine proposed it.
    It never touches the reflexes and life needs, and commands are not filtered.
  - Neither counts nor holds back a walk while the player is in water or lava, burning, short of air or under half its
    health (`walksMayBeSetAside`). A walk then is very likely its way out. The first push had no such guard: CI's
    `ReplayGameTests.echo-50149` drowned in all three replays (run 42748029429), and the replays' logs showed "Get out of
    the water: up for air" and "Climb out of the water: to the wall" set aside after three refusals while it sank.
  - `extraCandidatesForTest` is a fixture's own routine.
- `NativeWalkSetAsideGameTests` (new). The player is boxed in bedrock with no goal, and a routine with no back-off of
  its own offers a walk eight blocks off at every decision.

The routines' own back-offs (the campaign's per target, the faction walk's per key) are unchanged. The global one caps
what passes between them.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player on the portal run cooks the raw meat and fish it carries in its own furnace while the furnace is at hand and idle, as players cook before a trip, and waits for it before taking the furnace on. Cooked, a porkchop or steak fills eight where raw it fills three. It cooks only as much as the fuel it burns will cook, so none is left raw in a cold furnace, and not while raw iron waits for the furnace or with only the wood it keeps for a shield or a spare table to burn.

Found in B7's second pairs: no step of the run ever cooked. In the first run (strategist off) Kindred spent 18% of its time climbing out for food, hurt and with nothing left to eat.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunSnapshot` (core): `rawMeat`, the raw meat and fish carried, and `furnaceCooking`, its furnace has some to cook. A constructor with the old components is kept, with neither.
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core): a `COOK` step while its furnace is at hand, idle and empty, and a wait while the food cooks.
- `PortalRunCampaign`: the snapshot counts raw meat and what its furnace cooks; `cook` loads the first stack, as much as the fuel cooks; the furnace is not picked up to move on while there is meat to cook. `PortalRunCampaign` is changed on `modest-faraday` only in its hole-up lines.
- `NativePlayerBody.planSmelt`: a counted load puts in only the fuel that smelts the count (it put in as many pieces as items, so six porkchops would have taken six coal), and is matched to its fuel as an uncounted load was.
- `PortalRunPlannerTest`, `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one case and one fixture.

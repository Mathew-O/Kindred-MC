# F-34c1a — A person on the death screen is told of a member's death

Date: 2026-11-15
Agent / branch: Claude, Session B (claude/death-screen-told-1115)
Build: the branch's head (see the PR)
Status: complete

## The finding

RUNNER 2's keyed run f34c3-37adcdc (#346), in a faction world:
- Tester died at tick 15,589 and got its own notice.
- Echo, of Tester's faction, died at 17,207. The death was written as `faction.member_died` naming Echo, but never
  reached Tester. Tester was on the death screen, not yet respawned.
- Vanilla's "Echo was slain by Zombie" did reach Tester, so Tester was connected.
- It was not the Wire's 60-second dedupe.

## The cause

The Wire's roster (`KindredWire.roster`) left out every removed body. Vanilla takes a dead player's body out of the
world 20 ticks after its death (`LivingEntity.updatePostDeath`, `RemovalReason.KILLED`). The player stays in the
server's player list until it respawns. So a person on the death screen was off the roster, and was told nothing on any
channel.

## The fix

`KindredWire.roster` keeps a body that was removed because it died (`isDead()` and `RemovalReason.KILLED`) while it is
still in the player list. Every other removed body is still left out.

Which behaviour, and why: told at once, not on respawn. The M-06e contract tells "both factions' members online", and a
person on the death screen is online. Vanilla's chat reaches the death screen, as the vanilla death line in the finding
did. Nothing waits for a respawn, so nothing needs a queue or a second delivery.

A Wire change only: no body behaviour and no Record change.

The same rule keeps a dead AI player's body on the roster until it respawns. Hearing then only writes entries, traced
on this branch, and none touches the removed entity or starts an action:
- the receiver's inbox (`KindredWire.send`), read only by the watch page and the K panel (`toldLately`);
- its knowledge web (`KnowledgeWebs.told`, `WebFeed.told`);
- a teammate's update into its peer ledger (`PeerRadio.teamState`, `PeerLedger.update`);
- the delivery event in the World Record (`WebFeed.record`).

The roster reads the body's place (its coordinates) only to decide who is within earshot. Spoken lines go only to
persons (`Delivery.phrase`), through their connection, which the death screen keeps.

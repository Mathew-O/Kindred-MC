## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
- An operator sets the local hours a player may be active in with `/aiplayer limit window <start> <end> [name]`, or clears them with `/aiplayer limit window off [name]`.
  - Times are `18.00`, `18` or a quoted `"18:00"`. Brigadier refuses `:` in an unquoted argument.
  - An end before the start runs overnight. The hours are read in the player's accounting timezone.
  - `limit status` shows them.
- **Outside the hours:** a request releases its activity seat and the player is told "Outside its activity hours (…); they open …". Until then it is refused at once, as for a period not yet open (F-11e). The player is not stopped.
- **Inside the hours:** a slice never runs past their end. Dispatch reserves to the end of the hours it began in, and a renewal to the end of the hours it is in, from the player's latest saved limit. A running skill stops when the hours close, or when they are narrowed while it runs.
- **After a zone edit:** the hours are read in the zone the current period is kept in. That is the old zone until the period ends, matching how F-11e keeps the period itself.
- **Continuing a paused native skill:**
  - Before, it reserved to the period's end regardless of the hours. A refusal (a spent cap, a period over) was treated as an uncertain acknowledgement, which halted the player.
  - Now it reserves to the end of the hours it is in, and refuses outright when they or the period are over.
- **Renewals near a deadline (found while testing this slice):**
  - A renewal reaches no further than its deadline. So in the last 1.5 s before one (the end of the hours, or the period's end), each renewal left too little and the next tick renewed again: a durable budget write every tick.
  - A renewal is now made only when it gains at least a quarter second over the permit held. F-11c's long break still finishes at real time (below).

Changed modules/files:
- `ActivityLimit` (core):
  - An optional `Window`.
  - `editCap`, `editOfflineWork`, `editTimezone` and `editWindow`, each the next revision with every other field kept. The four limit setters use them, so no edit drops the hours.
- `V9__activity_windows.sql`: two nullable columns on `activity_limits`.
- `SqliteCompanionStore`:
  - Reads and saves the window.
  - A half-written window (one column set) is refused as uncertain rather than read as all day.
  - Schema version guard raised to 9.
- `ScheduleClock` (scheduler):
  - `windowInterval`: `activeInterval`'s window logic, in the period's own zone. `activeInterval` now delegates to it, unchanged in behavior.
  - `nextWindowStart`: passes over a day whose window a daylight-saving gap swallows.
- `PlayerRuntime`:
  - Dispatch: the window check and deadline.
  - `renewalDeadline` and `RENEW_GAIN_MIN`.
  - `continueSkillEntry`.
  - `setLimitWindow`.
  - `limitStatus`.
  - `activityLimitForTest`.
- `PlayerCommands`: `limit window`.

Dependencies satisfied: F-11e (dispatch through `chargeableBudget`, the wait gate), F-11c (renewal).

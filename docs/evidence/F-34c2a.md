## Scope and implementation

F-34c2 will write `territory.refused` where a refusal stops a real attempt on land a faction owns. Session A agreed on
condition that its bound comes first, as its own child on F-31b1's pattern. That bound is this slice. Nothing writes
the kind yet.

**The change:**
- `TerritoryRefusals` (core, new) names the kinds `territory.refused` and `territory.pruned`, and what may be tried:
  break, place, explode, mob_change, hit. Both kinds are registered in `EventKinds`.
- `SqliteCompanionStore.pruneTerritoryRefusals(check, olderThanMs, max)` runs on the ordered writer:
  - it removes up to `max` refusals older than the cut, in one transaction;
  - it writes one `territory.pruned` tally for each game day they were written on: each kind of attempt, the total,
    the first and last event id, `day_start_ms` and `older_than_ms`;
  - it prunes nothing unless the check it is handed agreed, as the action bound;
  - a pass that takes its full share leaves the rest to the next, so a day's figures are the sum of its tallies.
- Schema 15 (`V15__territory_refused.sql`):
  - It adds an index of the refusals by time.
  - It recreates the delete trigger as at 14, plus one allowance: a `territory.refused` event may be deleted once its own
    `real_time_ms` is past `REFUSAL_BOUND_MS` (30 game days) by the database's clock.
  - The database itself refuses a refusal's delete inside the bound, so a prune cut there fails and keeps everything.
  - Every other kind, `territory.pruned` included, still refuses a delete, and every update is refused.
  - This build refuses 16, and builds on main refuse 15 by their own check, so it cannot be undone.
- `PlayerRuntime.pruneRefusals` runs after the action bound's passes, on the same pass's check, with the same 30 game days.
- `tools/soak/report.py` counts refusals the bound pruned as pruned, not lost (`refusalsPrunedSince`,
  `refusalsLostBeyondPruned`), apart from the action bound's tallies.

Storage, in the month run's terms (the lead's correction, 2026-11-15): about 2,160 game days a real month.
- Written over a month: about 10 MB in normal play, about 432 MB at worst (8 factions at F-34c2's daily cap).
- Held at any time: at most 30 game days of it, about 6 MB at worst.

Not body behaviour: storage only.

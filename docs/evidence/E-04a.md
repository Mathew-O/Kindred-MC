## Scope and implementation
Implements native 2x2 player inventory crafting and crafting table creation ([NativePlayerBody](file:///c:/Users/matmu/Downloads/Kindred%20MC/companion-server-adapter/src/main/java/dev/kindred/fabric/NativePlayerBody.java)) to advance earned survival progression without cheats or manufactured items:

1. **Native Player ScreenHandler Crafting Execution**:
   - Executes 2x2 recipe crafting via [NativeCraftAction](file:///c:/Users/matmu/Downloads/Kindred%20MC/companion-server-adapter/src/main/java/dev/kindred/fabric/NativeCraftAction.java) and [NativePlayerBody.craft2x2](file:///c:/Users/matmu/Downloads/Kindred%20MC/companion-server-adapter/src/main/java/dev/kindred/fabric/NativePlayerBody.java).
   - Ingredients are drawn directly from owned player inventory slots (0..35) into the `PlayerScreenHandler` crafting grid (slots 1..4 / `craftingInput`).
   - Triggered through `PlayerScreenHandler.onContentChanged(craftingInput)` which evaluates vanilla recipes in the world, followed by quick-moving the result from slot 0 via `onSlotClick(0, 0, QUICK_MOVE, player)`.
   - Vanilla `CraftingResultSlot.onTakeItem` decrements input ingredients and unlocks recipes authentically.
   - Any recipe remainders or unconsumed items in `craftingInput` are immediately returned to inventory, ensuring 0 items lost.

2. **Durable Ledger Admission and Fencing**:
   - Fenced by [ActionAdmission](file:///c:/Users/matmu/Downloads/Kindred%20MC/companion-core/src/main/java/dev/kindred/core/ActionAdmission.java) (`native_craft`) with single-use PREPARED -> DISPATCHED -> VERIFIED transitions.
   - Captures [InventoryEvidence](file:///c:/Users/matmu/Downloads/Kindred%20MC/companion-server-adapter/src/main/java/dev/kindred/fabric/InventoryEvidence.java) before and after mutation. If inventory changes between prepare and dispatch, the craft is aborted without transfer.
   - Preconditions require paid activity authority ([PaidActivitySession](file:///c:/Users/matmu/Downloads/Kindred%20MC/companion-scheduler/src/main/java/dev/kindred/scheduler/PaidActivitySession.java)), active `ActionArbiter` lease, and `NativePlayerBody.Intent.CRAFT` permission in `PlayerRuntime.nativeMutationAllowed`.

3. **Strict Item Conservation Verification**:
   - `NativeCraftAction.verifyConservation` compares before and after inventory snapshots:
     - Exact consumed ingredients decrease by their expected counts.
     - Crafted product increases by exactly the recipe yield (e.g. 1 oak log -> 4 oak planks; 2 planks -> 4 sticks; 4 planks -> 1 crafting table).
     - All other inventory items and components remain unchanged.
     - Discrepancies block for reconciliation (`UNCERTAIN`) without speculative retry.

4. **Autonomous Candidate Selection**:
   - [PlayerRuntime.evaluateNativeCandidates](file:///c:/Users/matmu/Downloads/Kindred%20MC/companion-server-adapter/src/main/java/dev/kindred/fabric/PlayerRuntime.java) evaluates owned materials:
     - Gathered logs -> autonomous plank craft.
     - 4+ planks with no crafting table -> autonomous crafting table creation.
     - 2+ planks with no sticks -> autonomous stick crafting.

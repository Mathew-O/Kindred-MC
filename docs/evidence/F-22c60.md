## Scope and implementation

Found: `NativePortalCampaignGameTests.goalMakesObsidianFromLavaWithPouredWaterAndRecoversTheWater`, "The obsidian was
mined and picked up". It failed on #161's and #162's CI re-runs; #162 is docs only. From #162's run 42948818509:
- The player mined the obsidian and took its water back.
- Within a second, the goal flipped between "Wait for the drop to settle" and "Explore the cave toward lava". The
  waits completed one after another.
- It then went exploring with "Make and mine obsidian (0/10)": the drop was never picked up.

It does not reproduce locally:
- The class alone: 97 of 97, 3 times, on main and on the commit before #121.
- Eight copies in one batch with three cores kept busy: 8 of 8.
- The whole native suite on main with a per-tick log of every check: 703 of 703.

So `pickUpLoose` left out a drop it would normally pick up, and its waits did not hold, for a reason the log cannot
show. Its checks are: in sight, not a blocked cell, not watched by a mob, not in flowing water, within the wait's
reach, and the obsidian site no longer in use.

Changed files (diagnostics only):
- `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign.leftBehind`: when `pickUpLoose` returns nothing with a drop worth
  walking back for within eight blocks, it says at info, once per drop, which checks kept it. It gives the drop's cell
  and position, in sight, blocked, watched, in water (still or flowing), how long its settle wait has run, whether it is
  within the wait's reach, the obsidian site and where the player stands. Nothing else changes.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: the fixture's "The obsidian was mined and picked up" now also names how much is
  held, where any obsidian lies, the lava's cell, where the player stands and the goal's line. The assertion itself is
  unchanged.

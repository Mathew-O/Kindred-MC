- The east gold block, at x …865, in the plot's own chunk, was mined and picked up.

**Cause, in the fixture:** the test server forces only the chunks of a test's structure (`StructureTestUtil.forceLoadNearbyChunks`). It sets no simulation distance, so entities elsewhere tick only in a player's own chunk (as `FixtureGround.simulated` says). The test laid its portal outside its 8 by 8 plot and kept nothing there ticking.
- Locally the west drop did tick. In all 3 of 10 main runs that logged it uncollected at the bound, it was age 71, delay 0, landed, and picked up after.
- Why the player's chunk did not tick it on CI is not read from the log.

**The change (`NativeLootGameTests`, test only):**
- The portal test moves from `EMPTY_STRUCTURE` to `FixtureGround.FIELD` (80 by 32 by 80). Its ground is laid round `portalOrigin(context)`, the structure's (17, 1, 6), in place of where the player first stood. The player is set there as the ground is laid.
- Its kept ground, 16 west to 7 east and 5 north to 5 south of it, lies wholly in its own structure. The placement line shows it inside the plot across, and one below its floor, as Bring's does (F-22c119).
- A new test shares the scene: `aGoldBlocksDropNoPlayerCanPickUpIsLeftAndTheCheckCatchesIt` (batch `lootPortalGoldLeft`), the control. The west gold block's drop is staged as CI's was, one no player can pick up (STAGED: its pickup delay made endless as it is born; on CI it was never ticked, so its delay never ran down; it never became pickable, so neither does this one). It passes only once the player has gone on to its next goal with that gold block left lying.

Nothing is granted, and no check, bound or timeout is loosened. No product code changes.

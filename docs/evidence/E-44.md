## Scope and implementation

The owner's direction (2026-10-25): "When a player finds a chest that belongs to another player in non friendly modes it should ask the model to make the decision on taking a hostile action and taking valuables."

**Whose a chest is.** Only what the player saw: in a match whose sides are opponents (a manhunt, a duel), it notes a chest, trapped chest or barrel open beside another player, within that player's reach and in its own sight (`PlayerRuntime.noteChestUse`, every half second). Nothing else makes a chest anyone's. Outside a running match, a chest it sees used is nothing to it.

**Opponents.** `MatchRegistry.opponents`: both in one running match whose mode has PvP, and on opposite sides (a manhunt's runners against its hunters; the two players of a duel). Two hunters are on one side; racers in a speedrun are never opponents; nobody outside the match is anybody's.

**The question** (`Raid`, core, pure; `Strategist.ask`, companion-model). Asked once for each chest it saw used, apart from the planning lane's advice and within the same per-minute rates and daily token budgets, one question at a time for each player, off the game thread (`StrategistBridge.ask`). It says what the player knows and nothing of what is in the chest, which it has not seen: its name and side, whose the chest is and their side, how long ago it saw them, how far it is, how far the nearest opponent in sight is, its health, what of note it holds and what its run wants. The model answers RAID, LEAVE or WATCH, with a sentence why. Claude answers by structured output, other providers by a JSON schema with the choices as an enum (JSON mode where a provider has no schemas, with one repair); a reply that is not one of the choices is no answer.

**Its own rule**, with no model, or no usable answer, on the same facts: too hurt (health under 8), leave it; an opponent in sight within 24 blocks, watch it; a manhunt's runner raids only what is within 16 blocks of its way and otherwise leaves it, its run first; further than 48 blocks, watch it; otherwise raid it. A RAID or WATCH holds two minutes and a LEAVE ten, then it thinks again; a raided chest it lets be until it sees it used again.

**The raid.** A RAID is walked to and raided by the loot action (E-42) in its raid form: opened with a use click, what the value model judges worth taking shift-clicked out, best first, the rest left, exactly conserved in the durable ledger. Never while an opponent it can see stands at the chest. The roaming rule for a raid (`RoamingEdits.raidDenial`) needs the owner still its opponent in the running match, and the server's AI world edits on. What it took it tells its own side only: whoever controls it and the players on its side of the match, never an opponent (`sayToSide`): "I raided Rue's chest: 6 bread, 4 diamonds and 10 gold ingots."

**Found and fixed alongside.** When a hunter's fight killed the last runner and so ended the hunt, the match took back the hunter's tracking compass mid-fight, and the fight's conservation check, seeing an item gone, held the hunter for reconciliation. A hunt's compass is the match's, handed out and taken back by its rule (D6): the fight no longer counts it (`HuntCompass.handed`).

## Scope and implementation

The owner's direction (2026-10-25): villager stations "for ones with good trades". Whom to keep, and how a player gets a librarian worth keeping.

**Whom to keep** (`Trades.worthKeeping`, `keepWorth`, core, pure). A villager is worth a cell of its own when its offers are worth more than ten minutes of play to the player as it stands: the profit of each purchase worth making, once (a second Mending book is worth less than the first), or as many times as a restock allows of what its run wants many of (ender pearls for the eyes); and a share of its renewable income. A librarian with Mending is worth keeping; one with Bane of Arthropods II is not; a farmer's crops alone are not.

**When to reroll** (`Trades.reroll`). A villager that has never traded and is still a novice can have its offers rerolled: vanilla gives such a one new offers when its job block is broken and put back. Kept when worth keeping; rerolled while it has tries left (24 rerolls); else given up.

**A librarian of its own, in play** (`PlayerRuntime`, the reroll). As players make one. By a villager with no job (not a nitwit), within eight blocks and in sight, the player puts down a lectern it holds, on open ground beside it, where the edit policy allows. The villager takes it up by vanilla's own rules and is a librarian. The player looks at its offers at its screen (E-45b), never trading, since a trade would lock them. Not worth keeping, it breaks the lectern (an empty lectern may be broken, as an empty furnace may: nothing is lost), picks it up, steps back if picking it up took it onto the spot, and puts it down again once the villager has let the job go, which rerolls its offers. It stops when the librarian is worth keeping ("This librarian sells a Mending book for 12 emeralds and a book: I'll keep it."), or gives up after its tries, the lectern taken back ("No book worth keeping from this librarian in 25 tries."). A villager that does not take the lectern up in fifteen seconds is given it again, twice at most, then given up on, said ("The villager won't take up my lectern; I've taken it back."). Never trading with a villager it is rerolling; under the server's AI world edits only; never in a match.

**Found alongside and fixed.** A refused step of the reroll (the break, at first, by the rule against breaking block entities) was proposed again every tick, unlogged: refusals are now logged and counted, and three in a row end the reroll with the lectern taken back. A wait after a refused placement shared the placement's task id and was held back with it: each step has its own id.

Changed modules/files: `Trades` (`worthKeeping`, `reroll`, `keepWorth` counts what a run wants many of), `TradesTest`; `PlayerRuntime` (`rerollCandidates`, `rerollLooked`, the reroll's state, refusals counted), `RoamingEdits` (an empty lectern may be broken), `NativeRerollGameTests` (new).
Dependencies satisfied: E-45a's value model; E-45b's look at a villager's screen.
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: none. The reroll's state is kept in memory for the session.

## The gap
- **The recorder** (`DeathReplays`) keeps each scene's `dimension`.
- **`ReplayGameTests.play`** staged every scene in the test world, the Overworld, whatever its recorded dimension. A stall in the Nether, with its heat, its water that boils away and its own mobs, would have played on Overworld ground.
- **Why it matters now:** e40c3's two Nether stalls (#271: 303's blaze fight, 8675309's reconciliation hold) could not enter the inner loop as Nether scenes. RUNNER 2 kept their Nether regions, but no replay was captured at either moment.
- **The corpus today:** all 420 scenes that record a dimension are Overworld ones. This change readies the harness for the first Nether or End scene; it changes no scene in the corpus.

## The change (gametest: `ReplayGameTests`)
- **`recordedWorld`:** the world a scene was recorded in, when it is not the test world's own dimension. Null for an Overworld scene, or one recorded before scenes kept their dimension.
- **`stageIn`,** for a Nether or End scene:
  - the scene the test placed is copied into that dimension: its blocks, their data and its mobs;
  - it goes at the test's own x and z, at the height it was recorded at (64 for a scene with none);
  - the copies left in the test world are taken out;
  - it is walled with barrier, as the test world walls every test, and roofed, so the dimension's own lava and mobs beyond the scene stay out;
  - its chunks are kept loaded until its test has ended, and let go then;
  - the dimension's own ground under the box and its walls is saved before, and put back as its test ends, with the box's mobs taken out. That box is registered with F-22c75b's audit as ground the test keeps (`FixtureBounds.kept`).
- **The player is made in the test world, as before,** beside the scene as placed there. A native player is made only in the Overworld: the runtime refuses a body made elsewhere ("Native save dimension differs from metadata; reconcile before loading"), and that check is kept. Once established, it is moved into the scene's dimension, at its recorded place, as staging already moves every replay's body to where it was recorded.
- **Side effect of the move:** vanilla grants a player entering the Nether "We Need to Go Deeper". It is a replay's throwaway player in a test world; nothing else is given.
- **Overworld scenes take the same path as before.** The additions they see are one check step and one log line ("made in minecraft:overworld").
- **A fixtures-only hook:** `MUST_STAGE_IN_DIMENSION`. A player named in it must be made in its scene's recorded dimension, or the replay fails at its start.

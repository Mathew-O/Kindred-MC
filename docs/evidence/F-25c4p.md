## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player falling far with a water bucket in its hotbar pours the water before it does anything else, even fight a mob its goal wants fought. Before, a goal's urgent steps (fight back, back off, take cover, hole up) could outrank the water. They are ranked with how much the player likes its goal's work, mining. Each such step was cut short for the fall, chosen again, and the fall went unbroken.

Found in the long live run on `a286cc8` (seed 8675309, 240,000 ticks, strategist off):
- Echo had both buckets, one of water, and an iron chestplate. It was digging down for the cast's lava, one iron short of its flint and steel.
- On a ledge at Y=7 with no way on, its goal jumped it down to break the fall with water ("Jump down from the ledge and break the fall with water"). The jump's walk was cut short for the fall, as it should be ("Falling: the fall to be broken with water").
- Then its goal chose "Fight zombie" at Y=1, which was cut short for the fall in turn, and "Fight creeper" at Y=-15.
- At 14:49:16 Echo hit the ground at Y=-18 and died ("Echo fell from a high place"). The walk back for its kit, from spawn, was refused.

The water ranks as urgent, at 100. A goal's urgent step ranks as urgent at 99, plus the player's liking for mining at six tenths of its weight: from -3 to +3. Echo likes mining at 95, which puts its goal's urgent steps at 101.7; Kindred, at 70, puts them at 100.2; Sol, at 60, at 99.6.

Changed modules/files:
- `PlayerRuntime`: while a fall is to be broken with water, the water is the only step the decision is offered.
- `NativeFallGameTests`: one fixture.

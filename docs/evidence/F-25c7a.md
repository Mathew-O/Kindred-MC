## The loop
- **What it does:** on a travel step, the plan pass alternates two steps:
  - "take workstations along: Pick up the crafting table"
  - "on the way, for what comes later: Place a crafting table"

  Every step completes, so no timeout ends it.
- **How often**, counting spells whose step labels have both:

## The two callers
1. **`PortalRunCampaign.pickUpWorkstation`** ("take workstations along").
   - Runs on a travel step: `TRAVEL` holds the route's last step (here FILL_WATER and MINE_IRON at the Iron stage).
   - Holds back only for a fishing rod's makings or a queued COOK.
   - Picks up its own table within 6 whenever none is carried.
2. **`FollowUps.onTheWay` → `realize(BED)` → `PortalRunCampaign.craftAtTable(body, "white_bed")`** ("on the way, for what comes later").
   - With nothing crafted and no table near, it set the carried table down.
   - It never asked whether the bed could be made at a table.

## What each carries over, and why neither holds
- **The trigger,** in both builds: the third white wool. Sol at 12:22:32 (303, before) and Kindred at 12:57:58 (505, after) each pick one up, and the next step is "Ready crafting table".
- **The BED gate admits a bed the table cannot make:**
  - The gate is `craftable(BED)` and `wood(player) >= 3`, where `wood = planks + 4 × logs`. The core's BED recipe lists only the three wool.
  - At the table, `craft("white_bed")` finds "not the makings". Sol logged "cannot make white_bed at the table in reach" once, and Kindred five times. That line fires at most once a minute.
  - Inferred, not yet seen: it held logs but no planks. The consoles show no inventory. The fixture logs its own and settles it.
- **Nothing carries over between passes:**
  - With the table down, `craft()` gives nothing, so `onTheWay` gives nothing. Its placing completed, so it was never set aside.
  - The next pass picks the table up: the step is travel, and no table is carried. The pickup knows of no pending craft.
  - Then `craftAtTable` sees no table near and one carried, and sets it down again.

## The change
- **`Makings` (core, pure).** It takes a recipe's slots (each a set of item ids) and the holdings by id, and gives:
  - `HELD`: every slot filled;
  - `PLANKS_FIRST`: filled once the logs held are planks, four a log, by the 2x2 grid;
  - `NONE`: neither.
- **`NativePlayerBody.makings`.** It gives the vanilla recipe's slots and the inventory to `Makings`. The recipe lookup comes out of `planCraft` into `craftingRecipe`, so both use one lookup.
- **`craftAtTable`:**
  - `PLANKS_FIRST`: crafts planks first.
  - `NONE`: sets no table down, crafts none, and logs "sets no table down for … : not the makings" once a minute.
  - Otherwise: as before.
  - The BED gate's count of planks it can make (`wood`) stands, now that the planks are made first.
- **Unchanged:** the pickup's travel rule.
- **The guide's recipes name what is no need (the lead's addition).**
  - `MinecraftGuide.Recipe` gains `others`: item ids or tags, `#minecraft:planks` among them. No new `Need`: needs.txt's names are save keys.
  - The values:

    | Recipe | `others` |
    | --- | --- |
    | BED | `#minecraft:planks` 3 |
    | SHIELD | `#minecraft:planks` 6, `minecraft:iron_ingot` 1 |
    | IRON_SWORD | `minecraft:iron_ingot` 2 |
    | STICKS | `#minecraft:planks` 2 |

    BOW, ARROWS and GOLD_ARMOR were exact already.
  - `FollowUps.craftable` checks `others` against what is carried, a log as the four planks it makes, consistent with `Makings`. The bed's old `wood >= 3` gate is folded into it.
  - **`NeedsAssessment.ready` is unchanged:** it reads needs alone. A recipe with `others` is still "the adapter's call", made in `craftable` and again at the table by `Makings`. Making it refuse any recipe with `others` would have stopped the bed follow-up from ever coming up.
- **A guard: `NativeRecipeGuardGameTests`.**
  - For every recipe the guide knows, vanilla's from the server's recipe manager is compared with the guide's needs and `others`.
  - Each slot is read as the need its items satisfy, else planks, else the item's id. Counts, the yield and the table are all compared.
  - A missing or extra ingredient fails the test, naming the recipe.
- **A known gap, kept exact in the guard (not applied, by the lead's ruling):** vanilla's iron sword takes a stick, and STICKS is a need. Putting `STICKS 1` into IRON_SWORD's `inputs` would:
  - make `NeedsAssessment.assess` break an iron-sword want into one more stick, so the stick count queued grows and sticks are gathered for it;
  - make `ready(IRON_SWORD)` true once one stick is held, iron or none, since the iron is in `others`, which `ready` does not read. Today it is never ready from needs alone, so the planner's "on the way" would take an iron sword up with no iron.

  It wants its own slice, with `ready` taught `others`, if it is wanted.

## F-25c7b: the second half, found by the replays
- **The replays:** e40a21's 18 table-loop stuck spells. Each replay is matched to its spell as the first replay of that seed and player within 400 ticks after the spell's tick; it is captured about 160 ticks later.
  - They were imported for these runs only, not committed, into main 32b304a (with only the fixture added) and into this branch.
  - Each side made 9 runs of those 18 alone.
- **v1 (`Makings` and `craftAtTable`) alone did not help.**
  - Scene-runs failed: main 36 of 162, v1 43 of 162. One-sided Fisher for v1 worse: p = 0.22.
  - Tables placed a run: main 111, 76, 77, 76, 107, 58, 75, 75, 52; v1 75, 77, 76, 75, 76, 79, 79, 75, 77.
  - It ended one looper (35 placements on main, its bed now made from planks crafted first). Two remained, e.g. `20261030-sol-80040`.
- **Why two remained.** Those two held the bed's makings outright: white wool, spruce logs and planks, in a near-full pack (34 of 36 slots).
  - The table went down for the bed, but `pickUpWorkstation` comes before `FollowUps.onTheWay` in the plan pass, on any travel step with no table carried.
  - So the next pass picked the table up before "Craft white bed" had a pass: 37 times a replay.
  - The staged fixture happened not to meet that race. Its log does not say what held the pickup off there.
- **The change: a bounded hold.**
  - `craftAtTable` notes the recipe it sets a table down for.
  - `pickUpWorkstation` leaves its own table while `NativePlayerBody.makings(recipe)` is `HELD`, and never past 400 ticks.
  - The craft consumes the makings, so the hold ends with it.
  - The travel rule is otherwise unchanged, and no timeout is widened.
- **v1 with the hold, 9 runs:**
  - Scene-runs failed: 23 of 162, against main's 36. One-sided Fisher for better: p = 0.042; for worse: p = 0.98.
  - Tables placed a run: 3, 4, 3, 3, 4, 4, 3, 4, 4 (mean 3.6), against main's mean 78.6. Every run is below every main run.
- **`NativeTableLoopGameTests`, now 2 tests, passed 3 of 3 with the guard and the follow-up fixtures (8 of 8 each run):**
  - The craft before the pickup: the log shows "Place a crafting table", then "Craft white bed" COMPLETED, then "take workstations along: Pick up the crafting table". It made no pickup before the bed.
  - After the bed: one table carried and none standing in the fixture's ground, so the table count is conserved.
  - **The expiry (staged).** The bed's makings are held outright (3 white wool, 3 oak planks). Once its table is down, the fixture takes the wool, as though spent elsewhere. The hold ends with the makings. The table was taken along 97, 95 and 97 ticks after the wool went, none was standing, and no bed was made.
  - The 400-tick bound alone, with the makings still held and the craft never made, is not staged.

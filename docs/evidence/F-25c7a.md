## The loop
- **What it does:** on a travel step, the plan pass alternates two steps:
  - "take workstations along: Pick up the crafting table"
  - "on the way, for what comes later: Place a crafting table"

## The two callers
1. **`PortalRunCampaign.pickUpWorkstation`** ("take workstations along").
   - Runs on a travel step: `TRAVEL` holds the route's last step (here FILL_WATER and MINE_IRON at the Iron stage).
   - Holds back only for a fishing rod's makings or a queued COOK.
   - Picks up its own table within 6 whenever none is carried.
2. **`FollowUps.onTheWay` → `realize(BED)` → `PortalRunCampaign.craftAtTable(body, "white_bed")`** ("on the way, for what comes later").
   - With nothing crafted and no table near, it set the carried table down.
   - It never asked whether the bed could be made at a table.

## What each carries over, and why neither holds
- **The trigger,** in both builds: the third white wool. Sol at 12:22:32 (303, before) and Kindred at 12:57:58 (505, after) each pick one up, and the next step is "Ready crafting table".
- **The BED gate admits a bed the table cannot make:**
  - The gate is `craftable(BED)` and `wood(player) >= 3`, where `wood = planks + 4 × logs`. The core's BED recipe lists only the three wool.
  - At the table, `craft("white_bed")` finds "not the makings". Sol logged "cannot make white_bed at the table in reach" once, and Kindred five times. That line fires at most once a minute.
  - Inferred, not yet seen: it held logs but no planks. The consoles show no inventory. The fixture logs its own and settles it.
- **Nothing carries over between passes:**
  - With the table down, `craft()` gives nothing, so `onTheWay` gives nothing. Its placing completed, so it was never set aside.
  - The next pass picks the table up: the step is travel, and no table is carried. The pickup knows of no pending craft.
  - Then `craftAtTable` sees no table near and one carried, and sets it down again.

## The change
- **`Makings` (core, pure).** It takes a recipe's slots (each a set of item ids) and the holdings by id, and gives:
  - `HELD`: every slot filled;
  - `PLANKS_FIRST`: filled once the logs held are planks, four a log, by the 2x2 grid;
  - `NONE`: neither.
- **`NativePlayerBody.makings`.** It gives the vanilla recipe's slots and the inventory to `Makings`. The recipe lookup comes out of `planCraft` into `craftingRecipe`, so both use one lookup.
- **`craftAtTable`:**
  - `PLANKS_FIRST`: crafts planks first.
  - `NONE`: sets no table down, crafts none, and logs "sets no table down for … : not the makings" once a minute.
  - Otherwise: as before.
  - The BED gate's count of planks it can make (`wood`) stands, now that the planks are made first.
- **Unchanged:** the pickup's travel rule.

## Scope and implementation

**What it brews** (`Brewing`, core, pure). The potions a run wants, in the order it wants them: fire resistance for the Nether (magma cream), strength (blaze powder) and slow falling (phantom membrane) for the dragon, healing for any fight (glistering melon slice). It keeps three of each, a stand's load. `Brewing.next` gives the next brew from what it holds: the first potion it wants, has fewer than three of and holds the ingredient for; its three awkward potions brewed into it if it has them, else three water bottles made awkward with nether wart; none when it has nothing to brew with. `toFill` says how many glass bottles to fill at water first, up to three, and only while there is something to brew; `needsFuel` says when the stand's fuel is spent.

**The steps** (`NativeBrewAction`, new, `native_brew`). A recorded action in three modes, each as a player does it:
- `FILL`: a glass bottle used on still water within reach (`NativePlayerBody.fillBottle`). Verified: one glass bottle gone, one water bottle come, nothing else changed.
- `LOAD`: its stand opened with a use click, three bottles clicked into the bottle slots, the ingredient into the top slot, and, when the fuel is spent, one blaze powder into the fuel slot (`NativePlayerBody.useStand`). Verified: what left the inventory is exactly what the stand gained, counted by item and, for potions, by what each holds.
- `UNLOAD`: the stand opened and its three bottles taken out. Verified: what came into the inventory is exactly what left the stand's bottle slots.

Otherwise the step is held for reconciliation. Using a stand is a workstation's use under the server's edit policy, and the stand is claimed for the step.

**In play** (`PlayerRuntime.brewCandidates`). Out of any match: with nether wart and an ingredient it wants, bottles filled at still water close by first, three; then its stand loaded with the next brew; the brew waited out nearby (twenty seconds, as vanilla's 400 ticks); what it brewed taken out. With no stand standing but one in hand, it puts that down beside it first. Each step keeps one task id for as long as it is the step (`stepTask`). It says "I brewed three potions of fire resistance."

Changed modules/files: `Brewing`, `BrewingTest` (new, `companion-core`); `NativePlayerBody` (`fillBottle`, `useStand`, the `BREW` intent), `NativeBrewAction` (new), `NativePlayerSkills` (`native_brew`), `PlayerRuntime` (`brewCandidates`, `potionSlots`, the finish hook), `NativeBrewGameTests` (new).

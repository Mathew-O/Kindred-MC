## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player sent out of a hall through an open door no longer walks into the door's open leaf and stays stuck in the doorway.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-core `route/Terrain`: `leaf(x, y, z)`, the side of a passable cell that a thin leaf lies along. It is non-null only for an open door when doors count as a way through; a closed door reads null.
- companion-core `route/Navigator`:
  - A flat move does not cross a leaf on the side it leaves by, nor on the side it enters by, at feet or head height. A diagonal checks both of its L-shaped ways round.
  - A step-up also checks the raised cell's head height.
  - The memo is cleared with the passage memo.
- companion-server-adapter `NativeNavigator`: the leaf comes from the door's own collision box. Only hand-openable, open doors give one.
- companion-server-adapter `WorldDig`: passes the leaf through.
- gametest `NativeRaisedDoorwayGameTests` (new; registered in `fabric.mod.json`).

Unchanged: closed doors, block order, placement, the hall plan, timeouts and retry caps.

## The route, confirmed on main first

The scene comes from E-71b1's trace: a hall whose floor is one up from the outside ground, with its door open. The body is placed in the doorway, on the door's lower cell.

On main the route leaves the doorway going WEST. It steps up onto the one-course wall top beside the door, at (−1, +1, 0) from the door's lower cell, then runs west along the wall. Logged route: `[-9762002,-57,…94, -9762003,-56,…94, -9762004,-56, …]`.

That first step crosses the open leaf, which lies along the cell's west side (facing NORTH, hinge LEFT, open). The body jumps into the leaf, and every later walk logs "Route … is blocked" in the doorway, about 958 times a case.

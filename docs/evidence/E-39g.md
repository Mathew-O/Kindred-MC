## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: the End fight's needs now include a bed (`Need.BED`, with `Need.WOOL`): three wool of one colour, from sheep it hunts, and three planks, crafted at a table on the way, as the bow and arrows are. Wool counts by its commonest colour, so mixed wool does not pass for a bed's. In the portal room, before going through, the End fight's planner puts `SET_SPAWN` first: the player sets the bed down on two free blocks on solid tops within six blocks of where it stands (never on the portal, its frame or lava), walking up to them when out of reach, and uses it. `native_bed` works only in the Overworld (beds explode anywhere else), checks the server's edit policy for both blocks and claims them, spends exactly one bed, and is verified only once vanilla has set the respawn point at the bed. Vanilla sets the point before its check for night, so this works by day too. Back from the End with the dragon alive (a death there), the player is restocking: the End fight's gate is reopened, and it gathers the fight's kit again (a bow and arrows, blocks, a water bucket, food) with the gathering's own clocks before going back through. Without a stone pickaxe or better, the run's early stages make tools first. A bed already set down and used by the portal counts as the bed wanted.
Changed modules/files: `Need` (`BED`, `WOOL`), `MinecraftGuide` (the bed's recipe, wool's source, the End fight's bed), `EndFightPlanner` (`SET_SPAWN`), `EndFightSnapshot` (`bed`, `spawnByPortal`); `NativeBedAction` (new), `NativePlayerBody` (`placeBed`, `useBed`), `NativePlayerSkills` and `PlayerRuntime` (`native_bed`), `FollowUps` (the bed and wool chunks, held counts), `EndFightLeg` (`setSpawn`, `noteWhere`, restocking), `StrongholdLeg` (`restock`, `spawnByPortal`); companion-model's guide and need list; `NeedsAssessmentTest`, `EndFightPlannerTest`, `StrongholdRunPlannerTest`; `NativeEndGameTests.aBedSetDownAndUsedSetsTheRespawnPoint`, `NativeFollowUpGameTests` (the follow-up fixture now carries its bed).

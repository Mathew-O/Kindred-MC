## Scope and implementation

The owner's note of 2026-10-23 set how a build's needs work. They are a queue: the player does not halt everything to fetch one item at a time. It builds what it can and fills in what it has, then fetches the shortfall in one trip, using its time well across building and crafting. What is urgent to finishing, or easy to get (such as items in a chest it knows), it gets at once. The owner's example: building an enchanting room without enough bookshelves to ring the table, it finishes the room first, fills in what it can, and then goes for more bookshelves. This slice is E-08c ("a blueprint's bill of materials gathered and crafted through the needs queue"), in that order rather than everything before building.

**The queue** (`BuildNeeds`, core, pure). Each pass starts from the build's bill less what is placed, what the player holds, and what its own storage holds. From those it plans what to take from storage, what to gather (logs and cobblestone) and what to craft, inputs first. The next step, in this order:
- **Easy, at once.** What its own storage close by holds (within 24 blocks) is taken before anything is placed. What it can make from what it carries, with no walk at all, is made.
- **Then building.** It builds what it can with what it holds.
- **Stuck for blocks: one trip for all of it.** First its storage, then all the logs (or stone) the rest takes in one go, then the crafts. The trip ends when everything left is in hand.
- **Nothing it can do gets the rest.** It ends there and says what the build still wants: "hut 5x5: built but for 8 oak log, 5 oak slab, 20 oak stairs".

It knows vanilla 1.21.1's recipes for the blocks and parts a build is made of:
- Every wood's planks, stairs, slabs, fences, gates, doors and trapdoors.
- Sticks, a crafting table, chests, ladders and a wooden pickaxe.
- Cobblestone stairs, slabs and walls; stone bricks and their stairs and slabs.
- Glass panes, bookshelves and furnaces.

**Its own storage.** `/aiplayer storage at <x y z> [player]` assigns a container as a native player's storage. Only its administrator may assign it.
- **Saved.** The storage is saved with the player's setup, durably, as a resource patch is. The player is stopped until resumed.
- **Taking from it.** A new skill, `native_take`, takes items from the assigned storage within reach. Each take is one recorded action: the ledger, a claim on the chest, and conservation (what leaves the chest arrives in the inventory, nothing else changes).
- **The edit rule.** A roaming player (one with no work area) may use its assigned storage under the server's edit policy (`RoamingEdits.storageDenial`), and no other container beyond its own crafting and smelting. The policy is checked again just before the items move.

**Gathering and crafting for it.** These are the goal's own skills (`PortalRunCampaign`):
- **Trees.** Trees in sight within 64 blocks, never the build's own blocks.
- **None in sight.** It walks out over the ground to look, up to four times a trip.
- **Crafting.** At a table close by, one it carries set down, or one it makes (`craftAtTable`).
- **Seeing trees.** A build looks around as it goes, as a goal does.

**Back from the trip.** The planner's "no way to reach" now names the block. A player back from fifteen blocks off walks over to the build and plans again from beside it.

**The fixture.** Mason builds a 5×5 hut of 112 blocks: 40 oak planks, 34 cobblestone, 12 oak logs, 20 oak stairs, 5 oak slabs and a torch.
- **What Mason holds.** The 40 planks, 14 of the cobblestone, 4 of the logs, a crafting table, dirt to scaffold with, and bread.
- **Its storage.** A chest twelve blocks off, assigned with `storageSetAt`, holds the torch and 20 cobblestone.
- **The trees.** Four trees of six logs each stand 15 to 22 blocks to the east.
- **What must hold:**
  - the hut is exact;
  - the chest is emptied of the torch and cobblestone, with nothing placed while it still held them;
  - at least 79 blocks stand placed when the trip starts;
  - logs are cut from the trees;
  - the shortfall is fetched in exactly one trip.

**Hand controls.** Each reverts one rule. Four make the fixture fail; the fifth does not:

| Control | Result |
| --- | --- |
| No needs at all (the build given nothing to draw on) | Failed: it waited, short of blocks ("Built: hut 5x5: Nothing in hand to place") |
| Its storage close by not taken at once | Failed: "Its storage taken from first: 58 placed before" |
| No building before the trip | Failed: it set out before building, and the fixture's time ran out with 46 of 112 placed |
| A trip for each item (one item fetched, then back to building until stuck again) | Failed: "The shortfall fetched in one trip: 4" |
| The build's own blocks counted as a source | Passed. It walked four times toward its own corner logs, and each walk was refused for want of a route; then it went to the trees. This fixture does not show the guard failing. |

Found on the way:
- Assigning storage failed for native players: it went through the legacy setup path, which refuses native bodies. It now goes through the native setup path.
- The take was refused, since the container rule needed a work area. A roaming player now uses its assigned storage under the server's policy.
- The first gather went for the hut's own corner logs. A build's own blocks are now never a source. With this guard reverted, the walks to them found no route, so its control does not fail the fixture.
- Inside the hut's walls, no tree was in sight. It now walks out to look.
- Each time the walk was planned, a look was counted, so four went by before it had walked once. Only finished walks count now.
- A build without a goal never cut a tree: the gathering's edit policy was set only by the goal's own passes. A build now gives it the server's policy.
- Back from the trees, the planner had no target to walk to and the player waited. The unreachable block is now named, and it walks over.

## Later, with M-01c

Building a player's own base (M-01c) showed what more the needs took, all in [M-01c](M-01c.md)'s fixture:
- **Stone.** It takes stone in sight within six blocks, else digs a staircase down for it, as the goal does. It first walks eight blocks off the build's site and never digs the site, a block round it, or three below it.
- **Climbing out.** With everything in hand, it climbs out of where it dug before building again.
- **The trip ends** when everything is in hand, however the last of it came in.
- **A table set down to craft at** is recognised as its own even when the build planned again while it was being set down.

- A block's drop is set down within a quarter of a block of its middle.
- The boat's box reaches the front to z 4.18, so a drop is born inside it only when set down at z under 4.305: about
  11% of births.
- Being inside is not enough on its own. The caught drop also had a northward throw.
- Staged with the caught drop's own birth (place and throw), the loss came 1 run of 3. Staged inside the box with no
  throw, 0 of 3.
- The rest varies with where the boat, the cart and the villager are on that tick.

Status: complete (2026-11-15)

## The miscount
- **On F-25c4di1a's gate (#355), run 3,** the report gave "Replay players still acting after their own batch: … 1".
- **The one was `Rc0794a1_1`.** Its only line after its batch was "break at …: its collection ended (authority ended) after 48 ticks, its own drops uncollected". It was logged in the second the next batch began, beside another player's "dropped before it ran … stop=true".
- **What writes it:** `NativeBreakAction.close()`, when a break still collecting its drops is revoked (`noteUncollected("authority ended", …)`). That is the stop ending the action, not the player acting.
- **What the count skipped already:** the stop's "dropped before it ran … stop=true" lines (F-27a1). It did not skip this one.

## The change (`tools/corpus/report.py`, `leftover`)
- **Two more of the stop's lines are skipped,** as "dropped before it ran … stop=true" is:
  - "its collection ended (authority ended)": `NativeBreakAction.close()` ending a break still collecting its drops;
  - "The fight was interrupted": `NativeFightAction.close()` and `NativeDragonAction.close()` ending a fight in progress. Neither writes it anywhere but `close()`.
- **Harness only:** no game code changes.

## Tests: `tools/corpus/test_leftover.py`
- **Five tests:**
  - a stop revoking a break in progress, after its batch, counts 0;
  - a stop dropping an action, after its batch, counts 0;
  - a stop ending a fight in progress, after its batch, counts 0;
  - an action after its batch still counts 1;
  - nothing within its own batch counts.
- **Before each skip:**
  - with neither skip, the break's test fails (1, not 0);
  - with the break's skip only, the fight's test fails (1, not 0).
- **After:** all five pass, and so do all `tools/corpus` tests (`python3 -m unittest discover -s tools/corpus`).

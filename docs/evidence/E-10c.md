Status: complete

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
- The third starter player is called **Echo**, titled "Deep Dark Artisan & Cave Scholar". It was **Vesp** ("Evening Star Artisan & Deep Scholar"); the owner chose the new name on 2026-10-28.
- `/aiplayer roster spawn` suggests and spawns Kindred, Sol and Echo. The K panel, the control deck and the messages name Echo.
- A world saved before the rename keeps its one player. Spawning Echo, or the whole roster, finds the saved Vesp and reconnects it, with its body, items and skin, instead of making a second player. It keeps the name Vesp in that world: nothing saved is renamed.

Changed modules/files:
- Core: `CanonicalRoster`.
  - `ECHO_NAME` and `ECHO`; the former name is kept in `ECHO_FORMER_NAME`.
  - `RosterPreset` gains `formerNames`, `answersTo` and `forName`.
  - `deterministicId` is seeded by a starter's first name, so the rename keeps Echo's id.
- `CanonicalRosterSkins.ECHO` keeps the key and image `vesp`, so signed textures in `config/kindred_skins.properties` and profile markers made before the rename still match. `forKey` also accepts `echo`.
- `SignatureWeaponPolicy` uses `answersTo` for Echo and Sol.
- Adapter:
  - `PlayerRuntime`: `rosterEntry` takes the preset, `canonicalBotIdForControlSlot` uses it, and the spawn messages name the saved player.
  - `PlayerCommands`: suggestions.
  - `KindredPanelService`: a slot shows the starter under the name it has in that world.
  - `KindredControlScreenHandler`, `KindredPanelScreen`: text.
- Tests: `CanonicalRosterTest` (new), `CanonicalRosterSkinsTest`, `SignatureWeaponPolicyTest`, and `NativePlayerRuntimeGameTests.starterEchoReconnectsAPlayerSavedAsVespInsteadOfMakingASecond`.
- Art: `assets/skins/Echo.png` (was `Vesp.png`), `assets/faces/echo.png`.

Dependencies satisfied: E-10a (roster and its skins), E-10b (skin selector).
Schema/configuration changes: none. No saved key, table or file is renamed.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

Not tested: the full native suite was not rerun. The change is limited to how the roster finds its players and what it calls them; the runtime's other fixtures in the same class passed in all three runs.
Known limits:
- A world saved before the rename still shows its player as Vesp. Renaming a saved player would need a tested migration of the stored name, which is not done.
- The starters' personalities in `CanonicalRoster` are not applied to the players the roster spawns. That was already true before the rename and is unchanged.

## The treed line and the give-up (claude/treed-line-1123)

### The line (log only)

`PortalRunCampaign.defend`, at each change of the treed spell (`treedSince`):

```
<name> treed spell at <x, y, z> in <dimension>: <hoglin|zoglin> <d> away (<d> across), <h> below, <after it | near, not after it>, <in | out of> strike reach; armed <yes|no>
<name> treed spell at <x, y, z> still on after <N> ticks, <W> wait decision(s), <S> strike(s): <the same>
<name> treed spell over at <x, y, z> after <N> ticks, <W> wait decision(s), <S> strike(s); last: <the same>
```

- **"Still on"** comes at TREED_GIVE_UP and every 12,000 ticks (10 game minutes) after, so a spell that never ends
  shows.
- **"Wait decisions"** are decisions, not two-second waits: the step is decided again often, 56 times in a 201-tick
  spell in the fixture.

### The give-up (the lead's (a), 2026-11-23)

- **When:** up for TREED_GIVE_UP (1,800 ticks) with a hoglin still after it, and out of strike reach. A hoglin within
  3 blocks and the player armed is struck from above first, as before.
- **What it does:** it goes on with its work, as it already did once no hoglin was after it. It logs
  "<name> treed spell given up at … after N ticks with the hoglin still after it: …; going on (F-25c6f)", the gate's
  marker.
- **The hoglin it gave up on** is left be (`ignoredFoes`, the creeper's pattern) for another TREED_GIVE_UP. That
  holds for `hoglinAfter` and `hoglinNear` alike, unless the hoglin closes in within HOGLIN_CLOSE (4). Then it is
  fought or got away from, as any other.
- **No new constant.** The lead's (b), moving within strike reach, is left.

## Scope and implementation

**Found (REFACTOR, F-22c84 cause #2):** in CI on #354 (run 43719957093), #355 (run 43720019149) and #357 (run 43722834395,
attempt 1), the bring under the shut trapdoor walked Hal to its stand about 2,000 times in a row, about 6,000 ticks, until
the cart's and the boat's items on the cell's floor despawned at age 6000.

**Cause, in `PlayerRuntime`'s bring KEEP, under the trapdoor:**
1. The stand is asked for to within a quarter of a block (`squaredDistanceTo(stand) > 0.0625`), and the walk to it is a
   centred `native_goto`, which ends within 0.4 (`NativeGoToSkill.CENTRE_WITHIN`). Stood between the two, every walk ended
   where it began, and was asked for again.
2. The while given under the trapdoor (`UNDER_TRAPDOOR_TICKS`, 100) counted only from the step in, set after the stand
   check: with the stand never reached, the hold had no bound.
3. What lies in the cell counted only rails and carts: the bring's own boat item was neither fetched nor said.

**The change (three parts, each measured below):**
- **(1)** The stand asked for to within 0.2, and walked to within 0.2 (`native_goto`, `range` 0, `partial` false,
  `centre_within` 0.2). Not 0.25: the walk in to the front, one block wide, takes a body 0.6 wide only from within 0.2 of
  its line (`native_walk`'s footprint check at ±0.3); from 0.2 to 0.25 off it was refused and the keep went wrong.
- **(2)** The while given counts from the first walk to the stand too. Kept whatever the ablation shows: a hold with no
  reachable bound is the defect.
- **(3)** The bring's own boat item counts among what lies in the cell (`ItemTags.BOATS`).

**Nothing granted.** Changed files: `companion-server-adapter`: `PlayerRuntime` (the bring KEEP under the trapdoor). Test:
REFACTOR's `NativeBringGameTests.aThirdOffItsStandHalStillTakesUpTheCartAndBoatFromTheCell` (batch `bringOffStand`).

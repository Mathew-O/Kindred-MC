# F-22c13 — A swim out to a drop in the water is not a dip into deep water

Date: 2026-11-05
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/ci-green-1105 (CI fully green, F-22)
Build: on top of `b3f1590`; recorded in [validation](../validation.md)
Status: complete (native)

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. Taken up with the E-40 lead (owner of the water paths), who agreed the fix and asked for it
to land here. `PortalRunCampaign.wetDip` (F-25c4az) counts a walk the plan gave out just before it turned to getting out
of deep water. A third such walk for one place sets the place and its column aside. It also counted walks that were
meant to end in the water:
- the walk to a stump standing in a pool, followed at once by the chop from the water (F-25c4as);
- the swims out to the log come down afloat ("Pick up the log afloat"), whose target is the drop's cell, which here was
  the stump's own cell.

Three such walks set the stump's cell aside. The log floating in it then counted as `blocked` in both `woodAfloat` and
`pickUpLoose`. The player went ashore without it and walked off to explore.

- **CI on PR #25:** `NativePortalCampaignGameTests.withNoWoodItChopsATreeStandingInWaterFromTheWater` failed in both
  runs on `b3f1590` (42030222090, 42030233893) with "the log gone, no wood held".
- **Diagnosis, with temporary logging that was not committed:**
  - the drop logged as "keep true near true see true blocked true" for seconds on end, with no failed task;
  - the block came from wetDip's set-aside, after "Walk to logs at <stump>" and two "Pick up the log afloat" walks.
- **Why only sometimes:** on a fast store the log is usually picked up by contact before the third count. The CI
  runner's slower disk, imitated here with a 25 ms sleep per store operation (as in [F-22c5](F-22c5.md)), lets the
  plan alternate between swimming and getting out several times before a task starts.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign`. A walk is not counted as a dip in two cases:
- when the plan in the water goes on to a log it cuts from there;
- when it is a "Pick up …" of a drop whose cell is water.

Ordinary walks that end in deep water still count. F-25c4az's case is such a walk: iron seen across a lake that the
player cannot climb out of.

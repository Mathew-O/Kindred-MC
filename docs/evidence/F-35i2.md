## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (M-06i2c1's review). `FactionsPlayer.atSurface`, which says whether a player is at the surface for its faction walks' wait (F-35i: the refused walk's record, the wait, and places set aside as open water), read the top of its column from the world's height map (`MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES`). That is world data the player has not perceived. A roof over its head at a tunnel's mouth kept it "below", and water over its head counted as a roof.

**The change.** `FactionsPlayer.atSurface` is now the sky's light at its feet (`atSurface(int skyLightAtFeet)`: any light of the sky reaches it), as M-06i2c1's `saw` already reads it. Its three callers are unchanged.

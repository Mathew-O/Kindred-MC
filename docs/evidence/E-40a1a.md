## Scope and implementation

**Found:** in the busy-work census of step 8's consoles ([e40a25s-busywork](eval/e40a25s-busywork.md),
`claude/corpus-data-e40a25s-1121` at `fe0be8cd`), 505 on Echo let go of an iron sword it had made for Sol 24 times:
- 13 after six failed steps;
- 11 with Sol out of the team's range.

There were 78 walks blocked "from handOverStep". It recurs on 707 off and 101 off.

**The cause:** a hand-over let go by `revokeTask` is reopened (`task.reopen()` sets it OPEN). `shareOutFinds` then
claimed every OPEN or REVOKED hand-over it gives, at once, as soon as its reserved stock sufficed. A new find's share-out
could also grant the same piece to the same teammate again. So each new find of iron promised Echo's sword to the
unreachable Sol again, and Echo walked for Sol again, six blocked walks at a time.

**User-visible behavior:** after a player gives up on a hand-over, it does not promise the same piece to the same teammate
again until something changes:
- the teammate comes nearer: back within the team's range, 8 blocks nearer than when it let go, or within reach and in
  sight;
- the teammate's kit changes;
- the piece is not yet made and it holds more of the material.

Then it takes the hand-over up again, and logs "takes up handing over … again" with the reason.

A hand-over let go by a stop or a lapsed hold is taken up again at once, as before.

**Changed files:**
- `PlayerRuntime.shareOutFinds`:
  - a hand-over it let go itself is not claimed while `stillLetGo` holds;
  - a find's share-out leaves out a grant of the same piece to the same teammate;
  - the record is dropped when the task is claimed, done or failed.
- `PlayerRuntime.handOverStep` and `handOverStepEnded`: each give-up records, with `letGo`:
  - the teammate's distance (`Double.MAX_VALUE` if it is not in the same world);
  - the teammate's kit;
  - how much of the material it holds.
- `PlayerRuntime`: new `LetGo` record, `LET_GO_NEARER`, `letGo`, `mateOf`, `letGoTo`, `stillLetGo`, and the entry's
  `handOversLetGo`.
- **Gametest:** `NativeTeamShareGameTests.aHandOverItLetGoIsNotPromisedAgainUntilSomethingChanges`, batch
  `teamShareLetGo`.

Nothing is given or moved by the code: it only holds back a claim and a grant. It reads only the player's own inventory and
its teammate's position and kit, as the hand-over and the share-out already read them.

**Schema/configuration changes:** none. The let-go records live in memory only. After a restart a let-go hand-over is
claimed again once, and let go again after its six steps or waits.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- **The live check:** E-40 iteration 27's consoles, per seed:
  - the hand-overs held after a let-go;
  - those released, by trigger;
  - those never released.

  Any let-go never released in iteration 27 reopens this row.
- **Restarts:** the let-go records are not saved. After a restart a let-go hand-over is tried once more.
- **A teammate that never comes nearer** and never changes its kit keeps the piece held. It is still the giver's to use,
  with its stock reserved until the project ends.

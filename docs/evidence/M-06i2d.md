## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (CI, twice: #391's run 43816972536 attempt 1, job 133131131453, and #405's run 43851384322 attempt 1, job 133244525968). `NativeFactionBaseGameTests.withItsGoalRunningAFounderBeginsItsCabinOnItsOwnLandAndAWalkWaitsForIt` failed "It never set out east toward the walk while its cabin went up, the walk waiting: went at …, 0 ticks in, build oak cabin: 0/222 placed". It did not reproduce alone on main `576db13` (9 of 9).

**What the CI logs show.** In both, as the cabin began, its faction play ended a walk it had under way (`native_goto COMPLETED [from factionCandidates]`) and claimed a tenth chunk, one further east than the nine staged round its centre, and the cabin's stand was on that chunk:

The walk that ended was its own claim's, under way before the test staged its scout; the staged scout never moved it. Neither batch has a "Reservation expired before acknowledgement" line (0 and 0), and #405's six-second pause in its log (11:55:57 to 11:56:03) is twenty minutes after this batch ended: not the F-22c101 family.

**The cause.** The check read "gone east" as standing on a chunk more than one east of its land's centre: the nine staged chunks. Its faction may claim more as it builds, and did, so standing on its own tenth chunk read as setting out toward the walk. Intermittent: it hangs on whether that claim lands before the cabin begins.

**The change (test-only).** "Gone east" is now standing east of its land's centre on a chunk its faction does not hold by its claims as recorded at that tick (`projected().claims()`), not the staged nine. The test's flow moves into `cabinAndWalk`; its check, its bound and its time limit are as they were. A twin, `itsLandGrownEastItStandsOnItsOwnNewChunkAndAWalkStillWaits`, stages Red also holding the chunk two east of its centre and sets it down there as the walk is asked.

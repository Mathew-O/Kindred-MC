## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: anything an AI player tosses to someone lands just past their feet, within their reach, whichever
way the thrower is facing. That covers a delivery (E-01), a hand-over to a teammate (E-40a), a food offer's or gift's
yes (E-53, E-21a) and an ask in chat (E-20b). It never lands inside the thrower's own reach, where the thrower would pick
its gift straight back up.

Found:
- **Symptom:** a delivery between two AI players sometimes failed with the receiver holding nothing. In CI on 2 November
  and alone on main's code it failed in 1 of 3 runs, two of the three fixtures at once.
- **Cause:** the toss meant to aim the thrown stack at the receiver, but it looked the stack up by its thrower. A drop
  that keeps no owner (`dropItem(stack, false, false)`) has no thrower, so the lookup found nothing and the aim was never
  applied.
- **Effect:** every toss flew where the thrower faced, about two and a half blocks. A delivery worked only when that
  happened to be at the receiver's feet.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `NativePlayerBody.tossTowardPlayer` aims the stack `dropItem` returns.
  - Its speed comes from the distance (`TOSS_CARRY` 13.2 blocks a unit of speed, measured in the game).
  - The landing point is 0.6 past the receiver and at least 1.6 from the thrower (`TOSS_PAST`, `TOSS_CLEAR`); the speed is
    capped at `TOSS_MOST`.
- gametest: `NativeGiftGameTests.aTossLandsAtTheFeetOfWhoeverItIsForWhicheverWayTheThrowerFaces` (new).

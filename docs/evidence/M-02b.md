## Scope and implementation

The owner set the manhunt's roles on 2026-10-23: everyone starts from scratch. The runners beat the game quickly but reliably, not at speedrun pace, while evading the hunters. The hunters kill the runners while gearing up as the game goes on. This slice brings M-02a's AI players to those roles.

**The runner's pace.** An AI runner plays the dragon goal at its own steady pace (`OPTIMAL_SURVIVAL`, the playbook's normal ways), not the speedrun pace a race uses. A race's runner still goes at speedrun pace (M-03a). Its own pace comes back when it is out, as before.

**The runner keeps its distance** (`evadeCandidates`):
- **Moving off.** A hunter it can see within twenty blocks comes ahead of its goal. It runs off away from the hunter, sixteen blocks at a time at a sprint. Where the way straight off is shut, it goes at an angle (45 or 90 degrees either side), to ground it can stand on.
- **Back to the goal.** Out of the hunter's sight or reach, it goes back to its goal.
- **Caught.** Within arm's length (3.2 blocks) there is no getting away, so it fights the hunter with its best weapon, under the same strike rules as every match (D7).
- Each new way off is logged ("Rue keeps away from Hal, 13.9 blocks off: toward ...") and counted.

**The hunter gears up** (`hunterArmour`):
- **The kit first.** Once its kit is made (an iron sword and a shield, M-02a), a hunter puts on any armour it carries.
- **Then armour from its iron.** It makes the next piece of iron armour its ingots allow before following the needle on: a chestplate (eight ingots), leggings (seven), a helmet (five), boots (four). This is the order of protection for the iron. A piece is never made for a slot already holding iron or better.
- **At a table.** The armour is made at a crafting table close by, one it carries set down, or one it makes. This is now one helper, `PortalRunCampaign.craftAtTable`, also used by the follow-up queue.

Changed modules/files:
- `PlayerRuntime`: the hunt's runner pace in `startRace`, `evadeCandidates`, `hunterArmour`, `evasions`.
- `PortalRunCampaign` (`craftAtTable`); `FollowUps` (uses it).
- Fixtures, `NativeManhuntGameTests`:
  - the first now also checks the runner's pace;
  - the kit fixture extended to armour (`aHunterMakesItsKitThenArmourFromItsIronBeforeItHunts`, renamed from `aHunterMakesItsKitFromItsIronBeforeItHunts`);
  - an evasion fixture (new).
Dependencies satisfied: M-02a, E-41a (the fight).
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: none.

## 2026-11-05: blocking pursuit

Agent / branch: Claude / claude/modes-1105-2, on top of M-00 (`b55bef2`).

**What it does.** A runner going through a way one block wide, with a hunter in sight coming along it three and a half to sixteen blocks behind, shuts the gap behind it. It puts in two of its own filler blocks, feet first: cobblestone, cobbled deepslate, dirt, stone, andesite, diorite, granite, tuff, netherrack or deepslate. Then it runs on.
- **Where it does not.** Only where the gap is one wide (solid on both sides at the feet and the head) and has a floor. On open ground or in a wide way the hunter would only walk round, so nothing is spent.
- **Its own blocks only.** They come from its hotbar, readied there first if they are further in its pack.
- **The edit policy.** Each place goes through the world's edit policy at the moment of placing, as any other.
- **The rule's code.** It is in core (`PursuitBlock`). The adapter (`PlayerRuntime.shutBehind`) runs it in `evadeCandidates`, before the run away.

## 2026-11-05: a head start, gearing beyond iron, and no share-out to an opponent

Agent / branch: Claude / claude/modes-1105-2.

**The runners' head start.** `/aiplayer match manhunt <runners> vs <hunters> headstart <seconds>`, from none (the default) to two minutes.
- It is announced with the compass rule at the start.
- Until it is over, every hunter is held where it stands, human or AI alike: its walking and jumping are taken away by a modifier on its attributes, not saved, and removed when the head start ends or the hunt does. An AI hunter does nothing meanwhile, as a held human can do nothing.
- Nobody may strike: the registry holds back the leave to strike (`MatchRegistry.heldBack`).
- **A new attack hook.** A strike by one player at another in the same unfinished match is refused unless its rules allow it now (`PlayerRuntime.matchForbidsStrike`, an `AttackEntityCallback`). That covers its lobby, its countdown, a head start, the same side, and a mode with no striking. It is the same for a human's swing as for an AI player's. Outside matches the server's own rules stand.

**Gearing beyond iron.** A kitted hunter makes, in order of worth: a diamond sword, then diamond armour from the diamonds it holds, then iron armour. It never makes what it has as good or better, and it keeps three diamonds while it has no diamond pickaxe, which its goal's obsidian needs. Armour it carries that guards better than what it wears is swapped on. Gold stays on in the Nether.

**Fixed: finds shared with an opponent-to-be.** A find was shared out with the team, and the team's run divided, among players in a match that had not started yet: its lobby or its countdown.
- **What happened.** The diamond fixture's hunter promised its diamonds to the runner it was about to hunt: "I've got 13 diamonds: a diamond pickaxe, a diamond sword and a diamond chestplate for Fox". It then made the chestplate for Fox instead of wearing it.
- **The fix.** Neither the share-out nor the division takes in a player in a match that has not ended (`matches.active`).
- **Fixture first.** The fixture failed on it twice before the fix (the share-out logged both times) and passes after.

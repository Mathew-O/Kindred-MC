## Why
- **In `Digger.toward`, its staircase refused,** the tunnel's heading was set again on every call from where the lava lies: the major way, east while the lava's x was ahead.
- **Past the lava's x, the major way flipped west,** back along the tunnel just dug. An advance there is allowed, since rock it could dig lies along that way.
- **The next call's major way was north,** over the open slot to the cavern. The tunnel turned clockwise from the drop, to east.
- **So it advanced west and east for good.** The fixture's trace of its feet on main's dig code: (+7, 0, +3) at 2,807, (+6, 0, +3) at 2,817, (+7, 0, +3) at 2,827, … every ten ticks to the end.
- **Every advance moved it:** the goto's target was never its own cell. So a refusal for an advance that leaves the feet where they were would never have fired here.
- **Why CI met it:** the source south of the line, not load and not a tie in time. Set out for that source, the loop comes at any placement (below).

## The change (`Digger`, companion-core)
- **`toward`, its staircase refused:** the way along its level is kept from call to call, as the tunnel turned it, for the same lava cell (`alongLevel`, `alongLevelFor`).
  - It is set from the lava's major way only when there is none kept, or the lava cell changed.
  - After the tunnel's step, the heading it ended on is kept. Where the kept way is refused (rock it can't dig or set aside, liquid at or round the cell, an island's edge, a drop it won't bridge), the tunnel turns clockwise as before, and the turn is kept.
- **Cleared:**
  - when a staircase step opens (`digDown` gives a step);
  - when the lava cell changes;
  - on a new dig. `forgetTunnelled()`, which the campaign calls on relocating and at a new spell (`countSpell`), clears it too.
- **Nothing else in the dig changes.** No timeout or retry cap, and rescue's one-per-spell rule is untouched.

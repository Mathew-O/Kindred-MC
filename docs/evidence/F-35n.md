## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an AI player climbing out from under water that gets no farther for twenty steps stops that
climb and goes on to its goal's other ways, and does not come back into the same box while it is still within eight
blocks of where it gave up. Before, on 505 in E-40's iteration 21 (`e40a21-2354e67`) Kindred, at Y -51 under an aquifer
wanting water for its cast, went round within seven blocks for whole stuck spells: 518 to 570 of the climb's lines and 284
to 407 of them out from under the water in each of nine replays of that spell, and no bucket. The climb gave up only once
it was twenty blocks along, which it never got.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-core: `Digger.climb` (the under-water branch: `UNDER_WATER_STALLS` = `UNDER_WATER_REACH` = 20 steps with
  no new farthest point hands back; `MOVED_OFF` = 8, no re-entry within it of where it gave up, whatever the time;
  `underWaterGaveUpAt()` for the campaign's "no way up" line); `DiggerTest` (three cases).
- gametest: `NativeWaterBoxGameTests` (new).

No time bound: the give-up is counted in steps without progress, and re-entry is barred by distance, not by a timer
(the lead's condition (b) on F-35o). The rescue stays once per stuck spell.

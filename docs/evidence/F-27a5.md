# F-27a5 — A waking scene's campaign is told of the death it follows

Date: 2026-11-16
Agent / branch: LOOPS, claude/waking-death-1116
Status: complete (2026-11-16)

## Why
- **F-25c4di1f's "before"** (RUNNER 2, e40a24's two waking scenes with the whole kit in reach, each alone ×6 on main b47804b): pass 6 of 6 each, armour back on 0 of 6 each, and 0 "Recover dropped items" lines in all 12 runs.
- **In play, a death is heard by the campaign's own `died()`.** It learns where it died, the cause, whether a mob killed it, and when, and from that sets out to recover what it dropped.
- **A replay's campaign starts fresh,** and the goal memory restored from the scene does not carry its last death. So in a waking scene the player did not know where it had died, as it did in the run, and nothing it does about its drops could show.

## The change (harness only; no play changes)
- **`DeathReplays.captureWaking`:** a waking's record also writes
  - `deathAgo`: the ticks from its death to the respawn;
  - `deathByMob`: whether a hostile mob killed it, as the runtime told `died()`.
- **`PlayerRuntime`:** notes each death's tick and that mob flag beside its place and cause (F-27a4's), for the cut.
- **`ReplayGameTests.tellOfItsDeath`:** a respawn scene's replay tells its campaign of the death it records, through the campaign's own `died()`.
  - It passes: the record's `death`, moved to its place in the staged scene; the death's own words from the `cause`; `deathByMob`; and `now - deathAgo`.
  - Only when the death lies in the scene's box, and only for a player with a goal.
  - For a record from before this change: `deathAgo` is the 20 ticks a body lies before its respawn, and "by a mob" is read from the cause's words.
- **The bound, in the method's javadoc:** only what `died()` learned in the run. No drop's place and no item is read from the scene for it, and nothing the player did not know. The drops are the scene's own item entities, placed with its structure (F-27a4).
- **The replay logs** "its campaign told of its death at …, n ticks before, by a mob / not (cause), as it learned it in the run".

## Tests
- **`ReplayWakingGameTests.aWakingsCampaignIsToldOfTheDeathItRecords`, STAGED:** a native player on the Nether portal goal.
  - A record with its death outside its box tells its campaign nothing.
  - One inside it makes the campaign's last death that death, at its place in the staged scene (origin plus the record's offset), with its cause "Told was slain by Zombie".
  - The cause's words: "slain by Zombie" and "killed by Witch using magic" read as a mob's kill; "fell from a high place" and "drowned" do not.
- **`ReplayWakingGameTests` and `ReplayGameTests`:** all 14 required tests pass.

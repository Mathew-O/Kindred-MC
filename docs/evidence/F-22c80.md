# F-22c80 — Hostile mobs in GameTests come at the player

Date: 2026-11-15
Owner: REFACTOR (the lead's assignment; found by MODES staging F-25c4dh)
Scope: gametest only. No product code.

## The cause

A hostile mob given `setTarget(player)` in a GameTest stood where it spawned, AI on or off, at NORMAL difficulty, at
night. Vanilla's `TestContext.spawnMob` spawns the mob and then calls `MobEntity.clearGoalsAndTasks()` (1.21.1's
bytecode). Such a mob keeps a target but has no goal to act on it: no melee attack, no pursuit.

A probe (a scratch class on main `0eda619`, not committed) put a zombie 7.1 from a native player, `setTarget(player)`,
at night, and watched 300 ticks:

| Spawned with | Closest | Blows | Navigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `context.spawnMob` | 7.0 | 0 | idle throughout |
| `context.spawnEntity` | 0.6 | 8 | pursuing |
| `EntityType.create` + `world.spawnEntity` | 0.3 | 8 | pursuing |

In each, the chunk was ticking, AI was on, `canTarget` was true and the target was held. So it is not entity ticking,
target validity or the native player.

## The fix

`FixtureGround.hostile(context, type, relative)` spawns with `context.spawnEntity`, which keeps the mob's goals, and
marks it persistent. A fixture whose mob is to come at the player uses it. One that wants the mob still keeps `spawnMob`
or turns its AI off.

## Which fixtures

- **The 14 sites with `setAiDisabled(true)` stay on `spawnMob`:** Combat ×4, Gaze ×2, Initiative ×2, Ruleset's pigs,
  Tactical ×2, Social ×2. Their mobs are meant to stand still: targets to strike, threats to see or not see. Clearing
  their goals changes nothing that they test.
- **The 15 with `setTarget`, whose passes rested on a mob that never came:**

  - **Batch 3, after F-25c4dh (MODES):** `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`'s 10: `goalStopsToFightAMobThatIsAfterThePlayer`,
    `goalBreaksOffAFightOnceBadlyHurt`, `fightClosesInOnASkeletonBehindItsShield`,

## Batch 1: MobRhythm

| Test | Before, main `0eda619` ×3 | After, the branch ×3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `aZombieIsKilledWithoutLandingABlowInEightOfTenFights` | Pass 3 of 3. 10 of 10 untouched each run, closest 2.06: the player walked up to a zombie that stood still | Pass 3 of 3. 10 of 10 untouched each run, closest 1.9 to 2.4: the zombie came on and was still killed without landing a blow |
| `outmatchedTheBlockItAsksForCoverIsTakenToHand` | Pass 3 of 3. 0 blows taken, the zombie 3.9 off throughout: never came | Pass 3 of 3. 7, 3 and 3 blows taken from the zombies and spider that came; alive; the block to hand at tick 22, -1 and 21 |

The cover test's -1 in run 2 means no block reached its hotbar during the watch. The test fails only if a block is asked
for and is still not in hand 20 ticks on. That run never asked: it fought the spider, then dug in for the night. So
that pass did not exercise the block-to-hand rule. Runs 1 and 3 did, at ticks 22 and 21. Nothing in the test is
changed; this is for its owner to weigh.

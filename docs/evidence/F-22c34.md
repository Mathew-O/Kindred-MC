## Scope and implementation

The source is #74's CI, run 42556590202, attempt 1 (job 128963597249),
`NativePetGameTests.itTamesAWolfWithItsOwnBonesNamesItAndSaysWhenItDies`. A NullPointerException at the fixture's line 81
crashed the GameTest server. There were two causes, one in the product and one in the fixture.

**Root cause, in the product.**
- Hal tamed a wolf at once ("The wolf has taken to me").
- Hal then tried to put its anvil down to letter the name tag. `native_place` reported "Block placement produced no
  effect or was obstructed" 1318 times in a row, for the rest of the test's 8000 ticks.
- `PlayerRuntime.workstationSpot` chose a cell beside Hal's feet by its block, the block under it, reach and edit policy.
  It never asked whether something stood in the cell. The server refuses a placement into an entity's box.
- A tamed wolf sits or follows right beside its owner. The cell Hal chose was taken, and Hal chose that same cell again
  on every decision.

**Why the body was gone, in the fixture.**
- The fixture's runtime stop is scheduled at tick 8000, the test's tickLimit. On that last tick the stop removed Hal's
  body before the test's step ran.
- `f.player()` returned null, and the step's NullPointerException escaped the task runner and crashed the server.
- So the body was not killed. It was removed by the scheduled stop once the test's time ran out.

Changed files:
- **adapter `PlayerRuntime.workstationSpot`** (the anvil, enchanting table, smithing table and brewing stand) now skips a
  cell where `world.canPlace` refuses the block's state. That is the server's own entity check, for the player itself,
  its pets and any other mob in the cell. It tries the next cell, as for a cell that is not replaceable.
- **gametest `NativePetGameTests`:**
  - Every use of `f.player()` first asserts `player != null`, "Its body". A body that is gone is now an ordinary failure
    of the test, not an exception that stops the server.
  - New fixture: `itPutsItsAnvilDownBesideTheWolfSittingWhereItWouldHavePutIt` (batch `petsWolfInTheWay`).
    - Fixture staging: a wolf tamed to Hal by the fixture, sitting on the cell north of Hal's feet. That is the first
      cell `workstationSpot` looks at.
    - It checks that the wolf is named; that it sat in that cell throughout; that nothing was put down on its cell; and
      that one anvil is down and the tag used.

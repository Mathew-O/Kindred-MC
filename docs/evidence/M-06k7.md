## Scope and implementation

The lead asked for a check that a soak can prove before the owner's month-long Factions run (12 GB host, 8 players). It
is tooling and docs only, which the freeze allows, plus one metrics addition the report needs.

**The cost line's tick window** (metrics only). The daily `Kindred cost:` line had no tick timing: only evaluation runs
measured ticks (`EvalBootstrap.measure`). It now gives the server's ticks since the line before, and then begins
again:
- `server ticks N: mean M ms, p99 P ms, O over 50 ms, longest L ms (tick T)`;
- the heap's high water since the line before, beside the heap in use.

How it is done:
- `TickWindow` (core, new) is a fixed histogram of whole milliseconds to 2,000 ms, exact to the millisecond below that.
  It holds the count, the mean, the p99, the count over 50 ms, and the longest tick and when.
- `PlayerRuntime.onServerTick` adds the tick before (the server's own timing, `getTickTimes`) and reads the heap once a
  second.
- `logCost` writes the window and resets it.

**The report** (`tools/soak/preflight.py`, standard library only, with `test_preflight.py`) reads the console log and,
optionally, a stopped copy of the World Record. Per cost line it gives:
- the database's size and its growth a game day, measured between day lines;
- the Record's events;
- the bounds' prunes seen: the action fold and bound (F-31b, F-31b1) and the refusals' bound (F-34c2a);
- the Wire's refusals by reason and channel (F-34e);
- refused attempts on factions' land, written, the same minute's and over a faction's cap (F-34c2), when the build
  logs them;
- the ticks and the heap (above), and `native.disconnects` (#331);
- the WARN lines by kind (numbers, ids and names blanked) and every ERROR line with its time, the player it names and
  the tick.

From a copy of the Record it reads the integrity check, the schema, the ledger checks agreeing and differing, and the
unresolved actions.

Every figure is measured. A month's size is shown only as a projection, so labelled: the last measured day's growth
times 2,160 game days.

The verdict:
- **STOP** (exit status 2): the integrity check not ok; a ledger check differing, or an ERROR saying a ledger differs;
  the heap's high water over 90% of `-Xmx`.
- **WATCH:** a day's p99 above 50 ms or more than 5% of its ticks over 50 ms; refused attempts over a faction's cap;
  `native.disconnects` rising; any other ERROR; unresolved actions in a copy.

**The operator guide.** "Before the month run" heads the month run's runbook. It gives:
- the pack's settings as shipped, each confirmed against `packs/factions/config/kindred/factions.properties` and with
  Session A (2026-11-15);
- what to set and leave: bases off, the dragon fight off, `-Xms8G -Xmx10G`, the backups, the locked rules;
- the daily report and the thresholds above.

Not body behaviour.

## The soak for RUNNER 2 (to give the report real numbers)

Eight players, the factions pack's defaults, bases off, played at 20 ticks a second (sprint off), 36 game days: 12 real
hours. That is long enough to pass F-31b1's and F-34c2a's 30-game-day bound, so the prunes run.

```
tools/eval/run.py --ref <main after #330 and #335> --seeds 101 --policy maxNativePlayers=8 --memory 10G --timeout 46800 --keep-world \
    --scenario "starters=Kindred,Sol,Echo; players=Ada,Bram,Cleo,Dara,Eli; founders=3; goal=none; mode=factions; sprint=off; ticks=864000"
```

Then, on the run's console log and a copy of its kept `companions.sqlite`:

```
tools/soak/preflight.py --console <run>/console.txt --db <run>/world-kept/kindred/companions.sqlite --heap-max-mb 10240 --json <run>/preflight.json
```

To report, measured:
- each game day's database growth and tick window;
- the heap's high water;
- the bounds' prunes, and the ledger checks agreeing;
- the Wire's refusals and the refused attempts by day;
- the WARN kinds and every ERROR;
- the verdict.

The projected month only as the report labels it.

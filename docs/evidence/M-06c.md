## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/faction` (create, invite, uninvite, join, leave, kick, promote, demote, leader, ally, truce,
enemy, sethome, home, colour, disband, info, list, power) and `/faction as <player> <request>` for operators; in a
faction world PvP only between enemy factions outside safe zones, qualifying deaths cost 2 power, online time restores
1 an hour. Factions mode stays unplayable for operators until M-06d and M-06f.
Changed modules/files: companion-core `factions/` (`FactionSettings`, `FactionColour`, `Faction`, `FactionBook`,
`FactionLedger`, `Factions`, `PowerBook`, `PowerLedger`, `PowerRules`, `Zone`), `record/Ledgers` (factions and power);
companion-server-adapter `FactionsService`, `FactionCommands`, `PlayerRuntime` (unwritten events folded into
`projected()` for the rules, strike gates, death, online time, settings file), `KindredMod`.
Schema: none new: everything is World Record events (`faction.*`, `power.online|loss|set`, `account.seen`). Each power
event carries the power and progress after it, so a replay never depends on today's settings. (Design said
`factions`, `faction_members`, `power` tables; replaced by events per the brief: "every later slice writes events there".)
Configuration: `config/kindred/factions.properties`, seeded with the defaults.

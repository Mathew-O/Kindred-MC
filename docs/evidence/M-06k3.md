## Scope and implementation

Found by the item-source read (M-06k1), with MODES's design review. Free blocks exist only inside a Builder match (D6, the
mode rule). On main:
- **`startBuilding`** set creative and `stockFor` overwrote an AI builder's slots.
- **`leaveBuilding` and the stop path** cleared the whole inventory and set the game mode back from `modeBefore`, which
  was in memory only. `leaveBuilding` took the builder out of `modeBefore` before the online check, so a builder away at
  the end kept creative mode and whatever it held. After a crash nothing was reclaimed at all.
- **The ender chest** was never checked: a builder in creative could put anything there, and it outlived the match.

User-visible behavior: the operator guide's "Free blocks in this mode only", updated.

**The hold** (`BuilderHolds`, new):
- **At the start.** Before anything of the builder's is touched, what it holds, its ender chest and its game mode are
  written to `kindred/builder-holds/<uuid>.nbt` on a thread of its own: a temporary file, forced to disk, moved over
  whole. Only when that write is done is the builder cleared (inventory and ender chest), put in creative and, if an AI
  player, stocked. A builder in twice keeps its first hold; an existing hold is never overwritten.
  - The proposal's existing rule stands: every builder must arrive with nothing. The hold covers what it picked up
    since, its ender chest, its mode, and every ending.
- **At the end, or a stop, for an online builder.**
  1. Everything is cleared: inventory, cursor and ender chest.
  2. The hold is read back, wholesale, and the mode set.
  3. The player is saved: an AI player through its save receipt (`persistNativeSaveReceipt`), a person by vanilla's.
  4. Only then is the hold deleted.
  - A crash before step 4 restores again at the next join. Restoring is wholesale, so it comes to the same.
- **A builder away at the end, or a crash.** The hold stays on disk. A person gets it back at its next join
  (`playerJoined`), an AI player as its body is next connected (`completeNativeConnection`). One restore at a time per
  player, never for one in a running battle.
- **The arena** (`reclaimArena`, at the battle's end). Every container on the plots and in the arena is emptied, and
  every item entity lying in its box is discarded. The builds stand for the podium (open, below).

Also: `dispatchNativeTask`'s stale-dispatch check read `entry.arbiter.cancellationPending()` with no body. An AI builder
leaving mid-battle while a build task's budget loaded hit that NullPointerException (`builderHoldLeave`, first run). It
is now a dropped dispatch, logged "no body", as every other stale one.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `BuilderHolds` (new); `PlayerRuntime` (`startBuilding`, `beginBuilding`, `restoreBuilder`,
  `restoreHoldOnJoin`, `reclaimArena`, `leaveBuilding`, the stop, `playerJoined`, `completeNativeConnection`,
  `builderHold`, `holdsRestoring`, `arenaWorlds`; the null guard in `dispatchNativeTask`).
- gametest: `NativeBuilderHoldGameTests` (new, four).

Schema/configuration changes: none; the hold files are new, one per builder in a battle.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- **Open: the builds themselves.** After the podium they stand, and anyone may break them in survival and keep the drops:
  free blocks outside Builder. The lead's ruling is pending: (a) the arena restored once the podium has been seen, or
  (b) its blocks drop nothing while it stands.
- **Items thrown out of creative during the battle** lie in the arena until its end, when they are cleared. A person
  outside the battle could pick one up meanwhile. Refusing the throw itself is not done.
- **A hold's file is the only copy of what it holds while the battle runs.** It is forced to disk before anything is
  cleared, and deleted only after the player is saved.

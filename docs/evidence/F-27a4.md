# F-27a4 — Waking scenes: the corpus keeps a scene at each respawn, with where it died and what it dropped

Date: 2026-11-15
Agent / branch: LOOPS, claude/waking-scenes-1115
Status: complete (2026-11-15)

## Why
- **The corpus's scenes are cut only at deaths and at stuck spells** (`DeathReplays.capture`, kinds `death`, `stuck`, `spell`).
- **e40a23's 101 Sol** (#352) woke 9 times at its bed at −376, 31, −145 and died each time 15 to 50 blocks off, in the caves round it.
- **None of #370's 10 scenes of it holds the bed, the waking spot or its drops.** Each is cut round a death; the nearest misses the waking spot by 9 blocks in z.
- **So no change to what it does on waking can be measured on them:** F-25c4di1b's bed take-up, F-25c4di1e's dig-in (parked), or F-25c4di1f's drop recovery.

## The change (harness only; no play changes)
- **`DeathReplays.captureWaking`:** a `"respawn"` scene, cut on the server thread as the player wakes.
  - It has its own once a minute per player, apart from the death's capture, which it follows by a second.
  - Its record adds:
    - `death`: where it died, in world coordinates;
    - `deathInside`: whether the death is in its box;
    - `drops`: each item entity in the box, with its item, count, age in ticks and place in the box;
    - `ticks`: 2,400; `pass`: "alive at the end of 2400 ticks"; `reported`: "its armour back on (reported, not the pass)".
- **`DeathReplays.wakeBox`:** round the respawn spot, and, when the death is in the same dimension, round both.
  - Across: the usual margin of 16, narrowed to fit in 64 down to 8.
  - Up: 8, narrowed down to 2, at most 34.
  - Otherwise the usual 33 × 17 × 33 round the respawn spot, its death outside (`deathInside` false, said in its `cause`).
- **Scenes carry their own size** (`Scene.copy(world, origin, w, h, d)`, the structure's `size`). The usual capture is unchanged at 33 × 17 × 33.
- **The drops are recorded as the world held them:** item entities from an earned run, saved whole in the structure as every scene's entities are. Nothing is granted.
- **`PlayerRuntime`:** notes each death's place, dimension and cause, and cuts the waking as it respawns (`respawnNative`), with replays on only. With replays off (every corpus run), nothing is cut.
- **`ReplayGameTests`:** a `respawn` scene passes as a death's does, alive at its budget's end.
  - At the end it logs "its armour back on: yes / no (n of 4 armour slots; reported, not the pass)".
  - Its box (the mobs held still at its start, and the settling check) is the scene's own size.
- **`tools/corpus`:**
  - `import.py` classes a waking "waking: its death in the box" or "… outside the box";
  - `report.py` adds, from the runs' logs, "Waking scenes, its armour back on at the budget's end (reported, not the pass), by run".
  - The pass rule and the gate are unchanged.

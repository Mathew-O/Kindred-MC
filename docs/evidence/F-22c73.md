## Scope and implementation

**User-visible behaviour:** a player whose walks to pick up its own drops are refused still goes for logs it can reach. Before, three such refusals from about one place counted it as stranded, and while stranded its gather offered no walk at all.

**Found:** `NativeBaseGameTests.aPlayerWithStoneToolsSitesAndBuildsItsOwnCabinThenWaitsForIron` failed in four of about twelve PR runs on 12 November (#265, #268 twice, #271), each time the same way:
- At 62 of 222 blocks placed, the builder fetched all the cobblestone the rest of the cabin takes, first, mining it from a pit.
- Three or four of its walks to pick up its own cobblestone in the pit were refused ("No walkable route").
- `PortalRunCampaign.stranded()` (three refused walks from about one place within a minute) was then true. Stranded, `gatherFrom` returns nothing, so the trip for logs found none ("Out of the ground for logs") with a tree twenty blocks off and usable, until the test's end.
- In the passing runs there were no such refusals, or it fetched its logs before the cobblestone. #259's run and #265's second had refused pickups (11 and 1) and passed; why those did not strand it is not read here.

**Cause (product), in `PortalRunCampaign.taskFinished`'s stranding count:** every refused `native_goto` counted, a walk to pick up a drop among them. A refused pickup says the drop is out of reach, not that the player is shut in.

**The change:**
- A refused walk whose label starts "Pick up " no longer counts toward `stranded()`.
- Any other refused walk still counts, as before: walks that find no way from where it stands still strand it.
- `keptOut`, the diagnostic behind `knownLogsKeptOut`, names "stranded" (where, after how many refused walks) for a log that would otherwise read "usable", since stranded the gather offers none. No decision reads it.

**Why its pit drops were refused:** that is F-22c74, MOVEMENT's, staged on `claude/ci-green-1112-pitpocket` (`c3ff3d6a`) and handed to MODES.
- Each drop lay in a cell no body can get into: one down and one across, under a pocket one block high, with stone over the pocket.
- The walk aims at the drop's own cell. The route search spent all 6,000 of its positions on 18 of the 26 refused pickups.
- MODES takes the fix: the pickup opens the pocket's head room, and the navigator refuses a goal it has no way into before searching.

**Nothing granted.** Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign`: the stranding count, and `keptOut`.
- Test: `NativeStrandedPickupGameTests`, a new class, registered.

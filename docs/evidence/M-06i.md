## Scope and implementation

`companion-core` `factions/FactionPlay`: one pure decision over an immutable `View`. The view holds the ledgers' public
facts (factions, power, claims, safe zones, captures), the player's coins, its own sightings of enemy land, the
market's quotes for its spare stock and its wants, the auction house's listings, and its personality's bounded
weights. The decision returns the choices in order, the first to do now.
- Membership:
  - A sociable player (sociability 35 or more) joins the invitation with the most members.
  - Otherwise it founds its own, at once with initiative 50 or more, or after a game day without an invitation.
- Claims (officers and the leader):
  - With nothing held, it claims the chosen base site. After that, the free chunk touching its land nearest its
    first claim.
  - Each claim is tried against `Territory.claim` with the view's facts, so capacity, separation, the border, the
    spawn exclusion and the cost all apply.
  - Beyond the first claim it keeps a reserve back: one claim's cost, plus up to two more for a cautious player.
- Earning and spending:
  - It sells all spare stock.
  - For each want, it bids the least the house takes (`Auctions.least`, newly public) when that is under the market's
    ask, or buys at the ask. Either only within what it would pay and what is left above the reserve.
  - It never bids on its own listing.
- Defence:
  - A loss within ten minutes (a capture under way) puts going home first and alone.
  - A cautious player (caution 50 or more) also goes home while its faction is exposed, after earning and before any
    raid.
  - Neither applies when it already stands on its own land.
- Raids (officers, healthy, combativeness 40 or more), only from chunks it saw:
  - Standing in a seen enemy chunk that `Capture.capture` accepts now, it captures it.
  - Otherwise it scouts the nearest seen chunk of an enemy that is now exposed, unshielded and on that enemy's border.
  - A chunk it never saw is never chosen.

Order (revised while wiring): a capture needs claim capacity to spare, and a player with capacity to spare claims free
land first, so as first written it would almost never raid. A combative player (`RAID_FIRST`, 60 or more) with an
exposed enemy border in sight now raids before it expands; any other player expands first. No raid is chosen while a
defence is.

Runtime (companion-server-adapter):
- `FactionsPlayer`, per AI player:
  - its sightings: claimed chunks of another faction that it stood in or beside, kept to the last 512 and not across
    a restart;
  - its spare stock: common building blocks beyond 64 of each;
  - its faction name;
  - where a walk ends: three blocks inside the chunk.
- `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates`, run among the routine candidates after a match's own (duel, hunt, raid):
  - once every 30 seconds it builds the `View` and takes `FactionPlay`'s first choice;
  - weighing only chooses: it never acts.
- The choice becomes an ordinary task:
  - a walk (`native_goto`) to a claim elsewhere, a border it saw, or its faction's home;
  - or a `native_faction` request (`NativeFactionAction`), made only when that task is dispatched under the player's
    lease and written to the action ledger like every recorded skill;
  - nothing is asked again after a stop or restart, and a stale copy of the task is answered "Already asked".
- The request goes through the same `FactionsService.request` / `MarketDesk` / `AuctionHouse` calls a person's
  commands use, with every rule checked again where the player stands.
- A first version acted while candidates were only being weighed. On a body whose operator had not set it playing,
  it founded and claimed. That was replaced with the dispatched task above before anything was committed.

The evaluation scenario (`mode=factions`):
- `EvalScenario.Mode`: the run's faction world.
- `EvalBootstrap`:
  - once the starters are in, it sets the mode (`PlayerRuntime.factionsWorldForEval`) and the hub safe zone;
  - at the end it audits the Record before writing the result.
- Core `FactionsAudit`: the locked rules checked from the Record alone.
  - Each claim and capture is decided again by `Territory.claim` / `Capture.capture` from the state just before it,
    under the run's settings.
  - No player kill inside a safe zone.
  - Coin balances chain and are never negative; coins come only from starting coins and market sales; the auction
    house only moves or destroys coins.
  - Each sale has its seller's uncancelled intent; deliveries are begun once and handed over at most once.
- `EvalRecord.factions`: the audit in `eval-result.json`. `score.py` adds the audit's table to the summary.
- `tools/eval/README.md` documents the key and the result.
- `FactionPlay.beyondSpawn`: a faction whose site lies inside the spawn exclusion (a run's starters spawn at spawn)
  claims its first land just beyond the exclusion on the same bearing, walked to in legs of 48 blocks
  (`FactionsPlayer.LEG`).
- `GameModes` FACTIONS stays unplayable for operators until M-06i is complete; the run sets the mode for itself.

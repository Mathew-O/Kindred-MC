# F-22c66 — A barrier, which a survival player cannot see, is neither ground to walk on nor a height to aim a look at

Status: in progress (the PR's CI and the corpus gate).

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a player's routes no longer stand on barrier blocks or take a step onto one, and its looks for
animals never aim at the top of a barrier column. A barrier stays an obstacle it walks round, as a player bumps into one.
Before, a hurt player looking for animals near the GameTest's own barrier fence round a scene aimed at the fence's top,
climbed a tree's leaves toward it, and fell off the canopy to its death.

Found: under the corpus gate for F-22c65, `e40a14-80b7150-8675309-kindred-48005` died in 2 of 3 full runs on that branch.
The player was Kindred at 3.8 health, food 8, on easy; the baseline was 6 of 6. A local, uncommitted probe (position,
velocity, fall distance, ground, climbing and in-wall every tick for a player under 6 health; every planned path's nodes)
showed:
- **Not a planned drop:** death from 3.8 health needs a fall of about 7, and the planner drops at most 3
  (`MAX_SAFE_DROP`).
- **Not fall distance carried over:** it reset to 0 on every tick on the ground.
- **Not a block mined under its feet** (F-35l's case): no break near its feet before the fall.
- **Not pushed up out of a block:** it got up by ordinary step-up jumps, never climbing and never in a wall.

What happened instead:
- **The look's target:** `explore` reads the surface 28 blocks out with `MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES`, which leaves out
  leaves but counts barriers. Every GameTest area is fenced with barriers to one block above its structure. The look
  aimed at the fence's top.
- **The route:** it climbed oak leaves, a birch log and birch leaves, then went 20+ nodes along the barrier fence's top,
  since the navigator counted a barrier as ground.
- **The fall:** off the canopy's edge mid-jump, a 4-block drop. 6.3 of fall cost 3 health, and the second time, its life.

Cause (product): the navigator and the look took a barrier for ground. A survival player cannot see one, so it is
neither a place to stand nor a sign of the surface.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `NativeNavigator`: `unseen(state)`, a barrier. The live world's terrain says it neither
  supports nor bears a body. Its collision box is kept, so it is still no way through. The other blocks a survival player
  cannot see, light and structure void, have no collision box and were already passed through as air.
- `PortalRunCampaign.explore`: a heading whose surface 28 out is a barrier column is not looked along. The next
  heading is tried, as for one over water.
- Test: `NativeBarrierGameTests.aHurtPlayerLookingForAnimalsNeitherAimsAtNorWalksOnABarrierFence` (its own batch
  `barrierFence`). A hurt, hungry player (health 4, food 8) is on an open stone field with no animals.
  - Staged: a barrier fence 6 high stands 28 out every way, where each look aims, with a staircase of persistent oak
    leaves up to it from 22 out.
  - It fails once the player stands on a barrier, or on leaves with a drop of 4 or more beside it. It passes after 600
    ticks of neither.

Nothing is granted.

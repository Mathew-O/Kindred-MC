## Scope and implementation

**Found:** #263's run 43250292010 (2019dc6) ran 15:40 to 17:10 and was cancelled by the build job's `timeout-minutes: 90`. It was partway through the native GameTests, after about 539 "Running test batch" lines. #262 took 88 minutes. The suite has outgrown the limit, as it outgrew 60 minutes on 2 November.

The day before, the same workflow's runs took about 60 to 80 minutes from start to finish:
- 61 min: 42999746619, 43001879290 and 43015604379, on the morning of 11 November;
- 69 to 80 min: 43023784724 to 43032970476, that afternoon.

The JUnit step alone took 17 to 25 minutes of each.

**The change, step 1:** the build job's `timeout-minutes` goes from 90 to 120.
- This is CI's wall-clock limit on the job, not a game-side timeout or tick budget.
- No test's limit changes. No test is skipped or loosened.
- It is not the rule against widening a timeout to hide a stuck spell: a stuck test still fails at its own tick limit, as before.

**Step 2, the real fix, to be proposed before it is built:** the GameTest run split across two or three parallel jobs, by batch or by class list. That brings the wall time well under 60 minutes, with one combined evidence artifact and the same pass/fail semantics.

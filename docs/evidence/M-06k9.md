## Scope and implementation

**What a player does differently:** a player far out in water, with no dry ground it can reach, no longer searches the whole water for dry ground on every plan.
- **Where it stands:** a search that found nothing is not asked again from the same cell for 20 ticks.
- **When it moves:** it asks again at once from the new cell.
- **Dry ground that comes in reach is still found:** within 20 ticks where it stands, and at once once it moves.

**Why:** in M-06k's shipped-configuration read (2026-11-22, main `8d4d30c2`), the way out of the water's searches for dry ground were 67.7% of the ticks over 200 ms. Players went "up for air" hundreds of times from much the same cells, every search finding nothing.

**Changed** (companion-server-adapter, unless marked):
- `PortalRunCampaign`:
  - `DRY_MISS_TICKS` (20).
  - `drySearch(which, …)` wraps `surface`'s three searches: the 400-node one, and the two 4,000-node ones.
  - Per search, it keeps the cell its last miss was asked from and when.
  - It keeps no hits: a stand found is searched for again as before.
- **The counts, kept for the cost line and the fixtures:**
  - `PortalRunCampaign.surfacePlans`, `drySearches` and `drySearchNodes`;
  - companion-core's `Navigator.lastDryStandExpanded()`, from the search's own `Path.expanded`;

  - **What it counts:** a player's campaign is made once and never replaced, so the counts run for its life.

## Acceptance evidence

### The fixture (`NativeDryMissGameTests`, 3 runs each)

**The staging:** a pool 20 out each way and four deep. Its rim stands three above the water, out of reach to climb. An island lies at 18 east, beyond reach of the middle. The player is held at the pool's middle with its head out, its goal on.

| Case | Before (`3d581b94`) | After (`6e9c065a`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HELD: at most one dry-ground search in two plans | 0 of 3: 2.00 searches a plan ("700 in 350") | 3 of 3: 0.38–0.42 a plan |
| Plans out of the water a second | 4.7–4.8 (a plan every ~4 ticks) | 4.7–4.8 |
| MOVED within reach of the island: "to dry ground" within 10 ticks | 3 of 3 (2 ticks) | 3 of 3 (2 ticks) |
| PLACED, stone set beside it where it is held: "to dry ground" within `DRY_MISS_TICKS` + 10 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 (20 ticks) |

**The expiry:** 18–20 searches in 200 ticks is the floor for a 20-tick expiry, one search a window for each of the two searches a held swimmer asks.
- **What 20 costs:** a still swimmer would wait up to 1 s for dry ground that appears beside it.
- **What 100 would cost:** most of what remains would go, for a wait of up to 5 s.
- **The decision:** the lead kept 20 (2026-11-23), leaving any rise to the shipped-configuration evidence below.

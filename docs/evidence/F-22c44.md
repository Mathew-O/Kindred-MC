## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. The source is #101's CI, run 42584569435 (job 129051312498),
`NativeCompoundGameTests.aPlayerBringsAPairInByThePasturesDoorwayBreedsThemAndShutsItBehindThem`.
- The ranch penned the pair and a calf was born. Then shutting the pasture's doorway stopped at 6 of 8, "Nothing in hand
  to place", and waited from then on.
- Opening the doorway had broken its 8 blocks with collect on: 4 oak planks and 4 cobblestone. The shut placed 3 planks
  and 4 cobblestone. One plank's drop was never picked up: the break's collect follows its drops for 70 ticks at most,
  then completes anyway.
- `RanchRunner` built the shut with `BuildRunner.next(body)`, with no supply. A build without a supply places only what
  it holds, so one lost drop left the doorway short for good.

Changed files: adapter `RanchRunner`, `BuildRunner`.
- **`RanchRunner`:** the doorway's shut is built with the run's own supply,
  `BuildRunner.Supply(campaign, null, animal -> true, 0)`, as any build of the player's own. A block short is then
  fetched, gathered or made from what it holds, through the build's existing needs. No animal is hunted for it.
- **`BuildRunner.fetch`:** a drop of a block the build is short of, lying in sight within twelve blocks, is picked up
  first, once per drop, before anything is fetched, gathered or crafted.
  - This is the wool pick-up made general (`pickUp`). Wool keeps its six walks to each drop.
  - Nothing is free: what is placed is a block the player dug out itself, or one it gathered or made.

New fixture: `NativeCompoundGameTests.withADoorwayPlankLeftLyingItPicksItUpToShutTheDoorway` (batch
`compoundCattleDrop`).
- It is the pasture-doorway fixture, its body now `pastureDoorway`.
- Fixture staging: once the doorway is open and the leading has begun, one oak plank is taken from the player's pack
  and left lying in the pasture's south-west corner. That stands for the drop its break did not pick up: in sight of
  the doorway, off the player's ways to the pen.

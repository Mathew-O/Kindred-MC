## Scope and implementation

**Where a torch goes** (`Lighting`, core, `survival`, new, pure). Hostile mobs spawn only where no block light reaches (block light 0, since 1.18), so every floor cell of the area needs a torch within its light. A torch gives 14 and its light falls by one a step; `Lighting.next` picks, of the dark floor cells, the one whose torch would light the most of the others within ten steps (a margin under the thirteen it reaches in the open, for walls and corners), the nearest to the player on a tie. `steps` counts the steps as light spreads; `said` is what it says.

**In play** (`PlayerRuntime.lightCandidates`). A player with an approved work area (WorkSetup's area), out of any match and inside the area: the floor cells within sixteen blocks of it, where a player could stand (a solid top below, room for two above, no fluid) and block light is 0, read from the world's own light every two seconds and again after every torch. On the floor of the cell `Lighting.next` names it puts a torch it holds, walked to first; with none, it makes four of a coal or charcoal and a stick, or sticks of its planks first. Placing is the work area's own edit decision. Once nothing is dark it says "I lit the work area: 5 torches." The chore ranks ahead of its own progression's steps, as a chore of the area it was given.

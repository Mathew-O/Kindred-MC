## Scope and implementation

The owner's mechanics-first reorder (`docs/proposals/mechanics-first.md`): MOVEMENT's first move. A player jumps a gap
of one or two blocks. A walk could not: with no floor ahead and no safe drop, the planner had no move and the walk was
"No walkable route". In e40a17's ten seeds about 2,750 of 4,222 refused lines were "No walkable route", most of them
cave walks.

- `NativeNavigator.jumpingGaps(n)` (opt-in): a move straight across a gap of 1 or 2 blocks, in the four directions, to
  a stand at the same level. The gap cells must have no floor and no fluid, with head room for the arc above them and
  above the take-off, and nothing harmful below a gap cell. Cost 3 for one block, 5 for two.
- `NativeGoToSkill` asks for it on its own routes only, never on a walk leading animals (`hold`): two blocks with food
  to sprint (7 or more), one block otherwise. Other planners and reachability checks keep to the ground, so nothing
  plans a jump its walk cannot make.
- `NativeGoToSkill.steer`: the next waypoint two or three blocks on from the last at the same level, no floor between,
  is a jump. It lines up on the landing, runs (a sprint for two), jumps once past the middle of the block it takes off
  from, and holds the jump to the landing.

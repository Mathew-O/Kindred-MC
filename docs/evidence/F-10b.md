# F-10b — Bounded paid native 3D terrain traversal
Date: 2026-10-17 EDT; report 2026-10-17.
Codex / uncommitted main, no commits. Complete for this child; parent F-10 remains open.

`/aiplayer native traverse <north|south|east|west> <1..3 blocks> <UUID>` uses the existing paid admission, SkillRunner, ActionArbiter and durable writer. Traversal supports authentic 3D terrain elevation changes: 1-block step-up jump using native `LivingEntity.jump()` and forward momentum, and 1–3 block safe step-down drop onto solid support. Ordinary forward/yaw and vertical physics move the body; no manual tick, coordinate relocation or teleportation is permitted. A 3D corridor profiling pass checks loaded clearance, world borders, full-square support surfaces, jump headroom, fall columns and environmental hazards (magma, cactus, lava, fire, powder snow) at dispatch, tick, and mutation. Dynamic obstructions cancel motion ahead of collision and settle to idle without mining or altering blocks. One five-second prepaid slice bounds the traversal; no renewal or route replay.

| 1-block step-up jump | Advance 2 blocks east with +1 block elevation: jump clears 1-block step, horizontal displacement 1.8..2.3, vertical elevation 0.8..1.2, yaw maintained, landed on ground | Pass |

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a builder clearing a block from the way of its build is no longer sent to a spot from which
the server says the block is out of sight. That happens when a torch, sign, flower or other block with no collision
stands on the line from its eyes to the block. It breaks the block from a spot with a clear view.

Found: `NativeBunkerGameTests.itDigsOutLinesAndFurnishesItsHideoutUnderTheGround` on #148's CI (run 42843095037),
recorded in [F-22c56](F-22c56.md):
- At 344 of 345 placed, the hideout's last block in the way was refused, "out of reach or not visible", from both
  spots the plan gave.
- Its builder waited until the 40,000 ticks ran out. The hideout is lit and furnished.

The disagreement, in `ConstructionPlanner.clearSight`:
- The planner samples the cells along the eyes' line. It counts a cell clear if the feet could pass it (`passable`),
  so a `PASSABLE` cell with a torch, sign, flower or button in it counted as clear.
- The server's check, `NativePlayerBody.canMine`, raycasts against the blocks' outline shapes. A torch has one, so the
  look meets the torch first and the break is refused.
- CI's log could not say which check refused it; F-22c56 now names it. The torch case is the one the planner
  provably gets wrong, and the pure test below shows it.

Changed file:
- `companion-core` `ConstructionPlanner.clearSight`: on a break's line, a `PASSABLE` cell (a block of no collision)
  bars the eyes' line, as the server's outline raycast does.
- On a placement's line it does not. The adapter's placement clicks a face with a hit it builds itself, with no outline
  raycast, so a flower or torch between does not stop it. Air and replaceable cells are unchanged on both.

First barred for placements too, the change cost a pasture ward on a field of flowers:
- The ward's builder was sent to farther spots to place from. It stopped after eight walks in a row to one of them,
  "Route … is blocked", 204 blocks short.
- `NativeCompoundGameTests.withItsWardsStandingAPlayerBuildsItsPastureWardAndWalksInByItsOwnWay` failed 2 of 3 class
  runs, and 1 of 3 run alone, against main's 3 of 3.
- Narrowed to breaks, it passed 3 of 3 alone.

Not changed, and remaining:
- Replaceable plants (short grass, ferns) have outlines too, and the planner still sees through them on a break's line.
- A line that clips a solid block's corner can pass between clearSight's samples (eight to a block). In the class runs,
  the server refused 1 to 3 breaks a run as out of sight, each naming a grass block or cobblestone corner (F-22c56's
  diagnostics). The plan went on each time from another spot; none stopped a build.

Nothing is granted.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player with work of its own that is out exploring when night falls goes to its own bed. Before this, it set off back to where its expedition began. When that spot was its bed, behind its cabin's walls, it stopped at the wall and stood there all night.

This was the second cause of the night-and-bed fixture's intermittent failure (loose end E21, `NativeLifeFirstGameTests.atNightItLeavesItsBuildForItsOwnBedThenBuildsOnInTheMorning`, "Its body"). The first cause, the corner posts cut out of its own cabin, is [E-08d](E-08d.md). The chain, from a diagnostic copy of the fixture that logged the player's decisions and its expedition state every five seconds of game time:
1. Its first night fell while its cabin was still going up. It slept in the cabin's bed, and the bed became its spawn.
2. The runtime keeps one expedition state for each player, made the first time it is needed and anchored then: to the work area's home if it has one, else to its spawn. Made while it lay in the bed, it was anchored to the bed (`spawn_bed`, recorded at tick 3,652).
3. As the hut was begun, its expedition state went from at base to exploring outward.
4. When the next night fell, with the hut under way, the expedition turned for home: an urgent walk (need 92) to within two blocks of the bed. The walk to its bed for the night (life first, need 88) comes after anything urgent.
   - The walk home goes by compass steps, not by a route.
   - From the hut it went north to just outside the cabin's south wall, eight blocks short of the bed, which is in the cabin's north-west corner. The cabin's door is in that wall, but the next step north was into the wall itself.
   - From night on it was the decision at every check in the log (187 of 187, one every five seconds of game time) and never ran or ended, so the walk to bed never came.
5. The fixture ran on to the runtime's scheduled stop (tick 29,500), and its last check found no body: "Its body".

The failure came only when the first night fell before the cabin stood. When the cabin was built by day, the expedition state was made before any sleep, anchored to open ground, and the walk home ended.

Changed modules/files:
- `PlayerRuntime`:
  - The urgent return to shelter at night or in a thunderstorm now ends at once when life comes first for the player and it has its own bed to sleep in (or is asleep in it). Its walk to its bed then takes it home.
  - A return for low health is unchanged, as is any return for a player without a bed of its own or without work of its own.
  - `lifeFirst`: the one condition for "life comes first" (no running goal, and a ranch, a field, a build or its base), used by this and by the life-first candidates.
  - `PlayerRuntime` is also changed on `modest-faraday`, but not in these lines.
- `NativeLifeFirstGameTests`: the night fixture is now a helper taking the hour its day begins. The original fixture is unchanged at 1,000, and a second fixture starts at 9,000, so its first night falls before its cabin stands.

Requirements covered: F-25c9's "sleep at night in its own bed". AGENTS.md's "survival, combat, follow and work cannot own independent body controllers": the expedition's night return and life first both decided where the player went at night, and now only life first does when it applies.

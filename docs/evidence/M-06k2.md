## Scope and implementation

Found by the item-source read (M-06k1). `HuntCompass.point` handed a hunter a new tracking compass every second it held
none. So a compass stored in a chest, dropped or listed at the auction was replaced while the old one lived on: a
copied usable item. The lead's ruling, 2026-11-14 (D6, "no copied usable items"): one compass per hunter per match, by
the announced rule, the same for people and AI players, replaced only when the old one is provably gone. MODES approved
the design and added the keepInventory case, the AI's own put-away paths, and the stolen-compass rule.

User-visible behavior (the announced rules line, Manhunt and the speedrun-hunt variant): "lost to a death or destroyed,
replaced; set down or taken, not; taken back at the end".
- **Issued once.** Each compass carries an issue id beside its match id. The runtime keeps each running hunt's one
  issue per hunter (`huntIssued`). A hunter holding none gets another only when its own issue has been marked
  destroyed:
  - **at a death:** the curse of vanishing deleting it (`HuntCompassVanishMixin` on `PlayerEntity.vanishCursedItems`,
    which vanilla calls only with keepInventory off; with it on, the compass is still held and nothing is marked);
  - **on the ground:** an item entity destroyed with its stack whole: burnt, pricked or blown up (`ItemEntity.damage`'s
    discard), despawned (`ItemEntity.tick`'s discard), or fallen out of the world (`Entity.tickInVoid`).
    `HuntCompassDropMixin` and `HuntCompassVoidMixin`.
  - A pickup by a player, a hopper or a mob moves it and marks nothing. So does a chunk unloading or a change of
    dimension.
- **Set down, stored or taken: not replaced.** A compass in a chest, on the ground or in another player's inventory
  (a runner who took it, by MODES's point and the lead's ruling) stays the hunter's one; the hunter fetches it.
- **An AI hunter never puts its own away.** The body's deposit into a container (`useContainer`'s in-slots), its toss
  to a player and its discard all skip a `HuntCompass.handed` stack, so an AI player does not lose its compass in a
  way a person would know not to.
- **It makes nothing.** A crafting grid holding one, at a table or the player's own, shows no result
  (`HuntCompassCraftMixin` on `CraftingScreenHandler.updateResult`), so it cannot become a map or a recovery compass
  that outlives the hunt.
- **It is not sold.** The auction house refuses to list one. It builds its facts without the unknown-component check
  on purpose. The market already refused it for its CUSTOM_DATA.
- **After the hunt.** Every one held is taken back at the end, as before. One left in a chest or on the ground is taken
  back from whoever next holds it (inventory, cursor or ender chest). The runtime sweeps once a second for the hunts it
  saw end, a restart's stopped hunts included.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- **The crafter block** (an automatic crafter fed a compass) is not covered; only crafting tables and players' own
  grids are. A crafter fed the hunt's compass could make a map. Rare, and its own small slice if wanted.
- **A compass taken by a runner** stays taken until a hunter takes it back. That is the announced rule; the owner may
  want it otherwise.
- **The issue map is in memory.** A restart ends the hunt, so nothing outlives it but the sweep's set, which a restart
  rebuilds from the hunts it stops.

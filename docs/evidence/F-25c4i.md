## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: with its wooden pickaxe made and trees round it, a player on the portal run takes the wood for its trip below before its first cobblestone: sticks for its stone tools and their reserve, a spare table's planks, a shield's, and the fuel for the route's iron beyond the coal it carries. That is about nine logs from an empty start, as players take a stack of logs before going down. Below, a spare pickaxe it has neither sticks nor wood for is no longer fetched from the surface: it goes on with the pickaxe it has.

Found in the live runs that checked F-25c4b to h (`fb02bc3`, seeds 8675309 and 20261030, strategist off):
1. Three of the five players ran out of sticks and wood below and were left with no pickaxe. They spent 35 to 43% of their time gathering logs for sticks and another 24 to 29% waiting for daylight to go up for them.
2. On the second seed, Sol's first cobblestone came four blocks below its spawn. From then on it was underground and its plan counted no tree. A tree counts only while the player is up among them, so none of the packing that follows the first stone (sticks, a spare table's wood, F-25c4d's fuel) ever saw one.
3. With a stone pickaxe and no sticks, the spare-pickaxe rule sent Kindred up for logs from Y=8 and Sol from Y=18. Each climbed a fresh staircase and wore out the pickaxe it had, three blocks dug for each block climbed.
4. Kindred and Echo hunted coal 18% of their time on the first seed: the fuel packing had come too late for them as well.

Found making the fixture: stepping in under a trunk it was cutting, to pick up a log it had dropped, the player counted as below ground. There was no sky over it and the column's top was the logs above, so its trees went out of its plan and back each time. The trip's wood was left half packed: 19 and 11 planks' worth at the first stone, where it packs 34 to 37.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core):
  - `tripWood`: the trip's wood, in planks, from the reserve and fuel rules the iron stage already uses;
  - at the wooden pickaxe, with a tree within 24 blocks, logs for it before the first cobblestone ("Gather logs for the trip below");
  - the spare stone pickaxe only from sticks or wood in hand, or a tree close by.
- `PortalRunCampaign.facts`: a column covered by nothing but a trunk's logs and leaves up to the sky (`underTrunk`) is not below ground for counting trees. `PortalRunCampaign` is changed on `modest-faraday` only in its hole-up lines.
- `PortalRunPlannerTest`, `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one case and one fixture.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- Packed only from trees within 24 blocks of the wooden pickaxe: a player that made it far from any tree packs as before.
- Sized from the iron still wanted: digging after the iron (water, lava, the cast) is not counted.
- A player with no pickaxe deep below still climbs out as before; this keeps it from getting there.

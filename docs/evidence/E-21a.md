## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: someone tells an AI player "I need iron" (any of E-50's need claims: "I need", "I could use", "I
could do with"). The player has no goal running and holds iron ingots nobody has a claim on. It offers half of them, at
most 16: "You said you need iron? I've got 4 iron ingot spare. Want some?". As with the food offer (E-53), nothing moves
before "yes please". Only then are the ingots tossed through the toss method, a recorded toss within E-49's daily cap,
and the gift counts once picked up (E-22a). "No thanks" withdraws the offer.
- **Never offered:**
  - its tools, weapons and armour;
  - buckets, flint and steel, ender pearls and eyes, totems, elytra and beds;
  - food (left to E-53's food offer);
  - anything while a goal of its own runs (the run's stock is committed);
  - what the team's share-out holds for someone else.
- **Never offered to:** someone it is wary of (E-49).
- **How often:** each person's attention budget (E-53) bounds it as an `OFFER`, at most once an hour for each item and
  person.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-core`: `gift.GiftPolicy` (new: spare is half of each unpromised thing, at most 16, never the list above;
  a need matched to it by its words, singular or plural).
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `InitiativeLane`: the offer after the food offer, its yes through the same toss path.
  - `PeerRadio.claimedNeeds`: a person's need claims to this player, newest first.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: hook lines (`spare`: its stacks less share-out reservations; `wary`).
- JUnit: `social.RapportAndGiftsTest.giftsAreHalfOfWhatIsSpareAndNeverItsTools`.
- gametest: `NativeGiftGameTests.someoneWhoSaysTheyNeedIronIsOfferedHalfAndItCountsOncePickedUp`.

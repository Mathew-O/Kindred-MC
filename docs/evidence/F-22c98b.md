## Scope and implementation

**Found:** in CI on #367 (run 43772853585), #372 (run 43769479722) and #355 (run 43788229521), a bring given up ("The cart
wouldn't take the librarian up.") ended "… I couldn't put my hall's door back.", with no door build begun. The same
ending showed in local bring runs after other give-ups ("The librarian kept wandering off the line.", "The cart rolled
past the librarian without it.").

**Cause, in `PlayerRuntime`'s bring:** after a give-up, UNDO takes back what was put down. While the broken cart's item
lies about, each decision walks to it: `if (!lying.isEmpty() && b.doorTries++ < 40) … goNear`. DOOR_BACK then puts the
door back by the build runner, bounded by the same counter: `if (++b.doorTries > 3 || startBuild(...) != 1) … "I couldn't
put my hall's door back."`. `giveUp` zeroes it, but a few walks to the cart's item left it past three before DOOR_BACK
tried once. In each CI log the give-up is followed by a run of `goNear` walks, then the door message, with no "Hal build"
line between.

**The change:** UNDO's walks to the cart's item are counted on their own (`Bring.cartPickWalks`, bounded at 40 as
before). DOOR_BACK keeps its own three tries. The counter is neither zeroed again nor shared.

**Nothing granted.** The door is still put back by the build runner from Hal's own inventory, as players place it.
Changed files: `companion-server-adapter`: `PlayerRuntime` (UNDO's cart-pickup walks). Test:
`NativeBringGameTests.givenUpWithTheCartDownHalStillPutsItsHallsDoorBack` (batch `bringGivenUpDoorBack`, new).

## The fixture (STAGED)

The strolling bring (`scene(…, 6)`). Each time the cart pushed at the librarian held in its boat rolls, the boat,
librarian and all, is set three blocks off the line, crosswise, until the bring gives up. The give-up's reason varies by
copy ("kept wandering off the line", "no rail line from where the librarian wandered to", "the cart wouldn't take the
librarian up"); each is a give-up with the cart down. The hall's doorway is read on the tick the given-up bring says how
it ended. A first version read it later, after a new bring had begun and opened the doorway again; it was corrected in its
own test-only commit (`c7c59fb`), and both sides were run again on the corrected fixture. Nothing is given; the staging
moves the boat.

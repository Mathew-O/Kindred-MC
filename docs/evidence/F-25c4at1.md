## Scope and implementation

**Found:** in E-40 iteration 26's consoles (`claude/corpus-data-e40a26-consoles-1122` at `1b99f522`), 8 hole-or-pit spells,
24 stuck minutes, cycled "Climb toward the surface …: step up", "back the way it came", "step aside". In 5 of them most
walks back ended lower than they began:
- batch 1's 31102026 Sol, 7 of 8, 8 of 8 and 8 of 8 at 57–59, 63–64 and 171, three spells, about 21 game minutes;
- batch 2's 8675309 Kindred, 6 of 10 and 12 of 18.

**The cause:** the walk back (`retrace`) was stalled only while the player stood within Manhattan 1 of where the walk
ended, no higher. A walk back is a partial `go(target, 0.8, true, …)`. From the higher cell it went toward a cell of its way
in it could not reach and ended short, a block lower. The climb's next step up took it out of the stall's reach, and the
walk back was offered again from where it began.

**User-visible behavior:** a player whose walk back along its way in ended lower than it began is not sent the same way
again as soon as its climb steps it back up. The climb's step up and its cut through have their turn. After the while (600
ticks), or once it has moved off, the way back is worth a try again.

**Changed files:**
- `PortalRunCampaign.taskFinished`, its walk-back branch: a walk back done with its feet lower than where it was chosen
  sets `retraceLowerFrom` (where it was chosen) and `retraceLowerUntil` (600 ticks on).
- `PortalRunCampaign.retrace`:
  - the hold: no walk back while the hold stands, the player within 3 of `retraceLowerFrom` horizontally and no higher
    than it. This is the climb's own rule, that the way back up never leads down, read from where the walk ended. No
    threshold or timeout is changed: the while is the stall's own.
  - `retraceChosenAt`: where each walk back is chosen.
- Two fixture setters, package-private, called from no main source: `wayInForTest` and `walkBackForTest`.
- **Gametest:** `NativeRetraceLowerGameTests` (new, registered in `fabric.mod.json`).

Nothing is given or moved by the code: it changes only when the walk back is offered. The world it reads is what it reads
already: its own way in and where it stands.

**Schema/configuration changes:** none.

## Scope and implementation

**Found:** a read of E-40 iteration 29 (main `9a0921b5`; `claude/corpus-data-e40a29-consoles-1123`), stuck spells with three or more
walks back in them (`rows.py`, F-25c4at1's read). There are four.
- **The variant, batch 2's 31102026 Echo** (spell at tick 107,680, 27 walks back): the walk back was offered at y 26, offered again at
  y 24, then ended BLOCKED ("Route … is blocked") with it at y 7. The climb's step-ups took it back up to 25 or 26, and the walk back
  was offered again, about every seven seconds.
- **The same shape, batch 2's 707 Sol** (59,800, 23 walks back): y 65 down to y 43, and back up.
- **Neither of the other two:**
  - Batch 1's 31102026 Echo (101,140) went "Climb out of the water" and "back the way it came" by turns along a shore. No walk
    finished, and the spell was spent "waiting for a feasible step" there.
  - Batch 2's 202 Sol (118,620) was in water at y 20 to 21.
  - My first note to the lead named batch 1; the shape is batch 2's.

**The cause, two gaps in F-25c4at1's hold:**
- The hold was armed only when a walk back completed lower than it began. These walks ended refused.
- Where the walk began was taken from its latest offer. Offered again a block or two lower before it ended, the start moved down with
  it.

**User-visible behavior:** an AI player whose walk back along its way in took it down again, done or refused, is not sent the same way
again as soon as its climb has brought it back up there. The climb's own step up has its turn.

**Changed files:**
- `PortalRunCampaign.retrace`: a run of walk-back offers is recorded from its first offer. An offer again before the walk ends does
  not move it; a run with no offer for 200 ticks is over (`RETRACE_RUN_GAP`).
- `PortalRunCampaign.taskFinished`, the walk back's branch: on any end of the walk back, done or not, the hold is armed when it ended
  lower than where its run began. The run then ends.
- **The hold itself is F-25c4at1's, unchanged:** within three across of where the run began, no higher, for 600 ticks.
- **Not held from where the walk ended:** in 707 Sol the bottom is four across from where the walk began, and from the bottom the walk
  back is what takes it back up.
- **A line, said at a walk back's end** wherever this rule and F-25c4at1's would decide differently, or from different cells:
  "F-25c4at2-marker: <who> walk back ended <done|refused> at <pos>, its run began at <start>, its last offer at <last>: F-25c4at1
  <hold|none>, F-25c4at2 <hold|none>". It is permanent at the lead's word, so iteration 30's consoles show how often the rule acts.
  `067aa0fa`'s message calls it temporary; it is not (`bde244d4`, no force-push).
- **The line's player, after the gate** (`8f71b3eb`): only `toldLava` set the campaign's player name, so in the gate the line read
  with none. It is now set in `retrace`. The gated code differs from the merged code only in that log field; no decision changes.

Nothing is given or moved: the change only holds a walk back for a while. No timeout or stuck threshold is changed.

**Schema/configuration changes:** none. No saved keys.

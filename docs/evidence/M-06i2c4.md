## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (M-06i2c3's trace, Factions seed 20261030, on M-06i2c2's code). Of the legs M-06i2c2 left as they were (no column along the way it saw and could stand on), the brink of the ground it stood on hid the slope below on 443 of 1,899 distinct legs with bases on and 321 of 929 with bases off: the sight line to the columns below met its own ground within 5 blocks of it.

**The change (Factions-only, `FactionsPlayer.destination`).** When the back search along a leg (M-06i2c2's `backAlong`) finds nothing from where it stands, it looks again, along the same columns, from the brink of the ground it stands on toward the leg: F-35m's `WorldKnowledge.looksOverTheBrink`, its eyes walked out over its own floor, at most 4 blocks, never past the brink or over air. That look has its own budget of sight lines (`BACK_LOOKS`). Where it sees nothing from there either, the leg is as before. No new seeing: a column's top is known only when a sight line from those eyes reaches it.

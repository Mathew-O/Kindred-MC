## Scope and implementation

The lead ruled (2026-11-14) that once LOOPS's F-25c6c gives each action its own rule for what the evidence shows,
F-31g1's settle after a restart calls those rules. The rules are `Before`, `Settled`, and
`NativePillarAction.settleFromEvidence`. The break gets a rule of its own with F-31g1's behaviour unchanged, and a
pillar step is settled by the pillar's rule.

User-visible behavior: `aiplayer native settle <name>` now also settles a `native_pillar` step a crash cut short. With
its cell still open and the saved inventory exactly as before the step, it is not done. Otherwise it is left for the
operator. Its block in its cell cannot be shown to be the step's from a fingerprint, so that case is never settled done
on its own. Breaks are settled as before, in the same words. The operator guide's two settle sections say so.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-core: `ActionSettlement`.
  - `breakRule(present, before, observed)`: F-31g1's comparison, now one function that `decide` uses.
  - `PILLAR` and `pillarCell(kind, preconditions)`: reads `block=<id>; pos=x, y, z; …`.
  - `decide`'s verdicts and words are unchanged.
- companion-core tests: `ActionSettlementTest`, two new cases.
  - The first is `breakRule`'s table, with `decide` agreeing for each.
  - The second reads the pillar cell, and checks `decide` still leaves a pillar step to the operator.
- companion-server-adapter:
  - `NativeBreakAction.settleFromEvidence(Before, InventoryEvidence after, ServerWorld, BlockPos, String block)`. It
    reads only the fingerprint, through `breakRule`; the live break's receipt-based close is untouched.
  - `InventoryEvidence.fromSave`, the save's stacks kept, with `describeSave` its description.
  - `PlayerRuntime`. `settleEach` reads a pillar step's cell as it reads a break's block, waiting for its ground to
    load. `settleDecision` calls the action type's own rule through `Before.recorded`, and `decide`'s words for a
    break.
- gametest: `NativeSettleGameTests`, two new fixtures.
- docs: `operator-guide.md`, this file, the tracker row.

Schema/configuration changes: none. The `action.settled` event's subject for a pillar step is now its cell, as a
break's is its block.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- **Other kinds** (a place, a craft, a take) still have no rule of their own and are left for an operator, as before.
- **The stacks branch of each rule** is not reachable after a restart. Only the fingerprint is recorded, so a restart
  never settles a pillar step done.
- **This branch carries #288's head** (`9a95ccf`). If #288 changes before it lands, this branch merges it again.

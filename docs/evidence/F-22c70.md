# F-22c70 — No torch in a cell of the way it is making

Date: 2026-11-12
Agent / branch: Claude / `claude/ci-green-1112-nightportal`, on main `32b304a1`
Build: `./gradlew --offline build` green, JUnit 1,244 tests, 0 failures, 7 skipped
Status: in progress (the PR's CI).

## Scope and implementation

**User-visible behaviour:** a player making its way up out of a shelter (a stair, a dig, a pillar) lights its way with torches beside the way, not in it. Before, it put a torch in the cell its way had just dug, walked into that cell, and could not pillar up from it.

**Found:** `NativeNightShelterGameTests.dugInForTheNightWithNothingToDoBeforeDaylightItStaysInAndWaits` fails about one run in 15:
- main `2c67d674`: 1 of 10 alone;
- `5cfe873f`, before #256: 1 of 10;
- #256's merge `0ed915a4`: 0 of 10;
- docs-only #261 on main: once in CI.

Each failure was "Morning: up to look for animals: End: Nether: …". #256 (F-35m) is not the cause: the failure is older.

**The probe: morning, not night.** The first reading was that the End goal's portal leg dug out of its sealed night shelter at night. A throwaway probe disproved that. It logged each part of `dugInForTheNight`, with the world time, wherever `StrongholdLeg.enterPortal` gave out a leg toward a far portal. In 2 copies:
- Every leg ("Walk to the portal", "Make a way to the portal") was given at world time 1003 or later, after the fixture's morning.
- None was given while it was night: 0 probe lines with `night true`.
- At night, F-35c1's guard turns the portal walk into the one walk into it, which is refused from the shelter, so the player waits.
- The failing runs fit the same timing.
So it does not leave the shelter at night. The failure is in the morning, on its way up 5 blocks to its portal. The lead dropped the night slice on that finding.

**Cause (product), in `PortalRunCampaign.light`:**
1. The torch is kept out of only one neighbour: the cell along the campaign's own dig heading (`digDirection()`), "the cell ahead, whose floor the next step digs away".
2. The portal leg's way up (`buildRoute`, "Make a way to the portal") digs along its own route, not that heading.
3. So the cell it had just dug, its next stand, was a free spot. "light the way" put a torch there, "walk on" took it into that cell, and "pillar up" from it was refused by `NativePillarAction`: "The cell it stands in is not open", because a torch is not replaceable.

This happened in every run of the test: 1 to 2 refusals each, in passing runs too. In about one run in 15 the player then cycled for the rest of the morning without getting up. The cycle ran between the way-maker, "Climb to find another way", "Jump down from the ledge" and "waiting for a feasible step", and the player wandered down to 7 to 9 below its shelter.

**The change:**
- `buildRoute` keeps the cells of the way it has just planned (`wayCells`): every step's start, its end and the cell above it, and the cells it digs or fills.
- They are kept for the plan's own life, not a time: until the next plan replaces them, or until the player stands more than 8 blocks (`WAY_LEFT`) from every cell of the way. A long pillar or stair takes as long as it takes.
- `light` puts no torch in one of them (`onTheWay`).
- A torch goes beside the way instead, or none at all this decision.

**Nothing granted:** its own torches, placed where it stands.

**Changed files:**
- `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign`: `wayCells`, `WAY_LEFT`, `onTheWay`; `buildRoute` records the plan's cells; `light` skips them. Also `blockedForTest`, a copy of its last few refused steps for the fixture.
- Test: `NativeNightShelterGameTests.itsWayUpFromItsShelterIsNotBlockedByItsOwnTorch` (batch `nightShelterWayTorch`). It stages the test above's scene: the night held 1,500 ticks, then morning. A latch fails it once its way's pillar up is refused, "The cell it stands in is not open", read from the campaign's own list of steps refused lately (`blockedForTest`). It passes once the player is back up at the surface, having placed at least one torch: a torch standing where its step said it would put one. Counted as placed, not as left in its pack, because a torch its way digs through is picked back up.
- The scene is now a shared `stage` helper, with the first test unchanged in what it sets.

## Known limits

- **Only the way planned:** a torch placed before a way was planned can still stand where a later way goes. Walking through its cell is fine, but a pillar up from it is still refused. **The follow-up**, clearing a torch in the cell it stands in before `native_pillar` (which also covers players' torches), is MODES's, after F-35o.
- **The cycle is not fixed here:** the way-maker ⇄ "Climb to find another way" cycle that followed the refusal is reported, not changed. It came after the torch's refusal in every failing run seen. Whether it comes without one is open.

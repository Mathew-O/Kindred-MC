## Scope and implementation

A review of the project ([review](../review-2026-10-24.md), D2, D3, D6) found three things wrong with how native players spoke:
- **They were outside the social lane.** `SocialBrain` and its consent, audience, cooldown and listener limits applied only to the legacy companion body. A native player, the product's real body, was never a participant.
- **What they said went to the whole server,** as eight direct broadcasts. Two gave coordinates: "I built and lit a Nether portal near x, y, z" told a manhunt's hunters where the runner was.
- **Hurt while idle, a player accused whoever stood near:** "Hey! Why are you hitting me?!", for a fall or a burn as much as a blow, every two seconds while it lasted, to everyone within sixteen blocks. The idle gaze also went on after a stop.

**The social lane for both bodies** (`CompanionSocials`, `SocialPerception`). A participant is any AI player's body, the companion's or the native player's.
- **When a native player takes part:** whenever it is in the world and not stopped, working or not. A companion still takes part only while a task holds it.
- **Another AI player** in either body is a companion contact, not a person: never a listener, and greeted only as a companion.
- **Everything else** is the lane's own, as before: who may hear (its owner, others only with public conversation), mute, sociability, the ambient interval (about two minutes), the listener cooldowns, finite exchanges.

**Hurt, from the game's own record** (`SocialSnapshot.Harm`, `SocialBrain`).
- **What hurt it** is read from the damage itself, never from who stands near:
  - a player who struck it, or whose arrow did, by name;
  - a mob, by kind;
  - the world: a fall, fire, lava, no air, thorns, a magma block, hunger, the cold, lightning;
  - nothing known: nobody named.
- **Its word on it** is one, when the hurt begins: "Hey, Steve, watch it!", "Ouch, a Zombie got me.", "Ouch. A fall." It says nothing more while the hurt goes on, and nothing again until the lane's interval has passed.
- **The idle observation** holds no one's eye while hurt and says nothing; it does nothing while the player is stopped.

**What it says of its own doing** (`PlayerRuntime.say`): a portal lit, the End portal found, the dragon killed, a build, a ranch or a field done, a base begun.
- **To whoever may control it:** its owner and its administrators, with where it is.
- **To others, without the place:** only when it talks in public, within 64 blocks, and never to an opponent in a match it is in.
- **The server log** keeps it all.
- **Not narrated:** the legacy lane's narration of every finished or blocked skill ("Finished native_place…") is kept for the companion body. A native player's work finishes hundreds of skills.

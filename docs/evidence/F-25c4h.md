## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player's own crafting table or furnace within reach counts as at hand for its plan, whatever walks failed round it. It no longer puts its table down and picks it straight back up, over and over.

Found in B7's second pairs (seed 8675309): with the strategist on, Kindred put its crafting table down 115 times and picked it up 113 times, most of them in one stretch of every two or three seconds, with nothing crafted in between. In the run before, with the strategist off, Kindred put it down 60 times and Sol 55; a player otherwise puts it down about fifteen times in a run. The strategist saw it: "Player is stalled picking up the crafting table with only one plank".
1. Three walks to stone just beside it had failed ("No walkable route"). A failed walk sets aside the cells round its target, a block either way and six up and down, for as long as the player stays within eight blocks.
2. It put its table down for a craft, inside those cells. A workstation in set-aside cells was not "nearby", so the plan sent it for logs to make another table: a gathering trip.
3. Leaving to gather, a player takes its workstations along. It picked the table up, and with the table in its pack, the plan said to put it down.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign.nearestWorkstation`: its own workstation within reach (4.5 blocks from its eyes) is at hand whether or not its cell is set aside; farther ones are still skipped where a walk to them failed. `PortalRunCampaign` is changed on `modest-faraday` only in its hole-up lines.

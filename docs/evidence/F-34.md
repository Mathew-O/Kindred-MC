## Design changes

- A `match` channel beside the design's seven (the owner, via the lead: the Wire is for every mode): every player in one match, any mode; a match without sides gives each player its own side key `match:player`.

- `Visibility` beside public is per group, not ordered; a group fact goes only on its own channel or privately, where the receiver must share the group with the sender.
- Announcements: whole-server public only from the server, only kinds the mode registers (Session A's), bypassing the budget, never dedupe.
- Co-op's team for the Wire is every AI player out of a match (`world`), as E-50's teammates; people are not on it (F-34c decides how team news reaches them). In a Factions world each faction's AI players are their own team (`world:<faction>`), so a team's news never crosses a faction.
- Teammates' updates (F-34c; the lead, 2026-11-09, by MODE-02) follow the Wire's per-mode table: a team's in Co-op, Factions and a world with no mode chosen; none in Speedrun or Builder, whose players compete; in a manhunt each side's own (`match:<id>:hunters`, `:runners`, from the match service's membership), so hunters coordinate and a runner never has the hunters' state. Fixed: before, a runner out of a match had the hunters' updates, and every AI player out of a match had every other's, rival factions included.
- A person's line budget (4 a minute; half for fewer, one and a half for more) and per-person dedupe sit on top of the channel's.

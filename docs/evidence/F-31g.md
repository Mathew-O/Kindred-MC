## The run

Seed 101; Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric Loader 0.19.5, Fabric API 0.116.17+1.21.1; OpenJDK 21.0.12.1; 4 cores, 15 GB, the
server's heap 6 GB; `maxNativePlayers=8` in the server's own policy.

```
tools/soak/soak.py --jar companion-server-adapter/build/libs/kindred-mc-0.1.0-SNAPSHOT.jar --seed 101 \
    --out run/soak/a --ticks 36000 --segments 4 --kill 2 --kill-after 900
tools/soak/report.py run/soak/a
tools/soak/restart_check.py run/soak/a
tools/soak/items.py run/soak/a/world-folder --console run/soak/a/world-folder/console-s0.txt ...
```

- **s0.** An evaluation run (`starters=Kindred,Sol,Echo; players=Ada,Bram,Cleo,Dara,Eli; founders=3; goal=none;
  mode=factions; ticks=36000`) makes the faction world. Then at the console, `aiplayer mode world factions`, the
  operator's own choice, saved to `mode.properties` (the evaluation's own choice is not saved). It stops itself.
- **s1 to s4.** Plain starts, no `KINDRED_EVAL`, as on the owner's server. For each player, `aiplayer native connect`
  then `aiplayer native resume` (below). Then `tick sprint 36000` and `stop` at the console.
- **s2.** Killed with SIGKILL, about 34 s after its sprint ended, with every player at work at the normal tick rate.
- **After each stop or kill.** The database with its WAL and the `kindred/memory` folder are copied as they lie on disk.

Five starts, 180,000 ticks (7.5 game days), four clean stops (s0, s1, s3, s4) and one kill (s2).

## Findings

1. **After a restart every AI player waits offline and stopped, until an operator acts on each.**
   - On an unattended restart all 8 players stayed idle: no player comes back by itself. The operator guide says so for
     native players ("Restart leaves them offline/stopped").
   - The operator must, for each player, `aiplayer native connect <name>`, then `aiplayer native resume <name>`.
     Connected, each is still "stopped. Resume explicitly".
   - The Factions setup's step 5 said list and connect and left out the resume; it is fixed here.
   - No bulk form exists (no `all` for connect or resume): 16 commands for 8 players.
   - The behaviour stays as it is: a legacy safety restriction, kept until an explicit, tested migration. Whether
     players come back on their own is the owner's policy decision (the lead has put an opt-in policy to them).
   - Proposed, not built here: a bulk admin command, `aiplayer native connect all` and `resume all`. Admin tooling
     only; it changes no decision.
2. **After a crash, a player caught mid-action stays out for good: there is no operator path back.**
   - The kill caught 5 of the 8 players each with one `native_break` dispatched: a stone, deepslate or andesite block
     being dug.
   - From the next start each is blocked: "Unresolved action ledger rows detected (1); operator reconciliation
     required … no replacement items are created". Connect is refused.
   - `aiplayer recover <name>` is refused for a native body: "Use native lifecycle controls; legacy skill/setup
     integration is unavailable for this body". It is the mob body's command.
   - No other command settles the action. The operator guide says "Blocked/incomplete records keep their slot until
     recovery/removal is implemented".
   - In s3 and s4 only 3 of the 8 played. Nothing was replayed or recreated, as the rules require.
   - For a month on one server, one crash (an out-of-memory, a host restart) can take most of the players out until
     native reconciliation exists.
   - The fix is its own slice with a fixture: an operator's reconciliation of a native action from its evidence. The
     before-evidence is the inventory's hash; the block named in the preconditions is still there or gone. It settles
     the action as done or not done, never replays it, then frees the player.
3. **The tick.** Under sprint the tick was 36.1 ms with 8 players (s1) and 20.4 ms (s2). Sprint measures the work of a
   tick, not the month's real rate. At 20 tps with 8 players, the mean was 23.6 ms and 1.3% of ticks were over 50 ms
   (above). There is headroom, but not much in the worst minutes. That is for the owner's 12 GB server to know; the
   perf campaign stays held.

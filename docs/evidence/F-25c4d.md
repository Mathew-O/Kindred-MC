## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: before the work below ground, while trees are in sight, a player on the portal run takes along enough wood to smelt the iron the route still needs, unless the coal it carries will smelt it. Below, with no coal, it smelts with that wood instead of hunting for coal, and the same wood is there to make sticks from.

Found in B7's second pairs: in the first run (strategist off), Kindred spent 16% of its time looking for coal to smelt its iron, most of it tunnelling.

- `PortalRunPlanner` (core):

  - Packing before the work below, after the spare table's wood: logs for that many planks more, while trees are in sight.
  - A smelt it has the fuel for now (the shield's first iron, the iron pickaxe's three) goes first; the packing follows.
- `PortalRunPlannerTest`: the iron stage's shared setup, and three cases that pack a spare pickaxe, a table's wood or a
  shield's, now carry coal for their iron, so fuel stays out of what they test; the new case is fuel's own.

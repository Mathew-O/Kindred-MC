## The failure

On main `c7b19178`, REFACTOR's 10 runs of `NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests` failed `aCausewayThroughTheSeaItSawIsStillWalked` once: "Along the causeway into Blue's land east: at …, move Choice[kind=SCOUT, …]; ended [… 8430, … 8634]".

**From the run's log:**
1. **Where Ada stood:** at z …565 when the walk was asked, three south of the island's middle (z …568). The causeway is three wide (z 567–569).
2. **Where the walk aimed:** the end is the spot in the target chunk nearest the player. `FactionsPlayer.legEnd` clamps the player's own x and z, so the end was the column straight east, over the sea.
3. **Why it was refused:** it saw no ground in that column, since water is not standable. So `destination` fell back to the player's own height, air over the sea: "No route … (the goal standable false, on Block{minecraft:air} …)", refused twice.
4. **Why it never walked:** it then went underground for iron, and no faction walk is asked below the surface (M-06i2c1).

**The lead's ruling (2026-11-23):** the test stays as it is. It found a scout that refuses a walk to land it can see three north. Widening the causeway would stage the defect away.

## Scope and implementation

**What a player does differently:** a player whose walk ends in a chunk across water now walks to the land it can see along that chunk's near edge, the causeway three north. It no longer aims at the sea straight ahead.

**Changed:** `FactionsPlayer` (companion-server-adapter):
- **`destination`:** only where the walk's end is in the target chunk (not a leg's end), and only where its own column shows no ground the player sees, it asks `alongEdge`.
- **`alongEdge` (new):**
  - **Which columns:** those of the inset edge the end was clamped to (x the same, every inset z; or z the same, every inset x), nearest the player first.
  - **How each is looked at:** as `destination` looks at its own column, its own height first, then down and up, eight each way.
  - **What counts:** only ground a sight line reaches (`WorldKnowledge.looks(player, EDGE_LOOKS)`, at most 8 sight lines). What the player cannot see sets nothing. With none, the end is as before.
- **Logged once a walk:** "<name> faction walk end moved to seen ground: <end> for <old end>, along <chunk>'s edge".

**Tests:**
- `NativeFactionWalkEndGameTests` (new; its batches registered after the open-water test's).
- `NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests`: `island()` and `factions()` made package-visible; its tests unchanged.

**No schema or configuration change.**

## The cause

`PortalRunCampaign.relocateFor(level)` chose the place as follows:
- It turns `digDirection()` once for each attempt.
- Every third attempt, it goes home if home is more than 16 away.
- Otherwise it picks `lastFeet.offset(heading, 24)`, at the player's own Y.
- It looked at neither what the plan's own step wanted, nor what the player had seen at the target.

## The change (adapter; one core accessor)

**`PortalRunCampaign.relocateFor`:**
- **Up with the climb:** when the plan's step, as the spell came, was a climb up toward something, the target is also `RELOCATE_RISE` = 24 up. `relocateStep` already digs up to a higher target's level, then along.
  - **The labels that count**, each the plan's step after " - ": "Climb toward the surface for food", "Climb toward the surface for logs", "Climb toward the surface for water", "Climb toward coal", and "Climb toward water at <cell>".
  - **Not counted:** "Climb to find another way", the plan's fallback with no way on. It was counted at first, and the gate showed that wrong (below). "Climb to the cave toward lava" does not count either.
  - 505's case is "Climb toward the surface for food". The climb fixture's marker lines show that label on the final tree.
- **Not to water it saw:** a heading is passed over when its target's column is water the player saw, or the target is open water by F-35j's `seenOpenWaterToward`. The next heading clockwise is tried, for four headings in all.
  - A shore cell fails the open-water test, because land within two makes the water not open. It is still water, so the column test is needed too.
  - If every heading is toward water, the move goes home when home is far, and otherwise on as before.
- **Not changed:** the every-third move home; the walk and dig of `relocateStep`; no saved key, event kind or schema.
- **Nothing is read from the world:** water is what the player's sight remembered (`Sight.seenWaterColumn`, new, beside `seenOpenWaterToward`). A column it never saw is not water to it.

**The marker, stated before the gate:**

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
- A native request is charged to the period F-11d chooses (`SqliteCompanionStore.chargeableBudget`), not to the local day containing now.
  - When no period is open, the request releases its activity seat. That happens when the clock is behind the last period, or a zone edit is before its first whole local day.
  - The player is told "No activity allowance until …" and is not stopped.
  - Before, the same case threw in `openBudget`, which dispatch turned into a metadata fault: the player halted pending reconciliation.
- Until the named instant a request is refused at once, so neither autonomy nor commands ask the store again every tick.
- `/aiplayer limit timezone <zone> [name]` works. It saves the zone and says what it means for the allowance.
  - The current period runs to its end as kept.
  - The new zone starts at its first local day beginning at or after that end, with the gap, if any, named.
- `/aiplayer limit status`, `limit cap` and `limit timezone` now reach native players. Before, every limit command refused a native player ("Use native lifecycle controls"). Its cap could be set only from the K panel (`nativeSetCap`), and its zone not at all.
  - They are checked against the player's native access, as its lifecycle controls are. Cap and timezone still require an operator.
  - `limit offline` stays a legacy-body control. It is an owner-presence switch, which a native player's decisions do not depend on.

Changed modules/files:
- `PlayerRuntime`:
  - Native dispatch's budget preparation, its not-chargeable path, and the `noPeriodUntil` gate on `Entry`.
  - `noPeriodReason`, `timezoneChangeReport`.
  - `limitEntry`, used by `limitStatus`, `setLimitCap` and `setLimitTimezone`.
  - `setLimitTimezone`.
- `SqliteCompanionStore.latestPeriod`, read-only, for the timezone report.
- `NativeAccountingGameTests` (new, registered after `NativePaidSkillsGameTests`).
- `SqliteCompanionStoreTest` (`latestPeriod` assertions).

Dependencies satisfied: F-11d.
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: none. A zone edit is one ordinary `activity_limits` revision; periods stay the existing `budget_buckets` rows.
Requirements covered (F-11 card: "safe period transitions and no replenishment on restart", native enforcement):
- A zone edit never resets spent time.
- No period overlaps or reopens an earlier one.
- A clock behind the last period, or a zone edit waiting for its first whole local day, is a wait, not a fault.

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. The source is #78's CI, run 42556777789 (head `0798a06`, job 128964173529),
`NativeLifeFirstGameTests.ledOffFromTheHerdItGoesBackToWhereItSawItAndHuntsFirst`: "8 blocks not in their state", the
roof's oak stairs and slab at y −47 left as air. #71's re-run showed the same in `NativeHouseGameTests` (5 stairs at
y −52), as MODES reported.
- Mason was building its cabin's roof from its scaffold and stood on the roof itself, at y −48.50: on a bottom slab or a
  bottom stair, half a block up in that block's own cell.
- Every walk from there, to the foot of its scaffold and its other stands, was "No route from … (on the ground true) …
  (expanded 1, standing false)". After eight in a row the build stopped, and its repair stopped the same way.
- `NativeNavigator` stands a body only in an empty cell over a full block's top. A slab's cell is not empty, and the
  cell above it has a floor half a block high. `find` already falls back, for a start that is no stand, to the block
  below or to a stand beside it. On a roof every cell beside a slab is another slab or stair, so the search had nothing
  to start from.

Changed file: adapter `NativeNavigator`.
- **`lowStand`:** a cell holding a bottom slab or a bottom stair, with room for the body above it.
- **`acrossLowStands`:** when the start is a low stand and nothing beside it is a stand, a search goes across the run of
  low stands, at most 16 cells, to the nearest stand beside one of them: level, a step up or a step down.
  - The route is searched from that stand as usual.
  - The cells crossed are kept at the head of the route, so the body walks along them in turn.
- **Unchanged:** a start that is a stand, or has one below it or beside it, behaves as before. So does every move the
  search makes.

New fixture: `NativeNavigationGameTests.aWalkFromASlabItStandsOnStepsOffIt` (batch `slabStart`).
- A roof of bottom oak slabs, three by three, on stone three high. East of its edge, a stair of blocks down to the
  ground.
- Fixture staging: the player set on the roof's middle slab, where every cell beside it is another slab.
- It walks to the ground five blocks east and checks that the player arrives.

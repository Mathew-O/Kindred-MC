## Scope and implementation

**Found:** on PR #259's CI (12 November), `NativeFactionShelterGameTests` had a player dug in four down by a stair sent to
"Explore the cave toward iron". The walk ended twelve blocks off at a cell below the ground. F-25c4cd's night hold asks only
where a walk ends, so it was not held. Its way went up the stair, over the ground and out of the shelter, and the player dug
in again (149 ticks dug in). F-22c72 removed the meanwhile step that gave that walk; any other walk of its work whose way
crosses open ground was not covered.

**User-visible behavior:** dug in for the night, a walk of its work to a cell below ground whose way leaves the shelter and
crosses open ground waits for daylight, as a walk ending on the surface already did. A walk it can make under the rock goes
on.

**Changed files:**
- `PortalRunCampaign`:
  - `outUnderTheSky(player, task, tick)`: the old end check first. Then, for a `native_goto` ending below ground, the way
    it would go.
  - `wayOutUnderTheSky`: the way is searched as `NativeGoToSkill` searches its route (`NativeNavigator.throughDoors()`, from
    `NativeGoToSkill.restingOn`, at most 4,000 nodes, within 96). It counts as out under the sky if any cell of a complete
    way, other than the one it stands in, sees the sky.
  - A way not found says nothing, and the walk goes on as before.
  - The answer for one walk's end is kept 200 ticks, because a held walk is planned again each tick.
  - The one call site is the hold's, behind `nightIn && planned.isPresent() && !passive(…) && !surfacing(…)`. `nightIn` is
    `dugInForTheNight`: night, a night shelter, sheltered and in it.
  - `outUnderTheSkyForTest` adds the way check, for fixtures only.
- **Gametest:** `NativeShelterWayGameTests` (new; registered in `fabric.mod.json`).

Nothing is given or moved by the code; the hold plans its existing "Seal the shelter" or "Wait for daylight" step. It reads
only cells the walk itself would go by, through the same navigator its walk uses: no hidden-world knowledge.

**Schema/configuration changes:** none.

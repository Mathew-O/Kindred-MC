2. Then "Cast: step closer", or "step up to the frame's floor", refused "No walkable route" to every stand.
3. Then "no way on from here - Climb to find another way", climbing away from the frame.

**An example, batch 1's 505 Kindred:**
- It refilled at y −54, and F-25c4dm1's dig back brought it to its frame's floor, y −51.
- There "Cast: step closer" was refused to all five stands, three to six blocks along through rock.

**The cause:** `backUp`, F-25c4dm1's dig back up to the frame, digs only from *below* the floor. At the floor's level with no
walk to a stand, nothing digs along to it, and the cast gives up to the plan's fallback climb.

**User-visible behavior:** an AI player back at the level of its half-built portal frame, with rock between it and every spot
it could pour from, digs its way along to the frame and pours the next block, rather than climbing away.

**Changed files** (`PortalRunCampaign`, adapter):
- **`backUp`:** at the floor's level it calls `alongTheFloor` (new). This is the same `Digger.toward` to the block under
  `site.stand()`, with the frame's cells and mould kept (`frameCells`), labelled "Cast: dig along to the frame". Its progress is
  the distance across to its stand, and the same two failures give the site up. Beside its stand, the stands are its to walk to
  again.
- **`within`, while the dig along is under way:** it asks `alongTheFloor` first, so no pour or mould is tried from inside its own
  tunnel. Without this, the next cell came within reach by distance two from the stand, and three pours refused for no face set
  the cast aside.
- **`within`, at the floor's level off its stands:** once a pour has been refused from where it stands, it tries a stand it can
  walk to, else `backUp`, before the same pour again (`3967f205`). This came from the gate's run 1 on `e412fb5a`: there a pocket
  within reach of the next cell by distance tried the pour first, was refused three times, and waited.
- **The marker,** said once a site where the dig along begins: "F-25c4bx2-marker: <name> at its frame's floor at <pos>, no walk
  to a stand: digs along to its stand at <pos>, <n> across".

It reads only blocks on the floor's level between it and its own stand, through the existing dig. No timeout or stuck threshold
is changed. No saved keys.

**The bound,** so a refused pour cannot alternate with the dig along at one place for good (line numbers on `3967f205`):
1. **One refused pour per cell.** A refused or failed `native_cast` adds its feet to `castFailedStands` (:1007). Only a cast or
   fill that takes clears it (:959). While its feet are on such a cell at the floor's level, off its stands, `within` does not
   pour (:7084).
2. **The dig along cannot stand still.** Its progress is the distance across, `digAlongBest` (:7195). 600 ticks
   (`DIG_BACK_TICKS`, :7101) with no nearer count a failure (:7199), which covers a dig whose steps leave its feet where they
   are. A tick with no cell to dig counts one too, once a while (:7215). At two failures it digs nothing more (:7203) and gives
   the site up (:7219). So each dig, move, refused pour moves its feet to a new cell, about six at most, each refused at most
   once.
3. **Beside its stand with still no stand to walk to:** `alongTheFloor` returns empty (:7182, across ≤ 1). `within` then falls
   through to the pour from that refused cell. Its third refusal sets the task aside for 1,200 ticks (:963), as on main. At that
   point there is no dig along left to alternate with.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: when life comes first for a player and its hunger calls for food (F-25c9), it takes the food it has put by in its own storage before it hunts. It walks to its storage if it is out of reach, then takes the most filling safe food there, as much as brings it to enough to last (a full bar and a reserve, in its body and its pack together). With no storage, or nothing safe to eat in it, it hunts, fishes and looks for food as before.

Changed modules/files:
- `PlayerRuntime`:
  - `storedFood`: its assigned storage, in its dimension, loaded and within a walk (96 blocks). It returns a walk there, or a `native_take` of the safe food it needs.
  - It is used first in the life-first food need. A take from storage is not recorded as one of the goal's hunt steps.
  - `PlayerRuntime` is also changed on `modest-faraday`, but not in these lines.
- `NativeLifeFirstGameTests`:
  - The hungry fixture is now a helper taking the loaves in its storage. The original runs with none and no storage, unchanged.
  - A second fixture has 20 loaves in a chest of its own, a walk off.

The take is the existing `native_take` (E-08c): only from the storage its administrator assigned, verified unchanged, exactly what left the chest arriving in its inventory, recorded in the ledger.

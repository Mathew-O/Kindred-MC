## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: each AI player keeps a ledger of the people and players it has met in a world. What it saw counts
most, then what a teammate's own record says (sent through the team, never chat), then what a person says of themselves,
which is only ever a claim: it holds a game day, is told as what was said and not seen, and is retired once seen. Whoever
said something can take it back ("forget that", "forget what I said about the Nether", "that's wrong"), the correction
kept with who said it and when. Asked what it knows of someone ("what does Sol need?"), it answers from the ledger,
without the model, as far as the asker may be told; its voice is told the same lines by the same rules. The ledger is kept
with the world, apart from what was said and where people were.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-core`:
  - `social.PeerLedger` (new): one per player per world; sightings, episodes, hand-overs each way (the game's record);
    teammates' updates; claims, their expiry, their confirmation by a sighting, and their correction with provenance; the
    trust order; the lines for the voice, each with who may be told it; a snapshot that leaves out claims and people's
    places. 64 peers, 20 episodes and 8 claims each.
  - `social.PeerUpdate` (new): a teammate's structured update, bounded.
  - `social.Claims` (new): first-person claims, takings-back and questions, read without a model.
  - `social.PeerAnswers` (new): the fixed answers from the ledger, by tier.
  - `ConversationAudience`: the situation carries the ledger's lines (a constructor without them is kept); `mayBeTold`
    says who may be told each.
  - Tests: `social.PeerLedgerTest` (9), `social.ClaimsTest` (4).
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `PeerRadio` (new): the ledgers, fed once a second (who each sees), every five seconds (teammates' updates) and as the
    game's events come; read by the voice and the fixed answers; loaded lazily and saved every minute and at a stop.
  - `PlayerMemoryStore`: `<uuid>.peers.json`, read and written off the server thread.
  - `CompanionSocials`: a hearing hook. A claim is noted before any limit on replies; a taking-back and a question about
    someone it knows are answered in the player's own words, without the voice.
  - `PlayerVoice`: the facts carry the ledger's lines.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: hook lines: the radio and the hearing at construction; its turn in the server tick; the members and
    each one's update (its goal and progress or its task; its follow-up queue's gathering; its unpromised finds; its
    place); the ledger's lines in the voice's facts; E-49's blow, gift and toss into the ledger; the save at a stop; a
    fixture accessor; and `memory forget <id> <player>` for a native player, which the legacy memory commands refused.
- gametest: `NativePeerLedgerGameTests` (3), a new entrypoint.
- No new dependency; the manifest's versions are unchanged.

Schema/configuration changes: none in the metadata database. The ledger is a JSON file per player in the world's
`kindred/memory` folder, as the atlas is.

## Design changes and what was not built

- Claims are first-person only, and are never written to disk: nothing said is kept across a restart. Taking back
  applies to one's own claims; nobody can correct the game's record or a teammate's update by saying so.
- Questions about someone it knows are answered locally, without the model, even with the voice on: the answer is from
  the record and costs nothing. An unknown name falls through to ordinary talk.
- A claim said in the second or so after a player joins, before its ledger is read from its file, is not noted.
- Teammates are AI players out of any match, as co-op's share-out reckons them, and they share places. A person is not
  sent updates; what people know of a player is still what they see and are told.
- Not built here: promises (`HandOvers`) and asks said yes to as ledger entries; feeling (wariness is already in the
  voice's situation through E-49; friendship is E-24); conversation topics from the ledger (E-51); needs feeding
  `ShareOut` and offers of what is spare (E-53's initiative and E-21/E-22's gifts); the watch page.

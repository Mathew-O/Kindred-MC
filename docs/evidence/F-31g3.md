## The failure

**What happened:** in E-40 iteration 30 batch 1 (`e40a30-63b97ac`), on seed 303 at 16:09:16, Echo was stopped mid-cast:

> Native placement effect cannot be verified; reconcile (placed=true, at -28, -52, 95: air; slots changed: 4: 33 cobblestone -> 32 cobblestone)

It was held for reconciliation for the run's last 37.2 minutes (filed as #521).

**The step:** "Cast: mould at -28, -53, 95", a `native_place` of cobblestone into the mould cell, against its top. The console has no outcome line for that mould, only its goal and then the block.

**Why it never cleared:** an UNCERTAIN action waits for an operator's settle (F-31g1). No one settles in a run with no humans.

## The cause

**How the target was resolved:**
- `NativePlaceAction` and `NativePlayerBody.placeBlock` chose where the block goes by `isReplaceable()`.
- A replaceable target is placed into. Any other target is clicked on its face, and the block is expected in the next cell.
- Echo's mould cell was not `isReplaceable()`, so the block was looked for one above, at -28, -52, 95.

**What vanilla actually does:** `ItemPlacementContext` places into the clicked cell itself whenever that block `canReplace` the context. Where the two tests differ, the block lands in the clicked cell, while the check reads the cell above and finds it air.

**The gap, probed on Minecraft 1.21.1:** a scratch GameTest set every one of the 26,684 block states and tested each against a cobblestone placed on its top.
- `canReplace` true but `isReplaceable()` false: only **sculk vein**, all 128 of its states, each passable.
- `isReplaceable()` true but `canReplace` false: **snow, two to eight layers deep**.

**Echo's mould cell:**
- Sculk vein is common at the depth of the mould cell (Y -53). It is the only block that gives this outcome: placed, one item used, the cell above empty.
- Iteration 30 kept no world, and Echo's nearest spell replay (tick 69520) does not cover the cell. So the block is named from the probe, not read from the world.
- In iterations 27 to 30 this check fired once, here.

## The change (adapter)

**One rule for where the block goes, vanilla's:**
- `NativePlayerBody.takesBlock` is `BlockState.canReplace` of an `ItemPlacementContext` hit on the cell.
- It replaces `isReplaceable()` in `placeBlock`'s and `NativePlaceAction`'s choice of `placedPos`.
- It is used too in the action's precondition, its revalidation before the place, and its no-effect check.

**A cell that will not take the block:**
- `NativePlayerBody.refusesBlock`: replaceable, but vanilla takes nothing into it (deep snow).
- Before, this acted and ended with no effect. Now it is refused before it is tried, with the same reason ("Block placement produced no effect or was obstructed", and why) and its cell.
- `PlayerRuntime`'s F-22c77 counter keys on that reason at that cell, to let a refusing cell be a while, and still does.
- No caller or test relied on the old path: the snow-site fixture uses one layer, which vanilla replaces.

**The roaming edit gate** (`NativeEdits.decide` for a PLACE): `RoamingEdits.placeDenial` gains a block-aware form.
- It runs the same checks as before first: rights, claims, protection and the server's policy (`common`).
- Only the "occupied" denial is then judged by the new rule.
- So it never allows what those checks deny, and never denies more than before.
- The cast's own bucket-pour calls keep the old form.

**Not changed:**
- F-31g1: a truly ambiguous outcome still blocks until settled, and nothing settles from the world on its own.
- No saved key, event kind or schema.
- Nothing reads unseen blocks.

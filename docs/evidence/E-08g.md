# E-08g — Up out of a hollow beside the build, on blocks the build does not take

Date: 2026-11-03
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/hollow-1103
Build: `abe0c20`; recorded in [validation](../validation.md)
Status: complete (this slice); E-08 stays open

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player building that ends up down in a hollow beside its build, below the build's floor, with
nothing of the build in reach, climbs out first once a walk round has failed, and then goes on building. Before, it went
on walking round to the far block, "no walkable route" each time, until the build stopped after eight. Its way up is
made of blocks the build does not take (dirt before cobblestone for a cobblestone wall), so it does not climb out on the
very block it came to place.

Found: 3 of the 17 roof-reach stops in the saved CI logs (E-08f) were a player below the floor ("expanded 6000,
standing true"). The one traced (CI 9, an oak cabin): just after it broke the top of a scaffold column, it was found
three blocks down, in a hollow beside the cabin, and its walks over to the last column found no route eight times.
- The runner already climbs out from where it dug for stone, but only off the site's near ground. A hollow within four
  blocks of the build is where it works from (a blast's crater beside a build), so from there it never climbed.
- Its climb out, once made to happen, spent the first block it held that a route builds with. In the fixture that was
  the wall's last cobblestone, so it climbed out and went off for more.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `BuildRunner`: blocked below the build's floor, on the ground, with a walk round already failed, `backUp` (a route
    up, or the campaign's climb out) before walking round again. `backUp` spends blocks the build does not take first
    (`buildItems`).
  - `PortalRunCampaign`: `wayBlockSlot` (the block a route's pillar, staircase, bridge or a pillar out is built with:
    one `spendFirst` allows, else the first held, as before); `buildRoute(…, Predicate<Item> first)` and
    `climbOut(…, Predicate<Item> first)`. Every other caller is unchanged: `spendFirst` allows every block.
- gametest: `NativeBuildGameTests.aBuildBegunFromAHollowBelowItsFloorClimbsOutAndIsFinished` (new).

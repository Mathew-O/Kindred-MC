## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an operator drops Minecraft skin PNGs into `config/kindred/skins` on the server. In game, **K** → select an AI player → **Skin...** opens a selector showing every skin there from the front, as it will look. Clicking one puts it on that AI player at once, for every player with the Kindred client. The first card brings back the player's own skin. `/aiplayer skin <name|default> [player]` and `/aiplayer skin list` do the same from the console or chat.
- File rules: 64×64, or the old 64×32 layout, at most 64 KiB and 64 skins.
  - The name is the file name: `Red Knight.png` is chosen as `red_knight` and shown as "Red Knight".
  - The arm model comes from a `_slim`/`_wide` ending, or else from the image: the wide model's outer arm columns are empty in a slim skin.
- What is refused: anything else, with its reason in the server log. The PNG header is checked before any decoding, so an image claiming an enormous size is never decoded.
- Who may choose: whoever may adjust the AI player's other settings (an operator, or its controller).
- Persistence: the choice survives restarts, in `config/kindred/skin-choices.properties`.
- Vanilla clients keep seeing the default (or operator-signed) skin, since they accept only Mojang-signed textures.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`: `SkinFiles` (new, pure): name to key, PNG header and size checks, arm model.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `KindredSkins` (new, server):
    - The folder, scanned on its own thread at most every two seconds, with a read-me added if missing.
    - The choices, saved on that thread by an atomic replace.
    - Each chosen PNG sent to every Kindred client when chosen, and to each client as it joins.
    - The catalog sent to a client that opens the selector, at most every two seconds per player.
  - `KindredSkinPayloads` (new):
    - `kindred:skin_binding`: body UUID, key, arm model, PNG; an empty PNG means the player's own skin.
    - `kindred:skin_catalog_request`.
    - `kindred:skin_catalog_entry`: one skin per payload, so a full folder stays under the payload limit.
  - `KindredClientSkins` (new, client): the chosen and offered skins held as textures, released on disconnect, and a front-view figure for previews.
  - `KindredSkinScreen` (new, client).
  - `PlayerListEntrySkinMixin`: a chosen skin first, then the roster marker.
  - Panel:
    - `KindredPanelScreen`: the Skin... button; the roster head shows the chosen skin; "World edits" is shortened to "Edits" to fit.
    - `KindredPanelService`: a `skin` action; the panel state carries each body's UUID and skin.
  - `PlayerRuntime.setSkin`: the authority check, as for the other settings.
  - `PlayerCommands`: `/aiplayer skin`.
  - `KindredMod`: registration.

Dependencies satisfied: E-10a.
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: new optional `config/kindred/skins/` and `config/kindred/skin-choices.properties`; no save or database changes. Without them, nothing changes.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

Not tested / known failures:
- Vanilla clients cannot show folder skins (Mojang signs every texture they accept).
- No capes.
- Only the front of each skin is previewed.
- One client at a time was checked, not two seeing each other's choice.
- Adding or removing files needs Reload in the selector (or any skin command) to be noticed.

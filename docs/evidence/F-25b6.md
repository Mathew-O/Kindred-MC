# F-25b6 — What the unhurtable body after a teleport did to earlier numbers (read only)

Date: 2026-11-23
Agent / branch: Claude, Session B (claude/teleport-measure-1123), at the lead's word; a child of F-25b, after [F-25b5](F-25b5.md)
Build: read of main `c7b19178`, the pinned 1.21.1 jar (yarn 1.21.1+build.3), and e40c5-b6f2d4b's consoles
Status: complete 2026-11-23 (read only: no code change)

## Why

[F-25b5](F-25b5.md) found that on main a native body, once through any `teleportTo`, stayed in vanilla's teleportation
state for good. In that state `ServerPlayerEntity.isInvulnerableTo` is true for all but an ender pearl, and the portal
cooldown never runs down. The lead asked what that did to the numbers already reported:

1. every path that sets the state on a native body, and which the evals and the corpus reach (respawn included);
2. per body, in iterations 27 to 29, e40c4 and e40c5: the time after such a teleport, the deaths and damage after it,
   and Nether crossings;
3. the corpus scenes whose bodies went through one;
4. the effect on the reported exit criteria.

## 1. The paths (a code read)

- `ServerPlayerEntity.teleportTo` is the only setter; `onTeleportationDone` is the only clearer.
- A same-world `teleport(...)`, with or without flags, goes through `requestTeleport` and leaves the state alone. Only
  a cross-world teleport reaches `teleportTo`. An ender pearl's landing calls it too (`EnderPearlEntity`).
- In product code on main:
  - portal crossings (vanilla);
  - the factions home travel, `PlayerRuntime.homeTravel`, when the home is in another dimension;
  - pearl landings.
  - Nothing else teleports a body. The other position changes are `refreshPositionAndAngles`, at creation and for a
    probe.
- **A respawn starts clear.** `NativePlayerBody` respawns through `PlayerManager.respawnPlayer` (at a death and at the
  credits' return), which makes a new entity.
- **Fixtures:** the cross-world ones set it (portal-return, tactical-refresh, hazard-edge, Nether, gold); F-25b5's
  evidence gives each class's result with the fix. `NativeMeleeMechanicsGameTests` and `NativeDuelBenchmarkGameTests`
  teleport within one world, so they never set it.

## 2. Per body

**Iterations 27 to 29:** no Nether crossing in any of them (RUNNER 2's counts). No iteration-28 or -29 goal throws a
pearl, and factions home travel is not in those goals. So no body there was in the state.

**Iteration 30** (the lead): its goal ends at the lit portal, so it never crosses.

**e40c4-b23acc0:** its build predates the fight-health line, so damage after a crossing cannot be read from its
consoles. Deaths can: none in the Nether, in about 1,300 Nether minutes. That fits an unhurtable body; it does not
prove one.

**e40c5-b6f2d4b** has the fight lines. From its consoles (`tools/eval/analysis/aftercrossing.py`; the data on
`claude/corpus-data-e40c5-consoles-1123`, 2ca00325), the three bodies that crossed:

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 202 Echo | 77,560 | 210,440 ticks, 175.4 game minutes | 102 | 19.0 | **0** | 51 (24 hoglin, 23 piglin, 3 wither skeleton, 1 blaze) | 0 | 0 | 49, 4 drops |
| 303 Kindred (cut at about tick 240,000) | about 63,785 (read between the console's tick marks: no eval result) | about 176,200 ticks, about 147 game minutes | 100 | 20.0 | **0** | 50 (all piglin) | 0 | 0 | 53, 5 drops |
| 31102026 Echo | 94,940 | 193,060 ticks, 160.9 game minutes | 142 | 20.0 | **0** | 71 (34 hoglin, 37 piglin) | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **Crossings:** one each. No body died, so none respawned, and with the cooldown frozen none could cross again.
- **Damage:** in 172 fights after their crossings, none of the three bodies' health ever fell, not once in 344
  readings. Before the crossing the same bodies' health fell (4 and 5 drops; 31102026 Echo has no reading before).
  202 Echo's lowest, 19.0, is its first reading after the crossing, carried over from before it; it only rises
  from there.
- So in e40c5 every minute in the Nether was spent unhurtable: about 483 game minutes over the three bodies.

## 3. The corpus

None of its scenes. The replay places a body with `refreshPositionAndAngles`, and teleports only a scene recorded in
another dimension. All 469 scenes with a recorded dimension are in the Overworld. So no corpus pass rests on this.

## 4. The reported exit criteria

- **Iterations 27 to 30:** unchanged. No crossing, so no body in the state.
- **Rung c (e40c4 and e40c5; e40c3 was not read here):** their Nether figures were not earned.
  - Nether survival and "no death in the Nether" read true by construction. e40c5 shows the bodies unhurtable, and
    e40c4 is consistent with it.
  - "A safe return" read 0 by construction: no body could go back through.
  - Its Overworld figures before a crossing are unaffected.
- The rung c probe has to be re-run with F-25b5 and F-25c6e in. e40c5's notes already say so.

## The read (main `d71b4dc6`)

`NativeKnockbackLavaGameTests` (new), STAGED as MODES's case:
- a tunnel one wide and two high, east to west, with an alcove south;
- the player at the meeting, five health, an iron sword and bread;
- a zombie in the alcove a little east of south;
- a lava source in the tunnel's floor four blocks west;
- night, NORMAL difficulty.

It asserts never in the lava and never killed over 600 ticks. **On main it fails 3 of 5**: two "tried to swim in lava
to escape Zombie", one "slain by Zombie". Each trail: "Back off from zombie" (two partial walks to x −3, by the lava,
the third refused), "heal before going on", "Fight zombie". Then the hazard spell: "saw lava under it (0, -1, 0);
feet air, floor lava; … east free, west free". Then `native_fight` was cancelled for the hazard three times, and it
died.

**What was weighed.** A temporary line, logging each decision's candidates during a hazard spell (not kept), printed
in both runs:

```
[native_retreat:URGENT:99.0, native_fight:URGENT:99.0] -> PROPOSE_TASK native_fight
[native_lava_escape:URGENT:99.0, native_fight:URGENT:99.0] -> PROPOSE_TASK native_fight
[native_lava_escape:URGENT:99.0, native_fight:URGENT:99.0] -> PROPOSE_TASK native_fight
```

So the hazard retreat was offered, and lost each time to the goal's urgent fight, level with it at URGENT 99. The
interrupt then cancelled the fight, and the next decision took it again. That is F-25c4p's clutch shape.

**But that alone does not save it.** With only the forced choice (C, below), the tunnel survived **2 of 10**:
- the retreat now ran;
- but the zombie's blow had already taken the player to 2.0 health and over the lava cell;
- it fell in within the same second, "doing native_retreat".

The cause that decides it is upstream: the back-off's partial walk ended within the blow's reach of the lava.

## The change

1. **(A) A back-off ends clear of edges** (where the lead and MODES put it: the partial walk's end, chosen by
   `NativeGoToSkill.plan`):
   - The goto's existing `safe` option (`safely()`, E-49) ends a walk, finished or partial, only on a
     `NativeNavigator.safeStand` node.
   - A new goto argument `edge_clear: <reach>` turns that on and widens it. The stand must also pass
     `Navigator.clearOfEdges(feet, reach)` (new, companion-core): no lava, and no drop deeper than `MAX_SAFE_DROP`,
     within the reach across its level.
   - It reads the same terrain the route planner reads. Walls are not counted as stopping a blow, so it errs toward
     refusing a stand.
   - `safeStand` alone looks only at the four cells beside a stand, and a zombie's blow from two cells out still lands
     over the lava.
   - `PortalRunCampaign.backOff` passes `edge_clear: 1.6` on both its gotos, the way-it-came trail and the straight
     walk. The creeper run passes the same.
   - 1.6 is how far `NativeFightAction.knockback` counts an ordinary mob's blow carrying a player. A heavier hitter's
     (`knockback` counts 3.6) is the repository's own estimate, not sourced from the pinned version, so every hunter
     gets 1.6 here and the heavier ones are left for later (the lead).
   - Marker: "<name> walk toward … kept clear of edges: N stand(s) within 1.6 of lava or a deep drop passed over
     (F-25c6h)", once per plan.
2. **(C) The hazard's way out is taken while the hazard holds.** In `decide`, as the clutch is forced first: while
   `isHazardousSituation` holds, a `native_retreat` or `native_lava_escape` among the essential candidates is the only
   candidate. Those are exactly the two the hazard interrupt lets run; nothing else changes rank.
   - Marker: "<name> takes native_retreat|native_lava_escape over N other candidate(s), the hazard still on
     (F-25c6h)", five seconds apart at most.

Not in this slice (the lead): a sidestep fallback in the fight for a one-wide tunnel; the hoglin trench toss (open,
unreproduced); the heavier hitters' reach.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player below ground at night on the cast route, whose water is up at the surface (so it waits for daylight, F-25c4k), first fills a bucket of lava for the cast, as players make the deep trip at night. It does so only with both its buckets empty, so one is left for the water, and only at lava it knows of below ground within 64 blocks. It does not dig into the dark for lava it has not seen, nor walk across the surface to lava up there. Then it waits for daylight as before.

Found in the live runs on `ace1d80` (seed 8675309, strategist off): Sol sealed itself in at Y=54 at nightfall and waited out the night for its water with both buckets empty, 17% of its time. In the morning it fetched the water, then dug down for the lava by day.

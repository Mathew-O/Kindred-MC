## The cause, measured

A logging-only diagnostic named, for every pick-up walk that ended with the drop it went for still lying, the walk's
end and the nearest player's place. It ran on `NativeCompoundGameTests`, `NativeBaseGameTests` and
`NativeTradingHallGameTests` ×3, 13 of 13 each time, and is gone from the PR (the marker replaces it).

- **Completed walks with their target still lying out of reach:** 12, every one 1.8 to 4.2 blocks short. All were out
  of reach, with the drop ready to be picked up. For example: "Pick up white wool ended (completed) with the drop it
  went for still lying, … walked to 1 of 6; the nearest player Mason …, 2.65 away, out of reach, the drop to be picked
  up".
- **One was a block's drop,** "Build oak cabin: Pick up cobblestone ended (completed) with the drop it went for still
  lying, … walked to 1 of 1". Its one walk was spent and it was not gone for again.

`BuildRunner.pickUp` walks to a drop's block with `go(drop.getBlockPos(), 0.6)`, and `finished` counted every walk
against the drop it was aimed at (`woolFor`). Wool has six walks, so a short end only costs one of them. A block's
drop has one, so a short end left it lying for good, and its build a block short. In both CI failures, "picked up 1"
comes before the walk's "native_goto COMPLETED [Pick up oak planks]": the walk took another drop on the way and ended
short of its own.

## The fix

`BuildRunner.finished`, for a block's drop only (one walk; wool keeps its six as they were):
- **Not counted:** a completed walk that ends with its drop still lying out of the player's reach (the pick-up box,
  the player's box grown by 1, 0.5, 1). This holds up to `SHORT_ENDS` (3) times a drop. After that such walks count
  again, so a drop that can't be reached is still let go.
- **Counted as before:** a failed walk, a walk whose drop is gone, and a walk ending within reach.
- **Unchanged:** what a player may pick up and what it walks to. `pickUp` records whose walk it is (`woolBy`) and its
  allowance (`woolTimes`) for that.
- **The marker,** each walk not counted where main would count it: "Build …: Pick up … ended N short of the drop it
  went for, out of reach; not counted against it (n of 3) (F-22c112)".

## The staged fixture

`NativeBuildNeedsGameTests.aWalkThatEndsShortOfADropDoesNotSpendIt` is STAGED:
- **The setup:** a two-plank wall, with no plank held, no storage and no trees, so the planks can only be two drops
  lying in sight. The build starts once drop A has landed.
- **The staging:** as the walk for A sets out, drop B is laid at the player's feet and taken on the way, and A moves
  four blocks on.

| Side | Result |

| Main's `BuildRunner`, ×3 | **fails 3 of 3**: "picked up 1" (B), "native_goto COMPLETED [Pick up oak planks]", then "out for the rest of the build, trip 1: {minecraft:oak_planks=1}", logs it can't find, and A left lying |
| The fix, ×3 | **passes 3 of 3**, with the marker "Pick up oak planks ended 4.47 short of the drop it went for, out of reach; not counted against it (1 of 3)" |

The first staging went another way. A was laid at floor height and wasn't yet on the ground when the build started,
so `pickUp` chose its one-second wait for a drop still landing. That wait was counted as A's walk while B was taken,
the same class of bug by another arm, and the fix covers it too. The fixture now starts the build once A has landed,
so it stages the walk.

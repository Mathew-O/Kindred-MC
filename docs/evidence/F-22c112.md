## The two explanations left, and the diagnostic for them

1. **A drop counted as walked to without being collected.** `BuildRunner.pickUp` lets a block's drop be walked to once
   (`woolWalks < times`, `times` 1). `finished` counts the walk against the drop it was aimed at (`woolFor`), whichever
   drop was collected. In both CI logs, "picked up 1" comes before the walk's "native_goto COMPLETED [Pick up oak
   planks]". If that walk was aimed at one drop and took the other, the one aimed at is excluded for good while still
   lying.
2. **A dug drop removed without any pick-up:** merged, or discarded.

Added, logging only (`BuildRunner`):
- **When a pick-up walk ends with its drop still lying:** "Build …: Pick up … ended (…) with the drop it went for still
  lying, N at x, y, z, walked to w of t".
- **The first time a build is short of an item and finds no drop to go for:** "Build …: short N …, no drop of it to
  pick up; within 24 blocks: …". It gives each drop of that item within 24 blocks: its count, place and distance,
  alive or removed, in sight or not, and its walks. Once per item per build.

The next failure's lines tell which. Then a fixture failing on main, the fix, and the same fixture passing.

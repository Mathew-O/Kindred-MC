# F-22c100 — The stranded staircase in a batch of its own

Date: 2026-11-16. Test-only. No product code.

## The failure

`NativeStrandedReturnGameTests.goalWalksBackUpTheWayItCameWhenStrandedByWalksElsewhere` failed on #385's CI. That was
attempt 1 of run 43799948268 (job 133077500456, head `cd7d2cd`), with "Down the staircase, on to the ore: Wood: Gather
logs for a crafting table - Explore south for logs". The test ran at 04:53:40–50.

**Not a neighbour's ground.** Its ground spans 5886931..943, −59..−34, −736226..−222.
- No kept box in the "Fixture placement" lines from 04:52:30 to 04:54:10 overlaps it.
- No neighbour in that window left ground outside its plot.
- The only audit warnings are its own: "kept none", which #387 has since fixed.

**What the goal did.** Its start is eight up on the rock (y −50). In a full suite's default batch, logs in two
neighbours' own plots were in sight from there:
- (5886920, −58, −736209), about 15 blocks off, in
  `NativeAttentionGameTests.sharedBlocksCanBudgetCapsAggregateChecksAcrossPlayers`'s plot;
- (5886974, −56, −736223), about 41 blocks off, in
  `NativeGatherTargetGameTests.theCellGatheredFirstIsTheSameForEveryKnowledgeAndInventory`'s plot.

The goal went for them.
1. "Iron: Gather a log for spare sticks - Walk to logs at 5886920, -58, -736209", then the same for 5886974.
2. One "Back to where it saw iron ore at 5886942, -58, -736224".
3. "Wood: Gather logs for a crafting table - Climb toward the surface for logs". It broke the top of its wall at
   (5886933, −49, −736226), stepped up, then "no way on from here - Jump down from the ledge".
4. "Explore south for logs" and "Cut through south for logs" until it timed out. It never went down the staircase.

#380's CI saw a neighbour's log the same way ("Walk to logs at 108432, -58, -13736624"), but turned to the ore and
passed. Alone, the class goes "Walk to iron ore", then "Mine iron ore", then "Wood: Pick up raw iron".

## The fix

`batchId = "strandedReturn"`: the test runs in a batch of its own, so no other test's plot is placed beside it. The
scene is unchanged: nothing walled off, no sticks given. Only other tests' ground is taken out of view. The javadoc
says why.

**Not this fix:** whether the goal should take wood before ore that is in sight. That is goal code.

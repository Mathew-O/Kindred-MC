## Scope and implementation

**The fishing** (`NativeFishSkill`, new, `native_fish`; `NativePlayerBody.useRod`, the `FISH` intent). As a player fishes: the rod in a hotbar slot, a use click looking at the water's surface casts it; the bobber watched; the moment a fish is on (the bobber's dip, vanilla's hook countdown, read through `FishingBobberAccessor`), a use click reels in and the catch flies to it as any drop. A cast that lands off the water (the water neither in the bobber's block nor under it, as the game reckons it) or hooks an entity is reeled back and cast again, three casts at most; nothing biting in its time (60 seconds asked, 5 to 120 allowed), it reels in and says nothing bit. Stopped with its line out, it reels in. The rod wears as vanilla wears it.

**When it fishes** (`PortalRunCampaign.fish`). With still open water within twelve blocks: its rod to hand, from the main rows into a free hotbar slot; or, with no rod, one made of two string and three sticks it holds, at a table close by or one it puts down; to within six blocks of the water, and a cast. On its run, out of food with no animal in sight it fishes (the goal's hunt), and when told to look for food it fishes before it walks out to look for animals; stocking up with food in hand it only hunts. In its life-first needs (F-25c9), with no animal in sight it fishes before it looks further. While it is to make a rod to fish for food, it does not pick the table it put down for it back up.

## Scope and implementation

The fixture: `NativePortalCampaignGameTests.goalClimbsOutOfAPitBelowItsWayBackWithNoPickaxe`. It failed on CI three
times on 8 November, on the runs of #41, #63 and #64, and #71 (run 42533877582): "Out of the pit and back up under open
sky", with the player some 90 blocks off, exploring for logs at the test world's floor.

Root cause, in the fixture:
- It means to test a player at the foot of a pit two deep, whose way back up the stair it came down is a two-block
  step. Its pickaxe breaks on the iron ore over the pit, and the drop falls into the pit. It expects the player to
  follow the drop down, then climb back out the way it came.
- The player no longer falls in. In #71's run it mined the ore from three blocks up the stair, then stood in the ore's
  own cell at the stair's level and took the drop from there. Something it set down over the pit held it there,
  most likely its cobblestone, as a walk to a drop bridges a gap. It never stood in the pit. In a local run that passed,
  it also took the drop from the stair's foot.
- The check for the pit asked only for a height under the plot's base minus one. It was met on the test world's floor,
  two below the plot's base, after the player jumped off the walled plot's top looking for logs. The next check, back up
  under open sky, could then never be met on the floor, and the fixture timed out.
- It passed only when the player's search for logs took it back down the stair and into the pit, where it pillared up
  and out.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` gametest `NativePortalCampaignGameTests` (this fixture only).
- Once the ore is mined and the pickaxe has broken, the fixture clears the pit's two cells and sets the player down at
  the pit's foot. That is where the fixture's players fell, with their walk down the stair behind them.
- The check for the pit is now the pit's own foot cell, on the ground. The check for the climb out is unchanged: six
  above the plot's base or more, under open sky.
- No product code changes. The behaviour under test is the one the fixture was written for: no walk back that ends where
  it started, and the climb out.

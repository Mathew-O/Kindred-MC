## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a native AI player crosses open water in its own boat, as players do.
- It puts the boat on the water and gets in, wading out first where the shore leaves no room for a boat.
- It rows with the inputs a player's client sets (forward, and left or right to turn) at a boat's ordinary speed, about eight blocks a second.
- At a shore it gets out, facing dry ground, breaks the boat with ordinary hits and picks it up again.
- A drowned does not stop it rowing; a boat outruns one.
- It gets back into an empty boat close by: one left on the water by a restart, or by a landing that went wrong.

The End goal uses it on its long walks (to the stronghold, to the side for the second throw, to dry land): wherever most of the next 30 blocks toward the place are open water and it carries a boat. Afloat, nothing on land is done from the boat (no night shelter dug, no fight chased). At the stronghold's spot at sea:
- it pins the spot with an eye thrown from the boat;
- it rows to the nearest land it can see to dig down from, and gets out there.
The End plan makes a boat from carried wood (five planks, or a log and a plank) while it prepares, but does not wait for one.

Changed modules/files:
- `BoatAuthorityMixin`, `BoatPaddleMixin` (new):
  - vanilla moves a player's boat only on that player's own client, which a native player does not have. With a native player at the paddles, the server moves the boat.
  - the paddles answer the native player's inputs at the point in the boat's tick where a client's would.
- `NativePlayerBody`: `launchBoat` (looks at the water and uses the item, as a player does), `board`, `row`, `leaveBoat` (sneaks, facing dry ground: vanilla sets a rider down on the side it faces) and `strikeBoat`; intent `BOAT`. Paddle inputs are cleared on every halt.
- `NativeBoatAction` (new, recorded): `launch` spends exactly one boat under the ledger; `board`; `land` gets out and breaks the boat, losing nothing owned, and reports where the boat's item dropped. Reach is vanilla's own reach to the entity's box.
- `NativeRowSkill` (new): rows toward a point; ends on arriving, on reaching a shore (land under the bow, or aground), or when its time is up.
- `NativePlayerSkills`, `PlayerRuntime`: `native_boat`, `native_row`, their intent gates, `nativeBoat` and `nativeRow` for fixtures, and the results passed to the End goal.
- `StrongholdLeg`: `byBoat`, `afloat`, `nearestLand` (the nearest dry, solid column out to 160 blocks with no water within two blocks: a place to dig down from), and the boat picked up after landing.
- `PortalRunCampaign`: the afloat rule; boat steps count as getting out of the water for the drowning guard; rows are not stopped for hostiles; boat recipes from carried planks.
- `StrongholdRunPlanner`, `StrongholdRunSnapshot` (core): boats, planks and logs; a boat in the preparation.
- `NativeBoatGameTests` (new, batch `boatCrossing`).

Dependencies satisfied: F-10c/F-10d (walking to and from the water), F-25c6 (the End goal that uses it).
Schema/configuration changes: none. The two mixins are added to `kindred.mixins.json`.

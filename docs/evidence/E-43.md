## Scope and implementation

The owner's direction (2026-10-25): what a player keeps for later it stores and adds to its extended inventory count; players in co-op or a team tell each other when they use what is in shared storage, so each always knows what it has; and when something it knows is in a chest is gone ("maybe it's stolen by another player"), how it responds, differently by mode.

**What it has** (`Storage`, core, pure, from `7a2738c`). Each store it keeps things in, what it last counted there and when, and whose it is; with what it holds, its extended inventory. What it puts in and takes out, and what a teammate tells it, change the counts as they happen; a look inside sets them to what it sees, and what is short that nobody accounted for is gone.

**Its stores, in play.** The storage its administrator assigned it, and the chests of the bases it built (M-01c). A chest assigned to two players is their team's, shared; otherwise a store is its own. Nothing else is: it never uses anyone else's chest as its own.

**What it puts away** (`Worth.stow`). What the value model says it keeps for later and has no use for as it goes, all of each: an enchanted book, a saddle, a name tag, rails; with no goal on, also what of its diamonds and gold it has no want of now. On a goal, only what no run uses: what it carries for the run stays with it, short of it or not. Never its kit, its food, blocks it builds and climbs with, nor what it keeps on it as it goes (a bucket, its bow and arrows, torches, pearls); nothing not worth a slot. It puts things away when a store of its own is within 24 blocks and it is in no match, by a recorded action (`NativeStoreAction`, `native_store`): the chest opened with a use click, every stack of what it puts away shift-clicked in, exactly conserved.

**What it fetches.** On a goal, what its run wants that it knows it has in its assigned storage within 48 blocks is taken out first (`native_take`), as many as the run wants or the store holds, before it sets out to find more.

**Its look.** Every time it opens a store of its own (to put things away, to take from it for a build or a run) it counts what it sees against what it knew. What is short that nobody accounted for is gone, and what it does about it is by how it plays (`Missing`, core):
- **Alone**, it reckons it gone and says so to those it plays for: "A saddle is gone from my chest at 12, 64, -30."
- **With a team** (a shared store, or other AI players in co-op), it asks its team first: "Did anyone take a name tag from our chest at ...?" A teammate who owns up in plain words ("me", "I took it") is taken at their word ("Thanks, Steve. I've counted them out."); with no word in thirty seconds it reckons it gone, blaming nobody: "Nobody took them, so a name tag is gone from our chest at ...".
- **In a match**, it reckons its store raided, says only what it saw, and with enough left worth moving, empties the store into its inventory and uses it no more. An opponent it sees at a store of its own makes it give up a walk and go to look (`sightingInterrupt`), so it learns what is gone as it happens: "I saw Hal take a saddle from my chest at .... I'm moving the rest."
- Whoever it saw open its store while it was away (in any mode, `noteChestUse`) is named, to its own team only; nobody it did not see is ever accused, and nobody is retaliated against.

**Telling its team.** What it puts in or takes out of a store its team shares, it says to its team ("I put an enchanted book, a name tag and a saddle in our chest at ...", `TeamTalk.stored`), and every teammate sharing that store counts it too, so each knows what the team has.

**What the strategist is told.** Its report (F-19b) now says what it has stored, as it last counted.

**Found by suite run 40 and fixed** (`d3cb788`). A bed was stowed, then walked back for at night in a loop: beds are kept on. A base's chests counted as stores while its cabin was unfinished, and the player walked in a loop to one it could not reach inside the half-built walls: only a complete base's chests count. A store, a kit naming or a walk to its bed that is blocked now waits a minute before it is tried again. In `93ce043`: a walk to look at a store where it saw someone, once blocked, cleared what it had seen, so it never looked (the match fixture failed once); a look, or the rest moved out, now waits ten seconds and tries again, what it saw kept for five minutes.

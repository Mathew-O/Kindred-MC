## Scope and implementation

- **One fight, one controller** (`Melee`, core, new). Against another player, in a duel both joined (M-00a, D7), the move each tick is chosen by a fixed utility over what the player sees. It never uses a model. The moves:
  - close in;
  - strike: at a full swing, or winding up its jump for a critical from half a swing, so the blow lands full on the way down;
  - strafe round the opponent;
  - step out of its reach: with no shield, whenever the opponent's swing is about to come and its own is not back, so the swing is spent on air (spacing), then in for its own;
  - raise the shield;
  - get away when the fight is being lost (low, against a healthier opponent);
  - eat, well out of reach and hurt.
  A move is kept five ticks unless another is better by a margin of 0.1. A strike and a getaway are taken at once. A jump for a critical, once made, ends in its swing on the way down: the jump itself lifts the player a little out of the reach the melee reckons by, and the melee would otherwise raise the shield in mid-air and never swing.
- **The shield in time.** A shield guards only after five ticks up. So the player reckons the opponent ready to swing five ticks before its swing recovers (from when it was last seen to swing and the weapon in its hand), and raises the shield then. With its own swing full, it takes the opponent's swing on the shield first and strikes while the opponent recovers. An opponent behind its own shield is as much a threat: a shield comes down and a swing is made in one tick. It waits behind the shield at most ten ticks for a swing that does not come.
- **The declared baseline** (the same body and fight with the melee off). It closes in and strikes whenever its swing is full, jumping for criticals.
- **Declared opponent styles for the benchmarks** (`NativeFightAction.styled`):
  - idle: stands;
  - turtle: shield up but for a full swing;
  - spammer: swings whenever in reach, however little recovered;
  - evade: only runs.
- **In a duel, a hard hit is the fight going on** (`FightOdds.answerInDuel`). A hit of more than three hearts (E-41g) is judged in a duel by the kit on each side, both at full health, not by the health it already took nor by the hardest hit it was dealt (one critical landed by an equal is how a fight between equals goes). Only a player outclassed by kit gets away, and only when it can walk away: in a walled arena it fights on. What it says is the odds it judged by.
- **No jump at a raised shield.** A blow on a raised shield only disables it (with an axe), so the player strikes it at once rather than jumping for a critical.
- **Worn out in a fight.** A shield, armour or a weapon that breaks in the fight is wear, as many as the game counts broken (the player's own statistic), not a loss to reconcile.
- **Beliefs after a death.** A death is told to everyone, and a player comes back at full health. So what any player believed of the dead player's health is forgotten (`PlayerRuntime.forgetHealth`).
- **Eating in a fight.** The runtime's gate let the melee choose to eat but refused the bite itself. It now allows the eat intent in a fight. Each bite the fight begins may take one of that food, whenever the game finishes it.
- **No stale action after a stop.** A fight stopped half way strikes nothing more, and nothing of it moves the body.

What the runs found, each since fixed in the code or in the trial (m1 first):
- **Eating** was refused every tick by the runtime's gate.
- **Getting away** worked, but natural regeneration lifted the player above the low mark and it turned back. The trial is now too hungry to heal.
- **The step back** trial had the opponent better kitted (a shield the player lacked), so a hard hit sent the player away, by rule. It now fights in equal kit.
- **Strafing** was tried against an opponent that stands. An opponent that never swings is always ready, so the shield or the step back is chosen, never the strafe. It is now tried against one that swings as fast as it can.
- **Three strikes in twenty never came** (m2 to m4). A trace of the misses showed the jump for a critical lifting the player a little out of the reach the melee reckons by, so in mid-air it chose the shield instead and never swung. A jump for a critical now ends in its swing.
- **The step back never showed** (m2 to m5). In an even trade its own jump in begins a tick before the opponent counts as ready, and the strike under way is kept. The trial now puts the move's own situation to it: no shield, its swing just spent, in reach of an opponent ready to swing.
- **The getaway ran into the arena's wall** (m2 to m9): straight away was the wall, and, along it, the two ways round took turns each tick. The flee now runs along a wall rather than into it, the least turn first and, of two as small, the side it turned to a moment ago. Keeping the very turn it took (m10) was worse: a wide turn kept takes it round toward the opponent.
- **A getaway with no room for one** (ga1): starts in a corner, with walls on every side away from the opponent, fail on the room, not the move. The trial now starts with six blocks of floor straight away from the opponent.

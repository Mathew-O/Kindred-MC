## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: each AI player has an expression preset, set by whoever may command it:

- `plain`: says things plainly, without jokes;
- `dry`: short and understated ("Rain. Lovely."), without jokes;
- `playful`: a light joke or a callback when it fits, and a little teasing of other AI players. A person is never teased.

The operator sets the most expressive any player may be (`KINDRED_EXPRESSION_CAP`, default `playful`). Every preset is
clean. A player with no preset keeps the rule from before presets: playful from humour 65 up, plain below it. So Sol
(humour 70) is playful and Kindred (60) and Echo (50) plain until someone sets them. Playful has more lines than before:
a hello, nightfall and the tease of another AI player are new, beside the rain, the passing word and the work-done ones
it had.

```text
/aiplayer expression [<player>]                         how it sounds, and why
/aiplayer expression plain|dry|playful|auto [<player>]  set it; auto goes back to sounding by its humour
```

## Design changes and what was not built

- The design named the command `talk`; `/aiplayer talk <message>` already exists, so a person's preferences are
  `/aiplayer prefs` and a player's preset is `/aiplayer expression`. `auto` clears a preset.
- The cap is read where the runtime is built (`TalkSettings.cap`), not in `KindredMod`; nothing else reads it. A value it
  does not know is taken as `plain`, the strictest, and logged.
- The voice's fixed instructions still describe the personality's humour (cached, and shared by every listener); the
  one line in the varying part is the one that says how to speak to this listener, and it wins where they differ.
- Not under presets yet: the peer conference whisper (`PeerProgressDialogue`, `[Nearby] X confers with Y`), which still
  picks its joke by humour and is sent to anyone within six blocks; it is rebuilt by E-51. `DuelPolicy`'s banter is not
  used by the adapter.
- Teasing is only the AI-to-AI hello's playful form; no other line teases.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: none on its own. This is the pure core of building, in `dev.kindred.core.construction`. It takes over from the world-design prototype; the review and the plan are in [the building plan](../building-plan.md).

- **`BlockSpec`**: a block in its exact state, `minecraft:oak_stairs[facing=north,half=bottom]`, parsed and printed in vanilla's form. Two states are the same block placed the same way when they differ only in properties the world derives (a stair's shape, a fence's connections, waterlogging, a furnace's light), so the shape a stair takes from its neighbours is not a wrong block. States turn with the blueprint: facing, axis, sign rotation, and the four sides.
- **`Blueprint`**: a bounded list of blocks at positions in its own frame (32,768 blocks, 96 a side at most), with cells to keep clear and its source, creator and license (D8). It can be turned a quarter at a time and placed at an origin.
- **`BuildSite`**: what the world holds round a build, cell by cell, as the adapter saw it on the server thread: air, something replaceable, solid (with whether its top is a floor), passable, dangerous (lava, fire), or protected (a container, another's). Unknown cells are never built against. A site with some cells changed shares the snapshot rather than copying it.
- **`Placement`**: how vanilla 1.21.1 decides a placed block's state, so the player can place it in the state wanted:
  - a stair faces the way the player looks, its half from the face and the height clicked;
  - a slab's half the same way;
  - a log's axis is the clicked face's;
  - a furnace, chest or pumpkin faces back at the player; a fence gate faces the way the player looks;
  - a wall torch or ladder hangs on the face clicked;
  - sand and gravel go only on a floor.
  
  Blocks with no rule yet (doors, beds, signs, redstone, plants) are reported, not guessed at.
- **`ConstructionPlanner.next`**: the next step from the world as it is, for a player whose feet are at a given cell, holding given items. The step is one of: place (with the spot to stand, the block and face to click, the point on the face, and the look's yaw and pitch), break, scaffold, done, or blocked with the reason. The rules:
  - What is in the way is broken first, but never what is protected.
  - It builds a layer at a time, the nearest block first, each against a face that is there now.
  - Its spots to stand are only those its feet can walk to: a step up of one block with head room, a drop of up to three, or down its own scaffold by digging out the block underfoot. It never stands where a block is still to go, and never where the next block would shut it in.
  - Each click must be within 4.5 blocks of the eyes, with a clear line of sight. A block whose state is the way it faces is placed from well inside that quarter (35° from its middle), so a body a little off its spot still faces the same way.
  - Where nothing is in reach, it puts up a scaffold column: the lowest one from whose top the block can be placed as wanted.
  - Scaffolding comes down at the end, the top first. Scaffolding it cannot reach is reported, never left behind as done.
  - Spots where the server refused a step can be passed in to be avoided.
- **`BillOfMaterials`**: the items still to place, less what is in place, against what is held; unsupported blocks listed apart.
- **`Generators.hut`**: Kindred's own blueprint, so there is no one else's work to credit. A hut 3 to 15 blocks a side with a plank floor, cobblestone walls on upright logs, a doorway, a wall torch, and a gabled roof of oak stairs. Each course of stairs rests on planks and the ridge slab sits beside the last stairs, so every block has a face to be placed against.

Found on the way, by simulating whole huts through the planner:
- **The first roof's ridge slab hung in the air.** It had no face beside or below it, so no player could place it. The roof was redesigned.
- **Scaffold columns were picked without checking what they reach.** A column could be in reach but on the wrong side for a stair's facing. Each column is now checked by planning the placement from its top.
- **The planner chose spots the player could not walk to.** One was the top of a leftover scaffold stub two blocks high, which the native fixture then failed to reach. Walking reachability was added.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

Not tested / known failures: no rule yet for doors, beds, trapdoors, signs, buttons, rails, redstone, plants, double slabs or chests joined in pairs. The planner treats a bottom stair or a slab as no floor to stand on, though a player can stand on one. Water and lava in a build are not modelled. A layer waits for the one below, so overhangs out of a wall are built only once the layer under them is done.

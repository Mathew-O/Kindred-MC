# E-07a — Autonomous health recovery, inventory food management, and environmental hazard retreat
Date: 2026-10-17 EDT; report 2026-10-17.
Codex / uncommitted main, no commits. Complete for this child; parent E-07 evaluation below.

Autonomous health recovery, inventory food management, and environmental hazard retreat advance autonomous AI players to durable physiological self-preservation and tactical safety in Minecraft:

1. **Health-Aware Urgency Scaling (`SurvivalPolicy.eatingNeed`):**
   - Eating urgency now dynamically accounts for both hunger deficits and health deficits.
   - When food level is below maximum (`foodLevel < 20`) or health is damaged (`health < maxHealth`), eating need is evaluated.
   - Normal hunger scales eating need from 20.0 to 55.0 (`Priority.LOW` to `Priority.NORMAL`).
   - Health deficit adds proportional urgency; when health drops to critical levels (`health <= SurvivalPolicy.LOW_HEALTH_THRESHOLD`, i.e., 6.0 HP / 3 hearts), need scales up to 95.0 (`Priority.URGENT`), prioritizing immediate eating over routine work and tool progression.

2. **Full 36-Slot Inventory Scanning and Hotbar Swapping:**
   - Candidate generator scans hotbar slots 0..8 for edible items; if no food is present in the hotbar, it automatically searches the player's 27 main inventory slots (indices 9..35).
   - When eligible food is found in slots 9..35, it automatically swaps the food stack into hotbar slot 0, strictly preserving stack counts and inventory items (100% item conservation).
   - Dispatches `native_eat` targeting the hotbar slot with calculated urgency.

3. **Environmental Hazard Detection (`SurvivalPolicy.isHazardousBlock`):**
   - Pure policy identifies dangerous environmental blocks: `minecraft:fire`, `minecraft:soul_fire`, `minecraft:lava`, `minecraft:magma_block`, `minecraft:cactus`, `minecraft:sweet_berry_bush`, and `minecraft:powder_snow`.
   - Runtime candidate generator checks whether the player's body is standing inside a hazardous block, standing on a hazardous support block, or burning (`player.isOnFire()`).
   - Generates an immediate top-priority evasion candidate (`need = 99.0`, `Priority.URGENT`), overriding all routine survival and progression tasks.

4. **Native Retreat Skill (`native_retreat`):**
   - Registered skill descriptor and body (`NativePlayerSkills.Retreat`): executes rapid sprint movement off hazardous ground.
   - Verifies target destination is non-hazardous, solid, and safe before settling.
   - Applies directional sprint movement and clears sprint flags upon reaching safe ground or pausing.
   - Dispatched either autonomously during hazard evasion or via operator administrative command (`nativeRetreat`).

5. **Synchronous Stop Cancellation and Item Conservation:**
   - Stopping revokes active retreat immediately, synchronously clearing the sprint flag and latching `entry.stopped`.
   - Stopped players cannot restart retreat or execute survival actions.
   - 100% physical item conservation: zero duplicate items, zero deleted items, and no direct health modification cheats.

# F-35j1 — A refused faction walk serves its wait; open water set aside counts toward the pause

Date: 2026-11-12
Agent / branch: Claude, MODES on MOVEMENT (`claude/movement-1112-seastreak`)
Build: this PR's working tree on main f740386
Status: in progress (the fixtures measured; the gate and seed 110 again remain)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: on a coast where most places its faction play chooses are water it saw, an AI player no longer
asks walk after walk to place after place in a second. A place whose walk was refused waits out its wait before it is
looked at again, as water or otherwise; a place set aside as open water counts toward the run of places set aside that
pauses every faction walk (F-35i), as a place set aside after its refusals does; and after such a set-aside the next
choice comes at the decision interval, not on the same tick. The pause rule itself is unchanged.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates` (the open-water check skipped while the place waits; no
  immediate re-decide after it), `FactionsPlayer.openWater` (from where it stands; counts toward the streak),
  `streakPause` (the pause rule, one method for both), test hooks `factionWalksEndedForTest`, `factionDecisionsForTest`.
- gametest: `NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests` (two fixtures added).
- docs: this file, `feature-spec.md`, `validation.md`.

## Why

RUNNER 2's F-35j-only pair (973cc12 against 0476ee5, `claude/corpus-data-f35j-1112` @ be0ab5e): on seed 110 faction
walks asked rose from 112 to 273, Echo's refusals from 14 to 189, 102 of them within two seconds of the last. At 05:54:05
in its console Echo, standing still, had walks to ten places refused in one second. `factionCandidates` looked at a move
as open water before it asked whether the move was waiting: a place refused a moment before was looked at again, its leg
end now read as water it saw, and it was set aside and another chosen at once (`decidedAt` cleared). No wait was served
and no place reached three refusals, so F-35i's pause, fed only by places set aside after their refusals, never came.

(Of Echo's 189, another 53 were F-35h's made way digging out through its own night shelter's seal and the goal sealing
it again: F-35h2, its own slice.)

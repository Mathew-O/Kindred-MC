## What this row claims
- **When it goes back:** woken at night just after a death, it walks back for its own kit before the night's shelter, when all of these hold:
  - the kit is armour or a pickaxe;
  - it lies within 24 blocks;
  - no hostile within 24 of it or of the kit is in its sight, or has the kit in sight;
  - four such walks fit in what is left of the kit's 6,000 ticks on the ground.
- **Otherwise** the night goes on as before.
- **It does not claim:**
  - the guarded-drops wait (707 Sol in e40a24, which waited for daylight, then walked away);
  - the give-up after three refused routes (e40a23's 101 Sol).

  Each of those is its own later slice.

## Why
- **The order of decisions:** in `PortalRunCampaign`'s step for the goal, "Nights on the surface belong to skeletons" (dig in, seal, wait for daylight) comes before `recoverDrops`. So at night the walk back is never reached.
- **The despawn clock:** a night is some 10,000 ticks and a drop lies 6,000. Woken at night, what it dropped is gone by morning.
- **The run:** in E-40 iteration 24 (`e40a24-83321ac`, #384), 8675309 Sol died at night and, woken, dug in at once.

## The change (`PortalRunCampaign`)
- **`kitBeforeNight(player, tick)`, asked just before the night's shelter:**
  - It takes the nearest piece of kit (`ArmorItem`, or `ItemTags.PICKAXES`) among the drops within 8 of `dropSite`. That is the spot the campaign marked at its own death, the same ground `recoverDrops` goes back to.
  - If the bounds hold, it offers "Recover its kit before the night near …".
  - The label starts "Recover", so a walk that ends short passes that drop over, as `recoverDrops`'s walks do (F-25c4s), three times at most.
- **The bounds are constants:**
  - `KIT_WALK_MOST` 24;
  - `KIT_HOSTILE_SIGHT` 24;
  - `DROP_LIFE` 6,000;
  - `KIT_TICKS_A_BLOCK` 5 (a walk is some 4.3 blocks a second);
  - `KIT_WALKS_LEFT` 4.
- **Nothing is given or moved by the code:** the walk is its own, and the pick-up is vanilla's.

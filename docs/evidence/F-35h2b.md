## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (traced by CI GREEN, on main). In a faction world at nightfall, not yet dug in, `factionCandidates` returned a non-urgent walk before the goal's campaign was asked: a scout of an exposed border chunk on Blue's land. The player walked onto Blue's land. There `mayWork` (`TerritoryGuard.mayChange`) refused every dig, and it waited out the night unsheltered. The existing night guard was only `dugInForTheNight`, which covers a player already sealed in.

- **Rates for the record:** main 0 of 10 twice locally; #254's head 1 of 11; CI failures on #254 twice and on the docs-only #261.

**The change.** In `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates`, after the dug-in check:
- **The rule:** at night, with a goal running and not yet dug in for the night, a faction walk other than DEFEND yields to the campaign (`nightYield`).
- **Never zero moves** (MODES's condition 1):
  - The night's first decision yields once, so that the campaign is asked.
  - After that it yields only while the campaign's step was a dig-in for the night (its progress reads `PortalRunCampaign.SHELTER_FOR_THE_NIGHT`, the words its dig and seal steps already used).
  - Where it could not dig, the walk goes on at the next decision, so a yield costs at most one decision.
- **A part-dug shelter keeps yielding** (condition 2). `PortalRunCampaign.wouldShelterForTheNight` is the night and not `dugInForTheNight`; being sheltered does not end it.
- **The walk is not cancelled.** It is kept, and asked again by day or once the dig-in is over (`FactionsPlayer.nightYieldAt` is reset by day).
- **Outside a faction world** `factionCandidates` returns at once (`!factionsWorld()`), as before.

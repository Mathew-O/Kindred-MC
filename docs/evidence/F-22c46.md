## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a builder standing up its own scaffold column, too high above the ground to drop, whose plan comes
back blocked, comes down its column first. It digs out the block underfoot one at a time, as a player gets off a pillar, and
plans again from the ground. Before, it asked for walks off from up there, which the navigator cannot take. Eight in a row
were refused and the build stopped.

Found: the cabin "No way to reach …_stairs" family, red on unrelated PRs today: #113's run 42740093751, #117's runs, #119's
run 42752284606 (the cherry cabin) and #123's run 42758107947 (the life-first cabin). From #123's log:
- The builder climbed its scaffold, placed a stair, and started down its own column ("Down the scaffold", three breaks).
- With four blocks of it still underfoot, at y −49 over ground at −53, the plan came back BLOCKED: "No way to reach
  minecraft:oak_stairs".
- `BuildRunner` comes down its own scaffold first (`down`) only for a PLACE, SUPPORT, SCAFFOLD or BREAK step. For a BLOCKED
  plan it asked for walks over to the ground, a drop of four past the walk's three. Eight "No walkable route" in a row, and
  the build stopped.
- The same holds when the blocked plan is "Nothing in hand to place": the supply's fetch then asks for a walk off its site
  for what it lacks, from up the column. The fixture below stages that form.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `BuildRunner`: a BLOCKED plan takes the column down first, before the fetch and before any walk.
  This applies only when all of these hold:
  - the player is on the ground;
  - the block underfoot is its own tracked scaffold, and solid;
  - the ground under the column is more than `ConstructionPlanner.MAX_DROP` (three) below its feet (`highOnItsScaffold`).

  The break is the existing "Down the scaffold", collected, underfoot. Nothing else changes: a blocked plan on the ground, on
  a column low enough to drop from, or on anything not its own scaffold goes on as before. Never into liquid, and never a
  break from where it could not walk back, both hold as before, since the planner is unchanged.
- `companion-core` `ConstructionPlanner`: `MAX_DROP` is made public, for the adapter's check.

Nothing is granted.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player that dies again before it has its things back from the last death leaves what it drops the second time too. That covers a death on the way back for them and a death by them. Its next life goes on with its goal and does not walk back into the place that has now killed it twice. Before, each new life walked straight back for the newest drops, unarmed and with no armour, and died there again.

Found in the live pair on `458d192` (seed 8675309, strategist off):
- Kindred died at Y=-4 to a zombie at tick 194934. On its way back for its things it died in a cave at 14, 40, 60, to a zombie.
- It then died seven more times in and around that cave in 7,600 ticks: to skeletons, drowned and a zombie, and once it drowned. Each new life set out at once for what the last one dropped ("Recover dropped items near …").
- Echo died to drowned in the lake beside its pocket (F-25c4w), went back for its things and died to drowned there again.
- In that seed, 11 of 21 deaths came while a walk back for the last drops was still under way. The pair on `03faab9` had 2 of 10 such deaths on the same seed, and the earlier pairs none.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign.died`: a death while the last death's drops are still to be gone back for (the walk back under way, or its look round after it got there) gives up the new drops. The event says so.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- The first walk back is unchanged. After a mob kill it waits only while a hostile mob has the drops in sight, and an unarmed player can still die on that first walk. Arming itself first would cost the five minutes the drops last.
- A death long after the last drops were picked up or gave out starts afresh: it goes back for those drops as usual.

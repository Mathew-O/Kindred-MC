## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
- `/companion deposit <item> <1..64> [UUID]` deposits items into assigned storage.
- Checkpointed progress tracks verified transfers (`deposited`, `requested`, `carrying`); resuming after pause/restart continues toward remaining need without re-depositing initial counts.
- Exact item conservation guarantees that any items the container cannot accept (e.g. partial container capacity) are automatically rolled back into the companion's inventory.
- Full or broken/replaced containers trigger honest blockers without item loss.
- Fenced resource claims (`ClaimRegistry`) prevent simultaneous deposits into contested containers.

- adapter `ChoreSkills.Deposit`: enhanced with checkpoint resumption, `alreadyDeposited` tracking, remaining need calculation, partial transfer progress persistence, and full container honest blocker.

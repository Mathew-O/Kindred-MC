## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/aiplayer native goto <x y z> [bot]`, and the Nether-portal goal, walk a pathfound route using only ordinary movement inputs (forward, yaw, jump). Nothing teleports or repositions the body.

Changed modules/files: `NativeNavigator` (bounded A* over standing positions: walk, diagonals without corner cutting, one-block step-up, drops of up to 3 blocks or 12 into water, swimming; lava, fire, cactus, berry bushes, cobwebs, powder snow, magma and dripstone are never entered, and cells beside lava cost extra), `NativeGoToSkill` (plans on the first tick, steers by waypoint, replans up to four times when stuck, optional partial routes, a range goal), `NativePlayerSkills` (`native_goto` descriptor: 60 s timeout, 10 s without progress), `PlayerRuntime` (`nativeGoto`, MOVE intent mapping), `PlayerCommands`.
Dependencies satisfied: F-10a/F-10b paid movement, F-04 runner, FND-02 arbiter.
Schema/configuration changes: none.

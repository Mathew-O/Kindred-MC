## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an optional, read-only web page. An operator sets `KINDRED_WATCH_PORT` and
`KINDRED_WATCH_TOKEN`, and opens `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/?token=<token>`. The page shows a card for each AI player:

- its health and food;
- its goal, how far along it is, and what it is doing;
- what it weighed at its last decision, with the scores;
- what it said last, and the lines before that;
- its habits and notes;
- a feed of highlights: deaths, stages, rescues, habits taken up, matches.

The page is laid out to be put on a stream with OBS. The same data is served as JSON, with the players also as
server-sent events, and an evaluation's result so far. Without the token a request gets 401. Anything but a read gets
405. Nothing on it changes anything. Without both settings, it does not start.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`:
  - `watch.WatchSnapshot` (new): the immutable, bounded snapshot and its JSON. A player has 8 candidates, 30 decision
    lines, 6 lines of talk and 10 notes at most; the snapshot keeps 50 highlights.
  - `watch/watch.html` (new): the page. It has inline CSS and JS, no CDN, and puts text in with `textContent` only.
  - `WatchSnapshotTest` (5).
  - `eval.Json`: made public for the snapshot.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `WatchServer` (new): the JDK's `com.sun.net.httpserver` on its own daemon threads, and the token. Every endpoint is
    a `GET`.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: the snapshot, taken at the end of its own tick when the page is on. It also keeps, while the page
    is on:
    - what each player weighed at its last decision;
    - its last thirty decision lines;
    - the highlights.
  - `RunObserver.resultSoFar`, and `EvalBootstrap`'s, for `/api/eval`.
  - `KindredMod`: starts the page from the environment with the server and closes it with the server.
- gametest: `NativeWatchGameTests` (3), a new entrypoint.
- No new dependency: `com.sun.net.httpserver` is the JDK's own `jdk.httpserver` module. The fixture's client is the
  JDK's `java.net.http`. The manifest is unchanged.

Schema/configuration changes: `KINDRED_WATCH_PORT`, `KINDRED_WATCH_TOKEN`, `KINDRED_WATCH_BIND`. Nothing is saved. With
the page off (the default), the runtime keeps nothing more and does nothing more.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- The page is plain HTTP. Across a network it needs TLS in front of it, as the operator guide says. The token can be in
  the page's address, so it can show in a browser's history.
- The page reads `/api/players` and `/api/events` once a second and does not use the stream. The stream is for other
  readers, four at a time, each for ten minutes.
- A player's head on its card is the first letter of its name, not its skin: the page loads nothing from elsewhere.
- The decision lines are kept from when the page is turned on. They are not in the world's saves.
- Updated docs: [operator guide](../operator-guide.md) ("The watch page (E-48)").

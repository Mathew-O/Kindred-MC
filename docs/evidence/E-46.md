## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player with no strategist, or past its budget, learns from its own deaths. Twice dead hungry,
it keeps three more food; three deaths below ground in a day, it digs its own way for ore instead of exploring caves; a
pickaxe broken below ground with none spare, it packs a spare; two deaths at night on the surface, it digs in at dusk.
Two habits only its own play teaches are new: torches as it goes, after a mob in its own tunnel kills it, and the
water bucket kept in the hotbar, after two fall deaths. A habit it learned so and has not needed for three game days is
dropped again. Each is a note in its memory with the habit, as the strategist's notes are, and the log says so.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`:
  - `survival.Lessons`: the rules, pure. Each names its trigger, its window, its habit, its note, and the note it is
    dropped with.
  - `survival.PlanPreferences`: two new habits, `TORCH_AS_YOU_GO` and `BUCKET_ON_HOTBAR`.
  - `eval.EvalScenario`: the `lessons` key, `on` by default.
  - `LessonsTest` (13), `EvalScenarioTest` and `EvalRecordTest` updated for the key.
- `companion-model`: `StrategistPrompt.HABIT` and the OpenAI-compatible prompt name the two new habits and tell the
  model that a habit marked "from its own play" came from its own deaths, not to be taught again, though it may be
  retired. The habit fields' schema comes from the enum, so it lists the new habits by itself.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `StrategistBridge`: a memory-only bridge (`memoryOnly`), the same memory with no model behind it, and the lessons
    review in its tick. Every death since its last look is recorded now, not only the newest: two deaths in a row,
    one soon after a respawn, were one before. The habits a goal holds change as the memory's do, and only so.
  - `ModelContextStore`: a death's lesson fields (the game time, night, under the sky, the Overworld, food, how, its
    own tunnel), episodes (a stranding), the lessons' habits with when, and those retired with when. The report marks
    a lesson's habit `from its own play`. All are new keys; older files read with none of them.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: the memory kept with or without a strategist; the death's facts passed to the goal; the switch
    (`lessons`); accessors for fixtures.
  - `PortalRunCampaign`:
    - the death's facts and whether its killer stood on its trail, below ground;
    - a stranding seen from vanilla's statistics of broken pickaxes;
    - `light` lights at block light 6 with the torch habit, about eight blocks from the last torch, rather than at 1;
    - the plan moves a water bucket into the hotbar with the bucket habit;
    - `deathsAfter`.
  - `KindredMod`: `KINDRED_LESSONS=off` turns the lessons off. `EvalBootstrap` applies the scenario's `lessons`.
- gametest: `NativeLessonsGameTests` (4), a new entrypoint.
- No new dependency; the manifest is unchanged.

Schema/configuration changes: new keys in the memory file (`model-context/<uuid>.json`): a death's `time`, `night`,
`sky`, `overworld`, `food`, `how`, `tunnel`; `episodes`; `lessons`; `retiredLessons`. A file from before reads with none
of them, and its old deaths count toward no rule. `KINDRED_LESSONS` and the scenario key `lessons`.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- The evaluation: ten seeds, lessons on against off, on the final build.
- "Its own tunnel" is read from the trail of cells it walked: a mob that killed it below ground while standing on that
  trail counts, whether it spawned there or walked in after it.
- The windows are game time, `World.getTime`, which a sprint advances and a `/time set` does not.
- At most four habits are kept, from lessons and the strategist together; the oldest goes first.
- Updated docs: [operator guide](../operator-guide.md) ("Lessons from its own play (E-46)", the scenario's `lessons`),
  `tools/eval/README.md`.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: with the strategist on, a player that is stuck, its own move elsewhere tried and failed, asks
the strategist for a way out, at most once for each stuck spell and five minutes apart. It sends what it can see round
it, what it carries, what it was doing and what it tried, about 800 tokens. The reply is a few steps from a fixed list:
walk, dig, pillar, place a block, wait, eat, back off. Each step has small bounded numbers. The game checks the
steps, then the player carries them out a step at a time as ordinary moves, each checked again as it is made. The
first step that does not go ends the plan. The log says `rescue: pillar 3, walk north 2 -> unstuck`, or `-> failed
<why>`, or `-> refused: <why>` when the reply was turned down with nothing moved. `KINDRED_RESCUE=off` turns it off.

## The vocabulary, as built

| Step | Bounds | Carried out by | From the design |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `walk <north\|south\|east\|west> <n>` | 1 to 8 | `native_goto` to the block that many away | `walk` also took up and down: those are a pillar or a dig |
| `dig <north\|south\|east\|west\|up\|down> <n>` | 1 to 4 | `native_break`, each block in the way: two high sideways, one up or down | as designed |
| `pillar <n>` | 1 to 6 | `native_pillar`, n times, with a block from the hotbar | as designed |
| `place <north\|south\|east\|west>` | | `native_place`, a block from the hotbar beside its feet | `place down` dropped: a player stands on what is below it |
| `wait <n>` | 1 to 4 seconds | `native_wait` | the design said 1 to 10; the installed skill waits at most 4 |
| `eat` | | `native_eat`, food from the hotbar | as designed |
| `retreat <north\|south\|east\|west> <n>` | 1 to 3 | `native_retreat` | the design's `retreat` had no way; the installed skill needs one |
| (`bridge`, `climb_out`) | | | dropped: `bridge` is a place and a step for each block, not one skill; `climb_out` is the goal's own climb |

## The compatible route's thinking (1 November)

The release jar does not carry the Anthropic SDK, so the rescue arm asked Anthropic's server through the compatible
route (`KINDRED_STRATEGIST_PROVIDER=openai-compatible`, its `/v1` base URL). Every rescue of its first two seeds came
back "no plan (stopped: length)". The model it named thinks unless told not to, and the thinking counts against the
reply's bound: a rescue's 300 tokens were spent before a word of the plan. The SDK route asks for no thinking on every
rescue, ask and chat line, and on advice unless `KINDRED_STRATEGIST_THINKING=on`; the compatible route asked for
nothing. Now every request it sends to Anthropic's server asks for no thinking. Other servers are not sent the field,
as some refuse one they do not know. The arm was stopped and run again on a jar with the change (below).

## ModelGateway's accounting retired

`ModelGateway`'s request admission, per-lane concurrency, rates, daily budgets and returned-plan checks are removed. They
duplicated `StrategistBridge`'s, which is what every request goes through, and nothing called them. The tests for them
went with them: `ModelGatewayTest` (8), `SupportBatch02ModelGatewayTest` (6) and `SupportBatch03GatewayRegressionTest`
(2), 16 in all. Their gate checks are covered in core by `SupportBatch04SkillProposalTest` and `RescuePlanTest`. Its
`Lane` (the chat and planning lanes' deadlines and reservations) stays, for `ReasoningPolicy`. F-19's row says so.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- The evaluation: ten seeds with the strategist on for rescues alone (`strategist=on; advice=off`), against the same
  seeds with it off, on the same build; stuck game minutes reported either way.
- A step is checked as every task is when it is given out, and the plan stops at the first that does not go. With
  edits switched off on the server the goal pauses whole, and a plan waits with it; a step given out that never ends
  ends the plan after a game minute.
- The design's `bridge` and `climb_out` are not in the vocabulary, and `wait` is 1 to 4 seconds (above).
- The fence's task revision is not compared: the goal works on while a plan is asked for, and every task it gives out
  moves the task revision on. The session and the intent revision are compared, and the player must still be where it
  asked from, in the same stuck spell.
- Updated docs: [operator guide](../operator-guide.md) ("Rescue plans (F-29)"), `tools/eval/README.md`.

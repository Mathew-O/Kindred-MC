## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: a push of a minecart (E-45e) ended as soon as the cart moved at 0.12 blocks a tick or more,
whichever way it moved. A bring's loaded cart that ran out of way short of its cell rolls back down its line toward the
player; pushed again, the push saw the cart moving, ended at once and counted, and the cart rolled on back past the line's
start. After eight such pushes the bring gave up with the librarian short of its cell.

- **CI on `5b130a6`** (run 42373115656), `NativeBringGameTests.aLibrarianOffTheLineIsBroughtHomeFromWhereItStands`:
  the first push sent the loaded cart to the climb into the cell at 0.26, where it ran out of way and rolled back. The
  next seven pushes "ended" at 0.14 to 0.20, the speed it was rolling back at, with the cart past the line's start each
  time; "the cart stopped at … rail 0 of 25, 8 pushes", and the bring gave up. Its second try ran out of the test's time.
  The same fixture failed so in CI on `35853b8` (run 42141909662, in its roll).
- **Locally** the fixture's second push met the cart rolling back, and it delivered; the push's flaw was not reached.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `NativePushSkill`. Rolling on is rolling away from the player: the push ends
only when the cart moves away from where the player stands. A cart rolling back toward it is waited for where it stands
(not walked away from as too far), met, and walked into until it rolls away.

## Scope and implementation

**Found:** `NativeBackOffFlipGameTests.hurtWithAZombieThatKeepsPaceAndASecondComingUpBehindItFightsBothAndLives` failed
4 times on CI from 11-16 on:
- #385 (run 43799948268) and #416 (run 44880507467);
- main, twice (runs 43829305872 and 44855662060).

Three of the four read the same: "Alive, never killed (switches 0: back off @5, second @64, gainless @99, gainless
@105)". The player was hurt to five, with a sword on its hotbar, and backed off from the zombie the whole time. Two of
its back-offs ended with the zombie still in reach. It never drew its sword, and the zombie killed it as it backed off
again. The fourth also died backing off, after one switch to holing up and back.

**The diagnosis:** a temporary log of each decision below six health was run in 8 copies of the test, 1 of which failed
as CI did. It showed, at the decision after a back-off ended with the zombie 2.2 blocks off:
- the gainless back-off seen;
- the retreat's hold brought down to that tick;
- yet the retreat still read as held, and "Back off from zombie" chosen again.

**Cause, in `PortalRunCampaign`:**
1. The goal step reads whether a retreat is held (`retreating`) before the check that a back-off gained nothing.
2. That check ends the held retreat, but only after `retreating` has been read. So for that decision the retreat still
   counts as held.
3. The fight that `defend` falls through to is then turned back into a back-off, by the rule that a held retreat turns a
   fight into a back-off.
4. That back-off holds the retreat again, so each later decision does the same.
5. The player backed off with the zombie at its back until it was killed.

**The change:** the gainless check is moved above the two reads of the held choices (`fighting`, `retreating`). Nothing
else changes. Once a back-off has gained nothing, the retreat is no longer held in the same decision, and the fight
`defend` chooses stands.

**Nothing granted.** No timeout, bound or check is loosened. The temporary log is not part of the change.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter`: `PortalRunCampaign`.
- Test: `NativeBackOffFlipGameTests`. `caughtAsItBacksOffAgainItTurnsAndFightsRatherThanBackOffOnce` is new (batch
  `backOffFlipCaught`).

## The fixture (STAGED)

`caughtAsItBacksOffAgainItTurnsAndFightsRatherThanBackOffOnce`, staged as the second-zombie test is:
- the player hurt to five, food in hand, an iron sword on the hotbar, a shield and leather armour on;
- one zombie after it, 2.3 blocks off.

The zombie is held still (its AI off) while the player backs off. Once the player is backing off again, more than 13
blocks from where it stood, the zombie is set 1.5 blocks behind it, toward where it came from, and let go. Its first
back-off ends within 10 blocks, so by then it has ended. The retreat is still held then, as in CI's runs.

The check: within three seconds, the player fights with its sword in hand. Only the zombie's place and its stillness
are staged.

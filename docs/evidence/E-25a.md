## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: the friendship an AI player keeps with each person and player (E-24a) now shapes how it is with
them, and only that:
- **Its words.** A friend gets a warmer hello ("Hi, Sol! Always good to see you.", "Hey, Echo! There's my favourite
  person." in a playful or rough manner), and a warmer answer to theirs. A friendly one gets a kind hello. A friend or a
  friendly one is called by name when it offers food or something it has spare ("Need food, Steve? I've got bread.").
- **Who comes first.** With several people near, it turns to its friends first. So where what it may say runs short (its
  attention budget, E-53), a friend hears it.
- **Small talk.** It starts no talk with a player it is cool with or wary of, and makes no small talk with a person it is
  cool with. Asked by one, it answers in a word ("Busy.", "Hm."), keeping what it is doing and needs to itself.
- **Sharing in talk.** It offers what it has spare to anyone but one it is wary of. A friend hears "I've got some iron
  ingot spare. It's yours if you want it."; with nothing spare, a friend hears "I'll keep an eye out for iron ingot for
  you." whatever its manner.
- **Outings.** Invited by a friend, an AI player goes at sociability 20 or more, by a friendly one at 30, by anyone else at
  40 as before. Invited by one it is cool with, it says no ("would rather not go with them"). Its own goal, work, danger and
  matches come first, whoever asks.
- **With a neutral one** (friendship 0 to 7), everything is as before.

Friendship never changes who may control the player, what they may do, any budget, a stop or a strike (AUTH-01, SOC-02).
Whether it warns someone of danger, or offers food to someone hungry, does not depend on it.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-core`:
  - `social.Warmth` (new): the bands as warmth, and what each changes.
  - `social.Conversation`: `Side.warmth`; the hello, the answers and the last word read it; no talk started by a side cool
    with the other.
  - `outing.OutingChoice`: `answer(…, Warmth toHost)`.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `PeerTalk`: each side's warmth; one cool with the other starts nothing, and the other, not cool, may.
  - `Outings`: an AI invitee's yes reads its warmth with the host.
  - `InitiativeLane`: friends first; a friend or friendly one called by name in offers; no small talk with one it is cool
    with.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: `friendshipOf`, the three lanes' `friendship`.
- JUnit: `social.WarmthTest` (new, 5 cases).
- gametest: `NativeConversationGameTests.twoPlayersCoolWithEachOtherMeetingAfterTenMinutesApartSayNothing` (new).

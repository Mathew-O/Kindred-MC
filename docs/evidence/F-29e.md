## Method

Four arms, all on `cd56501`, `goal=nether`, the three starters, nothing else on the cores. Two servers at a time, except `f29e-on-4h`, which ran three at a time (none under 20 ticks a second). The arms of a pair ran in different containers:

| Arm | Seeds | Game time | Scenario | Run by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `f29e-off` | `tools/eval/seeds-20.txt` (20) | 2 h (144,000 ticks) | `strategist=off` (rescue off) | this session's container |
| `f29e-on` | the same 20 | 2 h | `strategist=on; advice=off` (rescues alone, the compatible route to Anthropic's server) | the lead's container, which holds the key |
| `f29e-off-4h` | `tools/eval/seeds-10.txt` (10) | 4 h (288,000 ticks) | `strategist=off` | this session's container |
| `f29e-on-4h` | the same 10 | 4 h | `strategist=on; advice=off` | the lead's container |

`seeds-20.txt` is the ten evaluation seeds and ten rolled for this work with Python's `secrets.randbelow`, none of them
E-40's reserved gate seeds or named in the repository before. The two containers have the same shape (4 cores, 15 GB).
The arms of a pair run in different containers on the same build and seeds.

Reported, with medians and ranges across seeds: stuck minutes and spells, the time to the iron pickaxe and to a lit
portal, rescues asked (rounds), rounds in ended spells, spells out, recipes tried first and spells out by a recipe, and
tokens. A difference is worth noting only when the ranges do not overlap. Whatever the arms show is filed.

## Decision

At two hours and at four, the rescue gets players out of about half their spells, 49% and 42%, and one plan in four
that runs gets the player out. It costs 54,000 to 87,000 tokens a seed. It does not shorten stuck time, or get the team
further, by more than the spread between two containers running the same arm.

The lit portal is reached on fewer seeds with rescue on: 10 of 20 against 15 of 20 at two hours, and 5 of 10 against 8
and 7 of 10 at four. The ranges overlap, so the arms do not show that the rescue costs progress. They do not rule it out
either.

The clearest lever is the plans refused on arrival: 84 of 405 replies at two hours, 94 of 289 at four. In 38 of the 50
at two hours that the console could place, the player had been moved by its own early move while the ask was out. Of
those 84, 31 were "the stuck spell is over", the player's own try having got it out, and those count as outs here, not
as refusals. The F-29c child that holds the early move still for the ask, and takes a plan from the same cell and the
same kind of scene, is the next step. A second pair after it would show whether the rescue then moves the team.

## Method

Four arms, all on `cd56501`, `goal=nether`, the three starters, two servers at a time and nothing else on the cores:

| Arm | Seeds | Game time | Scenario | Run by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `f29e-off` | `tools/eval/seeds-20.txt` (20) | 2 h (144,000 ticks) | `strategist=off` (rescue off) | this session's container |
| `f29e-on` | the same 20 | 2 h | `strategist=on; advice=off` (rescues alone, the compatible route to Anthropic's server) | the lead's container, which holds the key |
| `f29e-off-4h` | `tools/eval/seeds-10.txt` (10) | 4 h (288,000 ticks) | `strategist=off` | this session's container |
| `f29e-on-4h` | the same 10 | 4 h | `strategist=on; advice=off` | the lead's container |

`seeds-20.txt` is the ten evaluation seeds and ten rolled for this work with Python's `secrets.randbelow`, none of them
E-40's reserved gate seeds or named in the repository before. The two containers have the same shape (4 cores, 15 GB).
The arms of a pair run in different containers on the same build and seeds.

Reported, with medians and ranges across seeds: stuck minutes and spells, the time to the iron pickaxe and to a lit
portal, rescues asked (rounds), rounds in ended spells, spells out, recipes tried first and spells out by a recipe, and
tokens. A difference is worth noting only when the ranges do not overlap. Whatever the arms show is filed.

Worth noting (ranges apart): only the rescue measures themselves (asks, plans, tokens). Stuck spells and minutes fall by
three quarters at the median, but the ranges overlap, and the off arm's spread between two containers (above) is as
large. The lit portal is reached on 10 of 20 seeds with rescue on, against 15 of 20 off; the ranges overlap. Read
together, the arms do not show that the rescue gets the team further; they show it costing about 54,000 tokens a seed
for a smaller, unproven, stuck time. A spell rescued inside its first three minutes never counts as a watchdog spell,
so stuck minutes measure only the spells that outlast three minutes, in both arms alike.

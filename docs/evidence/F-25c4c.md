## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: below ground at night, when the portal route's next step is logs from the surface (sticks for its next pickaxe, say), a player mines the iron the route still needs with the pickaxe it has, rather than waiting for morning. Below ground the night changes nothing; only the climb out to the trees waits for daylight. With no pickaxe that mines iron, or all the route's iron in hand, it waits as before.

Found in B7's paired runs: waiting below for daylight to go up for logs took up to a tenth of a player's time in the first pair (on `f7f206a`), and 19% of Echo's and 15% of Sol's in the first run of the second (on `4d0884a`, strategist off).

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunPlanner.withoutWood` (core): the iron still wanted, as a mining step, for a player with a pickaxe that mines it; empty otherwise.
- `PortalRunCampaign`: at night, below ground, when the route's next step is logs, that step is replaced by `withoutWood`'s when there is one. The wait that remains now names the step it waits for. `PortalRunCampaign` is changed on `modest-faraday` only in its hole-up lines.
- `PortalRunPlannerTest`, `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one case and one fixture.

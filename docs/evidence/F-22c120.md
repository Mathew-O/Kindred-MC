## Scope and implementation

In every failure the first step was the night's shelter, and in every pass it was "Wait for daylight".

**Cause, in the check:** the test sets `climbed` on any goal line with "Dig in for the night", besides "Climb toward the surface for water" and its feet above its start + 1. In the suite run's log, Nightowl's feet were at y −58 and its first step was "Obsidian: shelter for the night - Dig in for the night: clear 14934624, −59, −1486698": one block down. It then waited for daylight all night, which is what the test asks for.

The shelter came first because at the first look the chamber was not yet "underground" to it (`underground()` needs the sky not visible from its head, and the staging's sky had not yet darkened). So `sheltered()` was false and `digDown` dug in, downward.

The dig-in clause was written for the live bug on 10a60d5: Sol climbed out for water at night and was dug in again at the top. Its climb line and its feet going up are caught by the other two clauses.

**The change (`NativePortalCampaignGameTests`):**
- A dig-in counts as a climb only when the cell it clears, read from its label, is above where it began (`dugInAbove`). The climb-line and height clauses are unchanged.
- The test is now one scene, `nightWater`, with three modes. Two new tests:
  - `atNightBelowGroundADigInDownwardIsNotAClimbForTheWater` (batch `nightWaterDugIn`): the chamber's shaft is open to the sky at the first look, so its first step is the shelter's dig one down. The shaft is closed once it digs in. This is CI's shape. It must pass.
  - `atNightUpAtTheTopAndDugInThereIsStillCaughtAsAClimb` (batch `nightWaterUp`), the negative control: Nightowl is set at the top at night, as though it had climbed out, and digs in there. Its dig-in must be counted as a climb by the dig-in's own rule, not only by its height.

No product code changes, nothing is granted, and no check, bound or timeout is loosened.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: the player reckons damage as vanilla 1.21.1 does and fights for the most of it. `DamageModel` (core, pure) holds vanilla's numbers, read from the decompiled 1.21.1 sources: each sword's and axe's attack damage and speed (a wooden or gold sword 4, stone 5, iron 6, diamond 7, netherite 8, all at 1.6 swings a second; axes 7 to 10 at 0.8 to 1.0), the mace (6 at 0.6, with its fall bonus of 4 a block for the first three, 2 for the next five, then 1), the swing's charge (`0.2 + 0.8 × charge²`), the critical hit (×1.5 when the charge is above 0.9, falling and not sprinting), Sharpness (`0.5 × level + 0.5`, scaled by the charge but not by the critical), the ten ticks in which a struck mob takes only what a larger hit adds, arrows (`ceil(speed × damage)`, Power, a critical arrow's extra), and the Ender Dragon (the head takes the full blow, every other part a quarter plus up to one; a perched dragon takes nothing from projectiles, and leaves its perch after a quarter of its health there). `Weapons` (adapter) reads the held items into the model and picks the weapon that deals the most per second for the fight in hand, from the hotbar or the pack. With a sword and an axe of a tier, the sword's jumping crits deal more over a fight than the axe's slower ones; a hit that would kill takes the hardest single blow instead.
In the End fight: the perched dragon's head is struck with the weapon so chosen, swapped into the hotbar first if it is in the pack, each hit at full charge; arrows are never loosed at a perched dragon (`AbstractSittingPhase.modifyDamageTaken` returns 0 for projectiles), and in flight the shot aims at the head when it has a clear arc to it, else at the body. The goal's other fights (`NativeFightAction`, `PortalRunCampaign.strike`) use the same choice and wait for a full charge.
A heavy hitter's toss, found in the suite: after the change to full-charge swings the hoglin fixture (`aHoglinWithLavaAtThePlayersBackNeverThrowsItIn`) failed once in suite 8. Alone it failed 1 run in 12 at full charge and 0 in 12 at the old 0.9, which is suggestive but not conclusive. The cause is not the swing itself. Vanilla's `Hoglin.knockback` adds a second push to the usual one straight back, turned by a random whole number from -10 to 10 passed to `Vec3d.rotateY`, which takes radians, so that push can point almost anywhere. The fight's edge rule checked only the ground straight behind. In the failing run the player had stepped to where the ground straight behind was safe, jumped for its crits, and was tossed sideways into the lava. For the heavy hitters (hoglins, zoglins, ravagers, iron golems, piglin brutes) the rule now checks a fan, straight back and 45 and 90 degrees to either side. With that, the fixture passed 36 runs in 36 alone, each off the edge within 11 ticks.

# F-35o — The water fetch's search and climb no longer undo each other

The live measure is E-40's iteration 22.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an AI player that wants water for its portal cast no longer goes round between its search and
its climb. In E-40's iteration 21 (`e40a21-2354e67`):
- On 303 Kindred at the mouth of its cut into a hill, the search's tunnel stepped a block in for water and the climb
  stepped a block back out for the surface, by turns, some 160 times a replay of that spell.
- On 31102026 Sol and 707 Kindred, the tunnel (or its walk to look, cutting a way through) went a block along under
  cover, and the way back walked it out again.

Neither filled a bucket.

Now:
1. **The climb first, under cover, with no water seen.** Main already did this. The tunnel for water comes under cover
   only when the climb has no way up at all. That is said once for the place, with why:
   `No way up to the surface for water from X: its climb gave up under the water at Y | found no step up, its way back
   stalled | runs into its own dig for water | …, no shelter of its own to open | its shelter not to be opened; its
   search tunnels instead`.
2. **The hold.** A completed step of its own dig for water holds off the climb while the player stands in that dig. A
   dig is the search's "Tunnel for water" or its walk's "Cut through … for water". The hold ends on a bucket filled,
   water newly seen, or the player more than 8 blocks from where the hold began. Nothing is time-based, and it never
   makes zero moves: held, its search goes on.
3. **No walk back into its own dig.** Moved off, the dig is remembered until a bucket is filled. While it lies within
   20 blocks, the way up for water is not "back the way it came". On 505's replay, before this, the walk back led into
   the dig again, by turns: 76–149 walks back a run.
4. **No climb away from water it has seen** within 16 blocks. Its search digs on toward it. This follows the option-1
   ruling's "no water seen".
5. **Water in reach is filled from where it stands**, whatever became of a walk to it. A walk refused before its
   tunnel's floor was dug had set a cave's pool aside, and the tunnel went on past the pool beside it. A fill that
   fails sets that cell aside for a minute.
6. **On the surface,** with no water known: its walk to look, then a wider one, then its tunnel on its own floor, never
   a step down. The wider walk is the eight ways round at 56 blocks, then the four diagonals at 28, within the explore
   radius of home. It never goes to a place it has set aside or found no way to.
   - Its ground height comes from F-35m's `Looks`. A top out of its sight gives the walk its own height. No column it
     has not seen is asked its heightmap, and water it has not seen is never read.
7. **Every tunnel for water is tagged** with why it is not the climb, for the counts: "(no way up)", "(at night)",
   "(on along its own)", "(toward water seen)", "(on the surface)".

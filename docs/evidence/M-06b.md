## Scope and implementation

The owner's direction (2026-10-23): a bunker in five stages, multi-level and multi-room, an underground mini civilization grown as the game goes on, walls lined and entrances discreet.

**The hideout** (`Bunkers.hideout`, core, new; `bunker` in the generators). Its parts:
- **The shed.** A stone shed three by five with a door: the kind of hut nobody looks at twice.
- **The stair.** Under the shed, full-block steps down six blocks, three high above each step. It is lined each side and roofed where it runs under the ground.
- **The room.** Seven by seven and three high, lined: the style's stone floor and ceiling, planks round the walls.
- **What it holds.** A crafting table, a furnace, two chests and a torch at each end; another torch in the shed.

The build runner digs the cells kept clear and places the rest, as for any build. The stone it lines with is mostly what it dug.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a player who can see a stand of trees it cannot get to gives up the stand after one refused walk,
and goes for wood elsewhere. Before, it walked for each log of the stand in turn, each walk refused and each a new log,
then stood with nothing to do.

Found: in the corpus gate for F-22c66 (#235), in `diag-factions303-079bdc2-303-sol-102920`, its player alternated
between "Walk to logs at …" and "Explore north for water". The walks went to fifteen different logs past its scene's
barrier fence, each "No walkable route".

Cause (product), in `PortalRunCampaign`:
- **One refused log, its column only:** a refused walk sets aside the log and the column a block round it
  (`SetAside.avoid`), so the next tree of the stand is the next target.
- **The stranding check never held:** `stranded()` needs three refused walks within three blocks of one place. In
  sol-102920 its walks for water between them took it further than that each time. In the fixture, with no such walks,
  three refusals stranded it, and stranded it offered no walk at all: "Wood: waiting for a feasible step", the open oak
  never walked to.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign`:
  - **When a stand is given up** (`refuseStand`, judged at the next look at what to gather, where the player's place is
    seen): a walk for a log refused "No walkable route", when the player is in the open (`inTheOpen`: the sky over it,
    no side higher than a step) and the log more than 8 blocks off across (`STAND_FAR`).
  - **What is given up:** every log it knows of chained to the refused one within 6 blocks (`STAND_APART`), and any
    seen since within 6 of one of them (`standGivenUp`).
  - **For how long:** while the player stays within 8 blocks of where it gave the stand up. Once it has moved off, the
    stand is looked at again. A line is logged each time a stand is given up.
  - The gather (`gatherable`) and the look for trees (`treesInView`) both skip those logs.
- Test: `NativeUnreachableLogsGameTests.logsInSightBehindAWallItCannotCrossAreGivenUpAsAStand` (its own batch
  `unreachableLogs`, the WIDE template so copies keep apart). A player with no wood on open sand:
  - Twelve oaks 11 to 21 blocks north inside a barrier box 8 high and roofed, in sight and out of reach.
  - One oak in the open 30 south.
  - It fails once the player has walked for more than three of the fenced logs before it holds wood.

How it got there (each a run's finding, with the told-trees class as the check that mattered):
- **First, any refused log gave up its stand:** `NativeToldTreesGameTests` failed 2 of 3. Dune, in a pit at the edge of
  the told stand, had one log walk refused and gave up all 28.
- **Then, only a log more than 8 blocks off:** the fixture 8 of 9, told trees 2 of 3. The stand's logs seen after it was
  given up were walked for one by one, so those within 6 of the stand count too. In told trees the refused log was six
  across and five up, so the limit is measured across.
- **Then, only from the open:** told trees 2 of 5. Dune walked the told strip in a trench two blocks down, and its walks
  were refused there for where it stood, from twenty off. It now gives up a stand only from the open. Told trees then
  passed 5 of 5 with no stand given up in any run.

Nothing is granted: it walks to and cuts its own wood.

## Known limits

- **Logs only:** ore and stone seen past an obstacle are still set aside one cell (and its column) at a time.
- **Judged from where it stands:** a stand is given up only from the open and from more than 8 blocks across. From a
  hole or a trench it is set aside a log at a time, as before. A stand some of whose logs could be reached from another
  side is given up from here all the same, until the player moves off.
- **Stranding unchanged:** three refused walks from one place still leave it offering no walk until the stranding lapses
  or the no-progress rule moves it. The fixture's player now has one refusal, not three.

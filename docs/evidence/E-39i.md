## Scope and implementation

**The formula** (`BlastExposure`, core, pure, new in `tactics`). Vanilla's explosion damage, `((e² + e) / 2) × 7 × 2P + 1` with `e = (1 − d / 2P) × exposure`, where `d` is the distance from the blast to the player's feet and the exposure is the share of rays from the blast to a grid of points on the player's box that no block stops, as `Explosion.getExposure` counts them. `CRYSTAL` is power 6, `BED` 5. `exposure` takes the rays as an interface (`Rays`) the adapter answers with the world's own collision shapes; `damage` and `toPlayer` give the blast's damage before armour; `safe` says whether a blast leaves the player standing after its armour and toughness (`CombatRuleset.afterArmour`) with health to spare.

**In play** (`PlayerRuntime.findBestEscapeDirection`, `blastRays`). A creeper about to go off (power 3, a charged one 6): of the steps it could take, the one where the blast would do least, by the blocks between as well as the distance, the farther where two are alike. Before, it was the farthest step only.

Changed modules/files: `BlastExposure`, `BlastExposureTest` (new, `companion-core`); `PlayerRuntime` (`findBestEscapeDirection` with a blast's power, `blastRays`); `NativeBlastGameTests` (new).

## Scope and implementation

**Found:** the test server spaces its tests by their structures and runs several at once. A fixture that lays ground wider than its structure lays it over the plots of the tests running beside it. Its restore then leaves those blocks as they are, "its ground put back but N blocks … within another test's plot left as they are", while the other test runs on with them.

On #475's CI (run 45078128589, attempt 1, head 1575fd82) there were 105 such restores.

**Slice 1, Bring (test only, `NativeBringGameTests`):**
- All 23 of its tests in that log keep the same ground: from 9 west, 1 below and 8 north of the origin, to 25 east, 15 up and 34 south. That is 35 by 43, on a structure 8 by 8.
- Each of its 24 tests moves from `EMPTY_STRUCTURE` to `FixtureGround.FIELD` (80 by 32 by 80), with sky access as the other `FIELD` fixtures have.

- No check, bound or timeout is changed, and nothing is granted.

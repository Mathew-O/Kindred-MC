# F-22c106 — A walk at its period's end is not begun on its slice's last 250 ms

Date: 2026-11-21. Adapter, paid activity: one added condition on the skill tick, and a test seam that is off by default.

## The failure

`NativeAccountingGameTests.aWalkRunningAtItsPeriodsEndOnASlowStoreCarriesOnIntoTheNext` failed on CI twice:

- #408, run 43868643269;
- #417, run 44905765686.

Both failures read "native_goto BLOCKED: Movement authority denied". The walk ended partway along the way instead of
being carried into the next accounting period. Alone, the class passed 5 of 5 here. With the store's writes delayed
1000–1400 ms it still passed.

**Cause.** A skill's tick checks the paid slice once, at its start: `usable` needs `activity.paid()`. Each move then
asks `nativeMutationAllowed`, which ends with `activity.paid()` again. `PaidActivitySession.paid()` reads the clock
on every call (`System::nanoTime`).

On a slice booked to its period's end, a tick that began with a few milliseconds left could outlast the slice. The
walk's own move was then refused, and `NativeGoToSkill` ended the walk: `Step.blocked("Movement authority denied")`.
The period's hold (`holdOverPeriodEnd`, F-11g) carries a walk only when it is still `RUNNING` and the tick sees the
slice lapsed. Here the walk had already blocked itself inside the tick.

## The fixture

The fixture uses a test seam, `PlayerRuntime.holdSkillTicksForTest(Duration)`. It is off by default (`ZERO`), like
`delayBudgetWritesForTest`, so production never runs it. When it is set, a tick that begins with less of its slice
left than the hold first waits out the hold, as a long tick on a loaded server does.

The hold parks until its deadline. One `parkNanos` is not enough, because the server thread is often unparked early.
A first version with one park held nothing: the slice's remaining time was the same before and after it.

`aWalkWhoseTicksRunLongAtItsPeriodsEndCarriesOnIntoTheNext` is the same 68-block walk over midnight, with the hold
set to `RENEW_GAIN_MIN`. The existing test and the new one share `walkOverPeriodEnd`. The existing test's scene is
unchanged, with the hold at zero.

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| The class ×3, the seam and fixture without the fix | The new test failed 3 of 3: "Blocked: Movement authority denied", at 45, 45 and 41 blocks from the way's west end, CI's own message. The other 7 tests passed in all 3 runs. |

## The fix

`PlayerRuntime.endsItsPeriodWithin(entry, RENEW_GAIN_MIN)` is added to the `usable` line of the skill tick. The
margin is `RENEW_GAIN_MIN` by name, so the two stay tied.

The condition is true only when all of these hold:

- the skill is one of `HELD_OVER_PERIOD_END` (walk, traverse, retreat, goto, wait), and not a `NativeRecordedAction`;
- its slice is booked to the period's end;
- 250 ms or less of the slice is left.

Such a tick is not begun. The period's hold then carries the walk into the next period, as it does for a slice that
has lapsed.

**The one new behaviour.** A walk or wait leaves up to 250 ms of its last slice unused at a period's end, and is held
over instead. A lapse at the hours' end, at the cap, or on a late renewal mid-period halts exactly as before.

**A wider fix was tried and not kept.** That version applied the margin to every busy skill. It passed the new test
3 of 3, but failed `aRunningSkillStopsWhenItsHoursEnd` 3 of 3:

- The break halted with `activity=ACTIVE paid=true remaining=218ms`, with its hours still open.
- The test's second request was therefore admitted and halted ("Cancelled: Stopped or unavailable") instead of being
  refused as "Outside its activity hours".

That would have been a change a user can see, so the test was left as written and the margin was narrowed. The lead
approved the narrower fix.

**The corpus read, stated before the gate ran.** The changed line is evaluated on every paid tick, but it is true only
for a walk or wait within 250 ms of a slice booked to its period's end. Scenes with no period end inside them should
be unchanged. A scene that shows the change would be one where a walk is held over a period's end.

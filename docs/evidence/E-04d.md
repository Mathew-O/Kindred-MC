Assigned-furnace smelting and fuel accounting advance autonomous AI players to authentic pyrometallurgical processing and culinary survival in Minecraft:
1. **Furnace Workstation Interaction (`native_smelt`):**
   - Implemented authentic native furnace screen and block entity interaction (`Blocks.FURNACE`, `AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity`) within normal reach (4.5 blocks).
   - Targets furnace input slot 0, fuel slot 1, and output slot 2 under vanilla slot indices and stack limits.
   - Enforces world edit policy (`EditPolicy.Kind.PLACE`) and a 15-second reservation claim (`block:<worldId>:<dimension>:<pos>`).
2. **Fuel Accounting and Loading (`load` mode):**
   - Supports valid vanilla fuel items (coal, charcoal, wood planks, logs) into fuel slot 1.
   - Tracks fuel consumption and accounts for vanilla combustion initiation when both fuel and smeltable inputs are present (`AbstractFurnaceBlock.LIT`).
3. **Smeltable Ingredient Loading:**
   - Supports vanilla smelting recipes (`RecipeType.SMELTING`): raw iron/copper/gold, cobblestone to stone, food/meat, logs to charcoal.
   - Transfers owned items from player inventory to furnace input slot 0 with verified stack conservation.
4. **Output Extraction and Authentic XP Drops (`extract` mode):**
   - Extracts finished smelting products from output slot 2 into player inventory.
   - Invokes vanilla `AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity.dropExperienceForRecipesUsed` to drop authentic earned experience spheres for the player.
5. **Durable Ledger Integration and Mathematical Item Conservation:**
   - Checkpointed `NativeRecordedAction` ledger integration via `ActionAdmission` (`native_smelt`) through `PREPARED -> DISPATCHED -> VERIFIED`.
   - Single-take semantics and crash-safe reconciliation: ambiguous writes block for reconciliation, never duplicating items.
   - Mathematical item conservation check (`FurnaceEvidence.verifyConservation`) ensures that total player + furnace items are strictly conserved across transfers (0 duplicated, 0 deleted).
6. **Autonomous Smelting Progression (`PlayerRuntime.evaluateNativeCandidates`):**
   - High urgency candidate `extract_smelt` (urgency 62.5, `Priority.PROJECT`): detects reachable furnace with output ready and automatically dispatches extraction.
   - Project candidate `load_smelt` (urgency 61.4, `Priority.PROJECT`): detects reachable furnace with empty input, checks player inventory for valid fuel and smeltable items, and dispatches loading.
   - Refuses smelting and respects safety latching under default protection policy.

All operations execute strictly under the shared `ActionArbiter`, `SkillRunner`, and durable SQLite accounting; zero synthetic items, zero cheats, and zero teleportation shortcuts.

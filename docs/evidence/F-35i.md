# F-35i — Faction walks back off as a whole after a run of refusals

Date: 2026-11-11
Agent / branch: Claude, MODES on MOVEMENT (`claude/movement-1111-backoff`)
Build: uncommitted at the runs below (main a60f919 plus this change); the commit is this PR's head
Status: in progress (fixtures done; the paired Factions seeds remain)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: in a faction world, an AI player whose faction walks keep coming back refused (from a tunnel
underground, or an island, every place its faction play chooses is out of reach) stops asking for them for a while
after three in a row, whatever the places, and longer each time. Before, each place was backed off on its own (three
refusals, then set aside), so a player with many places in its faction play was refused over and over between its
goal's tasks. The wait ends early once it walks somewhere, moves more than 32 blocks from where the run began, or
comes up to the surface having begun it below.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `FactionsPlayer` (`refused` with where it stood; `paused`; `atSurface`; `STREAK` = 3,
  `STREAK_MOVED` = 32, `PAUSE` = 600 ticks doubling to a game day; `walked` clears the run; `blockedWalks` and
  `refusalsCounted` for the fixtures); `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates` (no faction walk offered while paused),
  `observeRunner` (the refusal recorded with where it stood; refused faction walks counted),
  `factionBlockedForTest`, `factionPausedForTest`.
- gametest: `NativeFactionBackOffGameTests` (new, two fixtures), its `fabric.mod.json` line.

The per-place back-off (`SET_ASIDE`, `BACKOFF`) is unchanged underneath. "At the surface" is within a block of the
column's topmost solid block, leaves not counted (a player felling a tree under its crown is at the surface).

## Why

RUNNER 2's trace of Factions seed 109 on main a60f919 (`factions-trace109main`): Kindred, branch mining for iron at
Y 19 for the last seventeen minutes of the run, had faction walks refused 89 times with legs ending in one chunk,
twice in one second at times, with no faction walk completed. It was first read as the per-place back-off being
bypassed. The fixture below showed it is not: every refused faction walk was counted, each place three times and set
aside. The legs from x 105–119 all end in chunk (9,5) whatever the place, so the 89 were many places, each refused in
turn.

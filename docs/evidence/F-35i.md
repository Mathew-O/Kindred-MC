# F-35i — Faction walks back off as a whole after a run of refusals

Date: 2026-11-11
Agent / branch: Claude, MODES on MOVEMENT (`claude/movement-1111-backoff`)
Build: this PR's head (the runs below on its working tree over main 48063d3)
Status: in progress (fixtures done; the paired Factions seeds remain)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: in a faction world, an AI player whose faction walks keep coming back refused (from a tunnel
underground, or an island, every place its faction play chooses is out of reach) stops asking for them for a while
once two places have each had their three tries and been set aside with no walk between, and longer each time after.
Before, each place was backed off on its own, so a player with many places in its faction play was refused over and
over between its goal's tasks. Each place still gets its own three tries first: the per-place rule and
`NativeFactionPlayGameTests`' no-way fixture are unchanged. The wait ends early once it walks somewhere, moves more
than 32 blocks from where the run began, or comes up to the surface having begun it below.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `FactionsPlayer` (`refused` with where it stood; `paused`; `atSurface`; `STREAK` = 2
  places set aside, `STREAK_MOVED` = 32, `PAUSE` = 600 ticks doubling to a game day; `walked` clears the run;
  `blockedWalks` and `refusalsCounted` for the fixtures); `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates` (no faction walk offered
  while paused), `observeRunner` (the refusal recorded with where it stood; refused faction walks counted),
  `factionBlockedForTest`, `factionPausedForTest`.
- test: `FactionsPlayerBackOffTest` (new).
- gametest: `NativeFactionBackOffGameTests` (new, two fixtures), its `fabric.mod.json` line.

"At the surface" is within a block of the column's topmost solid block, leaves not counted, from the world's heightmap
(a player felling a tree under its crown is at the surface).

## Why

- Factions seed 109 on main a60f919 (RUNNER 2, `factions-trace109main`): Kindred, branch mining for iron at Y 19 for
  the last seventeen minutes, had faction walks refused 89 times with legs ending in one chunk and none completed. It
  was first read as the per-place back-off bypassed; the fixture showed it is not (every refusal counted). Legs from
  x 105–119 all end in chunk (9,5) whatever the place, so the 89 were many places, each refused three times in turn.
- Factions seed 303 (`diag-factions303`, read by A): faction legs 62 completed against 494 refused for no way and 27
  route blocked; seed 110: 44 against 487 and 77.

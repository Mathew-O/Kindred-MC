## Scope and implementation

**User-visible behaviour:** a player that digs in for the night stays in its shelter, sealed, until daylight. It does not set off from that shelter to mine iron meanwhile.
- A player already below ground at nightfall still mines on as before (B7).
- So does one that is below ground without having dug in tonight.

**Found:** `NativeFactionShelterGameTests.dugInForTheNightItsFactionWalkWaitsAndNoWayIsMadeOutThroughItsSeal` fails when its player is dug in for fewer than 200 ticks of the night. Seven of the saved CI logs on 12 November failed it. Two modes:

**Cause (product), in `PortalRunCampaign`'s night "meanwhile":**
1. Dug in for the night by a stair, the player is `underground()` before it has sealed the way in: the stair hides the sky from its feet.
2. With the route's next step logs from the surface, the meanwhile then replaced that step with `withoutWood`'s "Mine iron ore while the night passes".
3. Its walk on into a cave it had seen ended at a cell below the ground's top. F-25c4cd's hold on walks out under the sky, while dug in, looks at where a walk ends, not the way it goes, so the walk was not held.
4. Its way went up the stair and over the ground, out of the shelter, and the player dug in again elsewhere.
5. In passing runs the walk's end was out under the sky, so the hold replaced it with sealing and waiting ("nothing to do before daylight").

**The change:**
- No meanwhile step while it is in the shelter it dug tonight: `nightShelterAt != null && inNightShelter(player)`.
- The night's logs step then seals the way in and waits for daylight, as in the passing runs.
- `nightShelterAt` is set only by "Dig in for the night", and cleared by day. A player below ground at nightfall that dug no shelter keeps B7's meanwhile.

**Not changed:** the hold's own gap (where a walk ends, not its way) beyond this step. It is filed as F-22c76 (#278), queued after this row.

**Nothing granted.** Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign`: the meanwhile step's condition.
- Test: `NativeNightMeanwhileGameTests`, a new class, registered after `NativeNightShelterGameTests`.

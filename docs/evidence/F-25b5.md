# F-25b5 — A native body goes through a portal again after its first crossing

Date: 2026-11-23
Agent / branch: Claude, Session B (claude/portal-return-1123), at the lead's word; a child of F-25b
Build: main `23dbe8fa` with this branch's change (the PR's head)
Status: complete on the branch: the corpus gate passes by the lead's ruling (below); CI on the PR

## Found

F-25c6e's native fixture (home from the Nether with the rods) found this. A body that had come through to the Nether
stood out of the portal over there and walked back into it, and nothing happened.

Its portal cooldown never ran down (`hasPortalCooldown()` stayed true) for as long as the test ran. That was some
thousands of ticks; vanilla gives a player 10.

## Cause

- `ServerPlayerEntity.tickPortalCooldown` counts the cooldown down only while `inTeleportationState` is false.
- Vanilla sets that state on every teleport, a portal crossing included. It clears it when the player's client says
  it has the new world (`onTeleportationDone`).
- A native body has no client. It called `onTeleportationDone()` once, at its join (`NativePlayerBody.connect`).
- So after its first portal crossing the state was never cleared, the cooldown never ran down, and the body could never
  go through a portal again.

That rules out home from the Nether whatever it carries, and any second crossing.

It does more, as the corpus gate found. `ServerPlayerEntity.isInvulnerableTo` returns true while `isInTeleportationState()`
holds, for all but an ender pearl. So on main, a native body that had been through any `teleportTo` was invulnerable for
good. In every rung c probe so far a player
in the Nether could not have come home: "a safe return" read 0 by construction.

## Fix

`NativePlayerBody`'s tick accepts the teleport (`onTeleportationDone()`) the tick after the body arrives, as a client
does once its world has loaded.

Nothing else changes:
- The cooldown itself is vanilla's: set on arrival, refreshed while it stands in a portal, then counted down.
- The 80 ticks a survival player stands in a portal before it is taken are vanilla's.
- Respawns and other teleports go through the same acceptance, as a client's would.

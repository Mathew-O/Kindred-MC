## Scope and implementation

**Found:** `NativeHearingGameTests.whatTheServerSendsItsConnectionIsTheSoundsAroundItLoggedAndNotActedOn` failed on #477 (run 45100827316, attempt 2) with "The creeper's priming, from behind it: []".

**The message misled.** The first look heard 9 packets ("E-71f heard 9 packets in all"). That step ends the body's listening, which clears its list, so each retry read nothing and the timeout showed `[]`. What was missing was only the creeper's hiss. In ticks after the staging, the 9 were:
- +0: the skeleton's shot, the block broken (2001), the explosion;
- +8 and +121: the skeleton's ambient;
- +21: the arrow's hit and the armor stand breaking;
- +59 and +190: the cow.

**Cause, in the staging:**
- The creeper stands four north of the pad, one chunk north of it. The cow starts in that chunk too.
- The test forces the chunks round the pad just before it spawns them, and took the creeper away at a fixed 10 ticks after it was lit.
- A creeper hisses on its first tick with its fuse lit. On that run the north chunk's first sound was the cow at +59, while everything in the pad's chunk sounded at +0. So the creeper most likely never ticked within its 10. The log then had no creeper age to show it.

**The change (`NativeHearingGameTests`):**
- The creeper is taken away once it has ticked with its fuse lit (`getClientFuseTime(1) > 0`) or its hiss is heard. It is also taken away by 29 ticks after it was lit at the latest, ticked or not. Its fuse is 30 of its own ticks, so it never goes off. The listening window for what the player hears stays as it was, 200 ticks.
- The heard list is read once, so a failure names what was heard.
- The log and the priming check's message name the creeper as it was taken away: its age, whether it had ticked with its fuse lit, whether its hiss was heard, and how many ticks after it was lit.
- The test is now one scene, `sounds`, with two modes. The new `aPrimedCreeperWhoseFirstTicksAreHeldIsStillHeardHissing` (batch `hearingHeld`) holds the creeper's first ticks: it is lit, then put in the world 15 ticks later, as though its chunk were not yet ticking.

No product code changes, nothing is granted, and no check, bound or timeout is loosened.

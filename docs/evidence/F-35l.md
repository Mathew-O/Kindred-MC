# F-35l — Stone mined from a thin floor over a drop: not from round its feet

Date: 2026-11-12
Agent / branch: Claude, MODES on MOVEMENT (`claude/movement-1112-footing`)
Build: this PR's working tree on main ff82997
Status: in progress (the fixture measured and the gate passed; a live run remains)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player gathering stone on a floor one block thick over a long drop (a bridge, a ledge, a sky
island) no longer mines that floor out from round where it stands. A block below its feet is not chosen to mine when
breaking it would leave no ground within a safe drop under it. On a floor with ground under it, it mines as before.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `PortalRunCampaign.gatherable` (a target below its feet only where `landingWithin` finds
  ground within `NativeNavigator.MAX_SAFE_DROP` under it; before, only the one block it stood on was left).
- gametest: `NativeFootingGameTests` (new, two fixtures), its `fabric.mod.json` line.
- docs: this file, `feature-spec.md`, `validation.md`.

`landingWithin` is the dig-down's existing check (F-25c4ch's family): it reads the column under a cell to refuse a step
whose landing is deeper than the navigator drops. It is used here the same way, only to refuse a block, never to choose
one, so it reveals nothing the player then acts on.

## Why

On #224's CI (job 130849133167) the back-off fixture failed with five faction walks refused and no pause. Its log: the
player, on its sky island one block thick and thirty up, worked its Stone goal: "Mine cobblestone for a stone pickaxe -
Mine stone at" the floor's blocks round and beside it, one after another; then, between -29 and -50 within a second,
"fell from a high place" and respawned at the world's spawn, its faction walks never asked again. `gatherable` left out
only the block under its feet (`!p.equals(support)`); the neighbours were taken first, and it stepped into the hole.

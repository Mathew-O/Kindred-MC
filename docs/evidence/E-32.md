## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
- `/companion quirks [UUID]` shows each quirk's saved setting (`auto|on|off`), whether the companion is currently doing it, and any chat-promised pause.
- `/companion quirk <sprintjump|lively|curiousglance|steady> <auto|on|off> [UUID]` edits one setting with owner/operator authorization, acknowledges, then reports durable success or a storage fault that stops the companion.
- During an authorized follow that is actually moving the body, an active `sprintjump` quirk sprints and queues a jump each time the body is on the ground; a ceiling two blocks up, a one-block gap beside the route, or an unsafe predicted landing falls back to plain sprinting; without a lease nothing happens. `haltControls` clears the sprint flag and queued jump.
- While settled beside the owner, a newly arrived visible person may receive a bounded glance (0.8–2.5 s) and a low-priority `NOTICE` remark under ambient limits.
- Addressed chat about a habit is answered in character; an agreeable personality pauses the habit for a bounded time (owner 30 min, visitor 5 min, one per speaker per 30 min); the saved setting is never edited by chat.

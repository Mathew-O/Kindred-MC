## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (M-06i2a's seed comparison, Factions seed 20261030, both arms). Founders held no land, and most of their faction walks were refused for no route.
- **The trace, per founder:** of 919 faction legs refused for no route across the two arms, 15 ended where a player could stand. The rest ended inside solid ground (637), in mid-air (211) or in or over water (56).
- **Where they began:** with bases on, Kindred's refused legs began below Y 50 215 times of 235, Sol's 56 of 68; with bases off, 77, 39 and 72 of Kindred's, Sol's and Echo's.
- **Why:** `FactionsPlayer.destination` ends each 48-block leg at the player's own height unless it sees ground to stand on within 8 blocks of it, and the walk is asked with range 3 and no partial route. From a branch mine at Y 19 that end is inside the rock. The navigator refuses it correctly; no route ends in stone.

**The change (Factions-only).**
- **Deep below, by what it perceived** (`FactionsPlayer.saw`, `deepBelow`, `DEEP = 8`): at each faction decision it notes the height it stands at while the sky's light reaches its feet. It is deep below when no sky's light reaches its feet and it is more than 8 blocks below that height. Before it has seen the sky, nowhere is deep. Nothing is read from the world's heightmap or from blocks it has not seen.
- **No walk asked from there** (`PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates`, after the night's dig-in): a faction walk other than DEFEND is not asked. Its move is settled, as on arrival, so its faction decisions go on at the decision interval. No refusal is counted and no way made (F-35i, #120 untouched).
- **What it chooses below** (`PlayerRuntime.factionChoose`): while deep below, the first choice it can do where it stands (a sale, a claim of the chunk it is in) or a walk home to defend.
- **Not deep:** a hollow, a box round it, a pit or a notch dug in a wall at the height it saw the sky from. There a walk is asked, refused and backed off, and a way made, as before.
- **For fixtures:** `PlayerRuntime.factionSawForTest`, `factionWalkingForTest`. `Territory.Chunk.nearestToSpawn` is public (as on main since M-06i2a).

**How it got here:**
1. **First cut:** walks waited while `atSurface` was false. Two Factions classes failed: the no-way fixture (a bedrock box, roofed) 3 of 3, and a basin walk-out (a notch dug in its wall) in 2 of 3 runs.
2. **Narrowed:** deep meant more than 8 blocks below the column's top, read from the heightmap. That is world data the player had not perceived (the lead's review), so it was replaced by the sky's light and the height it last saw the sky from.
3. **The seed comparison against main** then showed sales gone (32 and 14 on main, 0 and 0) and fewer claims: the wait held the move, and a new decision waits on the move, so below it decided nothing. Fixed by settling the move and choosing what it can do where it stands.

# E-07e1 — A table in the goal's reach is in the dispatch's reach

Date: 2026-11-12
Agent / branch: Claude, REFACTOR (`claude/gold-table-reach-1112`)
Build: the PR's final commit; the fix and its fixture in `b1166d5`, main merged in `c07b283`
Status: in progress (CI on the PR)

## The failure

`NativeGoldGameTests.inTheNetherAPieceOfGoldIsMadeFromTheOreAndWorn` failed on docs-only #261's re-run (run
43245862450, attempt 2): "A golden helmet worn: 1 minecraft:iron_helmet; nuggets 3, ingots 5 / End: Fortress: Craft 1
piece of gold armour for staying alive - Craft golden helmet". Alone on main `32b304a` it passed 10 of 10.

The CI log:
1. Goldsmith mined the seam, set down its crafting table and made five ingots, crafting each one at that table as it
   worked along the wall.
2. With five ingots, the goal asked to "Craft golden helmet".
3. Every few seconds after that: "native_craft dropped before it ran: no longer executable {recipe=golden_helmet}".
4. "Goldsmith has started nothing for 1201 ticks on its goal", until the test ran out.

## Cause

The goal and the dispatch measured reach to the table differently.
- **The goal** (`PortalRunCampaign.craft`) asks for the craft when its own table's middle is within 4.4 of the eye.
  `NativePlayerBody.planCraft` accepts a known table within 4.5 of the eye.
- **The dispatch** (`PlayerRuntime.validateCandidateStillExecutable`, through `primeWorkstationCaches`) found its table
  with `locateWorkstationBlock`. That measures from the block the eye is in to the table's block, a whole-block
  distance.
- A table on a diagonal, about 4.0 from the eye, is in the goal's reach but the root of 21 off by whole blocks, past 4.5.
- So the cache stayed empty, and `planCraft` fell back to `findNearbyBlock`. That scan runs nearest first under the
  runtime's budget of 128 blocks a tick, and ran out before reaching the table. The craft was dropped.
- The goal, with the table in its reach, never walked nearer, and asked again.

Locally the player's places along the seam differ run to run, so the diagonal is rare.

## The fix

- **`PlayerRuntime.workstationInReach(world, eye, block, reach)`** finds the nearest block whose middle is within
  `reach` of the eye, as the player's own use of it is measured.
- The four 4.5-reach checks use it: the dispatch's workstation cache (table and furnace), and the bring's and the
  kit's "a table in reach" checks.
- The wider "a table to walk to" searches (radius 8 to 16 from the feet) still use `locateWorkstationBlock`, unchanged.

# E-04c — Stone-tier progression and furnace crafting
Date: 2026-10-17 EDT; report 2026-10-17.
Codex / uncommitted main, no commits. Complete for this child; parent E-04 remains open.

Stone-tier progression and furnace crafting advance autonomous AI players to the second major technological tier in survival Minecraft:
1. **Pickaxe-mineable Block Harvesting (`native_mine`):**
   - Extended `NativeMiningAction` to support pickaxe-mineable blocks (`BlockTags.PICKAXE_MINEABLE`, `Blocks.STONE`, `Blocks.COBBLESTONE`) in addition to logs.
   - Enforces pickaxe tool requirement and applies authentic tool wear (damages pickaxe durability by 1 per block broken).
   - Generates attributable durable action receipts (`nativeReceipt=...`) under `ActionRecord.State.VERIFIED`.
2. **Stone Tool Workstation Crafting (`native_craft`):**
   - 3x3 workstation crafting of `stone_pickaxe` at a placed crafting table from 3 cobblestone and 2 sticks.
   - Strictly enforces 100% physical item conservation: exactly 3 cobblestone and 2 sticks consumed, exactly 1 stone pickaxe produced.
   - Rejects crafting attempts without item loss when crafting table is unreachable or ingredients are insufficient.
3. **Furnace Workstation Crafting (`native_craft`):**
   - 3x3 workstation crafting of `furnace` at a placed crafting table from 8 cobblestone.
   - Strictly enforces 100% physical item conservation: exactly 8 cobblestone consumed, exactly 1 furnace produced.
4. **Furnace Block Placement (`native_place`):**
   - Authentic block placement into the world within assigned work area under `EditPolicy.Kind.PLACE`.
   - Validates solid support block beneath and consumes exactly 1 furnace item from the designated hotbar slot.
   - Settles with verified `native_place` durable ledger receipt.
5. **Autonomous Stone Progression (`PlayerRuntime.evaluateNativeCandidates`):**
   - Evaluates pickaxe availability before selecting stone mining candidates (`playerHasPickaxe`).
   - Automatically selects `ItemTags.PICKAXES` for pickaxe-mineable stone blocks and `ItemTags.AXES` for logs.
   - Survival project prioritization: tool upgrade (`craft_stone_pickaxe`, urgency 62.0, `Priority.PROJECT`) and workstation deployment (`craft_furnace`, urgency 61.5; `place_furnace`, urgency 61.2) take priority over continuing to mine with inferior wooden tools (`native_mine`, urgency 60.0).
   - Automatically manages inventory by transferring crafted furnace into hotbar when placing.

All operations execute strictly under the shared `ActionArbiter`, `SkillRunner`, and durable SQLite accounting; zero synthetic items, zero cheats, and zero teleportation shortcuts.

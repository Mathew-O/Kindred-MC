## Scope and implementation

**Found:** `NativeRerollGameTests.aLecternPutDownWhereItsSpotWasTakenMeanwhileIsTheOneItRerolls` failed on #451's run 45033457942, attempt 1, with "Given up: the lectern taken back, 0". In its log:
1. After its 16th reroll, Hal stood on its lectern.
2. Its break was refused six times in a row: "Would remove the block the player stands on".
3. With no walk off the lectern between the refusals, the reroll gave up (`r.failed >= 6`) with the lectern still in the ground.

The refusal appears 0 times in two other CI logs of the same test (#454's, #457's).

**Cause:** the reroll's BREAK already stepped off first through `offTheLectern`. That tried only where Hal stood when the reroll began and the four sides at the lectern's height. Each needed to be clear at feet and head, solid below, and free of any mob. In a small pen with the villager beside the lectern none qualified, so it returned null, and BREAK then asked for a break it would be refused.

**The change (`PlayerRuntime`):**
- **`stepOffTo(world, block, first)`:** one helper, at the planner level, which `offTheLectern` now uses. It tries `first`, then the four sides at the block's height, then the four beside it one up. A cell qualifies if its feet and head are clear, the block under it has a full top face, it holds no mob, and it is not over the block itself. The break of the block underfoot stays `RoamingEdits.breakDenial`'s to refuse; the helper only finds where to stand, so that the break is never asked for.
- **BREAK, standing on the lectern, no cell found:**
  - it waits "For room off the lectern" for up to `JOB_WAIT` (1200 ticks);
  - then it lets the villager be, says "There's no room to step off my lectern; I've left it standing.", and comes back in 1200 ticks;
  - never through refused breaks.
- **PLACE, no lectern held:** its lectern lying within 8, out of the break's collection, is walked onto, bounded by `JOB_NUDGE` (300 ticks). After that it lets be, as before.
- **A fixture-only count of the reroll's refused steps**, `rerollStepsRefused`.

The break dispatch is unchanged, as the lead chose (option B).

**Two changes from the approved plan, both forced by the fixture, and both accepted by the lead:**
1. **No ring two out.** With no path check, a cell two across from a lectern by its pen's wall lies outside the wall, and a partial walk there would get no nearer. The helper keeps to the cells beside the lectern.
   - Its floor test is a full top face rather than `isSolidBlock`. `isSolidBlock` is false for glass, so the fill's top was not a floor to it, and the room case gave up with "no room".
2. **The walk onto its lying lectern.** Stepped off one up, Hal broke the lectern from 1.5 above. In 1 run of 3 the drop fell into its own hole, out of the break's collection ("its own drops uncollected … age 71"), and the reroll then let be with the lectern lying there. The walk ran in 2 of the next 3 runs, each time with a drop uncollected, and each time took the lectern back.

Changed files:

## Scope and implementation

The Builder mode (M-04) as a match on the framework of M-00a, with M-00b's presentation and the build runner of E-08b.

**A round.** `/aiplayer match build <builder...> [for <seconds>] [theme <name>]` (operators only) proposes a build battle for two to eight builders, AI players or humans online.
- **The plots.** One per builder, fifteen a side, in a row from where the operator stands, three apart. They need open, level ground, clear sixteen blocks up; elsewhere the proposal is refused with the block in the way. They are marked out in white concrete with a grey edge.
- **The round.** Joined and started like any match. The theme is announced as a title at the start: a cottage, a watchtower, a pyramid or a lighthouse, by chance or the operator's choice. The build lasts five minutes unless given another time (thirty seconds to half an hour).
- **Free blocks in Builder only (AGENTS.md).** Every builder arrives with nothing; one carrying anything is refused. It builds in creative mode, and leaves with nothing in its own game mode. When the build's time is up, whatever creative gave it is cleared and it looks on in adventure mode while the votes come in. When the round ends (or a builder leaves, or the server stops), its own game mode is back, with an empty inventory.
- **Each to its own plot.** While the build runs, a human builder may break or place only on its own plot, and nobody may on another's plot, in the round or not. An AI builder's scaffold may stand in the gap beside its plot, never on another's.
- **The vote.** Each builder scores every other build, one to five, the same for humans and AIs. An AI builder votes at once, as the judge scores what stands on each plot. A human is offered a [1] to [5] to click for each build (`/aiplayer match vote <builder> <score>`). Once everyone has voted, or after thirty seconds, the best mean wins, ties together; with no votes it is a draw. Fireworks go up over the winners' plots.

**An AI builder.**
- **Its design.** The theme turned into a design from Kindred's own generators (`BuildBattle.design`), its size and materials by chance, so no two rounds look alike:
  - a cottage: the hut in one of four woods and four stones;
  - a watchtower: open-topped with battlements, in one of four stones;
  - a pyramid: stepped, three sizes, three sandstones;
  - a lighthouse: a column striped in white and a colour, with a lamp of glowstone in glass under a slab roof.
- **Building it.** Stocked with what the design takes (creative's free blocks), it builds through the same planner and runner as a survival build, every block verified in its state. The body acts in creative only while the runtime has it build in a battle; otherwise its controls still work in survival alone. In creative, a placement conserves by changing nothing, as vanilla does, and a break needs no tool.
- **The judge.** It scores a plot from its blocks: size, kinds of block and height, height again for the tall themes, and a bare plot a one.

Designs are kept to what a builder on foot can reach (the build planner has no flight). A platform over a hollow tower could not be placed from any stance or scaffold, as the simulation showed, so the watchtower is open at the top and the lighthouse a solid column.

Presentation (M-00b): a boss bar with the time left to build, then to vote; the start ("Build!") and the theme as titles; the result as a title ("Victory": "Wren wins the vote").

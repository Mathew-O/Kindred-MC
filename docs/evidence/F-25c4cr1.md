## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player on the cast route, about to go down for the cast's lava with its last pickaxe, no sticks and less than two wood, first goes up for two logs. Those make the sticks for two more pickaxes and a table's planks. Then it goes back to the cast, with no second trip.

Found in E-40's iteration 25, second batch. On seeds 101 and 31102026 every player reached iron and went down for the cast's lava. They branch-mined for it until their pickaxes and sticks were spent at depth: 16, 11 and 12 stone pickaxes were made, and Kindred and Echo fell back to Wood at Y=-54. Then they climbed for logs by hand for the rest of the run; Kindred on 101 climbed from Y=-54 to Y=-7. Those two seeds are two of the iteration's six portals not lit. RUNNER 2 recorded the timelines and consoles on `claude/corpus-data-e40a25-portal-1122`.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-core `PortalRunPlanner`: a constant `RESERVE_LOGS = 2`, and one early return in the cast block, after the water bucket and the mould's blocks and before `CAST_PORTAL`. The condition is `caves && pickaxes <= 1 && sticks < 2 && woodUnits < 2`, and it returns GATHER_LOGS with the reason "Up for wood while its last pickaxe still digs".
- companion-core `PortalRunPlannerTest`: `onTheCastRouteWithItsLastPickaxeAndNoSticksItGoesUpForWoodFirst`.

Only the cast's descent, on purpose. At the iron stage, below ground with no sticks or wood, the pickaxe it has still goes on. That is `theTripsWoodIsTakenAmongTheTreesBeforeTheFirstStone`, unchanged here. Its evidence (F-25c4b to h, from Y=8 and Y=18) is that a spare fetched from the surface wore out the pickaxe it had on the way up the staircase. The cast's descent differs: the lava lies at Y=-54 to -57, far below the iron, and the branch mine for it runs fifty blocks and more (F-25c4cw). The last pickaxe is spent there, a long way below any tree, with nothing left to make another.

The reserve is fixed, not sized by depth or wear. `PortalRunSnapshot` carries neither, only `pickaxes` and `pickaxeTier`.

Unchanged:
- every other planner step, and the iron stage's below-ground wood (`tripWood`, `sticksReserved`, the spare stone pickaxe and the `SPARE_PICKAXE` habit);
- how the campaign treats a GATHER_LOGS step: the night wait, the night's mining below and the TRAVEL set;
- `stage()`, `frameMethod()` and `sticksKeptFromTorches()`.

The change crafts, keeps or holds back nothing; it only chooses this one step.

### After #455's first CI: the guard and the bound

#455's CI on `eb731844` (run 45015580199) failed two tests, and both failures were the change's:
- `NativeSnowSiteGameTests.onSnowyGroundTheCastClearsTheSnowByHandAndCastsItsFrame`: "Its time out with no lit portal", up for wood from @401 to the end, on the surface with the cast at hand.
- `NativeSpireGameTests.strandedOnASpireOverAMobItGetsOffOnceStranded`: "Still on the spire", up for wood from @801.

Both stage an iron pickaxe with lava in reach. The condition was broader than "only on the cast's descent": the turn held the cast to a wood search that never ended. The corpus gate could not see this because the turn fired 0 times there.

The guard (`1df397b5`): the turn fires only while it still has the lava to find with a stone pickaxe, `!facts.knows(LAVA) && lavaBuckets == 0 && pickaxeTier < 3`. The planner test adds three cases where it must not fire: its lava seen, lava in a bucket, an iron pickaxe. On the old head the first failed ("its lava seen: on to the cast … was GATHER_LOGS").

The bound (`06c6a42f`, at the lead's word): where no tree is in reach, as on 31102026's open sand (LOOPS's census: players idle on the sand, no tree known), the search would go on while the last pickaxe could still be finding lava.
- `PortalRunCampaign.boundReserve` counts the daylight ticks spent on the reserve's wood search. After `RESERVE_SEARCH_TICKS = 2400` with no wood gained, it sets the reserve aside for this descent and puts the cast's own step (`PortalRunPlanner.castStep`) in its place. Lava found or the wood in hand ends the descent's reserve.
- A walk refused or nothing left to explore gains no wood either, so the same bound ends those searches. There is no separate rule for them.
- Its console line, printed once: `<name> found no wood for its reserve in N ticks; set aside for this descent, on to the lava`.
- `NativeReserveBoundGameTests` (new, registered) stages a bare stone plot with no tree: a stone pickaxe, no sticks or wood, no lava seen, the rest of the cast's kit in hand. It must go up for wood first, then go on to the cast's step within the bound plus a margin.
  - On `9f22dcb4`, without the bound, it fails 2 of 2: up for wood to the end (4000 ticks).
  - With the bound it passes, set aside at 2402 ticks. At the plot's depth the cast's step is "Branch mine for lava".

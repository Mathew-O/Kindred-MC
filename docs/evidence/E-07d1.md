# E-07d1 — A clutch into shallow water is not held as a conservation failure

Date: 2026-11-22
Agent / branch: Claude (MODES on MOVEMENT) / `claude/movement-1122-clutchpool`, from main `97f7f1bc`
Status: in progress (the PR's CI)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player falling into water too shallow to break the fall saves itself with its water bucket and goes on. It is no longer held for reconciliation for the rest of the run when nothing was lost. A clutch that does lose something is still held, and the hold names what is short.

Found in E-40's iteration 27, second batch, on 31102026 (RUNNER 2's consoles on `claude/corpus-data-e40a27-consoles-1122`, `e40a27r-772dee3-31102026-console.txt`, about tick 131,900):
- Sol found no walkable route at 6, 73, 29, so it went to "make a way elsewhere: walk on".
- "native_goto CANCELLED: Falling: the fall to be broken with water".
- It fell to 5, 61, 29, then "Get out of the water".
- Then `IllegalStateException: Clutch conservation check failed; reconcile`, and "Native action outcome requires reconciliation; no replacement inventory will be created."
- Sol was held for the run's last 10.1 minutes. The run's item sources balanced: 8032 gained, 8032 paid.
- The "inventory full - Drop surplus …" lines during the fall are campaign goal lines only: no `native_discard` ran.

The cause, in `NativeClutchAction`:
1. `landing()` takes the highest collision top under the feet. Water has no collision shape, so a pool's bed reads as the ground, and a fall into water too shallow to break it is judged worth a clutch.
2. The pour lands on the pool's water. Vanilla places water on a still source and the bucket empties, so `water` is set to that block.
3. Down in the pool, the scoop's own aim (vanilla's source-only raycast from the eyes) can take a bucket from the pool beside the pour. The bucket comes back full while the pour's block is still a still source, so `scooped` (which needs that block emptied) stays false.
4. After `SCOOP_TICKS` (40), `settle()` applies the poured-and-left rule: water buckets −1 and empty buckets +1 expected. The inventory is exactly as before, so it failed.

A diagnostic line in `settle()`, never committed, showed this on the fixture's pool two deep:
- `water` at the feet, still: true;
- `scooped=false`, landed 41 ticks;
- water buckets 1 and 1, empty buckets 0 and 0, `conserved=true`.

Changed modules and files:
- companion-server-adapter `NativeClutchAction`:
  - After the scoop's use, a bucket full again with its pour still a still source is `takenBeside`. It settles on the scoop's rule: water buckets and empty buckets as before, and nothing else lower (`conserved`). It is recorded as "Native clutch: poured on water, a bucket taken back beside it", with `waterLeftAt` and `takenBeside` in its progress.
  - The water is found in both scoop cases: taken from where it was poured, or still there.
  - A failure now names the shortfall: "Clutch conservation check failed; reconcile: " followed by each item held fewer of, by count and slots, and the buckets before and after. The record's detail carries it.
- gametest: `NativeClutchPoolGameTests` (new, registered after `NativeFallGameTests`).

Unchanged:
- The water == null path: nothing poured, or a pour whose water is not where it was aimed. It stays blocked as before.
- The poured-and-left rule, the poured-and-scooped rule, `conserved()`, `close()`, and `landing()`, `pourNow` and `clutchSlot`, which decide when to clutch.
- The landing still reads a shallow pool's bed as the ground. That is what this case is: the clutch into shallow water now settles correctly rather than being skipped. A pool deep enough to break the fall still ends the clutch before any pour, as the player touches water first.

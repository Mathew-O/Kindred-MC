## Scope and implementation

**Why.** In RUNNER 2's keyed run, Echo's `faction.joined` was refused on the Wire as a duplicate of Sol's (F-34d). The
refusal was logged only at debug, and the Record notes only sent messages, so nothing in the console or the Record
showed it. A month-long run's operator needs to see news that is not going out.

**The change:**
- `RefusalTally` (core, new) counts refusals by `Delivery.Refusal` and the channel's registered id. It holds no
  message's kind, sender, scope or facts, so the line it feeds cannot leak anything the audience rules withhold.
- `KindredWire.send` counts every refusal there, beside its existing total, and gives `refusals()`.
- `RecordGauge`'s daily `Kindred cost:` line adds `Wire <n> refused since start (<REASON> on <channel> <count>, ...)`,
  or `Wire none refused since start`, after the inboxes.
- `operator-guide.md` describes the count and what a climbing one means.

Not body behaviour: metrics only. Nothing is kept or sent differently.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-core: `wire/RefusalTally` (new), `RefusalTallyTest` (new).
- companion-server-adapter: `KindredWire`, `RecordGauge`, `PlayerRuntime.logCost`.
- gametest: `NativeCostGameTests` (one fixture).
- docs: this file, `operator-guide.md`, the tracker.

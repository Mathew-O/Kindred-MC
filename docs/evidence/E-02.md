## Scope and implementation

**Crops** were sown, harvested and replanted on approved farmland before this slice (`NativePlantAction`, `NativeHarvestAction`, `NativeTillAction`; the M-06a farm ward's field, `FieldRunner`). They are covered by `NativeFarmGameTests.cropSowingAndReplantingConservation` and `NativeCompoundGameTests.withItsWardStandingAPlayerWatersTillsAndSowsItsField`.

**Approved trees** (`PlayerRuntime.replantCandidates`, `recordTrunks`). A patch of logs given to a player: where each trunk stands on dirt or grass is recorded when the patch is given, and again every five seconds, so a tree grown from its sapling is known in its turn. Where a recorded trunk has been cut away, and no log of it is left standing above, it plants a sapling of the patch's wood it holds on the dirt, walked to first. It says "I planted an oak sapling where the tree stood." The cutting is E-03's patch work: logs with an axe, the drops picked up.

Changed modules/files: `PlayerRuntime` (`replantCandidates`, `recordTrunks`, the patch's trunks recorded when it is given, the finish hook); `NativeTreeFarmGameTests` (new).

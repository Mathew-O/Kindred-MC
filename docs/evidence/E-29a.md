## Scope and implementation

The owner's idea of 2026-10-23, still being worked on: "To achieve dynamic complex building maybe we give a player a seed block like Cherrywood and have it builds whatever based around that block... We want to build different shapes of sizes. We don't want static repetitive builds across worlds."

**The style (`BuildStyle`, core, pure).** A build's style is grown from a seed block and a world.
- **Its wood.** The seed's own if the seed is wood (a log, planks, stairs, a slab, a door, stripped or not); else the wood the player has to hand; else oak.
- **Its plan and facing.** One of 32 variants of the stage's plan, and one of four facings, both drawn from the world's seed, the player and the seed block. Another world, another player or another seed grows another build. The same ones always grow the same build, so a base is rebuilt and repaired on its own plan.
- **Its stone** is cobblestone for now.

**The variants (`Houses`, core, pure).** Each stage keeps its first plan as variant 0, the plan bases were built on before. The others vary:
- **Size.** The cabin 7 or 9 blocks each way; the house 9 or 11 wide and 11 or 13 deep; the manor 13 or 15 wide and 15 or 17 deep.
- **Layout.** Laid out the other way about, west for east, every block turned to match.
- **The cabin's roof and windows.** A gable with its ridge along the cabin's length (either way for a square one), spanning seven blocks at most; a cabin nine by nine is roofed flat. One window or two in each side wall.
- **What stays.** Every room keeps its working blocks (beds, chests, table, furnaces, the enchanting room and its shelves), its door in the middle of the front wall with the way in and out clear, and a floor under all of it. A test checks every variant of every stage.
- **Facing.** It is turned on placing: the door faces any of the four ways.

**A player's base grown (adapter).**
- **The seed.** `/aiplayer seed <block> [player]` gives a player its seed block, kept with its bases across restarts; `/aiplayer seed clear` takes it back.
- **No seed.** Without one, its wood is the one it carries most of, as planks or logs, else that of the nearest tree it knows.
- **Each new stage** is grown so and sited on the ground its turned plan covers. Its record keeps the seed, wood, variant and turns, so repair (E-31a), its bed (F-25c9), its chests and the next stage's siting all read its own plan.
- **Old records.** A base recorded before has none of these: it is read as the first plan, in oak, as it was built.
- **Logs.** A build now cuts only logs of its own wood. A cherry build is not made of the oak beside it: oak logs would never have made cherry planks.

Changed modules/files:
- Core: `BuildStyle` (new), `Houses` (`Shape`, `shape`, `span`, `originFor`, `of(stage, style)`, the cabin's variants, `gableRoofAlongZ`, `mirror`); `BuildStyleTest` (new).
- Adapter:
  - `PlayerRuntime` (the grown style, the turned site, `styleOf`, `planOf`, `placedOf`, `footprint`, `woodAtHand`, `setSeedBlock`, `fixBaseStyle`; repair, beds and chests read a base's own plan).
  - `PlayerMemoryStore` (`Base` keeps its style; `Bases` its seed).
  - `PlayerCommands` (`seed`).
  - `BuildRunner` (logs of its own wood).
  - Fixtures: `NativeSeedBuildGameTests` (new, six cases); the base, repair and life fixtures build the first plan in oak, as their bills are made for.
Dependencies satisfied: M-01b (stages), M-01c (the player's own base).
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: new optional fields in `.bases.json` (`seed`, `wood`, `variant`, `turns` on each base; `seed` on the record). A file without them reads as before; an unknown wood or variant leaves that record out, as an unreadable stage does.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- Not yet:
  - A stone seed block (stone bricks, sandstone, deepslate) used for the walls: the walls are cobblestone, the one stone the needs can mine.
  - "Builds around that block" taken further: the seed block set in the build itself, a build sited round where the seed was placed, and plans grown rather than chosen from variants (wings, towers, porches, other roofs).
  - Builds other than its base (farms, pens, the Factions bases and bunkers of M-06) grown the same way.
  - A seed of a wood it cannot find nearby: it builds as far as what it holds goes.
  - The K panel's control for the seed.

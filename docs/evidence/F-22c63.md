## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a player cutting its tree-farm patch finishes each trunk, and then plants a sapling where the
trunk stood. That now holds even when a mine is refused at the last moment, before it broke anything. Before, one such
refusal left a trunk's upper logs standing for good, the patch was called cut down short, and that trunk was never
replanted.

Found: `NativeTreeFarmGameTests.itCutsThePatchsTreesThenReplantsWhereEachTrunkStood` failed on unrelated heads twice in
three hours, each time with "A sapling where the trunk stood at … −53 …: Block{minecraft:air}":
- #182's CI at f427b30;
- #185's re-run at 115c960 (run 43017539262 attempt 2).

Both logs show the same sequence:
1. `native_mine BLOCKED: Mining preconditions changed before dispatch; no break issued`, on a trunk's third log
   (y −51).
2. Three walks `No walkable route toward` the top log (y −50).
3. "could not get oak log … in sight to mine; set aside".
4. "mined out its patch: 6 oak log".

Alone on main 79a9aad it passes 3 of 3, and 8 copies at once passed 16 of 16 in two runs. Neither logged the refusal.

Cause (product):
- `PlayerRuntime.observeRunner` put any BLOCKED `native_mine` of the patch into `patchSkipped` at once. That included
  the refusal before dispatch, where nothing was done. The third log was set aside.
- The top log's walk to "the ground under it" needs open air under it. The third log was still there, so the walk
  aimed at the log itself and found no route. Set aside after three walks.
- `replantCandidates` waits until the whole trunk is down, so with those two logs standing over the cut, the trunk was
  never replanted.

Why a retry is safe: the refusal is `NativeMiningAction`'s own check at dispatch. When the setup revision, target,
tool, claim or policy changed since the plan, no mutation is issued, so the outcome is certain, not ambiguous. A mine
planned again re-checks policy, revision and claims as every mine does, so it is never a blind replay. Every other
BLOCKED or uncertain outcome keeps its set-aside:
- a claim held by another task;
- an uncertain acknowledgement or close;
- one needing reconciliation;
- one interrupted, denied or changed after it was issued.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `NativeMiningAction`: the refusal's text as `UNBROKEN`, and `nothingIssued(reason)`.
- `PlayerRuntime.observeRunner`: a patch's mine refused with nothing issued counts as one of the three tries that its
  walks already count (`patchWalks`). It is planned again with every check made afresh, and set aside only on the third
  try. Any other refusal is set aside as before.
- Tests:
  - `NativeMiningRefusalTest` (JUnit): only that refusal counts as nothing issued; each of `NativeMiningAction`'s other
    refusal and uncertain texts does not.
  - `NativeTreeFarmGameTests`: a `scene(context, refused)` helper, and the fixture
    `aLogWhoseMineWasRefusedUnbrokenIsTriedAgainAndItsTrunkReplanted`.

Nothing is granted.

Date: 2026-11-05

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: in a bring's ROLL phase (E-45e), a cart that runs out of way short of the cell rolls back,
and the player standing behind the line's start (`b.push`) is there to stop it, so it can be pushed on again. Stopped
against the player, the cart's middle lay a little into the player's own cell, off the rails.
`b.rails.indexOf(cart.getBlockPos())` was then -1, so the bring took it for a cart stopped off the line and gave up
("The cart with the librarian stopped short of its cell") with the librarian aboard.

- **CI:** `NativeBringGameTests.aLibrarianOffTheLineIsBroughtHomeFromWhereItStands` failed with "Brought home: Bringing
  the librarian home [ROLL]" on main's `f4bce7a` (run 42010885729) and on PR #25's `b3f1590` (run 42030222090).
- **Reproduced locally** on a 25 ms-per-operation store ([F-22c5](F-22c5.md)): 1 of 4 failed.
  - The line laid again from where the librarian wandered climbs a block; the first push left the cart at 0.25 blocks a
    tick, and it rolled back.
  - It came to rest at x+0.28 into the push cell. The log read "the cart stopped at (…535.28, -58, …) (air), rail -1 of 25,
    1 pushes", then the bring gave up.
  - In the copies that passed, the cart came to rest on the first rail and was pushed again.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime`: a cart stopped in the push cell counts as at the line's start,
and is pushed on again from there.

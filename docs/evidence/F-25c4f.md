## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player smelting without coal burns planks, then logs, and its sticks only last; with only logs to burn it makes planks of them first.

Found reading the smelt after B7's second pairs, where players climbed out for logs to make sticks again and again (15% of Sol's time in the first run, with the strategist off):
1. Sticks ranked above planks and logs as fuel. With no coal, the iron was smelted with the stick reserve kept for the pickaxes still to make: six sticks for three iron, where two planks do.
2. A log burnt whole smelts one and a half items; as four planks it smelts six.

Changed modules/files:
- `NativePlayerBody.fuelPriorityScore`: sticks last, below planks and logs.
- `PortalRunCampaign.smelt`: the iron's load names the fuel it takes, so a run's log shows what it burns.
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core): with no coal and too few planks for the smelt, logs are made into planks first.
- `PortalRunPlannerTest`, `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one case and one fixture.

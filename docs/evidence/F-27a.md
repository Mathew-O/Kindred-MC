## Scope and implementation

- A source set `corpus` and mod `kindred_corpus` in companion-server-adapter, run by `:companion-server-adapter:runCorpus`
  (results in `build/corpus-results.xml`). Only corpus scenes register; the contract suite is not run with them. The
  pinned Fabric GameTest API has no test filter, hence the separate source set.
- `CorpusGameTests`: each `data/kindred_corpus/gametest/corpus/<id>.json` is one test. It is either a replay (F-27 state
  plus a `corpus` object: class, run, seed, build, source, staged) or a staged fixture (`"fixture": "<Class>.<method>"`,
  a contract GameTest run as it stands). Ten scenes go to a batch.
- Structures: copied from the replay folder for replays on main, or gzipped under `src/corpus/structures/` (5.9 MB) and
  unpacked at build.
- `tools/corpus/import.py` (run folders to scenes), `report.py` (pass rate, by class, staged apart, gate) and `gate.sh`
  (three runs, then the report). `baseline.json` is the last three-run result on main.
- Scenes: 224 deaths. 78 come from the f29e-on and f29e-on-4h replays on main; 146 come from e40a13–e40a17 and e40c1
  (claude/corpus-data-1110@44234a0). There is one staged fixture (F-25c4dd). Earned: replays are the run's own ground and
  state; a fixture is staged as its javadoc says.

## Replays start as recorded: the harness made deterministic (2026-11-11, `claude/corpus-determinism-1110`)

Test-side only (`ReplayGameTests`, `CorpusGameTests`, `tools/corpus/report.py`); no product code changes.

**What varied, from three gate runs on 7ec519c (ten intermittent scenes sampled):**
- In 8 of 10, passing and failing runs of a scene part at the player's first choice (which mob to fight or back off
  from). The structure's mobs had their AI on from placement and moved for the 40 ticks the ground settles and the
  1–8 s the player takes to be made, a different amount each run.
- Ten scenes side by side share one world clock. Each set it to its own record as it started, so the last of ten set

  restored.
- 10–13 replay players a run lived on after their batch: a test that failed or timed out never reached its stop.

- (a) The scene's mobs are held still (no AI) from placement until the player is in place, then all let go on one

  new batch, and each replay sets its recorded weather and difficulty.
- (c) A server tick listener stops each replay runtime as soon as its test completes, passed or failed (read from its
  context), or its time is past. `report.py --logs` counts players still acting after their batch: 10–13 a run before,

## A stuck-spell scene passes on progress, not movement (2026-11-11, `claude/spell-pass-1111`)

#166's pass was "moved more than 8 blocks, or 3 up, within 3600 ticks". A loop that walks between two places meets that
while still in the loop: 39 of #182's 45 spell scenes passed it, 8 of its 10 hole-or-pit.

A spell scene now passes when its goal gains something: the watchdog's material progress (stage, tools, armour, wood,
stone, iron, …). New ground is left out, because a replay's goal starts with no ground seen. It also passes when the
player stays out of the 8 blocks round where it was caught for the budget's last 1800 ticks. Death scenes are unchanged.
The pass logs which way it got out.

| --- | --- | --- |

- **7 scenes passed the old condition and fail the new**, among them `diag-factions303-079bdc2-303-sol-27680`: hole or
  pit, "try elsewhere" walks in and out.
- **28 pass both.** In one extra run, 21 of the passes were by their goal's gain and 10 by a lasting exit. For example,
  `diag-holepit-079bdc2-606-sol-52260` (hole or pit) gained after 170 ticks, and `diag-factions303-079bdc2-303-sol-58760`
  (water loop) stayed out for its last 2718 ticks.

**Still a limit.** 8 of the 10 hole-or-pit scenes pass even so, most by a gain within 75–460 ticks. In the run they were
stuck for minutes. A replay restores the player's body, held items and the ground, but not what its goal had learnt:
places set aside after failed walks, trees given up, ground seen. Many of these loops lived in that memory, so a fresh
goal on the same ground does not loop. Restoring the goal's remembered set-asides with the replay is the change that
would let stuck scenes reproduce their loops. It's proposed, not done here.

## The dragon scenes play: the fight on in a dragon replay's own policy (2026-11-11, `claude/corpus-dragon-1111`)
- **The fault:** the corpus's 26 DRAGON-goal scenes (`e40c1-eed2dc5-*`) never played a tick. Every run since the corpus began (c266a7e3) failed them with "Its goal, DRAGON, accepted". `PlayerRuntime.startGoal` refuses the dragon goal while the server's `dragonFight` is off, and the GameTest server never turns it on. They count as fails in the 084dde5 baseline, about a tenth of the corpus. This was found while checking a suspected goal leak in MOVEMENT's #157 gate. There was no leak: the report had pinned a neighbouring dragon scene's line on `e40a17-7bc5b42-707-sol-48263`, whose own failures were "slain by Zombie" twice and one pass.
- **The change:** `ReplayGameTests.edits(scene)` writes the replay runtime's own edits.properties. It adds `dragonFight=true` only when the recorded goal is DRAGON, as the recording server had it. `dragonFight` belongs to each runtime and is read from that file at start. No other replay's runtime sees it, and it ends with its runtime. So the scenes need no batching apart and no server-wide restore.
- **Guard (staged):** `ReplayWorldStateGameTests.aDragonScenesReplayHasTheFightOnAndAPortalScenesBesideItDoesNot`. Two replay runtimes are live at once, one from a DRAGON scene and one from a NETHER_PORTAL scene. The fight is on in the first and off in the second.
- **Measures:**
  - `ReplayWorldStateGameTests`, `ReplayGameTests` and `NativeStuckSpellReplayGameTests`, narrowed (10 tests): 3 of 3.
  - Three dragon scenes through `runCorpus` (`101-sol-13531`, `505-echo-42820`, `8675309-kindred-171479`): each accepted its goal ("Beat the Ender Dragon."), and the three ran 131 native actions between them. ## Replays keep their goal's own memory (recorder, 2026-11-11, `claude/replay-memory-1111`)
A replay starts its goal fresh: the places set aside, trees given up, ground known and dug are gone. That's why seed 110's bamboo loop passes as a replay. This is the recorder half. The harness's restore follows in its own PR, then RUNNER 2 re-captures.

- **What is kept:** a spell, stuck or death replay's `replay.json` gains `memory`, copied by `PortalRunCampaign.memoryIn` from the campaign's own fields on the tick the replay is taken.
  - targets set aside, with ticks left;
  - failures and approaches;
  - targets unreachable, with ticks left and where from;
  - cover held;
  - trees given up;
  - cells dug and tunnelled, and caves walked;
  - steps set aside (skill, label, plain arguments, place);
  - blocks known by kind, from `WorldKnowledge` as it stands;
  - home;
  - its stranding, explore heading, stalls and failures;
  - stuck spells and level.
- **Rules:**
  - Positions are relative to the 33×17×33 box, and times are ticks left.
  - What lies outside the box is left out and counted in `leftOut`. Each kind is capped at 512 entries.
  - Steps that name a thing rather than a place (an entity `target`, a `cart`) are left out.
  - Nothing is read from the world, so a replay knows no more than the player did. Nothing is taken on an ordinary tick.
- **Restore:** `restoreMemory` puts back those fields and nothing else. An entry outside the box, malformed or of a kind it doesn't know is dropped and named, never guessed. A four-block cell (a cave cell, a given-up tree column) is put back as the cell its least corner falls in at the new place.
- **Fixture:** `NativeStuckSpellReplayGameTests.aStuckSpellsReplayKeepsWhatItsGoalSetAsideAndPutsItBack` (staged). A pocketed player with a block three east set aside by the fixture gets its spell captured. The memory holds that block. Put back into a fresh goal whose box stands 100 east and 40 north, the block is set aside there and not at the old place. One out-of-box entry and one unknown key are dropped and named. A null memory puts nothing back.
- **Measures:**
  - With `ReplayGameTests` and `ReplayWorldStateGameTests`, narrowed (10 tests): 3 of 3.
  - **Size:** the scene's memory was 4,009 bytes in each run. 592, 734 and 982 entries were left out as outside the box. Their kinds weren't counted.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player swimming in water with no dry ground it can swim to, as in a flooded pit, swims to the side and digs a step out of it, treading water to keep its head up while it digs, then climbs out onto the step. Before, its climb out was cut short each time, because nothing but a walk was allowed in deep water, and it stayed in the water until it drowned.

Found in the live pair on `03faab9` (seed 8675309, strategist off):
- At 16:33 Kindred mined coal at Y=17 and 18 that let water in, and fell into a pit four blocks below its tunnel.
- No dry ground was in reach ("No walkable route toward 113, 19, 65"). Its goal's climb out ("Climb to find another way: clear 112, 17, 66") was cut short each time ("In deep water; up for air first"), and its swim up for air ended at once at the surface.
- That cycle ran some 250 times a minute for 46 minutes, until Kindred drowned at 17:20. The run took 56 minutes, where the long run on `a286cc8` took 33, probably for the cycle's records.

Found making the fixture, each fixed in turn:
- With ordinary work to offer (cobblestone to mine), the goal never reached its climb out at all; it is now the swim's own step, once there is no dry ground to swim to.
- In the middle of a pit there is no side beside it to dig, and the way over to the nearest side needed ground under it.
- Digging with its eyes under water is five times slower, and a player that does nothing in water sinks. So while it digs above its eyes, it treads water.
- Bobbing at the surface, its feet went from one cell to the next, and each planned a different step. Before the climb started from the water's top cell, two of three runs climbed out, then one of three.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign`:
  - `surface`: with no dry ground to swim to and its head above water, a climb out ("Climb out of the water");
  - `stopFor`: a climb's step digs on in deep water while its breath is to spare;
  - `climb`: swimming, from the water's top cell, and over to the nearest side across water.
- `NativeBreakAction`: afloat under a block it digs above its eyes, it treads water.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

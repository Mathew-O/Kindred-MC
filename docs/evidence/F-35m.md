# F-35m — No far column's height that the player cannot see

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an AI player no longer knows the height of ground it cannot see. Before, explore walks, faction
legs, the stronghold walk, the look round for land and water from a boat, the End island's footing and the night's "out
under the sky" rule read the server's heightmap at far columns. That held for hills behind walls, ground under a canopy
and pits behind a rim, which is knowledge of the hidden world (#364). Now a far column's top is used only when a sight
line from the player's eyes reaches it, over ground that is loaded. Where it cannot see the top, the walk goes at its own
height, for the route to find the way, or the column is no land, water or island to it.

The rule (the lead's rulings on F-35m, 2026-11-12):
- A far top counts only if a clear sight line from the eyes reaches that block. The heightmap is read only to choose
  where to aim that one line. A failed line discards everything learned from that column: nothing is used or kept.
- Unloaded means unseen. A column whose chunk is not loaded is never asked its height, and no sight line is cast
  across ground that is not loaded (every chunk column along the line's ground track is checked first), so a look never
  loads a chunk.
- The sight lines are capped per decision, and each column is asked once a decision.
- A test asked before the sight line, to spare it, may only skip a place that could never be the answer: water is not
  the land it looks for, a cell with no room is no walk's end. The result is the one a sight line to every place in turn
  would give (`Sight.firstSeen`, pinned by a pure test).

Changed modules/files:
- companion-core: `Sight` (`seenTop`, `seenFrom`, `firstSeen`, `loadedAlong`; `treeInView` no longer casts across ground
  that is not loaded); `SeenTopTest` (new, six cases).
- companion-server-adapter: `WorldKnowledge` (`Looks`, `looks`, `looksOverTheBrink`; the sight lines counted), `RecordGauge`
  (the far looks on the daily cost line),
  `PortalRunCampaign` (`explore`, `outUnderTheSky`, two fixture hooks), `FactionsPlayer` (`destination`),
  `StrongholdLeg` (`walkToward`, `nearestLand`, `surfaceWater` and its three callers), `EndFightLeg` (`islandTop` and its
  eleven callers, `stepOff`, `downThrow`).
- gametest: `NativeSeenColumnsGameTests` (new, three fixtures), its `fabric.mod.json` line.
- docs: this file, `feature-spec.md`, `validation.md`.

## The open yard's intermittent on #275's CI (2026-11-14)

`NativeSeenColumnsGameTests.aTopInItsSightIsWhereItsWalksGo` failed on #275's CI (run 43546586275, 003bff4) with
"The explore walk to the tower's top it sees: -4378232, -28, -11806212". The explore walk was on the north tower's
column but at its own feet's height. So it had not seen that tower's top.

- **Alone, it does not fail.** On main dc5ad4d it passed the class ×10, all three tests each run. Its explore went
  north in 3 of those runs and saw the tower's top each time.
- **In CI it was a matter of place.** The failing step gave the same answer on every tick it was retried. Its count
  matched a passing run: 3 far columns asked, 2 sight lines. So the explore look did cast its sight line north, and the
  line did not end on the tower's top.
- **The fault is the fixture's.** It laid its floor, its walls and its towers, but never made the air between the yard
  and the towers its own. In CI every test shares the same ground, with others running round it at the same time. A
  block in that air, left by another test, hides the top. The log of that run names no block placed there by another
  test, so which test's block it was is not known.
- **The same failure, staged:** a stone in each of the four sight lines, 14 out and 3 up, laid before the yard. The
  fixture as it was failed 3/3 with CI's message (its explore walk at its feet's height). Fixed, it passed 3/3.

The fix is in the fixture, with the assertion unchanged:
- **Only the sight lines are cleared.** Before the yard and the towers are laid, the cells within a block of each of
  its five sight lines (its eyes to each tower's top) are set to open air, from its feet up. That is 1,451 cells, at
  x −29..49, y 0..12 and z −29..29 from the yard's origin. Nothing beside the lines is touched.
- **They are put back as they were.** All of them are inside the box it already kept (x −32..52, y −12..14,
  z −32..32), which is unchanged. `FixtureGround.keep` reads every state in that box before the clear. `restore` writes
  back each cell that differs when the test ends, so whatever stood there before the clear stands there again.
- **The failure message names what blocks the line:** the block the sight line to the tower's top meets, and the
  column's top.

Caveat, for F-22c75 and unchanged by this fix:
- The yard sits 30 up and outside its own GameTest plot, by design: its towers stand 28 and 48 blocks out.
- Its `restore` also writes back the kept box's old states over anything a neighbour changed inside that box during
  this test.

Both are F-22c75's general question.

# F-35c1 — No walk to the portal out of a night shelter

Date: 2026-11-11
Agent / branch: Claude, MODES on MOVEMENT (`claude/movement-1111-portalnight`)
Build: this PR's head (the runs below on its working tree over main 79a9aad)
Status: in progress (fixture done; the corpus gate remains)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player dug in for the night in its sealed shelter, its lit portal a few blocks off and below
or above, stays in till daylight; it does not dig its way out to the portal in the dark and back. By day the walk to
a portal far off or deep goes a leg at a time as F-35c made it. On the surface at night the campaign digs in first,
before any walk, as before.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `StrongholdLeg.enterPortal`: while `PortalRunCampaign.dugInForTheNight(player)` holds
  (night, its shelter sealed, it inside it), the walk to the portal is the one direct walk into it, as before F-35c,
  however far off the portal is.
- gametest: `NativeNightPortalGameTests` (new), its `fabric.mod.json` line.

"Dug in for the night" is the campaign's own state (F-25c4bx): where it dug in tonight, and its night shelter's depth
in the column it stands in. The change reads nothing else.

## Why

After F-35c (#126) the night shelter's fixture (`NativeNightShelterGameTests`, F-25c4bx) failed in the suite: on #146's
CI twice and on main by timing. In the CI logs, main's passing run and #146's failing one match step for step: through
the held night the End goal sent "Walk to the portal" and "Make a way to the portal" from the shelter. The portal was
about five below and four across, seven off in all, past F-35c's six for one walk into it, so the legs and the made
way took it out of the shelter and back.

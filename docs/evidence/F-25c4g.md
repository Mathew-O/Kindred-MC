## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: climbing a staircase out, a player does not dig out the block over its head room when sand or gravel rests on it. It climbs from somewhere else: the end of its passage, or wherever its next try takes it.

Found in B7's paired runs: three of the 26 deaths in the four logs were suffocation. Two were Kindred's, in the second pairs (seed 8675309, strategist on), both climbing out for food, hurt and with nothing to eat:
1. It cleared the block over its head room for the next step up.
2. The gravel resting on that block fell onto it.
3. It dug the gravel out of its head room, and the next block fell in, until it died.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign.climb`: the block over the player's head room is refused when a falling block (sand, gravel) rests on it. That block is the same whichever way the stair goes, so every way is refused and the climb goes to the nearest wall of the room or passage to dig its stair from there. `PortalRunCampaign` is changed on `modest-faraday` only in its hole-up lines.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

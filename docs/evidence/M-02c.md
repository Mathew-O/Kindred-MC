## Scope and implementation

The owner's note of 2026-10-23: while evading another player or a threat, a player should queue what keeps it alive and gets it to safety as its hunger runs down. It should eat at a safe distance to recover health, or change course. In a world where others are unfriendly, it should go somewhere else rather than back to its base, which would give the base away. This slice covers a manhunt's AI runner (M-02b), the one evasion in play so far.

**Not toward home** (`EvasionCourse`, core, pure).
- **Home.** The runner's home is its first base in the world it is in (M-01c), else the bed its spawn is set by. A home more than 160 blocks off is not counted: no single way off leads there.
- **The turns.** The runner tries ways off in this order: straight away from the hunter, 45 degrees to either side, square to it, then back past it at 135 degrees.
- **The cone.** A way off within sixty degrees of the way home is left out. Only when every way off leads home does it run that way all the same: away matters more than where.
- The turns are worked out as the adapter turns its way off (Minecraft's own rotation), and a test holds the two to the same sense.

**Fed once clear** (`evadeCandidates`).
- **Running first.** A runner moves off from a hunter it sees within twenty blocks, sixteen blocks at a time, as before (M-02b). Eating is urgent too, but it weighs less than running. So the runner eats only once no hunter is in sight within twenty blocks, the distance at which it moves off, never with a hunter at its heels.
- It logs when it is clear ("Rue is clear of its hunters").
- **Tried and left out.** A rule to run on until the hunter was 28 blocks off was tried and left out. Its control was not conclusive: one run without the rule still ate further off than 24 blocks. Against a hunter that keeps up, it would never let the runner eat at all.

Changed modules/files:
- Core: `match/EvasionCourse` (new) and its test (3).
- Adapter: `PlayerRuntime` (`evadeCandidates` with the home kept; `homeToKeep`).
- Fixture: `NativeManhuntGameTests.aChasedRunnerNeverRunsTowardItsHomeAndEatsOnlyOnceClear` (new).
Dependencies satisfied: M-02b (the runner's evasion), M-01c (its base), F-25c9 (a bed for its spawn).

**The fixture.**
- **The field.** Open ground walled round, 71 by 55 blocks, its chunks kept loaded.
- **Who.** Hal (hunter) is held still. Rue (runner) is eight blocks south of it, its spawn point set twenty blocks further south: straight away from Hal is straight home.
- **Once the hunt runs,** Rue is at 12 of 20 health and 10 of 20 food, with six bread in its hotbar.
- **What must happen.** Rue must eat, and Hal must be at least twenty blocks off when it first does. Its way off from where it stood, taken at that moment, must be more than sixty degrees from the way home.

**Hand controls.** Each reverts one rule, and the fixture fails on it:

| Control | Result |
| --- | --- |
| No home kept: the ways off tried as before | Failed: "Not toward home: 1.00"; Rue ran 13 blocks straight home and ate there, 21 blocks off |
| Eating weighed above running (running at 80 against eating's 82) | Failed: "Ate with Hal 15.3 blocks off" |

Found on the way:
- The first fixture took Rue's direction a tick after it was fed, and a step that fails is tried again the next tick. So the control with no home kept passed, its direction taken later, after it had moved on. The direction is now taken once, at the tick Rue first eats.
- The rule to run on until 28 blocks off (left out, above) was tried with its own control. Without it, Rue still first ate 29 blocks off in one run, at the end of a run sideways that carried it past twenty. Without the home kept, it ate 21 blocks off, straight after a short run home.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- Not yet:
  - **Other threats.** Evasion from a threat other than a manhunt's hunter: a mob it cannot fight, or a player in an unfriendly Factions world (M-06, which has no match mode yet).
  - **A night away from home.** Life first's bed at night (F-25c9) is not yet kept from pursuers: a runner sleeps only where its goal takes it.
  - **No food left.** A runner with no food left does not yet change course for food away from home.
  - **A hunter that keeps up.** A runner that cannot get out of sight or out of reach does not yet find a way to eat: it would need to break the hunter's line of sight first.
  - **Its base, once known.** Its base is kept from hunters only once its record is loaded, which happens when it has looked to its bases under the operator's leave this session. The bed its spawn is set by is always known.
  - **What its goal does next.** The runner's goal goes on once it is clear, and may take it home.
  - **In play.** A chase in play, with a human hunter.

## What it lost to

It lost to the tick budget: a stalled server thread. The model stub's latency and the listener limit played no part.
The model's line was never reached. The test failed at its step "At work on the gather", before the owner asks.

The same sequence is in both logs:

1. **The gather was requested** (`Requested native_gather`). The fixture's "Gather of three logs started" passes.
2. **Nothing was logged, by any thread,** for about 6 s (11-16: 11:55:57 to 11:56:03) and about 7 s (11-15: 05:35:33
   to 05:35:40).
3. **The gather was dropped:** "Echo native_gather dropped before it ran: … blocker=Reservation expired before
   acknowledgement". Its paid slice is `BudgetState.MAX_SLICE`, 5 s. The booking's acknowledgement was handled on
   the server thread after that slice had ended, so `ActivityPermit.acknowledged` refused it. Two or three
   `native_wait`s were dropped at the same moment.
4. **The player's scheduled routine then mined the patch's three logs on its own:** three `native_mine COMPLETED
   [from evaluateScheduledRoutineCandidates]`.
5. **The test waited out its ticks:** the work line stayed "Completed native_mine: …", and the step retried until the
   limit.

**What stalled the thread:** a test in the same `voice` batch. The first lines after each silence are its own:
"Waiting on you, mostly.", then "And now I'm answering your second question."
- That test is `messagesDuringAFlightWaitTheNewestInPlaceOfTheRest`, which calls `Talk.waitFor(3_100)` twice.
- Before `284a920d`, `waitFor` parked the server thread in real time (`LockSupport.parkNanos`), about 6.2 s in all.
  GameTests in a batch share the server thread, so every test beside it stalled.
- F-22c101 replaced the park with a social clock that only fixtures move (`PlayerRuntime.socialNow()`). `support.py
  entrypoints` now fails on any sleep or park in a GameTest source.

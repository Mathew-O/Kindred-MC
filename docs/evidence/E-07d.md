## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player falling with a water bucket in its hotbar breaks the fall with water when the fall would take four health or more, or all but two of what it has. This is the "MLG" water bucket. Vanilla 1.21.1 hurts a landing one point a block past three, and armour does not soften it. A landing in water hurts nothing: `LivingEntity.fall` checks for water before the ground. As soon as the block it will land on is within reach of its eyes (vanilla's 4.5), it pours water on its top face. The landing block is the highest top under the four corners of its feet. It lands in the water, then scoops the water back into its bucket.

The fall ends whatever it was doing, as other hazards do. The clutch is the most urgent thing it can choose, and it does not wait out the short hold that follows a commanded task. It respects the stop latch, the ledger and the server's edit policy (the water's block is a placement it must be allowed). In the Nether, where water boils away, it does not try.

With a bucket ready, a ledge's jump-down and a route's deliberate drop may go as deep as 40 blocks, not 12. At forty blocks down a fall is about two blocks a tick, inside the 2.8 blocks a tick at which the ground is surely in reach for one tick. In the End, a player stranded on a pillar's top with a bucket steps off onto end stone and breaks the fall instead of spending an ender pearl.

`native_clutch` is a recorded action of its own. It spends the bucket's water and takes it back. A pour it could not scoop up leaves an empty bucket and the water where it was poured, and both are verified.

Found on the way: in the first control run a twelve-block fall hurt nothing. Vanilla spares a player that has just joined from harm for sixty ticks (`ServerPlayerEntity.joinInvulnerabilityTicks`), and the fixtures dropped the player inside that window. The fixtures now wait it out. The native body already reports falls to vanilla (`handleFall`), as a client's movement packets would, so native players are hurt by falls as any player is.

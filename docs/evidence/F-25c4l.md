## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player killed by a mob goes back for what it dropped once no hostile mob has its drops in sight, within the five minutes they lie there. Before, a mob kill was never gone back for, in case the mob still stood over the drops. Meanwhile neither the recovery, the pickup of loose items nor the pickup of food goes near drops a hostile mob can see.

Found in the live runs on `fb02bc3` and `10a60d5` (seeds 8675309 and 20261030, strategist off): all fourteen deaths were to mobs (skeletons, zombies, a creeper, a slime, a drowned). Each left the player's whole kit on the ground for good, apart from the slime's: a slime is not a `HostileEntity`, so Sol went back for that kit, but its walk back was blocked.

Found making the fixture: the test's ground at Y=-60 lies among caves, with two dozen hostile mobs within twelve blocks of the drops behind the rock. So a mob counts only if it can see a drop, as the one that killed the player would.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign`:
  - `died`: the drop site is kept after a mob kill, marked as guarded;
  - `recoverDrops`: no recovery while `watched`;
  - `watched`: a hostile mob within sixteen blocks that can see a drop within twelve blocks of the site;
  - `pickUpLoose` and `hunt`'s food pickup: they skip watched drops.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- Only a mob that can see the drops counts. A creeper round a corner by them does not.
- The way back is an ordinary walk. A death deep below spawn is still too far, or its route blocked, as Sol's was.
- At nightfall the night's shelter comes first. Drops lie five minutes, less than a night, so a death at night is not gone back for.
- This picks up a lost kit; it does not make the deaths fewer.

## Scope and implementation

The Manhunt mode (M-02) as a match on the framework of M-00a, with the presentation of M-00b and the runner of M-03a.

**A manhunt.** `/aiplayer match manhunt <runner...> vs <hunter...>` proposes one for two to eight players, AI players or humans online.
- **Joining.** An AI player is entered by whoever administers it. A human is invited, with a **[Join]** that names its side and says that once it joins, the other side may attack it. It is in the hunt only once it joins itself (D7).
- **A fresh start for everyone.** Every player starts with nothing carried. It needs the server's PvP on. An AI player in it needs the server's leave for AI world edits, and an AI runner the dragon fight's too.
- **Who may strike whom.** A runner and a hunter may strike one another; two on the same side never may. Outsiders are never struck, as in every match.
- **How it ends.** A runner who falls is out; with no runner left, the hunters win ("hunted"). A runner who frees the End wins it for every runner. A side that gives up (forfeits or leaves) loses. Hunters only respawn when they fall.

**The tracking compass (D6).** The one item a manhunt hands out, by a rule announced to every player at its start and the same for human and AI hunters.
- **Where it points.** Every hunter holds one while the hunt runs (`HuntCompass`). Once a second it is set to the nearest runner in the hunter's world, or failing one, the nearest place a runner last left that world. With neither, it spins.
- **Vanishing and taking back.** It carries the curse of vanishing, so a hunter's death takes it rather than leaving it to be picked up. A hunter with none (new, or on its feet again) is handed one. Each is taken back from a player out of the hunt, and every one when it ends or the server stops.
- **What an AI hunter reads.** Only the needle's bearing, as a player reads a compass, never the runner's place.

**An AI runner** plays the dragon goal at speedrun pace, as in a race (M-03a). Its own pace and goal come back once it is out.

**An AI hunter.**
- **Its kit first.** On the run's own route (the portal goal), which earns its tools and mines iron, it makes its kit from the iron it has: an iron sword and a shield, crafted at a table of its own. These are two wants in its follow-up queue (`Need.IRON_SWORD` is new, with its recipe and sources in the guide), taken ahead of the route's own uses of iron and wood.
- **Then the hunt.** A runner it can see within 24 blocks is fought, with the weapon that deals the most. Otherwise it follows its compass's needle, sixteen blocks at a time, as far as a route goes.
- **Afterwards.** Its own pace and goal come back once it is out.

What a hunt shows its players (M-00b):
- A boss bar: "Manhunt: Rue vs Hal" and the time since the start.
- The start: "Run!" to a runner, "Hunt!" to a hunter.
- The result: Victory to the winning side, with how it ended ("The hunters caught Rue").

The start title is now each mode's own: "Fight!" for a duel, "Go!" for a race.

## 2026-11-05: hunts in play, and a hunter that makes its way to a runner

Agent / branch: Claude / claude/modes-1105-2 and -3. Live runs on a dev server with unmodified vanilla 1.21.1 clients ([tooling](../../tools/ordinary-client/README.md)); the operator worked over RCON.

**A hunter makes its way to a runner it cannot walk to.** Two loops were found in live hunts:
- A hunter in a hollow below a runner started the same fight every fifteen seconds and never reached it (50 times).
- A hunter in a cave walked at rock along its compass's needle 9,435 times in fifteen minutes.

Now:
- **After a stalled fight.** If a fight broke off or lost its target, the hunter makes its way to where it last saw the runner, for thirty seconds after. If the runner is two or more blocks up, it walks to the foot and pillars up with its own filler blocks. Otherwise it uses the run's route builder (bridges, stairs, digging).
- **After a failed walk along the needle.** It makes a way along the needle instead. With no way to make, it gets on with its goal for a while rather than trying again at once.

# F-22c5 — The GameTest server gives a durable write the tick a live server's would

Date: 2026-11-05
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/ci-green-1105 (CI fully green, F-22)
Build: on top of `6effd66`; recorded in [validation](../validation.md)
Status: complete (native test infrastructure)

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, systemic, in the test server's pacing:
- **Writes take wall-clock time.** A player's skill acts only once its paid slice and its action record are durably
  written (F-05a, F-11a), each through its runtime's one ordered metadata writer.
- **The test server is unpaced.** `GameTestPacing` gave each tick 5 ms: game time ran about ten times faster than on a
  live server's 50 ms tick.
- **So writes cost ticks.** A write the length of a live tick, harmless there, cost ten ticks here. On a CI runner with
  a slow disk, every fixture whose claim is counted in ticks raced the disk. That is why the slowest builds failed the
  most (the brief: 9 failures on the 59-minute build, 1 or 2 on the 45-minute ones).

Seen in the CI logs (job logs of the runs named):
- **run 388:**
  - the clutch began after the landing ("Unhurt by a sixteen block drop: 7.0"; the goto cancelled "Falling", then the
    clutch completed 0.2 s later with `poured=false, fallen=0.0`);
  - the pillar from a hoglin was "CANCELLED: The hoglin is on it" before it began;
  - the edge fight spent 43 ticks with its back to the lava against 4 in passing runs.
- **run 380:** the stair stood still 32 ticks on its step waiting on the next step's records ("No stop between steps",
  `NativeStairAction.hold`). Every other CI run logs 0 ticks for that fixture and 1 for the walled one.
- **run 391:** the hideout's 800 recorded blocks used its 40,000 ticks. Passing CI runs used 2:44 to 3:22 of wall time
  against a limit that is 3:20 at 5 ms a tick.

Batching was looked at and is not the cause:
- **How vanilla 1.21.1 batches:** `Batches.createBatches` groups tests by `batchId` and splits each group into batches
  of 50. `TestRunContext` runs one batch at a time; the tests within a batch run together.
- **This suite:** 287 batches. 271 of them hold one test, and the 201 tests without a `batchId` make up `defaultBatch`'s
  five batches of up to 50.
- **Where the failures are:** almost every flaky fixture above has a batch of its own, so it runs alone on the server.
  Concurrency within a batch is not what fails them.

Changed files:
- `companion-persistence` `SqliteCompanionStore.workInFlight()`: every open store's queued and running work, counted in
  `submit`.
- gametest `GameTestPacing`: a tick lasts at least 5 ms, as before, and on until every store's queued work is done, up
  to a live tick's 50 ms. A write up to a live tick's length now costs the player at most the tick it was issued in,
  as on a live server. Every test, assertion and tick limit is unchanged.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- Only the metadata store's work is waited on. A player's native save (`NativePlayerSaves`), its memory files and the
  model calls are not; none of the failures above came from them.
- A store slower than 50 ms an operation still costs ticks, as on a live server.

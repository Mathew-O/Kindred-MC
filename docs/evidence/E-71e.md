## E-71e1 — A block asked for as cover is swapped to the hotbar before it is placed

**User-visible behaviour:** a player that is outmatched and taking cover gets the block it asked for into its hand,
and seals itself in.
- Before: the one-tick swap of a block from its pack to the hotbar was cut off "A hostile mob is attacking; fight back
  first". It asked again on the next look and was cut off again, so it fought on with no cover until killed.

**Found:** in `factions-e40a16seeds-b7b4bec-707-kindred-21883`, failing alone on main 2 of 3. Its walks off were
refused, and its plan was to take cover ("Hole up: seal …").
- The swap's label was "Ready a block", which the interrupt does not exempt. It was cut off 25 to 35 times a run.
- It never had a block to hand, and a zombie killed it.

**Cause (product), in `PortalRunCampaign.placeBlock`:** a block outside the hotbar was readied under the label
"Ready a block". The fight-back interrupt exempts the labels of taking cover ("Back off", "Run from", "Take cover",
"Hole up", "Hold the passage"), but this swap did not carry its step's label.

**The change:** the swap now carries its step's label: `label + ": ready a block"`. So a swap readying the block to
"Hole up" or "Take cover" is exempt as its step is, and one for any other step is not.

**Nothing is granted:** the block is its own, moved from its pack to its hotbar.

**Changed files:**
- `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign.placeBlock`: the swap's label, as above.
- Test `NativeMobRhythmGameTests` (new, registered after `NativeCombatGameTests`):
  - `aZombieIsKilledWithoutLandingABlowInEightOfTenFights`, the no-worse check above (batch `mobRhythm`).
  - `outmatchedTheBlockItAsksForCoverIsTakenToHand` (its own batch `mobRhythmCoverBlock`, the WIDE template). Staged
    as kindred-21883's scene: a small closed room, 11 health, two zombies and a spider after it, a stone sword in its
    hotbar, and its only blocks, cobblestone, in slot 10. It fails once a block asked for is still not in its hotbar
    20 ticks on.
  - Under 6 health no threat is counted, so the staging needed 11 health and three hunters to reproduce the scene.

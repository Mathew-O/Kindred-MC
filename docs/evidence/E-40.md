### Fixes so far

- **F-25c4az** (505's swims): walks that keep ending in deep water set their place aside; a sighting there let go.
- **F-25c4ba** (31102026's sticks): a spare stone pickaxe is not made from the sticks the iron pickaxe needs.
- **F-26a** (the branch mines): the pace, more than the mine. A step of a branch mine took about 100 ticks in
  the evaluation, against 42 for the same mine off a sprint, and at that pace the third leg of a cell column outlasts
  the watchdog's 3,600 ticks. The engine sprints its servers (`/tick sprint`). Vanilla then runs a task handed back
  from another thread only once it is more than three ticks old, and every task waited on two to four of them. Work
  handed back now runs as the next tick starts. All evaluations before F-26a measured the players at the slower pace.
- **F-25c4bb** (505's cavern rim): the way over a drop of two or more is not dug into; it climbs away.
- **F-25c4bc** (20261030's seal): with no pickaxe, cobbled deepslate is punched by hand, so its own night seal of it no
  longer shuts it in.
- **F-25c4bd** (31102026's sand, 101's water): with the block over its head not to be dug, for sand on it or water
  beside it, it gets out from the cell beside it and climbs.
- **F-25c4be** (20261030's lava island, iteration 3): under the hole it came down through, with nothing to climb onto,
  it pillars back up into it.
- **F-25c4bf** (303's aquifer ledge, iteration 4): on a ledge below a wall it may not climb for the water behind it,
  it pillars up beside the wall and steps onto its top.
- **F-25c4bg** (the water loop's usual start, iteration 4): the block a lake's bed of sand or gravel rests on is not dug
  out, which let the bed and the water down onto the climbing player on 101, 202, 404 and 606.
- **F-25c4bh** (505's portal, iteration 4): its frame cast and its flint found away from it, it walks back to the
  frame's stand to light the portal, rather than try from where it made its flint and steel.
- Not reproduced: 505's stone search in iteration 5 (Sol, 29 spells, on the side of a cavern in an aquifer's thin wall).
  The seed's fresh terrain with Sol's 17 dug cells opened, and again with the aquifer's water let in through them, gave
  the player its stone in under 1,000 ticks. The run's own ground had changed in ways its console does not record.
  `run.py --keep-world` now keeps each run's region files, so the next such place can be staged from its own ground.
- Not made: a stop for water coming down a flooded stair (F-25c4ay). A staged stair from 202's own terrain got out of
  the water in under 90 ticks on main, so nothing showed the stop was needed.
- Not changed: the watchdog (its 3,600 ticks and what counts as headway), and the branch mine's legs.

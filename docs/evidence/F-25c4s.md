## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player going back for what it dropped at death walks to the nearest drop. If the walk ends short of it, refused or stopped at the nearest point it could get to, it passes that drop over and goes for the next nearest. It tries three drops at most. Before, the first walk that ended short gave up all the drops.

Found in the live pair on `03faab9` (seed 8675309, strategist off):
- Sol died three times in one cave at Y=36 to 39, to a creeper, a spider and a skeleton. Each time it went back from spawn, 25 to 30 blocks above.
- Each walk back got most of the way down into the cave. It then stopped eight to fifteen blocks short of the one drop it went for ("Route to 12, 38, 84 is blocked"), and the rest of the kit was given up with it.
- A kit scatters over a few blocks as it falls. One drop may lie where no walk reaches, in a hole or on a ledge.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign`:
  - `taskFinished`: a walk back that ends by its drop gets one more look round, as before. One that ends short of it passes that drop over, or, at the third, gives the drops up;
  - `recoverDrops`: drops passed over are left out;
  - `died`: a new death starts with none passed over.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

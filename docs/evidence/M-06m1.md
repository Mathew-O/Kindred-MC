## The cause (code read, main `513366c4`)

**The decision clock:**
- **When a choice is recorded:** only where `play.move == null` and 600 ticks have passed since `decidedAt` (`PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates`). Both ends use `server.getTicks()`, so no clock skew or skipped tick makes a recorded gap under 600.
- **What a 595 gap means:** `decidedAt` was set back.

**The path, one step at a time:**
1. **Set aside as water:** `FactionsPlayer.openWater` sets a place's count to at least `SET_ASIDE` (3), its wait 800 ticks.
2. **Chosen again:** the places set aside at 388582 and 389182 were choosable again at 390382, their waits over (`factionChoose` leaves out only `setAside(tick)`).
3. **Not seen as water:** asked from the cave, out of sight of the water, the place was not set aside as open water. F-35j: only what it saw counts.
4. **Refused once:** that took its count from 3 to 4 (`refused`), which put it straight back in `setAside(tick)`.
5. **Weighed again at once:** the live-move set-aside branch sets `decidedAt = MIN`, "chosen without", for any place it found no way to (F-35i).

**So:** a place it had set aside as water, set aside again by a refusal, was weighed again at once. F-35j1's acceptance has the next choice after a place set aside as water come at the decision interval, and only the `openWater` branch kept to that.

**Why the walk waited 590 ticks:**
- **The pause:** the water set-asides counted toward F-35i's run, whose wait was doubled to 390982.
- **The early release:** the walk was asked at 390972, 10 ticks early. That fits F-35i2's came-up release as Ada climbed out of the water. The fixture shows that release (below).
- **Had it waited the pause out:** the gap would have been 605 or more, and the test would have passed.

## The change

**The record:** `FactionsPlayer.Refusals` keeps whether the place was ever set aside as open water it saw (`water`).
- `openWater` sets it.
- `refused` carries it forward from the record before.
- `setAsideAsWater(target)` reads it.

**The branch:** in `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates`, the live-move set-aside branch sets `decidedAt` to:
- **the tick (the next choice at the decision interval):** for such a place;
- **`MIN` (at once):** for any other, a dry place set aside after its third refusal, as F-35i has it.

**Why this form, not a refusal's reason** (the lead's choice between the two):
- It keeps to the player's own perception, the water it saw.
- A navigator's check of the goal block ("the goal standable false, on water") is not something the player saw, and reading it would mean matching on a failure's text.

**Unchanged:**
- the paced test and every other check;
- the open-water look;
- the refusal back-off;
- the pause and its releases.

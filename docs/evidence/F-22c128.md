## Found

`PortalRunCampaign.castPortal` waits on a frame cell, and on the cell over it, while either holds any fluid. That wait has no bound, and it does not depend on where the player stands. Water a cast leaves behind drains in a moment. A source, or a flow from elsewhere, never drains. `portalSite` survives "try elsewhere", so the next call made the same wait again.

## The change (adapter `PortalRunCampaign`, `castPortal`)

- **The wait is bounded.** The wait on one cell is timed from its first tick, for `DRAIN_WAIT` (200 game ticks, 10 seconds). A gap of `DRAIN_GAP` (60 ticks) in the waiting starts the timer over. Once a cell's bound has passed and been told, that cell is not waited on again.
- **At the bound, it says so,** once per cell: the log line "<name> cast: the water at … has not drained in 10 seconds; filling it in to dig it out", and the same as an event for the strategist.
- **Then it sets that pour aside.** It puts a block from its own stock into the water, under the label "Cast: block the standing water at …". This goes through `within` and `placeBlock`, so reach, `mayWork` and the faction guard apply as for any placement. The cell then holds a block rather than fluid, so the existing clearing digs it out ("Cast: clear …") and the cast follows.
- **If no block can be put there,** for lack of stock or of a placement, it logs one line: "<name> gives up the frame site at …: the water at … does not drain and no block can be put in it from here". It then gives the site up the way `clearOrApproach` gives up a site whose cell it cannot clear: the site goes into `abandonedSites`, `portalSite` is nulled, and the plan chooses again.
- **No longer waits without bound:** no path through the change.
- **Left alone:** relocation's handling of the site, and every other step of the cast.

The new label does not begin "Cast: fill", so it does not touch the failed-stand bookkeeping kept for the bucket fill.

Nothing is granted: the block comes from the player's inventory, and the cell is dug with its own pickaxe. No check, bound or timeout is loosened.

**Dropped during review:** a branch that gathered a block of stone close by when none was in stock. `PortalRunPlanner.castStep` gives the cast only "once the frame's water and mould blocks are in hand", so with its stock gone the plan leaves the cast to get more before `castPortal` runs again, and the branch could not be reached. The lead agreed it should go.

## The tests (native, staged)

Both are in `NativeCastFloorGameTests`, staged in `FixtureGround.FIELD`:

- **The ground:** a block of stone with the frame along north and south, facing east. Its bottom corners and three of its ten obsidian are set, and the rows in front and behind are open and lit. The frame is taken as the player's own with `portalSiteForTest`.
- **The player:** on the frame's floor at its stand, with the cast route's kit (cobblestone among it) and its lava refilled.
- **The water:** a source in the next cell to cast.
- **Held:** daylight.
- **Both fail** once the player has waited on the water more than 300 ticks.

The two tests:
- **A,** `aFrameCellsWaterThatDoesNotDrainIsFilledInAndTheCellCast` (batch `castDrain`). It passes once that cell is obsidian within 1,200 ticks, and fails at once if the player gives the site up.
- **B,** `aFrameCellsWaterThatDoesNotDrainWithNoBlockToBeHadIsNotWaitedOnForEver` (batch `castDrainBare`). As the wait begins, the player's cobblestone is taken from it and the stone of the staged block is turned to bedrock. It passes at 1,200 ticks, or at once if the cell is cast or the site given up.

**B is a guard, not a before-and-after.** It passes on main too, because the planner leaves the cast first, for "Mine stone" and then "Explore" and "Tunnel for stone". It pins the lead's case, nothing to fill the water with: it must not end in an endless wait.

**The give-up branch is not reached by any native test.** A frame cell always has the cell below it as a support face, so a block can always be placed there short of a faction guard. The branch reuses `clearOrApproach`'s own give-up (`abandonedSites`, `portalSite` nulled), which is already exercised. It is covered by review, at the lead's word.

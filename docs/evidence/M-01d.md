## Scope and implementation

The owner's note (2026-10-23): farms and other builds use working redstone mechanics.

**The design** (`Machines.smelter`, core, new; the generator `smelter`). A furnace fed by hoppers: an input chest on top, its hopper pointing down into the furnace's top; a fuel chest to its east over a hopper pointing west into its side; a hopper under it pointing south into an output chest at the ground in front. Seven blocks; its bill a furnace, three chests and three hoppers.

**Placing a hopper** (`Placement`, the `HOPPER` rule). A hopper faces the block whose face was clicked: set against the block it feeds, on that block's face toward it. The planner's supports-first order places what each hopper feeds before it, and the body sneaks for every placement, so clicking a chest or a furnace places against it rather than opening it. `BlockSpec` counts a hopper's `enabled` with the states derived from redstone power. `BuildNeeds` knows a hopper: five iron ingots and a chest, at a table.

Changed modules/files: `Machines`, `MachinesTest` (new), `Placement` (`HOPPER`), `BlockSpec` (`enabled` derived), `BuildNeeds` (the hopper's recipe), `Generators` (`smelter`), `PlacementTest`; `NativeSmelterGameTests` (new).

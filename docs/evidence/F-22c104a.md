A child of [F-22c104](F-22c104.md), filed for its residual: with F-22c104, a player caught after a back-off that gained
nothing turns and fights, and at health 5 against two zombies it can lose that fight. F-22c104's corpus gate counted
fighting deaths 70 with the change against 59 on base (p = 0.19).

## The fixture (STAGED)

`NativeBackOffFlipGameTests.caughtByTwoAtFiveItLivesRatherThanLoseTheFight` (batch `backOffFlipCaughtTwo`) is staged as
F-22c104's caught test is, with a second zombie:
- the player hurt to five, food in hand, an iron sword on the hotbar, a shield and leather armour on, no blocks;
- the first zombie held still until the player backs off again, then set behind it, as in F-22c104's fixture;
- the second held still too, then set 1.5 blocks to the side of the first, and both let go.

It ends when the player is killed, both zombies are dead, or 1200 ticks pass. The check: it lived.

## The variant tried

In `PortalRunCampaign.defend`: below six health, with a shield and two zombies on foot within four blocks, the fight is
held as a turtle for 100 ticks: the shield kept up except for each full swing (`native_fight`'s "turtle" style), labelled
"… behind the shield".

## Results

The read was stated before the runs: the variant lives more often than main, by the lead's ship rule (one-sided Fisher
p < 0.10 at 12 against 12; not worse on the CI second-zombie test).

| Side | Copies | Lived | The step at each death |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The turtle variant | 12 | 5 | All 7 "Fight zombie behind the shield" |
| Main `2c51fcb1` | 12 | 9 | All 3 "Fight zombie" |

The variant is worse (one-sided p ≈ 0.98 for "more lived"), so it is not shipped. The rule's next step, ×24 on the
better side, would be main, so it was not run.

Main lived 2 of 6 in an earlier batch and 9 of 12 here: 11 of 18 in all. The fixture reproduces the loss about 39% of the
time, so it is not fit for CI as it stands.

The deaths were not split by where the killing hit came from. With the two zombies about 90° apart, hits from the shield's
open side may be the variant's expected cost, not a bug; this was not measured.

The fixture and the variant are kept on `claude/ci-green-1122-twoatfive` (commit `22991220`), in no PR. Nothing is
granted and no test or bound is changed on main.

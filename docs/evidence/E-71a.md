## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior, per [the design](../proposals/veteran-mechanics.md#e-71a-timed-crouch-bridging-the-owners-requirement)
and the owner's direction of 2026-11-12 ("when bridging it should use the timed crouch method"):
- **The move.** Its back to the gap, the head down at the edge of the block it stands on (pitch 80), the player walks
  backward upright. It crouches only as its feet reach the edge: the tick before the next step would take its box off
  the block. Vanilla's own edge rule then holds it there. The moment its look meets the side of that block, it places
  the next one, by a raycast along its own view. Then it stands up and walks back onto the new block. The head stays
  down the whole run.
- **When it holds the crouch instead.** With `crouch=auto`, the default, the choice is made every tick from what the
  player itself perceives. It holds the crouch the whole way:
  - over lava under the next cell, whatever it holds: water does not save a fall into lava;
  - over a drop it would not survive, or no ground within 24 blocks (unseen counts as a drop), unless it has a water
    bucket in its hotbar;
  - with a hostile in sight within 8 blocks;
  - for 40 ticks after a hit, or after an arrow in flight toward it.
- **Refused part way.** A refused block ends the run: a claim, the policy, or the blocks run out. The player walks back
  crouched to the edge, comes to a stand, and steps back onto its last block before the sneak is let go. Vanilla's edge
  rule stops the step but not the velocity, so letting go at the edge with speed left would slide it off; the fixture
  caught exactly that. The run reports `reason`, `stopped_at`, `at_edge` and `crouched`, and the cells placed.
- **Where it is used.** The route's bridge steps: `buildRoute` takes a straight run of them as one `native_bridge`, beside
  the staircase's `stairs()`. That covers the portal goal's made ways, F-35k's "make a way elsewhere" and F-35h's faction
  walk, all through `buildRoute` (agreed with MODES, 2026-11-12). A failed run sets aside the cell it stopped at, for a
  claim, the policy, no blocks, no way made or a fall, so the same run is not started again from where it landed. A run
  a hit stopped sets nothing aside, as the danger passes. A report it cannot read sets nothing aside.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter:
  - `NativeBridgeAction` (new);
  - `NativePlayerBody`: the held sneak as a movement input (`crouchWalk`), slowing the walk to three tenths as a client
    does; `move`, `walk` and every halt let it go, and `placeBlock` restores it after the click;
  - `NativePlayerSkills` (`native_bridge`);
  - `PlayerRuntime`: the task, its intents, the post-edit refresh, the campaign's `bridgeEnded`, the measure, and two
    fixture accessors;
  - `PortalRunCampaign` (`bridges`, `bridgeEnded`, the set-aside);
  - `RunObserver` and `EvalBootstrap` (`bridged`).
- companion-core:
  - `RoutePlanner.BRIDGE_COST` 6 → 5 (below);
  - `EvalRecord.bridged`: `bridgeBlocks` and `bridgeSeconds` per player in `eval-result.json`.
- tools: `eval/score.py` (bridge blocks a second, per player and for the team) and `eval/test_eval.py`.
- gametest: `NativeBridgeGameTests` (new), registered in `fabric.mod.json`.

Interface (agreed with MODES):
- **Arguments:** `native_bridge x,y,z dir n slot [crouch=auto|timed|held]`.
  - `x,y,z`: the feet at the start.
  - `dir`: one horizontal direction.
  - `n`: 1 to 16 blocks.
  - `slot`: a hotbar slot.
- **The crouch choice.** The caller passes `crouch` from a mode's rules, so the skill stays mode-blind. No mode sets it
  yet.
- **On a stop short.** BLOCKED with `reason`: one of `claim`, `policy`, `no_blocks`, `hit`, `fell`, `stuck` or
  `unverified`. It also gives `stopped_at`, `at_edge` and `crouched`.
- **The cells placed.** A run is straight, so its placed cells, in order, are `placed_first` along `dir` to
  `placed_last`, `blocks` of them. A checkpoint's entries are at most 256 characters, too short to list sixteen cells.
- **Unverified.** An outcome it cannot verify blocks for reconciliation and is never replayed.

Schema/configuration changes: none. No saved key, NBT or SQL changed.

## From MODES' review of #263

Three points, all taken, in `daa4b23`:
1. **A water bucket no longer excuses lava.** The design reads "over lava or the void without a bucket", but water does
   not save a fall into lava: a clutch onto lava makes obsidian at best, and the player lands in fire. So `danger()`
   holds the crouch over lava whatever the hotbar holds. A bucket still excuses a lethal drop or no ground in sight.
   This departs from the design's wording; the fixture above shows it.
2. **`bridgeEnded` reads `stopped_at` guarded.** It runs on the server thread, so a report it cannot parse now sets
   nothing aside instead of throwing.
3. **A fall sets its cell aside.** `fell` joins `claim`, `policy`, `no_blocks` and `stuck`, so the next plan does not
   start the same run again from where it landed. `hit` still sets nothing aside.

The six fixtures, `NativeBridgeGameTests`, pass 3 of 3 on `daa4b23`.

## The corpus gate

`tools/corpus/gate.sh 3` on `848b9dc`, with main `38bb509` merged. Both it and its confirm ran on the harness with #241's
spacers.
- **Flagged:** 5 scenes.
- **Cleared:** 4, by the confirm.
- **Confirmed:** 1, `e40a20-79a9aad-31102026-echo-106260`. The branch failed it 2 of 3; main passed it 3 of 3 in the confirm.

The confirmed scene, run alone 6 times on each of three trees, alternating. Every failure was "still in its stuck spell
after 3600 ticks", and no `native_bridge` ran in any of the 18 runs.

| Tree | Failed |
| --- | --- |
| main `38bb509` | 5 of 6 |
| the branch `848b9dc` | 5 of 6 |
| the branch with `BRIDGE_COST` back at 6 | 4 of 6 |

It clears on these grounds:
1. **Main fails it alone.** Main's 3 of 3 came from the gate's five-scene confirm; alone, main passed it 1 of 6.
2. **The branch is not worse at p < 0.10.** Over the confirm and the runs alone, the branch failed 7 of 9 and main 5
   of 9: one-sided Fisher p ≈ 0.31.
3. **Not the cost.** The branch failed it as often at `BRIDGE_COST` 6 as at 5, so the cost is left at 5. Its value was
   not chosen by this scene.

What a bridge did in it:
- In the gate's two failing branch runs, the player's "make a way elsewhere" ran `native_bridge` of one block four times.
  Each placed its block and was verified.
- The player then alternated between "walk on" and "dig toward elsewhere: advance". The advance went toward a cell
  standing on the replay box's barrier, which is refused, until the replay ended.
- Main's passing confirm took stairs up instead.
- The branch's failures alone ran no bridge and stuck in the same way.

So the bridge is one way into a stuck spell the scene has without it, not its cause.

**How it reads in an unbounded world.** The escape target lies 8 blocks past the barrier. Without the box, the eastward
advance is over ordinary ground, and the walk-on and advance would not alternate. The barrier is what refuses the
advance and turns the alternation into a livelock.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- **CI** on the PR's final commit is still to run.
- **Other callers.** Only the route's bridge steps use `native_bridge`. These still bridge their old way: the rescue
  `bridge` verb, bridges as builds (E-62b), the End's islands and towers.
- **No mode chooses the crouch.** Nothing passes `crouch` from a mode's rules yet: a race's timed crouch over a deadly
  drop (the design's speedrun and manhunt runner) is for MODES to pass when wanted.
- **Hearing** (E-71f) is not in its perception. A mob it cannot see does not hold the crouch unless it hits.
- **The kit** (E-71h): a run is capped by the blocks in one hotbar slot.

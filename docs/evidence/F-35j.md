## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: in a faction world, an AI player whose faction play chooses a place out on open water it has
seen no longer asks for a walk there. Before, it asked, the walk was refused, and it asked again three times before
setting the place aside, then again once the wait was over. Now the place is set aside at once, with no walk asked and
no way made toward it, and looked at again after the same wait, twice as long each time. Water it has not seen is not
known as water: there the walk is asked as before, and only what it finds sets the place aside.

"Open water it saw":
- A walk's end within its sight (32 blocks): every column within two blocks of the end is one where it saw still water
  open to the air.
- A walk's end beyond its sight (a leg of 48 toward a far chunk): the last 10 columns along the straight line toward it,
  out to the edge of its sight, are such columns. Land it saw beyond a strait breaks the run, and so does water past what
  it saw, so a crossing to land in sight is still asked.

Only the player's own perception is used: `WorldKnowledge` remembers still water open to the air when a line from its
eyes reaches it, and now also remembers the columns of that water, the last 16,384, past its cap of 2,000 blocks of a
kind (a sea fills that cap before it has looked round). Nothing is read from the world for the test.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `WorldKnowledge` (`waterColumns`, `seenOpenWaterToward`); `FactionsPlayer` (`openWater`,
  `overWater`, `walksEnded`, `OPEN_WATER` = 10, `dryAt`); `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates` (the check, after the wait
  as a whole and before any way is made; not for a walk home), `seenOpenWater`, `observeRunner` (faction walks that
  ended counted), test hooks `factionOverWaterForTest`, `sweepsForTest`.
- test: `WorldKnowledgeOpenWaterTest` (new).
- gametest: `NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests` (new, two fixtures), its `fabric.mod.json` line.

A set-aside as open water is not a refusal: no route was searched, and F-35i's run of refusals is not counted on.

## Why

Factions seed 303 (`diag-factions303`, read by A): faction legs 62 completed against 494 refused for no way. RUNNER 2's
trace and the offline terrain (generated with the pinned server, for diagnosis only): the players' land is an island
(a flood fill with the walk's rules from 408, 64, 210 reaches 395 cells, fenced mostly by water); the refused legs went
east, e.g. from 408, 64, 210 toward 457, 63, 205–245, open sea from the surface at 62 down for more than 85 blocks.

A faction walk's end over water is at the player's own height (no ground within eight blocks of it), in the air over
the sea, and the navigator refuses it ("No walkable route"); it is not a swim. The fixture shows the same refusal.

Measuring choices, recorded:
- A first version tested only the walk's end, within two blocks. In the fixture's first staging, the island level with
  the sea, a line of sight from its eyes 1.6 above the water met the surface short of anything past 17 to 19 blocks, so
  the end 15 to 19 off was never seen. 303's legs end 48 off, past its sight of 32. Hence the second rule, toward the
  edge of its sight, and the island staged two above the sea, as 303's is.
- Before that, the columns came from the blocks it remembers, capped at 2,000 of a kind: the cap filled before it had
  looked round. Hence the column memory.
- The staging lets it look round first (its goal started before the factions are founded, so that its perception runs);
  a walk asked before it has seen the water is asked as anywhere it has not, which is the rule.

Not run yet: the paired Factions seeds (303, 109, 110) with the faction-leg completed/blocked ratio before and after; the
corpus gate; a live run.

## Known gaps

- A column seen as water is remembered as water until the player changes world; a sea it later sees bridged or filled is
  not forgotten. The set-aside is time-limited (as a refusal's), so the place is looked at again.
- A large lake with land beyond out of sight counts as open water past the edge of its sight. The lasting fix is
  terrain-aware ranking in `FactionPlay`'s choice from immutable observations, after 2026-11-17.

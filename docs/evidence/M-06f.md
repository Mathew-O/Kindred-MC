## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/faction admin hub` (operators): spawn's chunks within `safeRadius` (64) of (0, 0) made the
`hub` safe zone, the border set, and the hub built at spawn from the builder's generators (a lighthouse and a
twelve-stall trading hall as the market district); `/faction admin hub zone` does the same without building. In a safe
zone nobody's hit lands on a player (melee, arrows, anything a player's hit is) and the striker is told why; nobody
claims, captures or changes a block but an operator. Borders: the overworld `border` (8,000) across round (0, 0) with a
20-block warning, the Nether an eighth (1,000), the End the same as the overworld, re-applied once a second. A Nether
portal that would lead beyond the border takes nobody anywhere and the traveller is told.
Changed modules/files: companion-core `factions/SafeZones` (ledger and the hub's plan), `factions/FactionSettings`
(`safeRadius`), `record/Ledgers` (safe zones); companion-server-adapter `FactionsService` (zones, `strikeRefusal`,
`applyBorders`, `leavesBorder`, `tell`), `TerritoryGuard` (`strikeRefusal`, `portalRefused`), `PortalBorderMixin`,
`PlayerRuntime.factionsHub`, `FactionCommands` (`admin hub`), `KindredMod` (`ALLOW_DAMAGE`).
Schema: none; `safe.zone` events.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/faction admin hub` (operators): spawn's chunks within `safeRadius` (64) of (0, 0) made the
`hub` safe zone, the border set, and the hub built at spawn from the builder's generators (a lighthouse and a
twelve-stall trading hall as the market district); `/faction admin hub zone` does the same without building. In a safe
zone nobody's hit lands on a player (melee, arrows, anything a player's hit is) and the striker is told why; nobody
claims, captures or changes a block but an operator. Borders: the overworld `border` (8,000) across round (0, 0) with a
20-block warning, the Nether an eighth (1,000), the End the same as the overworld, re-applied once a second. A Nether
portal that would lead beyond the border takes nobody anywhere and the traveller is told.
Changed modules/files: companion-core `factions/SafeZones` (ledger and the hub's plan), `factions/FactionSettings`
(`safeRadius`), `record/Ledgers` (safe zones); companion-server-adapter `FactionsService` (zones, `strikeRefusal`,
`applyBorders`, `leavesBorder`, `tell`), `TerritoryGuard` (`strikeRefusal`, `portalRefused`), `PortalBorderMixin`,
`PlayerRuntime.factionsHub`, `FactionCommands` (`admin hub`), `KindredMod` (`ALLOW_DAMAGE`).
Schema: none; `safe.zone` events.

## Free travel and free items (2026-11-10, mod-support decision 6)

`EditPolicy.ServerPolicy` gains `teleport` and `freeItems`, forbidden by default, kept in `edits.properties`
(`teleport=allow|forbid`, `freeItems=allow|forbid`; the edit switch keeps them) and set by an operator with
`/aiplayer teleport|freeitems allow|forbid`; `/aiplayer policy` tells the standing rule. Each change is announced to
every player online in the same words, naming humans and AI players alike (D6), and recorded in the World Record as
`policy.set` (the switch, `allowed`, `by`; the operator as actor). Factions reads `teleport` in `/faction home`: forbidden,
home is told; allowed, a person who asks is taken there. An AI player is only told: no recorded skill travels this way
(one comes to the lead first). `freeItems` is read with no effect: Factions hands out nothing. The eval engine records
both in `eval-result.json` (`policy`); a run with either allowed is staged, `score.py` names it and `compare.py`
refuses it.

Tests: `ServerPolicyTest` (3 JUnit: forbidden by default, the edit switch keeps them, the announcement the same for
everyone); `FreeSwitchTest` in `tools/eval/test_eval.py` (3: teleport allowed is staged and named, both forbidden stays
earned, the comparison refuses free items); `NativeFactionPlayGameTests.freeTravelIsForbiddenByDefaultAndAnAiPlayerIsOnlyToldItsHome`
(forbidden by default; the operator's change in the World Record with who made it; an AI player told its home, not taken
there). The fixture failed 3 of 3 on the first build: the AI player was taken home, as `homeTravel` looked its body up
by the entries' key (the player's record id, not its body's); fixed by `entryForPlayer` and the body's kind
(`PreparedNativePlayer`). Not tested natively: a person's teleport home (a GameTest has no person).

## The safe zones' chunks gathered once (2026-11-11, claude/m06i-safe-chunks-1111)

In a faction world the thinking cost grew with play: diag-factions303 (main `079bdc2`, `mode=factions`, `bases=true`,
alone) climbed from 182 to 3,600 µs a slice by tick 120,000, and diag-factions110 (288,000 ticks) in a sawtooth with a
one-day period, its highs 1,685 to 8,432 slices refused for time a 12,000 ticks; five co-op runs that also lit a portal
stayed at 250 to 680 µs. A JFR profile of 303 run again (`079bdc2`, the same scenario, alone): in the dear window
(ticks 36,000 to 72,000, 1,002 to 2,050 µs a slice) `SafeZones.Book.chunks()` was 5.7% of the server thread's
samples, 0.5% in the cheap one (96,000 to 144,000), at the end of one chain: the goal's water search
(`PortalRunCampaign.fillWater`, `WorldKnowledge.nearest`) asks `mayWork` of every water cell it knows, which asks the
territory guard (`TerritoryGuard.mayChange`, `FactionsService.safe()`), and `chunks()` built a new sorted set of every
zone's chunks on each question. No guard is active outside a faction world, so co-op asked nothing; and the water it
knows grows each day, so each night's search cost more.

Fix, no behaviour change: `SafeZones.Book` (immutable) is a final class with the same API (`zones()`, `chunks()`,
equal by its zones, `EMPTY`), its zones copied in and the chunks gathered once when it is built.

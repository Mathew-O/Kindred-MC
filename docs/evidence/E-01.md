## Scope and implementation

**The ask** (`PlayerRuntime.nativeDeliver`; `/aiplayer bring <count> <item> [target]`). Whoever controls a player asks it to bring them a number of an item, at most a chest's worth; a second ask replaces the first, and `0` calls it off. The receiver is the player who asks.

**The errand** (`PlayerRuntime.deliveryCandidates`). Ahead of its own work (life first still goes before it): with fewer in its pack than it still owes, the rest taken from its assigned storage with the recorded `native_take`, walked to first; then to its asker, and a toss across from within three blocks with them in sight (the recorded `native_toss`), a stack or what is still owed of it at a time. What it has handed over is counted by the tosses it finished, each toss by the task it was planned as, so a stop part way and a resume never hand over more than was asked. With not enough between its pack and its storage it says "I have only 6 iron ingots of the 10 you asked for; bringing those." and brings them; with none it says so and stops; done, "I brought you 10 iron ingots." After four failed steps it gives up and says how many it handed over.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: the player shoots the End crystals off their pillars with its own bow and arrows. `Ballistics` follows vanilla 1.21.1's arrow (velocity applied, then drag 0.99 and gravity 0.05 a tick; three blocks a tick at full draw) and gives the low arc to a target. `native_shoot` draws for a full second, keeps the aim (with lead for a moving target) and looses one owned arrow. Each crystal is shot from where the player stands or from a stand on end stone whose whole arc is clear, 16 to 56 blocks off, not by the island's edge and not within 12 blocks of the exit portal's pillar. It keeps to one crystal until it is down or given up; a stand that misses four times or cannot be reached is dropped for that crystal; a crystal with no stand is left for a minute (1,200 ticks) and tried again, with its count of arrows and its stands starting afresh. A crystal given up on for now does not hold up the fight with the dragon.
Changed modules/files: `Ballistics` (core, new), `EndFightPlanner` (crystal stage only while one can be broken); `NativeShootAction` (new), `NativePlayerBody` (`SHOOT`, draw/aim/loose), `EndFightLeg`, `PlayerRuntime`, `NativePlayerSkills`.

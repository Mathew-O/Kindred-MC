## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. The source is #86's CI, run 42566156809, attempt 1 (job 128993082064),
`NativeLifeFirstGameTests.ledOffFromTheHerdItGoesBackToWhereItSawItAndHuntsFirst`.
- Mason was taking its cabin's scaffold down. Breaking a scaffold block it had walked over to, it fell three blocks off the
  field's edge, to y −56.
- Its next walk, to a spot to break the next scaffold block, was "No walkable route". After that the planner had
  "Scaffolding at … is out of reach to take down" eight times in a row, and the build stopped.
- MODES saw the same message on #78 (run 42556777789, attempt 2), with the builder three blocks above the column's top.

The forcing fixture showed two parts.
- **The hollow.** `BuildRunner` climbs out of a hollow below the build's floor (E-08g) only after a walk "over to" a block
  has failed. In the fixture that climb ran once Mason's walk round to the scaffold failed, and it came back up.
- **The column.** Its take-down cut short by the fall, the scaffold was a free-standing column whose top was four
  blocks over the ground. `ConstructionPlanner` takes scaffolding down only from a spot it can walk to, or from on top of
  the column. No walkable spot was in reach of that top, so "out of reach to take down" held for good.

Changed file: companion-core `ConstructionPlanner`.
- **`scaffoldToBreak`:** a scaffold top out of reach from every walkable spot is reached as a block to place is. A
  scaffold column goes up beside it, from walkable ground within `SCAFFOLD_RADIUS`, just high enough that from its top
  the block is in reach and in sight: "Scaffold N up to take down …".
- **The new column** is the build's own scaffold, taken down from on top of it afterwards as any column is.
- **Unchanged:** a top in reach of a walkable spot, or stood on, is taken down as before.

New fixture: `NativeBuildGameTests.aScaffoldTakenDownFromAHollowBelowTheFloorIsClimbedOutOfAndTakenDown` (batch
`buildHollowTakeDown`).
- It is the E-08g hollow scene with the wall nine high, so that its top block is placed from a scaffold.
- Fixture staging: once its scaffold has started coming down ("Scaffolding down"), the player is put in the pit three
  deep beside the wall, a fall off the column.
- It checks the wall built and its scaffold all taken down.

## Scope and implementation

**User-visible behaviour:** a player whose eyes are in a column of sand or gravel beside it, with nothing over its own head, steps to the middle of its own cell and is out of the wall. Before, it could walk to the cell it already stood in again and again, its eyes still in the column, until it suffocated.

**Found:** E-40's iteration 22 (`e40a22-d2389c9`) had four "suffocated in a wall" deaths. Three of them came this way: 707 Echo, 31102026 Echo and 404 Sol (corpus scenes `e40a22-d2389c9-707-echo-2597`, `e40a22-d2389c9-31102026-echo-30737`, `e40a22-d2389c9-404-sol-50932`). In each:
- the falling column stood in the cell beside the player's feet, not over them;
- the player stood off its cell's middle (707 Echo 0.36 north of it), so the thin slab about its eyes that suffocation tests reached into that cell;
- `digOut` logged "Dig out of the wall: out from under the falling sand to" the player's own cell 60 to 72 times, each walk ending where it began, until the player died.

**Cause (product), in `PortalRunCampaign`:**
1. `digOut` asks `clearOfTheColumn` for a cell clear of the falling column, and walks there with `go(clear, 0.3, …)`.
2. `clearOfTheColumn` returns the player's own feet cell first whenever the column is in another cell and there is room to stand.
3. A walk to the cell it already stands in, to within 0.3, ends at once: the player is not moved, so its eyes stay in the column beside it.
4. The eye slab suffocation tests is 0.8 of the body's width across (0.48). From 0.36 off the middle it reaches 0.6 from it, 0.1 into the next cell.

**The change:**
- **`EYES_CLEAR_WITHIN = 0.15`:** within 0.15 of its cell's middle, the eye slab reaches 0.39 from it, short of every cell beside.
- **`digOut`:** when the cell clear of the column is the player's own, it walks with `native_goto` (`range` 0, `partial` false, `centre_within` 0.15) to that cell's middle: "Dig out of the wall: to the middle of its cell, clear of the falling … beside it". Any other cell is walked to as before.
- **`clearOfTheColumn`:** the player's own cell is offered only while the player is more than 0.15 off its middle on x or z. Centred, a column it is still in is over it, and the search for a cell beside goes on as before.

**Nothing granted.** Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter`: `PortalRunCampaign` (`EYES_CLEAR_WITHIN`, `digOut`, `clearOfTheColumn`).
- Tests: `NativeFallingColumnGameTests.itsEyesInAColumnOfSandBesideItItGetsThemOut` (batch `fallingColumnBeside`), new.

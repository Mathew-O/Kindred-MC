## Scope and implementation

The Speedrun mode (M-03) as a match on the framework of M-00a, so the run the AI players already play (the dragon goal at speedrun pace, F-25c8) becomes a race with a timer and splits.

**A race.** `/aiplayer match speedrun <runner> [runner...]` proposes a race for one to eight runners, AI players or humans online:
- **Joining.** An AI runner is entered by whoever administers it. A human is invited, with a **[Join]** to click, and is in it only once it joins itself. It starts as any match does: three seconds of countdown, then the race.
- **No striking.** In a race nobody may strike another player, whatever the server allows. The match rule allows strikes only in a duel.
- **A fall is only a setback.** A runner who dies respawns and runs on. A runner who forfeits or leaves is out, and the rest run on. Once nobody is left running, the race is over with no winner.
- **The first to finish wins,** and the race is over.

**A fresh start, the same for everyone.** Every runner starts with nothing: an empty inventory, and none of the splits' advancements. A player that carries anything, or has already reached a split, is refused with the reason. An AI runner also needs the server's leave for AI world edits and for the dragon fight, as the dragon goal does. A world fit for a speedrun (a new world, a random seed) is the operator's to make.

**An AI runner.** When the race starts, the runner is set to the dragon goal at speedrun pace (`SPEEDRUN_ANY_PERCENT`), its own pace kept aside. When it is out of the race (the race over, or it dropped out), the race's goal ends and its own pace comes back, a server stop included. Everything it does on the way is the goal's ordinary play; nothing is given.

**Splits from the game itself.** The splits are vanilla advancements, so the game says when a runner got there, the same for humans and AIs: Iron (Acquire Hardware), Nether (We Need to Go Deeper), Blaze rods (Into Fire), Stronghold (Eye Spy), The End (The End?), and Dragon (Free the End), the finish. Once a second each runner's advancements are read. A split is taken the first time it is seen, at the game's own time: ticks since the start, twenty to the second, shown as a timer shows it (`4:12.30`, with hours once past the hour). Native players earn vanilla's advancements as any player does; the suite's logs show Stone Age, Acquire Hardware and We Need to Go Deeper earned in other fixtures.

**Shown as a minigame shows it** (M-00b):
- A boss bar: the runners and the time since the start, filling as the leader reaches each split.
- Each split, to every runner, in chat and over the hotbar ("Ada: Nether at 4:12.30"), and a chime for the runner who reached it.
- The result as a title: Victory for the first to free the End, Defeat for the rest, with who freed it first.

**The fixture's advancements.** A fixture cannot play the run, so it grants the two advancements on the runner's own tracker as vanilla grants them when their criteria are met (`grantCriterion`). This tests that the race reads its splits from the game, times them and finishes on Free the End. It does not test that the runner earned them, and it is no evidence of a run.

Found on the way:
- **A race refused on a PvP server setting.** Starting a match checked the server's PvP, which a race does not need; now only a duel does.
- **A check the fixture could not see.** Its first fresh-start control passed: the race carrying dirt was refused, but because the dragon fight was still off, not for the dirt. The fixture now allows the dragon fight first, and the control fails.
- **A pace given back, then overwritten.** Ending the race's goal went through the goal's start, which turns an idle-observing player to optimal survival. That overwrote the pace just given back. The goal now ends directly.

**Hand controls.** Each reverts one rule, and the fixture fails:

| Control | Result |
| --- | --- |
| The fresh-start check removed | Failed: proposed carrying dirt |
| The finish removed (Free the End does not end the race) | Failed: the race never finishes |
| The pace not given back | Failed: the runner left at speedrun pace |

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a native AI player that dies now settles whatever it was doing, drops its real inventory where it died (vanilla), and respawns as the same identity about a second later at its vanilla spawn point (bed or world spawn). It then carries on with its goal and makes one attempt to pick its dropped items back up while they last. Previously a death halted the player with "explicit respawn/recovery required" and no command could recover it.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-server-adapter/.../NativePlayerBody.java`: `onDeath` hook runs before vanilla drops the inventory; `respawn()` calls `PlayerManager.respawnPlayer` and rebinds the body and network handler to the replacement entity; a replaced entity no longer drives the body.
- `companion-server-adapter/.../mixin/PlayerManagerMixin.java`: `@WrapOperation` on the `ServerPlayerEntity` construction in `respawnPlayer` so a native body's replacement is a native body. Human respawn is untouched.
- `companion-server-adapter/.../PreparedNativePlayer.java`: `kindred$respawned(ServerWorld)`.
- `companion-server-adapter/.../PlayerRuntime.java`: `onNativeDeath` cancels the runner through the shared lease/arbiter barrier, closes the recorded action while the inventory is intact, settles the paid activity, revokes team assignments (`TeamCoordinator.handlePlayerDeath`), and respawns once vanilla would remove the dead body (death time 19). A failed respawn blocks for reconciliation.
- `companion-server-adapter/.../PortalRunCampaign.java`: remembers the death site and tries once to collect its own drops (never in lava or fire).
- `companion-server-adapter/src/gametest/.../NativeRespawnGameTests.java`.

Dependencies satisfied: F-25b2 registration/prepared save, F-05 durable action admission, F-11b activity settlement.
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: none. The native save written after respawn is an ordinary live save (health > 0).
Requirements and AT portions covered: F-25b "death/respawn" for a registered native body. Restart while dead, keepInventory, hardcore and End-exit respawn are not covered.

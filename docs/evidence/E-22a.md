## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
- **When a toss counts:** whatever an AI player tosses to someone through the toss method (an ask in chat, a command, an
  offer's yes) is recorded as given only once that person has picked it up.
  - Before, the ledger noted it as given when it was thrown.
  - The game's own record decides: the recipient's `minecraft:picked_up` statistic for that item, read when it was thrown,
    has risen by the count thrown.
- **On a pickup:**
  - the giver's ledger notes the gift (E-50's "gave");
  - the giver's friendship notes it (E-24a, `GAVE`);
  - an AI recipient's ledger and friendship note it too (`RECEIVED`).
  - The log says `<giver>'s gift of <n> <item> was picked up by <who>`.
- **With no pickup in ninety seconds:**
  - it is noted as an episode ("tossed 2 iron ingot, not picked up") and nothing else;
  - nothing is tossed again in its place, since a second toss could be a copy;
  - a toss left lying is no slight: it changes no friendship.
  - The log says `<giver>'s toss of <n> <item> was not picked up by <who>: noted, nothing tossed again`.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `GiftWatch` (new: at most 64 watched tosses, checked once a second).
  - `PlayerRuntime`: the toss method watches instead of noting at once; `GiftOutcome`; a tick hook.
- gametest: `NativeGiftGameTests` (the iron fixture of E-21a; `aTossNobodyPicksUpIsLeftNotGivenAndNotTossedAgain`).

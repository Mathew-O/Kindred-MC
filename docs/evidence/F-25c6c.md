## The stall: E-40c3 (#271), seed 8675309, Echo
- **Source:** RUNNER 2's kept data, `claude/corpus-data-e40c3-nether-1112` @ 9ae049b; the console, line 25923.
- **Where:** the Nether, at -75 43 -142, on "End: Fortress: fight back - Out of the hoglin's reach: pillar up".
- **What happened, all in the same second (20:07:00):**
  - One `native_pillar` was BLOCKED ("The player is no longer where the pillar was planned").
  - One was COMPLETED.
  - The next was CANCELLED, "The hoglin is on it; fight back".
  - Then: "Native player … blocked: Durable native action requires reconciliation", with `IllegalStateException: Pillar step cancelled with uncertain outcome`, and "no replacement inventory will be created."
- **After that,** Echo has no goal or task line for the rest of the run, some 194,000 ticks.
- **Why:**
  - A goal's stop aborts the running step, and `NativePillarAction.close()` then settles the step's record.
  - It verified only a step that had already ended COMPLETED. It called a step with "no effect" only if nothing was lost and the cell was still open. Anything else was UNCERTAIN.
  - A step cut short after its block went down, but before it landed on it, is neither. So it was UNCERTAIN, even though the world and the inventory show exactly what it did.
  - UNCERTAIN holds the player for reconciliation (`reconciliationBlocked`). That clears only through `/companion recover`, which needs an operator. A run with no human in it has none.

## The fix
- **In `NativePillarAction.close()`:** a step cut short with its block placed is recorded VERIFIED only when the evidence shows it:
  - its own block is in the cell it jumped from;
  - at most one of that block item is gone;
  - nothing else owned is lost (`conserved(after, 1)`, the same check as a completed step);
  - the step had not already been marked unknown.
- **The record's detail** says so: "cut short after placing".
- **Everything else still reads as before.** Nothing placed and nothing lost is "no effect". Anything that cannot be seen to match is UNCERTAIN and held for reconciliation. Nothing is replayed or recreated.

## One rule for the live close and a restart's settle
- The lead and B (F-31g1, the restart settle) agreed the rule should live in one place. It is now `NativePillarAction.settleFromEvidence(Before, InventoryEvidence after, ServerWorld, BlockPos cell, Block block)`, returning `Settled.DONE`, `NOT_DONE` or `HELD`. Both types are new and package-private in `dev.kindred.fabric`.
- **`Before`** carries the stacks while the step is live (`Before.live`), or only the recorded fingerprint after a restart (`Before.recorded`).
- **With the stacks:**
  - DONE when its block is in its cell and at most one of it is gone, nothing else lost;
  - NOT_DONE when nothing is lost and the cell is open;
  - else HELD.
- **With only the fingerprint:** NOT_DONE when the cell is open and the fingerprint is unchanged; else HELD. A block gone cannot be shown from a hash.
- **`close()` calls it** for any step that did not complete. It verifies a DONE only for a step that placed its block and never went unknown, records NOT_DONE as no effect, and holds the rest.
- **No behaviour change from 52fbcc6.** Before the extraction, a step that went unknown could still be "no effect" when nothing was lost and the cell was open. It still can.
- `conserved` is now also a static helper over (before, after, block, allowed).
- B's `NativeBreakAction` follows the same shape, in B's own follow-up.
- **After the extraction:** the fixture passes 3 of 3 again (record VERIFIED, not held, 15 cobblestone), and the 14 pillar fixture classes pass in one run (154 tests).

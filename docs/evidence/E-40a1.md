# E-40a1 — A hand-over to a teammate it cannot get to is let go

Date: 2026-10-30
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/loose-ends-1029
Build: on top of `a4df9b4`; recorded in [validation](../validation.md)
Status: in progress (native passed; live runs pending); E-40a stays open

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player carrying a piece it promised a teammate walks over to hand it across. If a walk ends with the teammate still out of tossing reach, and no nearer than its last walk ended, that is now a failed step. After six failed steps the hand-over is let go, as for walks that are refused, and the player goes back to its work. Before, a walk that could get no closer ended at once as done, and the same walk was chosen again and again. A hand-over step ranks above a goal's step, so the goal did not run meanwhile.

Found in the live pair on `a4df9b4` (seed 8675309, strategist off):
- From 22:00:48 Sol, with an iron sword and boots promised to Echo, walked toward Echo 7,389 times, each walk ending at once ("native_goto COMPLETED", no goal label). That was about twenty minutes of its forty-four: 22:00 to 22:06, 22:20 to 22:23 and 22:26 to 22:36.
- Its goal could not go on, and its watchdog gave up on the spot again and again ("no progress here", 40% of its time).
- Echo did the same 546 times. Neither run on `458d192` had it: the chestplate made first (F-25c4x) put iron in hand sooner, and the share-out's promises with it.

Changed modules/files:
- `PlayerRuntime.handOverStepEnded`: a walk that ends with the teammate out of tossing reach (three blocks and in sight), and no nearer than the last one ended, counts as a failed step. Six failed steps let the hand-over go, as before, and say so in the log.
- `PlayerRuntime.handOverStep`: a teammate beyond the team's range (48 blocks) is waited for a minute at a time, the log saying so, and six such waits let the hand-over go, so the piece's material is not held for good. Found in the live pair on `41f9de9`: Kindred's walk to Echo, beyond one route's reach (160 blocks), was refused as it was made; never run, it never counted as a failed step, and it was proposed again and again for five minutes.
- `NativeTeamShareGameTests`: two fixtures.

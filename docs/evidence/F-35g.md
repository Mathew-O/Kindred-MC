## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a player in its own tunnel under the sea, wanting water for the cast, with an empty bucket,
now fills it from the sea over its head:
- It opens the one block of its ceiling between it and the water.
- It fills its bucket from the source cell behind that block.
- It puts a block of its own back at once.
- It waits for what came into its tunnel to drain.

Before, every step up was refused for the water over it. It went "out from under the water" into the next cell and "to
the wall" back, for good.

Found: E-40's iteration 18 (`e40a18`), Kindred on 606. It had 12 stuck spells there, the run's biggest "hole or pit"
stuck cause. The console lines are on `claude/diag-e40a18-606-1111`, `diag/e40a18-53cd517-606-Kindred-pocket.txt`.
- Its goal step was "Iron: Fill a bucket with water - Climb toward the surface for water".
- It stood at feet Y=56 in its own two-high tunnel under one block of dirt and four of the sea (from 59 to sea level
  62), with open sea over the whole area round 195, 56, -126.
- It went "out from under the water: advance" to 196, 56, -126 and "to the wall" back to 195, both completed, some
  thirty times each a 3,600-tick stuck spell.
- At 196 every step up met the water over its ceiling, so the climb tunnelled one cell out from under it, back to 195.
  At 195 every way was open or set aside, so nothing refused it for the water, and the climb walked over to its
  nearest wall: 196 again. The guard F-25c4cq put in covers only a step aside paired with the wall.

Where the knowledge of the water comes from: the player's own `WorldKnowledge` sightings. Its sweep remembers a water
source only once a sight line from its eyes reaches it (`needsSight`, `visible`), and only one with air beside it
(`exposed`): the sea's surface it saw from the shore or swam over. The rule fires only when such a sighting lies in the
column over its ceiling, at most `OVERHEAD_DEPTH` (8) above it. The seabed and the water under the surface are never
read before the block is opened. Opened, the cell behind it is in plain sight, and only a still water source there is
filled from, as a bucket takes nothing else. In the fixtures the sea is seen the same way: the player stands on the
sea's wall and its campaign's own sweep runs, sight lines and all, before it is set in its tunnel. Nothing is told it
with `remember`.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-server-adapter`: `PortalRunCampaign`.
  - `waterOverhead`, first in the water step (`FILL_WATER`), before `fillWater` and the climb. It opens the block over
    its head (`native_break`) only when all of these hold:
    - It is below ground, on its feet in a dry two-high cell.
    - The block over its head is solid, not sand or gravel, not set aside, and one it may break under the edit policy.
    - No water it knows of is in reach where it stands; that is filled from as before.
    - It saw water in that block's column.
    - It holds an empty bucket, with room for the full one, and a block to reseal with (readied to the hotbar first).
    - Its breath is full.
    - No hostile mob it can see is within six blocks (`COVER_MOB_NEAR`).
  - `overhead`, early in `plan`, before the way up for air, carries it through:
    - It waits for the opened block's drop to come down to it (at most 30 ticks).
    - It fills the bucket from the source (`native_bucket`, aimed as the bucket aims: `NativeBucketAction.fillAim`).
    - It reseals with a block it holds (`native_place`).
    - It waits while its own cells drain, at most 400 ticks (`OVERHEAD_DRAIN`).
  - A fill or reseal that did not go is an ambiguous outcome. So is water still in its tunnel 400 ticks after the
    reseal, or a reseal it cannot place from where it stands. Each holds the goal (`reconcile`): nothing more is
    planned, and the runtime blocks the player for reconciliation (`PlayerRuntime.observeRunner` →
    `nativeFailure`), as for an uncertain action. Nothing is tried again. An action whose own outcome is uncertain
    still blocks through its record, as before.
  - If what it opened onto is no source, or its breath falls below five sixths before the fill, it closes the opening
    again with its bucket empty. That place is then set aside.
  - `stopFor`: in deep water these steps are not stopped as "up for air first" while breath lasts, as a bucket filled
    from the water is not. The reseal is not stopped even short of breath: it is what stops the water coming in.
  - `climb`: under water, a climb that has gone `UNDER_WATER_REACH` (20) blocks along from where it began with no dry
    ground over its head is given up. The climb returns nothing and the goal goes on with what it does next. Within 24
    blocks of there, for 2,400 ticks, climbs under the water are given up the same way. The event log says "too wide to
    get out from under".
  - `climb`: no walk "to the wall" into the cell it last went out from under the water from (`underWaterAt`), for
    1,200 ticks. This pairs with F-25c4cq's guard for a step aside.
- `companion-server-adapter`: `NativeBucketAction.fillAim(player, target)`, the fill's aim as a static, so the campaign
  can ask before it fills.
- `companion-server-adapter`: `PlayerRuntime.observeRunner`, the block for reconciliation on the campaign's
  `reconciliation()`.
- gametest: `NativeSeabedWaterGameTests` (new, two fixtures), registered in the gametest `fabric.mod.json`.

Why N = 20 (`UNDER_WATER_REACH = BRANCH_LEG`):
- It is a leg of the campaign's branch mine, the longest straight run it digs anywhere.
- It is more than the 707 lake's 18 blocks across, which the climb is known to get out from under (F-25c4cq's fixture
  passes with it, below).
- It is well under the 40 blocks of open sea round Kindred on 606.
- The other ranges in the code are too short or too long. `diggableFaceAhead`'s eight and the wall's four would give
  up under lakes the climb gets out from under. The `underWater` memory's 24 is already the radius in which the climb
  counts itself under the water.

How the fix was settled, on the fixtures as committed unless said:
- The first form of the fixture had a uniform seabed and the two cells alone. Without the fix it never went to and fro:
  it tunnelled straight north out from under the sea and climbed the wall. The ground round Kindred's cells was then
  set as 606 generated it, with Kindred's corridor and the turns off it, and without the fix it went to and fro 3 of 3,
  as in the run.
- The first form of the rule left the opened block's drop lying. A passed 2 of 3: once the drop came down to the
  player and was picked up as the reseal was placed, the reseal was refused ("Placement preconditions, claim, or
  policy no longer valid; no block placed") and the goal held for reconciliation, as the rule requires.
- Taking the drop up as part of the break kept the player under the falling water longer. `stopFor` then stopped every
  step, "In deep water; up for air first", and A failed 3 of 3: the player drowned or drifted from under the opening.
- The next form waited up to 30 ticks for the drop to come to it before filling. It let its own steps go on in the
  water while breath lasted, and the reseal even short of breath. On it A and B passed 3 of 3.
- The form committed also lets the way up for air go first once the opening is closed, if it is short of breath in
  what came in, so a wait for the drain cannot drown it. The runs in the table are on it.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- Live: the effect on 606 is for E-40's next iteration to show. In the run itself Kindred may not have seen the sea
  over its tunnel: its water step climbed "toward the surface for water", which it does when it knows of no water, so
  there the knowledge rule would not fire. Then the "to the wall" guard and the hand-back are what end the loop.
- The rule opens only the block directly over its head, where the source is in a bucket's reach and the reseal has a
  face to go against. A source behind a wall, beside it, is left to the climb.
- A hold for reconciliation is final for that goal: the runtime takes the body out, as for an uncertain action, and an
  operator reconciles it.
- Not run: the failure corpus gate (`tools/corpus/gate.sh`), the full native suite, live playthroughs.

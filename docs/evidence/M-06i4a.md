# M-06i4a — The create command finds dry ground as a fresh player's spawn does

Date: 2026-11-22
Agent / branch: Claude, Session B (claude/create-dry-ground-1122), at the lead's word
Build: read against main `06f97b12` and the pinned Minecraft 1.21.1 (yarn 1.21.1+build.3); built on this branch (the PR's head)
Status: built; the proposal accepted as written by the lead, 2026-11-22

## Why

M-06i4's defect 4: on seed 101 the operator guide's create command,
`execute positioned ~20 ~ ~20 positioned over world_surface run aiplayer native create <name>`, was refused with "Make
room on solid ground in an already loaded area". The world spawn is by a lake, and water lay under that spot. Eight
offsets out to 40 blocks were refused the same way, so no player could be made. The guide now gives a `spreadplayers`
marker recipe as a workaround. A human player joining the same world is never refused: vanilla puts them on dry
ground near the spawn.

## What `aiplayer native create` does today

`PlayerRuntime.createNative` refuses unless `nativeSpawn(source)` finds a spot. The fresh path of `connectNative`
calls it again when the body is made, and `nativeSpawnable` exposes it to `EvalBootstrap.spawnSpot` and F-26's
starters.

`nativeSpawn` tries the eight blocks around the command's position, and only at the command's own Y: ±1 on each axis,
then the diagonals. The command's own column is never tried. A spot must meet all of these:
- its chunk is loaded;
- it is inside the build limit and the world border;
- a player's box (0.6 × 1.8) there is empty;
- no entity is in that box;
- there is no fluid at the feet;
- the block below is a solid block.

Nothing further out is tried.

From the console the position is the world spawn's corner, `Vec3d.of(getSpawnPos())`. With `positioned over
world_surface` the Y is the top of the water (the world-surface heightmap counts water). So by a lake the feet are
over water, the eight neighbours are water or at the wrong height, and it refuses.

## What a fresh human player's spawn does (vanilla 1.21.1)

`ServerPlayerEntity.getWorldSpawnPos(world, spawnPos)`, read from the pinned jar:

- **Radius.** In a dimension with sky light, and not in adventure mode, the radius is the `spawnRadius` game rule
  (default 10; the pack leaves it at that). It is reduced to the distance inside the world border, and is at least 1.
- **Order.** Every column of the (2r+1)² square is tried once, in a random order. The order is a random start plus a
  stride from `calculateSpawnOffsetMultiplier`, with `Random.create()`.
- **Each column.** `SpawnLocating.findOverworldSpawn(world, x, z)`:
  - start at the `MOTION_BLOCKING` heightmap + 1 and go down;
  - at the first fluid, refuse the column;
  - take the first block whose collision shape has a full square top face, and the spot above it;
  - also refuse a column whose `WORLD_SURFACE` is at or below `MOTION_BLOCKING` and above `OCEAN_FLOOR`.
- **Placement.** A column is used only if the player's standing box is empty there.
- **Fallback.** If no column passes, the player goes at the spawn point itself, raised until the box is empty. That
  can be in water.

It reads only those columns' heightmaps and blocks, as any spawn does. It loads a column's chunk if needed, with
`world.getChunk`. Around the spawn those are the spawn chunks, already loaded.

## Proposal

1. **Today's check first, unchanged.** An operator on dry ground, `EvalBootstrap.spawnSpot`, F-26's starters and every
   existing fixture get exactly the spot they get now.
2. **Only if it fails, vanilla's search, centred on the command's own position.** This applies in a dimension with sky
   light, as vanilla's does. From the console the centre is the world spawn, which is where a joining human starts.
   The search is vanilla's in every part: the same radius from `spawnRadius` and the border, every column once in the
   same kind of permutation, and each column judged by `findOverworldSpawn` itself (it is `protected static`; reached
   through an `@Invoker` accessor mixin, so the rule is vanilla's own code, not a copy).
3. **Narrower than vanilla in three ways, never wider:**
   - only columns whose chunk is already loaded, so the command never generates or loads land;
   - the spot found must also pass today's checks (box empty, no entity, no fluid at the feet, solid block below,
     inside the border);
   - no fallback into water. If nothing passes, it is the same refusal, saying it looked `spawnRadius` blocks round.
4. **One spot per creation.** Today the fresh connect calls `nativeSpawn` a second time. A random search could return
   a different spot, so the spot found at `create` is kept on the pending creation and used by the connect. The connect
   still checks it is free, and refuses as now ("Creation area is no longer available") if it is not.
5. **Reproducible.** The permutation's random is seeded from the creation's id (the new player's UUID) instead of
   `Random.create()`. The order is still an even spread over the square; it is the same each time for the same
   creation, so a fixture and a log can be checked.
6. **Earned and fair.**
   - It is one placement at creation, as now, and at most `spawnRadius` blocks away: no more than a fresh human gets.
   - The body is placed only at creation; saved bodies are not moved.
   - It reads nothing a vanilla spawn does not, and only in loaded chunks.
   - It gives no item and has no effect on death, respawn or travel.
7. **The guide.** Step 4 goes back to the plain command at the console (`aiplayer native create <name>`). The
   `spreadplayers` recipe stays as the way to choose a different place.

## Tests it would need (native; each fails on main first)

- **Spawn by a pool.** The command's spot and its eight neighbours are water (a 7 × 7 pool, 2 deep), with dry ground
  4 to 6 blocks away and `spawnRadius` 10. On main it is refused. After the change the body is made on that dry
  ground: on a full square top, no fluid in its box, within 10 blocks.
- **Nothing dry.** Water everywhere within the radius: refused, no record, no body, no slot taken.
- **Dry spot.** The command on dry ground: the same spot as today's ring gives, so no existing creation moves.
- **Unloaded land.** The only dry column in the radius is in an unloaded chunk: refused, and the chunk is not loaded.
- **Same spot at the connect.** The connect's body stands where `create` found.
- Then the narrowed creation, roster and eval-bootstrap classes ×3, `./gradlew build`, and the corpus gate because the
  starters' path is touched, even though it should not change.

## The lead's decisions (2026-11-22)

- Centred on the command's position: it is what the operator chose, and from the console it is the world spawn anyway
  (said in the guide).
- A player source standing in water gets it too: one rule whatever the source, only when today's check fails.
- One more test: no dry ground within the radius still refuses, with today's message, and never places in water.

  recipe kept for another place.

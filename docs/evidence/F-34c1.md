## Scope and implementation

F-34c's faction news on the Wire, one kind at a time (the lead's order, 2026-11-14): first a member's death, the
clearest verified fact.

**The change** (`PlayerRuntime.recordDeath`, the one place every death is written, AI players' and people's):
- In a faction world, when the one who died is in a faction, its `death` event is written and then told
  (`recordAndTell`). The message is the server's, `faction.member_died`, scoped to that faction alone, and it cites the
  death's event id.
- Its facts, both faction-only:
  - `who`: an AI player's name. A person is "A member": nothing records a person's consent to be named, so none is.
  - `cause`: the damage type as the game gives it (`generic`, `fall`, `player`, …).
- Never the killer or where it fell.
- Anyone else's death, or any death outside a faction world, is only written, as before.
- The line for a person: `[faction] Ada died: generic`, through `Wording`, within each person's listener limits like
  every Wire line.

Not body behaviour: what is told of a death the Record already writes.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `PlayerRuntime` (`recordDeath`, `memberDiedMessage`, `MEMBER_DIED`, its wording).
- gametest: `NativeFactionDeathGameTests` (new, two fixtures), registered in `fabric.mod.json`.
- docs: this file, the tracker.

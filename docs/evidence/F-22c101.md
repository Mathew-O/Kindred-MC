## The diagnostic (this change, log only)

Nothing in behaviour changes. No IO is added on the tick beyond the logger.
- **`TickPauseLog`** (new), hooked at each tick's start and end in `KindredMod`, measures each span of server time: a
  tick's own length, and the time between one tick's end and the next one's start. A span of 4 s or more logs one line,
  "Server pause (F-22c101)", with:
  - its length and where it fell;
  - the garbage collections run in it, count and time, from the JVM's `GarbageCollectorMXBean`s (in the JDK, no new
    dependency);
  - the chunks loaded and generated in it (Fabric's `ServerChunkEvents`);
  - the world saves in it, with their time (`mixin/ServerSaveTimeMixin`, new, on `MinecraftServer.saveAll`).

  Shorter spans log nothing.
- **An expired drop**, in `PlayerRuntime`'s dispatch, logs:
  - how long after its booking it was acknowledged;
  - the tick, and the last tick's length.

  Then it logs once which came first:
  - a skill next started: which one, the same or another, and after how many ticks and milliseconds;
  - the player stopped, or lost its body ("never offered again").

  The drop's own once-a-minute line is unchanged.
- **The halt line** from #401 stays as it is.

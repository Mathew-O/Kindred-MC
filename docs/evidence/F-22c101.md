## The diagnostic (this change, log only)

Nothing in behaviour changes. No IO is added on the tick beyond the logger.
- **`TickPauseLog`** (new), hooked at each tick's start and end in `KindredMod`, measures each span of server time: a
  tick's own length, and the time between one tick's end and the next one's start. A span of 4 s or more logs one line,
  "Server pause (F-22c101)", with:
  - its length and where it fell;
  - the garbage collections run in it, count and time, from the JVM's `GarbageCollectorMXBean`s (in the JDK, no new
    dependency);
  - the chunks loaded and generated in it (Fabric's `ServerChunkEvents`);
  - the world saves in it, with their time (`mixin/ServerSaveTimeMixin`, new, on `MinecraftServer.saveAll`).

  Shorter spans log nothing.
- **An expired drop**, in `PlayerRuntime`'s dispatch, logs:
  - how long after its booking it was acknowledged;
  - the tick, and the last tick's length.

  Then it logs once which came first:
  - a skill next started: which one, the same or another, and after how many ticks and milliseconds;
  - the player stopped, or lost its body ("never offered again").

  The drop's own once-a-minute line is unchanged.
- **The halt line** from #401 stays as it is.

## What fills the silences (2026-11-21)

**The pause lines on CI.** #401's own run on `1fd2d3e` (run 44859320443) logged 11 pauses:
- All were in-tick.
- One had 1 garbage collection of 37 ms and 50 chunks loaded and generated, at a nether entry. None had a world save.
- Seven were almost exactly 6.2 s (6205–6230 ms). Five of them fell in `NativeChatAskGameTests` (ticks 190439, 190440,
  190441, 190466 and 190467), each tick ending on one "Echo asked in chat by test-mock-player: …" line.

**The cause: a fixture's real-time wait on the server thread.** `NativeVoiceGameTests.Talk.waitFor(ms)` parked the
server thread for real time (`LockSupport.parkNanos` until the time was up):
- It exists because the social lane keeps its gaps in real time (a player's three seconds between direct replies, the
  typing delay) while the test server runs faster.
- `NativeChatAskGameTests.says()` waits 3.1 s before each ask, twice: 6.2 s.
- 9 classes called it, at 30 sites: PeerLedger, ChatAsk, Gift, Ask, Initiative, Voice, Trust, Outing and Conversation.
- While it parked, every GameTest running beside it stood still. That fits the in-tick pauses with no garbage
  collection, chunks or saves; the SETTLING halts, a slice expiring during the park; and #405's dropped
  `native_gather`, a 5 s booking expiring before acknowledgement while its own voice fixture parked the thread.
- No product code blocked a tick in any pause traced.

- **`PlayerRuntime.socialNow()`** is the social lane's clock: real time, as before, plus what a fixture has moved it on
  by (`moveSocialClockForFixtures`), which is 0 in play.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/aiplayer goal end [bot]` gives a native AI player the End goal. It earns a Nether portal first, as the Nether goal does, then goes on as players do:
- Stock up: food, blocks, a spare pickaxe, a sword, and iron into a sword, a shield, a helmet and boots.
- Go through. In the Nether, find a fortress, take blaze rods from its blazes, and take ender pearls from endermen. An enderman met anywhere is worth the fight while pearls are short.
- Come back and make eyes of ender.
- Throw one and watch it fly, walk 200 blocks to the side and throw another; where the two flights cross is the stronghold.
- Travel there, pin the spot with one more eye thrown close by (within twelve blocks an eye flies straight to it), dig a staircase down beside it, and look for the portal room.
- Done when an End portal frame is in sight; it says where in chat. Nothing enters the End.

Portal travel is allowed for that player only while this goal runs, since the operator's goal is what allows the trip. Any lit portal the player has seen will do, its own or one it came across.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`:
  - `StrongholdRunPlanner`, `StrongholdRunSnapshot` (new, pure): the stages and steps above. Twelve pearls and six rods are wanted. A pearl hunt stalled for ten minutes moves on with six eyes, enough to find the place. An eye lost in flight never sends it back to the Nether while it can still find the place.
  - `EyeFlights` (new, pure): where two flights cross, refusing near-parallel ones, crossings behind the throws, and crossings beyond any stronghold; and where to stand for the second throw.
  - `SurvivalGoal.STRONGHOLD`.
  - `ActionArbiter.relocate`: its record follows the body into another dimension (see the crossing bug below).
  - `SkillRunner.lastProgress`: a completed skill's result for the caller.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `NativeThrowEyeAction` (new, recorded): throws one owned eye and watches the entity fly, reporting only what was seen (where it was thrown from and its heading). Exactly one eye is spent under the ledger; an eye with no stronghold to fly to is kept.
  - `NativePlayerBody.throwHeld` with intent `THROW`; `native_throw_eye` in `NativePlayerSkills` and the runtime's skill factory and intent gate.
  - `StrongholdLeg` (new): the leg's own memory (both portals, the fortress, flights, the estimate), its snapshot, and each step made into a task. Hunting, stone and crafting reuse the portal campaign's own ways. Nether exploration holds a heading to standing ground ahead, a little up or down, cutting through netherrack where there is none, and turns only after twelve passes without headway.
  - `PortalRunCampaign`:
    - the leg in place of the portal's steps once a lit portal is known;
    - none of the Overworld's below-ground rules in the Nether;
    - the portal site read only in the Overworld (from the Nether the same coordinates are somewhere else);
    - the portal's no-progress watchdog off while the leg runs;
    - progress lines named by the leg's stage.
    - jump-downs: never beside lava, and in the Nether never below the shore of the lava sea (Y 40).
  - `WorldKnowledge`: Nether bricks, spawners, warped nylium, portal blocks, stone bricks and End portal frames. Spawners, bricks and frames need a line of sight.
  - `PlayerRuntime`:
    - the goal's dispatch and portal-travel authority;
    - the throw's result to the leg;
    - the End-portal announcement;
    - `nativeThrowEye`;
    - a fight goes on while the player is merely burning (a blaze's fireball), not while it stands in fire or lava.
  - `PlayerCommands`: `goal end`.

Found and fixed on the way (staged runs below):
- Crossing a portal blocked the player. `persist()` writes the arbiter's record, which still held the old dimension, so every write set it back and the crossing was seen again, with a fresh save and receipt each time. Their revisions collided and the runtime blocked the player for reconciliation in the Nether. Now:
  - the arbiter's record crosses too (one transit per crossing, from three);
  - a late receipt reply no longer winds the held revision back.
- Swimming across the ocean, it drowned at Y 49. Routes now price swimming with the head under three times over swimming at the surface, and diving more. Surfacing no longer drags it back to shore on every pass of a deliberate crossing while its head is out and breath is fine.
- The stronghold (Y unknown, X 1376, Z 848 on seed 8675309) lies under an ocean, and digging down refuses water. On arrival the leg now stays in its digging stage, goes to the nearest dry land it can reach, digs down to Y 8 and tunnels across under the bed to beside the start staircase, then down to Y -40 and round about.
- On that shore (staged run 5) it could dig down on no side for the sea, jumped back in and swam out again, along the coast for ever. Now, where water refuses every step down, it first walks inland to dry ground with no water or lava where a staircase from it would pass.
- Twice (staged runs 5 and 6) the player drowned mid-ocean, at Y 49 and 50, fighting drowned on the way to the stronghold. Swimming across an ocean is the wrong way; boats are next.
- Exploring the Nether (staged runs 6 to 9), with routes that build and dig ([F-10e](F-10e.md)):
  - headings are chosen by the longest open view, as a player heads for open caverns; a turn is to one side, never straight back, and seldom toward ground it has already explored (run 8 walked two hundred blocks back along its own tunnel);
  - where a walk on fails, or a ledge leaves no stand ahead and no tunnel on, it makes a way: digging, bridging or pillaring by cost;
  - an explorer's tunnel keeps to its heading (run 6's tunnel turned and walked 13 blocks back);
  - it keeps above the lava sea (Y 40), never steps down below it, and climbs back above it by the cheapest way;
  - with no way on along any heading it looks round rather than giving up the turn: an empty turn left the campaign its last resort, a jump down, and in run 7 that led down toward the lava sea, where a hoglin knocked it in.
- A restart while the player was in the Nether (run 7) blocked it for reconciliation for good: its save stood in a chunk nobody had loaded, and the connection refused the position. The body's chunk is now loaded first, as a joining player's is, and it connects once the chunk is in.
- Every restart lost what the leg had learnt, so it threw eyes again and looked for the fortress again. Now `PlayerMemoryStore` keeps it per player per world in the world's `kindred/memory` folder: the portals, the fortress, each eye flight, where they cross and whether an eye pinned it. It is written whenever the leg learns something, off the game thread, and recalled when a campaign starts. Run 13 was restarted twice mid-crossing and went on each time without a throw ("Remembered: ... 2 eye flights, stronghold 1376 848 (pinned)").
- The ocean on the way is crossed by boat ([F-10f](F-10f.md)). At the spot at sea it pins the spot with an eye thrown from the boat. Close by, an eye flies straight to the spot and drops, and the player can pick it straight back up. The throw counts that flight, and a flight too short for a heading still marks the spot.

Dependencies satisfied: F-25c4/F-25c5 (the portal), E-15's portal mechanics, now carried on since Codex stopped working on the project.

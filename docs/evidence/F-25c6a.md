## The stall: E-40c3 (#271), seed 303, Kindred
- **What the console shows,** in RUNNER 2's kept data (`claude/corpus-data-e40c3-1112` @ eee1f15):
  - Backing off a piglin at the fortress's edge, Kindred went from y 33 to 31, then 30, then 24, onto the gravel at the bottom of the Nether's lava sea, at 43 24 -90.
  - From about tick 182300 to the run's end (288000), every task it began was cancelled "Hazard/threat detected: urgent retreat required": "Fight blaze" 390 times, explores, "Look round for a way on", "Take cover".
  - The decision loop then found no candidate: "waiting for a feasible step", "No executable local task".
  - Its position never changed: 265 goal lines at 43 24 -90.
  - At tick 183190 its body was connected, ticking and in the Nether (`removed=false listed=true connected=true inWorld=true`).
  - There is no death, and no stuck spell after tick 43280: the End leg is exempt from the watchdog.
- **The kept region** (`DIM-1/r.0.-1.mca`, read offline) has lava in both of its cells and in every neighbour: y 24 to 26, gravel at 23.
- **Why it was stuck:**
  - The hazard abort (`PlayerRuntime`, `isHazardousSituation`: `isInLava`) cancels whatever is running.
  - Its only way out, the hazard retreat, steps to an adjacent cell that is not lava. In a lava sea there is none.
- **How it lived is unknown.** No effect, potion, barter or health line is in its console. The run kept no playerdata (fixed for later runs by #272). It must have had fire resistance from somewhere, or the lava came in after it stopped.

## The probe: a native body in lava is hurt and dies
- **`NativeLavaProbeGameTests` (staged)**, kept as a permanent test.
  - Setup: a native body with full health and no effects, set at the bottom of a lava pool (five by five, three deep, in stone), its runtime on the portal goal.
  - Passes if it is first hurt by the lava within 20 ticks, and within 200 is dead or out of the lava on solid ground.
- **First version:** it put the body in the lava within its first 60 ticks after joining, when vanilla spares a joining player all damage. It measured that window: first hurt at 58 ticks.
- **Now:** the probe waits 80 ticks after the body is established.
- **Result:** 3 of 3 passing on main 3196401. First hurt 1 tick after going in, dead after 51 ticks each time. A native body is not immune to lava.

## The fix
- **A skill of its own, `native_lava_escape`** (`NativePlayerSkills.LavaEscape`):
  - It heads for a dry stand the runtime chose, forward with the jump held. The jump swims the body up, and at the bank's wall vanilla's fluid-edge hop lifts it out.
  - It is done once the body is out of the lava and on the ground. It gives up if the stand stops being dry, solid, loaded ground, and after 4 s with no progress or 40 s in all (its descriptor).
  - Its moves are permitted only while the stand is still dry (`NativePlayerSkills.permits`, and the runtime's move permit).
  - Inputs only: no blocks placed or broken, no teleport, nothing granted.
- **The candidate** (`PlayerRuntime.evaluateEssentialSurvivalCandidates`):
  - It is offered when the body is in lava, the one-step retreat found no cell, and a stand is in sight. Priority is URGENT 99, the same as the retreat.
  - The stand comes from `lavaBank`. It searches up to 16 blocks across and 0 to 8 up, never below the feet. A stand is kept only if a sight line from the player's eyes reaches it (a collider raycast, fluids ignored). So a pocket of air behind stone or under the lake's floor is never chosen, as the first version did (it chose one under the pool's floor).
  - With no stand in sight it logs that once a minute and offers nothing.
- **The hazard abort** lets `native_lava_escape` run, as it already let the retreat and the clutch run.
- **Also:** the voice's "getting out of the lava", and the peer-talk busy list.

## Fixtures (staged; `NativeLavaProbeGameTests`)
- **What both stage:** a natural lake of lava source blocks, filled level with its stone bank, open above. The player is set in the middle at the bottom, with a stone pickaxe, 32 cobblestone and 8 bread, on the portal goal, 80 ticks after joining.
- **`stagedWithFireResistanceAtTheBottomOfALakeOfLavaItGetsItselfOut`:**
  - The lake is 9×9 and 5 deep. Fire resistance (2 min) is given by the fixture, and it says so.
  - Passes if it is out on solid ground within 600 ticks.
- **`stagedWithNothingToProtectItInLavaWithLavaOnEverySideItMakesTheShoreAlive`:**
  - The lake is 3×3 and 1 deep, so every cell beside the player is lava. No effects.
  - Passes if it is out on solid ground and alive within 200 ticks.
  - It then waits out the burning carried out of the lava and logs the outcome, without passing on it (see below).
- **How the staging changed before it was measured:**
  - The first staging capped the lake with stone and left the bank a block above the lava. Out of a pit like that nothing swims: the edge hop lifts a body about 0.6 of a block. Measured on the branch, the body bobbed against the wall for the rest of the test.
  - The first no-effect lake was 5×5 and 2 deep. The branch got the body within a moment of the bank before it died. What a body can do in lava, measured here:
    - it moves at most about 0.04 of a block a tick across, and about 0.04 rising (vanilla's lava drag);
    - unprotected, it loses about 3.3 health every 10 ticks, and dies in about 51 ticks (the probe).
  - So from the middle of a 5×5 lake no player gets out alive. The no-effect lake was sized to that before its before-runs were taken.
- **Before** (base c7c7a34 = main 3196401 plus the probe, with only this test file changed): 3 of 3 runs, both escape tests fail.
  - With fire resistance it stays in the lava to the limit.
  - With no effect it dies in the lava, around tick 1950: "tried to swim in lava".
  - The probe passes (dead after 51 ticks).
- **After** (this branch, the code as committed), 3 of 3 runs, all 3 tests pass:
  - Fire resistance: out in 123–124 ticks on 20 health; the fire out at 415–416, still on 20.
  - No effect: out in 38–39 ticks on 6 to 9 health.
  - Probe: dead after 51 ticks, as before. It dies while running the escape: the bank of its 5×5 lake, 3 deep, is out of reach in that time.
- **The burning after the shallow escape:** in all 3 after-runs the body burned to death on the bank, 212 to 291 ticks in.
  - Lava leaves a body burning for about 15 s, about 15 health. It came out on 6 to 9 health and had no water.
  - In a trial run it ate bread while burning, once two runtime changes let it (no one-block retreat while only burning; eating not cut off by the hazard abort). That bought too little, and it died all the same.
  - Those two changes were taken back out of this slice, since no fixture shows they help.
  - Whether the bar should include outliving the burning, with what a player could earn (a water bucket to stand in, or fire resistance), is put to the lead. It is not claimed here.

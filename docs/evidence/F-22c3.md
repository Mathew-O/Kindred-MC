lays a plain 105 blocks across, its four cows 45 blocks out, on the default 8-block structure. The test server

  on with "Hunt cow" until the test ran out.

  the cow at 11814320, in the next chunk. Then nothing until the timeout ("It found a cow and has its beef: now 0, 0, 43,
  Iron: hurt and out of food - Hunt cow").

(`FixtureGround.simulated`), as a server with a simulation distance would.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an AI player holds back from whoever the game saw harm it. It keeps a wariness of each person,
raised by:

- a blow;
- harm just after it came where they asked;
- its chest emptied while they were seen at it;
- asks for more than it hands out.

Wariness falls a little each quiet game day, and with a gift. Two blows are enough. While wary, it refuses that
person's asks, all but a stop, in its own words ("Not after last time."), and tosses them nothing. Two rules hold for
everyone:

- it hands one person at most 16 items a game day, owner or not;
- a come-to-them never ends beside lava, fire or a long drop.

For a minute after a blow, it walks off from whoever struck it if they come near. It never strikes back.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`:
  - `Trust` (new): the score per person (0 to 100) and the decay; the gift; the toss allowance; "struck lately".
  - `TrustTest` (6).
  - `ConversationAudience`: the situation tells the voice what the player holds against the one speaking. A
    constructor without it is kept, so the existing callers and tests are unchanged.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `PlayerRuntime`:
    - the sources: a blow (E-41g's `hitByPlayer`), harm after a come-to-them, a chest's loss while someone was seen at
      it (E-43), an ask past the cap, a gift;
    - the effects: the ask refused, the toss cap, the come-to-them's safe end, keeping away;
    - the voice's facts, and a fixture accessor.
  - `PlayerVoice`: a reply carrying an ask the player withholds is said as its refusal, and nothing goes in.
  - `NativeNavigator.safeStand`, and an unfinished route ending only where a predicate allows.
  - `NativeGoToSkill.safely`, and `NativePlayerSkills` (the goto's `safe` argument).
  - `NativePlayerBody`: hooks for any harm, and for a pickup of something another player threw.
- gametest: `NativeTrustGameTests` (7), a new entrypoint.
- No new dependency; the manifest is unchanged.

Schema/configuration changes: none. Wariness is kept in memory for the session, and nothing is saved.

## Design changes, and what was not built

- **A stop is never withheld.** A wary player still stops when asked. A stop is a control, and the contract has stop
  revoke at once.
- **Come-to-them's distance is unchanged.** The design asked for three blocks short. E-20b's own fixture has the walk
  end beside its owner, within 2.6 blocks, and stopping three blocks short would change that ask's meaning. That is
  left to the owner. What is built is the safe end: never beside lava, fire or a drop. The walk also now goes as near
  as it safely can when there is no way all the way: it used to give up, "No walkable route".
- **The scope of the toss cap.** It covers the chat ask's and the command's toss, `nativeToss`. Team hand-overs between
  AI players go their own way and are not capped.
- **Not kept across restarts.** Wariness and the day's tosses are kept in memory for the session.
- **Teammates.** A teammate seen at its chest is not counted, as E-43 asks the team. A blow from another AI player is
  counted like anyone's.
- Not fixture-tested: harm after a come-to-them, a chest's loss and a gift. Each is wired at the place the game records
  it and logged, and `TrustTest` covers the scoring.
- Updated docs: [operator guide](../operator-guide.md) ("Wary of someone (E-49)").

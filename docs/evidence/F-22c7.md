# F-22c7 — A walk to a block's middle ends with the body there, now and at rest

Date: 2026-11-05
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/ci-green-1105 (CI fully green, F-22)
Build: on top of `6effd66`; recorded in [validation](../validation.md)
Status: complete (native)

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: `NativeGoToSkill` ended a centred walk once the body was within 0.4 of the block's middle,
stopping its inputs, while it was still moving.
- **What followed:** on the ground the body slides on, about 1.2 times its speed per tick, often out of the 0.45 that
  `BuildRunner` counts as standing in the spot. The build then sent it there again.
- **The cost:** instrumented hideout runs counted 2,783 walks "To break" to some 400 breaks, over half the hideout's
  ticks.
- **Why the old pacing hid part of it:** with writes costing extra ticks (F-22c5), the body had settled by the time the
  build planned again. Without them, the build planned while the body was still short of the spot, and dropped the
  placement it had walked over for.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `NativeGoToSkill`:
- `atRest`: where the body comes to rest if its inputs stop now (ground slip 0.6 × 0.91);
- a centred walk ends only when the body is inside 0.4 of the middle both now and at rest. Otherwise it brakes and
  steers back as before.

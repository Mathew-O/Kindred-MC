# F-35h — A faction walk out of a basin: a way made where the walk finds none

Date: 2026-11-11
Agent / branch: Claude, MODES on MOVEMENT (`claude/movement-1111-basin`)
Build: ceed62b (the change), merged with main a60f919 as d2e0bf0, then with main 40330ab (#191, F-35i) as 0781fae
Status: in progress (fixtures done; the paired seed runs and the live before/after remain)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: in a faction world, an AI player whose walk to a place its faction play chose is refused for
no way there (walled in by rises a walk cannot climb) now makes a way, as a player climbs out of a hollow: a step dug
into the wall, a stair cut, or a block of its own put, then walks on. Before, it asked for the same walk again and
again, and set the place aside after three refusals. It never left.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates` (a refused leg's next step is a way made, before the
  wait), `PlayerRuntime.factionWay` (new); `FactionsPlayer` (`ways`, `wayMade`, `waysMade`, `WAY_STEPS` = 48;
  `walked` forgets them); the wait as a whole (F-35i) is checked before any way is made, a place's own wait after its
  way; `PortalRunCampaign.buildRoute` (an overload that takes the edit policy and `needEdit`).
- gametest: `NativeFactionBasinGameTests` (new, two fixtures), its `fabric.mod.json` line.

What it uses: the run's own route builder (`buildRoute`, F-10e), with `needEdit` so that a way that only walks (which
was just refused) is never offered. Every dig and placement goes through `RoamingEdits` under the server's edit policy,
which includes the faction land and safe-zone check (`TerritoryGuard`). No change to the shared goto, the navigator or
the route planner; no new route moves.

## The fixtures' staging isolated (2026-11-12)

The two fixtures assert what they did before: out of the hollow and in the border chunk east at the check. Their staging
now isolates the walk under test from the rest of the player's play, three ways. None of them changes the assert.
- No goal, its operator's choice (`startGoal NONE`, as `NativeFactionPlayGameTests`'s raid fixture has it): only faction
  play moves it. With the faction world's standing goal, the bedrock fixture failed on main's own CI twice on heads that
  do not touch faction walks (#206's run 43114167671, #227's 43127685464). Its choice was right both times, the scout of
  keys[1], but once out of the hollow its goal went exploring west for logs (the staged ground has no trees), and it
  stood five or six chunks off at the check. No tree is staged and no logs are given: a tree only postpones the
  competition, and logs in hand are items not earned.
- Its power too low to claim more, as the open-water fixture stages it: its choice stays the scout. Goalless with power
  to spare, in a local run its stair over the wall had already brought it into keys[1], and it went on to claim its own
  land west (Red 1 to 5 of 10), standing there at the check.
- The hollow at the middle of its chunk's width, not where it spawned: a spawn within two blocks of the chunk's east edge
  put the hollow's east wall in keys[1], so that its stair over the wall was arrival (about one spawn in eight). The case
  of a wall across the border is not kept as a fixture of its own: arriving on the wall is arriving, and what these
  fixtures test is the way made out to land beyond it.

With the three: the class alone five times, both fixtures each time, 10 of 10. The spawns lay 5, 2, 1, 14, 3, 0, 13,
10, 3 and 0 blocks into their chunk; at 14, staged where it spawned, the wall would have been in keys[1].

A diagnostic run (a temporary log line of each faction walk's end, the block under it and where the player stood, not
kept) showed every end on standable ground (an end at the height it spawned at stands on the fixture's floor, one block below): no walk ended in the air.

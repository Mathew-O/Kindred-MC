**Release timing is not the lever.**
- **Variant 2's releases are rare:** 4 a run.
- **The refused share barely moves:** 68.6% → 68.8% on 109.

**The faction walks are being asked from underground in the first place.**
- **Walk ends in the dark:** 175 to 904 of its own walks a run ended more than 16 blocks out with no sky over them, while its faction walks waited.
- **Variant 1's releases:** 21 of its 46 bases-off releases ended 8 or more blocks down, and its next faction walk was refused at once.
- **So:** the waits are mostly founders whose goal has them below the surface, digging. A faction walk asked from there is refused whatever the wait does.
- **Where the next change belongs:** where faction walks are asked while below (M-06i2c1's ground, reached by other paths), not in when the wait ends.

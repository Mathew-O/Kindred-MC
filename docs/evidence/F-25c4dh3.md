## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a hungry player walled in behind its own blocks eats, even with a zombie pressed against the far side of the wall within two blocks of it.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter `PlayerRuntime.mobCanReachToEat`: before, any mob within 2 counted as one that could get at the player, before sight was checked, so a zombie on the far side of a wall held the meal back. Now a mob within 2 counts only if it can see the player; one within 2 that cannot see it counts only if its path reaches the player, the same check the mobs further off already had.
- gametest `NativeBackOffFlipGameTests`: the walled-in case moves into a helper `walledIn`, and a staged twin, `walledInWithAZombieHeldJustOutsideTheWallItStillEats`, holds the zombie 2.0 off outside the wall every tick. The class was already registered.

Unchanged: sight and path checks for mobs further than 2 off, the hunger thresholds, threats, back-off and hole-up.

## The failure

`NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests.theNextChoiceAfterAPlaceSetAsideAsWaterComesAtTheDecisionInterval`, on #427's CI (run 44934790203, attempt 2, batch `factionOpenWaterPaced`, 20:36:06–20:37:19 UTC): "Its next choice 240 ticks after the last, before the decision interval: [376208, 376808, 377408, 377648, …]; walks completed at [377674]". It passed locally 6 of 6 on main and on #427's branch (REFACTOR), and CI GREEN ruled out the leaked sea water on distances (the nearest earlier sea in that run about 1,000 blocks off).

## The reading (not proven)

- **Where Ada was:** from the job's log, at 20:37:11–13 she was in the sea at x 7010259, y −32, climbing out of the water for her goal. The island's middle (x 7010244) is in chunk 438140. She was in chunk 438141, Blue's chunk east, where the faction play's choices go.
- **What re-weighs at once:** of the places in `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates` that set the decision time, two re-weigh at once:
  - a move whose target is already set aside;
  - arrival in the move's chunk ("There: weigh again at once", `FactionsPlayer.arrived`).
- **Why arrival, not a set-aside:** no faction walk ended in that test ("walks ended []"), so the set-aside-by-refusals path is unlikely.
- **Why the test would fail on it:** the test lets an interval be only where a faction walk completed in it. An arrival with no walk completed would read as an early choice, while the intended behaviour is to weigh again at once.

## Why not reproduced

Two diagnostics (never committed), three runs each on the test as on main:
1. Ada set down in her move's chunk, in the sea, at 1,500 ticks. Each set-down fell on a choice's own tick, and that move was set aside as water at its first look.
2. Ada set down in Blue's chunk east, in the sea, at 1,500 ticks, whatever her move. No move was live ("move null").

In all six runs her choices came every 600 ticks, and the test passed. In this fixture every move is set aside as water on its first look, so there is never a live move to arrive at; in #427's CI the move chosen at 377408 stayed live 240 ticks, and why is not known.

## The change (test hook and test message only)

- `FactionsPlayer.arrivedForTest` records the tick of each move settled because it got there, for the fixtures. `PlayerRuntime.factionCompletedAndOverWaterForTest` returns it third.
- The paced test names those ticks in its failure message ("moves settled there at […]"). **Its assertion is unchanged.**
- The exemption (an interval with an arrival in it let be, unless a place was set aside as water in it) is kept on `claude/m06m-paced-arrival-1122`, for when a failure names an arrival: then it is a fix with evidence. Shipping it now would loosen a test's check on an unproven reading (the lead's ruling).

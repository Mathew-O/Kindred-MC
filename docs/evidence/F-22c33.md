## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the fixtures. The source is #73's CI:
`NativeLifeFirstGameTests.itsFirstNightFallingAsItsCabinGoesUpItSleepsThereThenGoesBackTheNextNight` ran out of its
41,500 ticks ("Its body"), with its first night lasting about two minutes.
- `MockPlayers.creative` and `MockPlayers.survival` log a stand-in person in to the server. Fixtures call them from 38
  places.
- Nothing logged the stand-in out when its test ended. Some fixtures remove their own, most only once they pass; the
  rest leave it in the world, awake, for the rest of the suite. CI's log shows `test-mock-player` still connected at the
  server's shutdown.
- A night passes by sleep only when every player is asleep, and a stand-in left behind is awake. So every later night
  ran its full length.
- The second-night fixture has to get through two nights and a cabin within its time. On CI's runners, two full-length
  nights did not fit. Locally they still fit, so the runner's speed is what exposed it.

Changed file: gametest `MockPlayers`.
- Each stand-in it makes is logged out as its test ends, passed or failed, through `FixtureGround.whenDone`, unless the
  test has removed it already. The 38 fixtures are unchanged.

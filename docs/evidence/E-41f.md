## Scope and implementation

- **Validated compositions** (`CombatProposals.Tactic`, core). Four tactics, each a lean over the hand-set melee's own moves:
  - press: close in and trade;
  - counter: shield up for its swing, strike after;
  - hit and run: strike, then step out of reach;
  - disengage: get away.
  A tactic is offered only where it makes sense:
  - press, not when losing or low;
  - counter, only with a shield;
  - hit and run, not when low;
  - disengage, always.
- **Bounded** (`CombatProposals.lean`):
  - a proposal moves any one move's score by at most 0.3;
  - it opens no move the melee would not make, a getaway aside;
  - it holds ten seconds unless dropped sooner.
- **One outstanding, rechecked, dropped** (`CombatProposals.Gate`).
  - A question goes out only when none is out and ten seconds have passed. The strategist bridge also holds each player to one question at a time, within its budgets.
  - The answer is adopted only if its tactic is still valid for the fight as it is when the answer comes. It is dropped the moment it stops being valid (a shield gone, say) or runs its time.
- **Advisory only** (`NativeFightAction.advised`, `PlayerRuntime.combatProposal`).
  - The fight goes on locally while the question is out, and with no usable answer at all.
  - The model is told the fight's facts, offered only the valid tactics, and nothing else it says is used.
  - It is asked at a fight's start, and when the player's health has moved by six since it last asked.
  - Off by default; an operator turns it on with `/aiplayer combat advice on`.

## Scope and implementation

**The finding.** In RUNNER 2's keyed run f34c2-37adcdc, all 10,402 of the model's tokens went to Chronicle pages. Three
day-1 pages were kept. The later ones were refused for "'before' orders events itself", and the plain list was kept
instead.

Why:
- `Chronicle.check` refuses any model page with earlier, before, between, meanwhile, previously, prior to or
  beforehand, because the sentences are put in order by their line numbers (`ChroniclePrompt.page`), never by the
  model.
- But `ChroniclePrompt.SYSTEM` never said so.
- The event lines carry none of those words; the model adds them.
- One word refused the whole page.

**The change** (the lead's (a) and (c), 2026-11-15; model and core only):
- (a) `SYSTEM` says the rule: "Never put events in an order yourself: no "before", "earlier", "between", "meanwhile",
  "previously", "prior to" or "beforehand". Your sentences are put in the order things happened for you."
- (c) The sentence drop, in `ChroniclePrompt.page`, before the sentences are joined, where each sentence's line numbers
  are still its own:
  - A sentence with such a word (`Chronicle.orderWord`, the check's own list) is left out, and its numbers with it.
  - The rest is joined in the lines' order as before, and must still pass `Chronicle.check` whole: a citation to a
    line that does not exist still refuses the page.
  - A page with nothing left fails ("every sentence ordered events itself"), and the plain list is kept.
- `Strategist.Page` gains `dropped`: each sentence left out, with its word.
- `StrategistBridge` logs them, one line a page: `<name> chronicle: N sentence(s) left out, ordering events itself: [...]`.
  RUNNER 2's next keyed run can then count what remains and why.

**Held: (b).** Narrowing "between" is held, as the lead decided. "Between" two players ("a trade between Wren and the
market") is still left out, deliberately, until that count is in.

Not body behaviour.

## Why

From [the rung c read](eval/rungc-read-1123.md) of e40c4-b23acc0, consoles only:
- Five Nether players stood at one spot until the run ended, 37 to 130 game minutes each: 303 Echo, 31102026 Echo,
  404 Echo, 606 Kindred and 20261030 Kindred. Together that is about 436 of the batch's ~1,300 Nether minutes.
- Each freeze starts the same way:
  - every task in turn is cancelled with "Hazard/threat detected: urgent retreat required";
  - then "waiting for a feasible step";
  - then one report, `candidates=0 ... runner=CANCELLED ... blocker=Cancelled: Hazard/threat detected`;
  - no retreat ever runs.
- e40c3 shows the same freezes.

The hazard retreat offers only:
- a cell one block N, S, E or W at the same level, on a full solid floor that is not magma or cactus, with no fluid and
  room to stand;
- the lava escape, and only when in lava.

With none of those there is no candidate at all. The consoles do not say which block or which trigger it was. The
interrupt fires on any of three triggers: the hazard test, a lit creeper, or a fall to break with water.

### What the consoles already rule out

The hazard lasted: on 404, Echo's walk was tried again every 15 seconds or so and cancelled at once, for 100 minutes,
at one cell. Yet it took no harm: no death, no lower health shown. At b23acc0 the body did not sneak (E-71a's sneak came
later), so lava, fire or magma underfoot would have killed it within minutes.

That leaves the interrupt's other trigger: a lit creeper (ignited, or with a fuse of 25 ticks or under) in the **last
tactical observation**. Its abort text is the same, "urgent retreat required". It offers no hazard retreat, since the
hazard test is false. And `refreshTacticalObservation` swallows any failure of `observe` (`catch (Exception ignored)`),
so a failing refresh would leave the last observation, perhaps from the Overworld before the portal, in use for good.

This is a hypothesis from the consoles, not shown. The diagnostic below is widened so e40c5 can tell it apart.

## The cause, shown natively

`isHazardousSituation` reads the block under the player's **middle** (`getBlockPos().down()`). A player can stand on
the edge of a block beside it with its middle over the drop. Over lava, the cell under its middle is lava, so the test
calls it a hazard, though the body never touches it. Vanilla's own damage reads the block that holds it up, so
neither lava nor magma hurts it there.

When that block is a **corner's** (diagonal), the four same-level side cells can all have lava for a floor, so every
one is refused. Every task is cancelled, there is no candidate, and the player waits: the freeze, unharmed, as in
e40c4. The places fit: 303 Echo froze at y 32, one above the lava sea (31); 404 Echo at y 35 on "Climb above the lava
sea".

`NativeHazardEdgeGameTests`, STAGED in the Nether: a closed netherrack room, with a three-by-three of lava sources
one below the player's feet except one corner cell of netherrack. The player is set at (x + 0.75, z + 0.75), its box
resting on that corner, its middle over the lava, the lava's top below its feet. A goal runs.

- **On main's rule (the four sides alone): fails 3 of 3.** Each run has
  `candidates=0 decision=WAIT No executable local task` in the Nether, as e40c4 did. Its line:
  `Edgewise hazard spell at … in the_nether: trigger hazard; saw lava under it (0, -1, 0); feet air, floor lava;
  retreat cells: north floor lava not a full top, south …, east …, west floor lava not a full top; lava escape: not in
  lava; creepers: none; cancelling native_wait`.
- **With the fix: passes 3 of 3.** It retreats to the corner's cell (`native_retreat COMPLETED`), then gets on with
  its goal (digging toward iron), never in the lava.

## Part 2: the fix

When none of the four sides qualifies, and it is **not in lava**, the hazard retreat tries the four corners' cells:
- In lava, F-25c6a's escape still makes for dry ground, as before.
- Each corner cell gets the same `retreatRefusal` checks (`cornerRefusal`).
- It also needs a way past the corner: one of the two side cells it passes between must have room for the body, so
  it never squeezes between two blocks.
- `native_retreat`'s Retreat takes the four diagonals as directions for its target cell; the walk to the cell's middle
  is as before.
- The hazard spell's line lists all eight cells.

Unchanged:
- the interrupt's condition and `isHazardousSituation`;
- `hazardInterrupted` (REFACTOR's F-25c4dm2 relies on when it clears);
- the fall-to-break clutch, which is still the only candidate when due, before this block (MODES);
- no step up or down, and no block broken or placed.

REFACTOR and MODES were sent the shape before the push.

Not covered: a hazard with no free cell among the eight. Steps up and down, and breaking or placing a block, are left
for when e40c5's lines show one.

# F-25c4dh4 — A fight's start and end, and a meal held back, logged with how the player stood

Date: 2026-11-22
Agent / branch: Claude (MODES on MOVEMENT) / `claude/movement-1122-fightlog`, from main `9c8342fc`
Status: in progress (the PR's CI)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: none. The player plays exactly as before. The server console gains three diagnostic lines, so that E-40's next runs can show why zombie fights kill players.

Why: in E-40's iteration 27, zombie deaths rose from 10 of 38 in iteration 26 to 18 of 34 (one-sided Fisher p = 0.019). MODES's read of 27's consoles found the rise in long back-and-forth fights before the first iron chestplate, in which the dying player never ate:
- of 27's 17 zombie deaths, 12 had six or more fight goals and none had a meal;
- such fights without a chestplate ended in death 33% of the time, against 18% in 26.

The consoles could not say why. They have no health line, light only on a death line, no count of the mobs about, and nothing on whether a meal was refused (a mob near) or never asked for. The lead asked for this diagnostic so that iteration 29's consoles can settle it.

The three lines, in companion-server-adapter `PlayerRuntime`:
- At a fight's start, where `native_fight` is dispatched: `<name> fight starts with <mob>: <state>`.
- At its end, beside the outcome line every skill prints: `<name> fight ends <state> (<reason>): <state>`.
- When a meal is held back by the mob rule: `<name> meal held back: <mob> <d> blocks off can get at it (<state>)`. It prints once per spell, when the hold begins, and only when a meal is wanted (`canEatVanilla` or hurt), food it would eat is held, and it is not out of breath.

`<state>` is `fightState(player)`: health, food, saturation, armour points, the weapon in hand, the hostile mobs within 8 blocks by kind and count, and the light at the feet with Y.

To name the mob in the third line, `mobCanReachToEat` is now a wrapper over `mobReachingToEat`, which returns the mob it found. The test it applies is unchanged. One boolean is added to the entry, `mealHeldBack`, so the line prints once per spell.

Unchanged: every decision. Nothing reads the lines. The meal rule's result, the fight, its ending and its outcome line are as before. The fight's lines are built twice a fight, never per tick. The meal line adds one `holdsEligibleFood` check, and only while a mob is already holding a meal back.

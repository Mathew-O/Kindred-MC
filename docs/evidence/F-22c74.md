## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player that mined a pocket one block high beside its feet, and the cell under it, leaves a
drop in that lower cell. Before, it asked for a walk into the cell. No step, drop or jump ends there while stone lies
over the pocket, so the search spent all 6,000 of its positions over the open ground, refused, and the pickup asked
again. These were the refused walks F-22c73 found stranding the stone-tools cabin's builder in its pit.

1. **A goal no move ends in is known before the search.** `Navigator.enterable(target, goal)` looks at the stand and
   water cells of a tight goal (all within 3 of the target) and the cells a move could start from. If no move from
   any of them ends in the goal, `find` caps its search at 512 positions (`UNENTERABLE_NODES`). It still returns its
   partial approach. A goal whose box reaches its own edge, or an unloaded cell, is taken as enterable and searched
   as before.
2. **The pickup opens the head room, or sets the drop aside.** Before walking to a loose drop, `PortalRunCampaign`
   asks whether its cell is enterable. If not, and the pocket over it is passable and its head (two over the drop) is
   solid, breakable now with what it holds, and has no fluid or falling block over it, it breaks the head ("Open the
   head room over the drop"). Otherwise the drop is set aside for 1,200 ticks and no walk is asked. Every edit goes
   through the same policy and claim checks as any other break.

Nothing is given or moved: the drop is still picked up by walking over it.

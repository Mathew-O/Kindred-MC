## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: to hold a strolling villager (its AI on) for its ride, a bring puts a boat down beside it,
six times at most, and then lays its line to the villager as it stands. A boat it went to put down that was not put
down (the villager moved into the spot, and the game refuses a boat over a mob) counted as one of the six, though the
boat never left its hand. A librarian shuffling at its lectern used up the tries that way and was let go, and a
strolling villager with no hold wanders off each line laid to it.

- **The local full suite on 7 November**, the strolling fixture: two boats put down and not taken, and four "not put
  down"; "the librarian not held (6 boats put down, no boat)". It then rode home in the boat left lying (F-22c22).
- **PR #34's push build on `26d1cb2`** (run 42397269342), the same fixture with F-22c22's fix: "The librarian kept
  wandering off the line"; its log is not kept far enough back to show the tries.
- **Alone**, the strolling bring held its librarian at the first to third boat in every one of eleven runs.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime` (the bring's hold). A boat not put down is counted apart
(`boatRefusals`), up to twelve, and only boats put down count toward the six. The "not held" line says both.

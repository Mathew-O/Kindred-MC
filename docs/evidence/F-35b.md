## Scope and implementation

e40a17's ten seeds: 972 "Explore the cave toward iron" refusals. 618 of them came from small searches (under 500
nodes), most toward a pocket more than 2 up or down a cave wall. Each refusal set aside only that one cell, so the next
cave walk went to its neighbour in the same pocket.

- `PortalRunCampaign.cave()`: the cave floor its walk is bound for is remembered. Once that walk is refused (the cell
  set aside), the next `cave()` handles the pocket in a helper (`pocket`).
- A pocket at most 6 up or down and 12 across, with autonomous edits allowed, is climbed to once. The climb uses the
  existing route planner (`buildRoute(…, needEdit)`: pillar, stair, bridge, dig, with its own blocks), stepped until it
  stands in it. While mid-jump it waits to land, and a climb that runs past 40 steps is given up.
- Otherwise, or with no climb found, the pocket is let go (`letPocketGo`): the cave floors within 3 across and 2 up or
  down are passed over by `cave()`'s choice for 2,400 ticks, pocket-level, not cell by cell. Only that choice: set aside
  for every route and edit, as at first, a pocket three across took in the player's own cell and the rock beside it.
- A climb never bridges out over a drop deeper than 3 (`bridgesOverADrop`), and a climb that falls more than 3 below
  where it began ends there, the pocket let go.
- Local to `cave()`; LOOPS' global ten-minute set-aside of refused targets is separate, and applies as well.

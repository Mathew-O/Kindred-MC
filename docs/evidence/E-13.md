# E-13 — Establish and maintain a home shelter and farm
Date: 2026-10-18 EDT; report 2026-10-18.
Agent / branch: Antigravity / uncommitted main, no commits. Complete for this feature.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
Autonomous AI players can establish and maintain an agricultural food source and home shelter:
- Craft hoes (wood, stone, iron) and prepare farmland near water sources under server edit policy.
- Sow crop seeds (wheat, carrot, potato, beet) onto prepared farmland.
- Inspect crop maturity and harvest mature crops with strict replanting conservation (ensuring seed replanting occurs without depleting seed stock, collecting surplus crop yields into inventory).
- Place beds within designated shelter areas and sleep during night or thunderstorm conditions, anchoring the native player's respawn position to the bed head position.
- Coordinate shared multi-task farm establishment and shelter projects (`FarmHomeProjects`) with task dependencies, stock reservations, single-assignee ownership, and stop revocation.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`:
  - `dev.kindred.core.team.FarmHomeProjects`: Shared multi-step project factory methods (`createFarmEstablishmentProject`, `createShelterBedProject`) with explicit dependency chaining (crafting hoe -> tilling soil -> planting seeds; placing bed -> anchoring sleep) and shared stock reservations preventing double-spending of seeds and beds.
  - `MilestoneId`: Added `CRAFT_HOE`, `ESTABLISH_FARM`, `CRAFT_BED`.
  - `ProgressionObservation`: Added `hasNearbyFarmland`, `hasNearbyBed`, `hasCultivatedFarm` fields and helpers.
  - `MilestoneGraph`: Added postconditions for agricultural hoe crafting, cultivated farm establishment, and shelter bed crafting.
  - `ProgressionPolicy`: Added recommendations for hoes, farming, and beds.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `dev.kindred.fabric.NativeTillAction`: Native soil preparation action converting dirt or grass blocks into `minecraft:farmland` when within hydration distance of water (within 4 horizontal blocks) and under `EditPolicy.Kind.PLACE` authority, with authentic tool wear (1 durability per till) and reach/obstruction enforcement.
  - `dev.kindred.fabric.NativePlantAction`: Native seed planting action sowing valid seeds/crops onto unplanted farmland under `EditPolicy.Kind.PLACE` authority, consuming exactly 1 seed item and enforcing reach/clearance checks.
  - `dev.kindred.fabric.NativeHarvestAction`: Native mature crop harvest action breaking mature crops (`CropBlock.isMature(state)`) under `EditPolicy.Kind.BREAK` authority with immediate replanting conservation (consuming 1 seed from drop/inventory to reset block state to age 0) and routing surplus drops into the player's 36-slot inventory.
  - `dev.kindred.fabric.NativeSleepAction`: Native bed sleeping action checking for nearby placed bed blocks, validating night time (`world.isNight()`) or thunderstorm conditions (`world.isThundering()`), putting the player body into bed sleeping state, and anchoring the player's spawn point (`player.setSpawnPoint(...)`) with bed respawn position.
  - `PlayerRuntime.java`:
    - Registered `native_till`, `native_plant`, `native_harvest`, and `native_sleep` command handlers and action dispatchers with `ActionArbiter` lease and `ActionAdmission` tracking.
    - Added skill descriptors to `NativePlayerSkills`.
    - Integrated candidate evaluation for autonomous hoe tilling, crop sowing, mature harvesting, bed placing, and sleeping.
  - `NativeFarmGameTests.java`: 4 comprehensive Fabric acceptance GameTests testing hoe crafting and soil preparation, crop sowing and replanting conservation, bed placement and night sleep spawn anchoring, and shared farm/shelter project team coordination.
  - `fabric.mod.json`: Registered `dev.kindred.fabric.NativeFarmGameTests` in GameTest entrypoints.

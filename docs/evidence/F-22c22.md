## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: to hold a strolling villager for its ride, a bring puts a boat down beside it; one the
villager did not take is broken, picked up and put down again, six times at most. After the sixth, the bring went on
without a boat ("laying the line to it as it stands"), but the sixth boat was left lying where it was put down. The
villager strolled into it later; the cart, rolling along the line, took the boat up with the villager in it, and rode
it home high in its boat under the hall's doorway and the cell's lintel, which are opened only for a boat the bring
planned. Its head met them on the way in.

- **A full native suite on 7 November** (on `eae8161` with F-25c4bx),
  `NativeBringGameTests.aLibrarianThatStrollsIsHeldInABoatWhileTheLineIsLaid`: "the librarian not held (6 boats put
  down, no boat)"; then "riding BoatEntity" as the cart came; home, "Unhurt by its ride: 18.0" until the test's time ran
  out.
- **Alone**, ten runs of the bring fixtures (one before the fix, nine after): the six-boat path did not come about.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime` (the bring's hold). The boat it put down that the villager did
not take is broken and picked up after the last try too, before the line is laid to the villager as it stands. The
fixture's trace line now also says the villager's health and, when it was hurt, by what.

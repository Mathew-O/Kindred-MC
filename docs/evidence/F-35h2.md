## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an AI player in a faction world that has dug in for the night and sealed its shelter stays in
till daylight with its faction play's place outside: no faction walk is asked and no way made out through its seal while
it is dug in. Its choice is kept for the morning. Its portal walk already does the same (F-35c1); the check is the one
the portal walk uses, the campaign's own `dugInForTheNight`.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates` (nothing while the campaign has it dug in for the night),
  test hook `dugInForTheNightForTest`.
- gametest: `NativeFactionShelterGameTests` (new), its `fabric.mod.json` line.
- docs: this file, `feature-spec.md`, `validation.md`.

**Found:** `NativeObsidianPickupGameTests.aWalkToLookForLavaBeforeAnyObsidianIsMadeIsNotExploringWithItsObsidianLeft` (F-22c118's test) failed on #502 (run 45186083711, attempt 1) with "On its stand, the obsidian mined, its water still poured: ...".

**Cause, in the check:** the scene asks the player's next steps at one moment, read from the world: the obsidian mined, its water still poured, the player on its stand. With the drop picked up at once, the water was back within the tick the player reached its stand. That moment never lasted a checked tick, so the check never armed.

**A dead end, recorded:** the lead's first choice was to hold the player on its stand from "mined" to "water back", as the draining case does (F-22c94). With the drop staged at its feet that failed 8 of 20 under load, against 19 of 20 without it.
- Every staged copy failed "The step asked for and not taken was to take the water back: [native_goto ]".
- The water had come back within about a tick of the mine. Holding the player there afterwards made a moment after the fact.
- Two plain copies still failed CI's "On its stand, …".

**The change (`NativeObsidianPickupGameTests`):** for the tests whose lava is out of sight at the start, the check is armed by whichever comes first.
- **The moment, as before.** The step asked must be to take the water back, unchanged.
- **Its own goal line "Obsidian: recover the water".** Then the step must have been taken before any other goal starts: native_bucket COMPLETED (read from the run's own "task ended" report), a water bucket in hand, and the poured source gone from the shore. The check after it (site kept, water back, the obsidian in hand, nothing explored first, nothing uncertain) is unchanged.
- **Unchanged:** `AS_IS` and the draining case, whose lava is in sight from the start, are armed by the moment alone, as before.
- **The arming logged:** each arming logs what armed it, the shore's water as the stage reads it, where the player is and its action.

**Two new tests share the scene:**
- `itsLavaUnseenAndItsObsidianDroppedAtItsFeetTheWaterIsStillTakenBack` (batch `obsidianPickupUnseenAtFeet`). As F-22c118's hidden test, and the obsidian's drop set at the player's feet as it is born (STAGED), as CI's came to it at once.
- `itsStepToTakeTheWaterBackHeldBackTheGoalLineArmingStillCatchesIt` (batch `obsidianPickupWaterHeldBack`), the positive control. As the last, and its step to take the water back held back (STAGED: its empty bucket taken away as the goal line comes). It is armed by the goal line alone, because alone the moment comes first and would leave the goal line's arming unexercised. It passes only once the check reads the water as not taken back before the next goal.

**How the stage reads the shore's water:** it reads standing water as `isStill()` water, a source, not flowing water. At all 70 of the moment's armings in the after runs the shore read `WaterFluid$Still`, level 8. At the two passing goal-line armings it read `WaterFluid$Flowing`, level 6: the source was already in the bucket. At the control's three it read `Still`, level 8, as the goal line came before any bucket was filled. So flowing water is not read as standing, and that is not what made the hold's moment after the fact. That moment came with the source still on the shore after the bucket was full; what put it there was not measured, as the hold was dropped. What the stage reads is unchanged.

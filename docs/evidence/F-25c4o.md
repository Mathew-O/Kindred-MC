## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player whose pickaxe, axe, sword, shield or armour breaks from wear in a fight carries on without it. Before, a fight fought with something other than the weapon it named broke the record's count. With no sword or axe, that was a pickaxe in hand from mining. The record called it "owned items changed beyond wear and pickups", and the player was taken out of the world to be reconciled by an operator.

Found in the long live run on `a286cc8` (seed 8675309, 240,000 ticks, strategist off):
- Sol had no sword or axe to hand. It could not back away from a zombie ("No walkable route"), so it fought it with the stone pickaxe in its hand.
- The pickaxe broke on the fifth blow, which killed the zombie.
- At 14:50:43 the log said "Native player … blocked: Native action outcome requires reconciliation", and Sol's body was gone from the world for the remaining seventeen minutes of the run.
- The fight's encounter record shows the item held turning from `stone_pickaxe` to air on that blow.

The check allowed one lost item only when it was the weapon the fight named. A fight against something it could not avoid names none when there is no sword or axe, since a pickaxe is not a weapon to it.

Changed modules/files:
- `NativeFightAction.conserved`, and the same check in `NativeDragonAction`: one damageable item lost counts as worn out and broken when it was in a slot a fight wears. Those are the named weapon's, the hotbar (whatever is held), the armour and the offhand. Anything else lost still makes the outcome uncertain.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

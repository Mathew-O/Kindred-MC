## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior. A player on the End goal knows from the start what the End fight will need, and works on it while it does everything else:
- the fight's needs are a bow and 32 arrows (the End crystals heal the dragon, and an arrow from the ground breaks one), 64 blocks (two crystals are caged and must be pillared up to), a water bucket and 16 food;
- it breaks each into the work it takes from what it holds now. A bow it lacks is three string to gather and three sticks, then the bow. Thirty-two arrows are eight flint and eight feathers to gather, eight sticks, then eight crafts. What it holds counts once against every use: the same sticks serve the bow and the arrows. A bow looted from a skeleton, or arrows picked up, change the work at once;
- it takes the chance when it comes, as a short detour before the route's next step:
  - a chicken in sight while feathers are wanted;
  - a spider it can take on, or a cobweb (broken with its sword), while string is wanted;
  - gravel close by while flint is wanted;
  - wood for sticks, or string and sticks for the bow. It sets down a crafting table it carries, or makes one, to craft;
- what is still missing is gathered at the stronghold before it digs down: hunting, looking for animals, digging gravel and stone, filling a bucket. It goes down anyway, and the log says so, if half a day passes there without any of it getting smaller, or if for half a minute none of it can be had from there at all;
- items the queue wants are never dropped to make room, and it walks back for them when they drop nearby;
- the optional strategist can add to the queue (F-19e), from the same fixed list.

The user asked that larger tasks be broken down and assessed for what they will need: the player will need a bow to shoot down the End crystals. It should be dynamic, with a queue to which follow-up tasks are added while the player does the current one.

Changed modules/files:
- `dev.kindred.core.guide` (core, new):
  - `Need`: what a player can be short of: bow, arrows, shield, water bucket, string, feathers, flint, sticks, blocks, food. Each is one the planner can act on, with a cap.
  - `Objective`: the Nether trip, the stronghold, the End fight, staying alive.
  - `Want`: at least a count of a need, for an objective, and why; parsed from a model's reply only from the fixed sets.
  - `MinecraftGuide`: recipes, where each need comes from, and what each objective needs in hand before it begins.
  - `NeedsAssessment`: wants and holdings into chunks of work (gather, then craft, inputs before what they make).
  - `TaskQueue`: each objective's requirements, assessed afresh every pass, and the strategist's wants, kept until met or until their objective is behind the player.
- `StrongholdRunPlanner`: `Provisions`, the `PROVISION` stage before the descent, and the `FOLLOW_UP` action. Follow-ups the player can do now come after the survival-first steps and before the stage's own.
- `StrongholdLeg`: holdings, the chances at hand, each chunk as an ordinary recorded skill, the stall clock, the queue in the strategist's report, spiders not given a berth while string is wanted.
- `PortalRunCampaign`: the queue, wanted items kept and walked back for, `huntFor` (one kind of animal), `tableNear`, `placeTable`.
- `WorldKnowledge`: cobwebs seen.
- `NativeFollowUpGameTests` (new), `NeedsAssessmentTest`, `TaskQueueTest` (new), `StrongholdRunPlannerTest`.

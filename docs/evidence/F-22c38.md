## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. The source is #82's CI, run 42566194927 (job 128993113262), the only failure:
`NativeHorseGameTests.withItsCabinStandingItTamesAWildHorseItSeesAndSaddlesIt`, at "Saddled with its one saddle".
- Wrangler tamed the horse and got off it. It then planned "The saddle to hand", a `native_swap` from its pack to its
  hotbar.
- The very next line was "A horse of its own, tamed; its saddle could not be brought to hand". Only then did the swap
  log `native_swap COMPLETED`. The saddle stayed in its pack, and it later put it away in its chest.
- `StableRunner.next` counts a swap each time it plans one, and gives the saddle up after three. The horse's candidates
  are asked again while a step runs, and a swap waits on its durable record before it acts. Every plan made while it
  waited counted as another try.
- Mounts were counted the same way: "on it, 12 times so far" for three rides. The runner gives a horse up after 24.
- On a quick runner the record is kept within a plan or two. On a busy one it is not, which is what CI's speed exposed.

Changed file: adapter `PlayerRuntime`, the horse's candidates (`stableCandidates`).
- **The step kept:** the step handed out is kept on `Entry.stableStep`. A `native_swap` or `native_ride` is handed out
  again as it is, and the runner is not asked for a new one, until a task of it has been chosen and has ended. So a
  plan is counted once per step that ran.
  - A step never chosen is let go after `STABLE_HOLD` (200) ticks, and planned again.
- **Walks unchanged:** a walk up to the horse is planned afresh as before, so it follows a horse that moves.

**Reopened.** The first form of the change kept the step only while it was the running task, with the runner RUNNING.
#87's CI (run 42584555878, job 129051137428) failed the new fixture: 24 mounts counted for 5 rides, "The horse would
not bond after 24 tries".
- Locally it over-counted the same way: 24 for 4 rides. Its first 3 of 3 had passed only because the horse bonded in
  time.
- A debug line showed three windows where a ride was handed out and counted, with no ride running:
  - handed out while the "Hold on" wait still ran, the player thrown off during it;
  - between the wait's end and the ride's choosing, the current task none;
  - chosen and waiting on its record, the runner still showing the wait's end (COMPLETED).
- The fixture now also checks the mounts counted against the mounts made, which it sees tick by tick: "Each mount
  counted made". That check does not depend on whether the horse bonds.

New fixture: `NativeHorseGameTests.withItsStepsSlowToBeRecordedItStillSaddlesTheHorseItTames` (batch `ownHorseSlow`).
- It is the own-horse scene: the cabin, the saddle in its pack and the wild horse staged as before.
- Fixture staging: the durable record of each swap and ride is kept 1.5 seconds late, through the fixture's own action
  writer. That is a busy runner's slow record.

## Scope and implementation

M-01's card: "Progress tracks the human: the AI player does not run ahead of the group's milestones." Co-op Survival's announced rules already said AI players progress at the group's pace (M-00). This slice makes that true for a player's base, the first progression that waits on it.

User-visible behavior:
- **The group's milestones** are vanilla's own story advancements, announced to every player when earned:
  - mined stone;
  - smelted iron;
  - found a diamond;
  - enchanted an item;
  - entered the Nether;
  - entered the End.
- **The group's furthest step** is the furthest any human in the world has reached (`GroupPace`, core). It is read from the humans' advancement progress every five seconds, and again whenever an AI player is about to choose its next base stage. AI players are not the group.
- **Kept when that human logs off.** The step is saved in the world's `kindred/pace.properties`, off the game thread, and read back on start.
- **A base waits for the group.** In a Co-op Survival world, a player whose base would move on (M-01b, M-01c) waits until a human has reached the stage's step, and its base note says why:
  - a house until someone has smelted iron;
  - a manor round an enchanting room until someone has enchanted an item;
  - its first cabin never waits.
- **A trip into another world waits for the group** (`044eeab`). In a Co-op Survival world, a goal's step through a portal from the Overworld waits until a human has entered that world:
  - the Nether, through a Nether portal;
  - the End, at an End portal. The dragon is in the End, so in Co-op it is never fought before a human has gone there.
  - The player stays by the portal, and its goal's progress says why ("Keeping to the group's pace: into the Nether once someone has entered the Nether ..."). Portal travel stays unauthorised meanwhile.
  - The way home is never held.
- **Not gated on humans being there.** A world in which no human has reached any step yet is not paced. AGENTS.md does not let human presence gate decisions, so the pace only keeps an AI player from outrunning humans once there are some. Other world modes are not paced.

Found on the way: the fixture began the house in 3 of 3 runs before the fix. Bases came on before the five-second poll had seen the human's progress, and with no step known nothing was held. The pace is now read as the stage is chosen (`92a36fe`).

Changed modules/files:
- Core: `GroupPace` (new); `GroupPaceTest` (new).
- Adapter: `PlayerRuntime`:
  - `groupStep`, read from `pace.properties` on start;
  - `pollGroupPace`, every five seconds and as a stage is chosen;
  - the hold in `baseDecision`;
  - `tripHold`: the hold on a goal's trip through a portal, in `campaignCandidates`;
  - `stageBaseForTest` (fixtures only).
- Fixture: `NativeCoopPaceGameTests` (new).

Dependencies satisfied: M-00 (the world mode), M-01b and M-01c (the stages and the player's own base).
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: a new file, `kindred/pace.properties` (`furthest=<STEP>`). It is absent in an older world, which is then not paced until a human reaches a step.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

**What M-01e paces, by design:** the steps that move the group's game on, its bases and its trips into new worlds. An AI player's gathering, smelting, tools and armour are not held: it needs them to stay alive, as a human does, and holding them would leave it unable to defend itself beside the humans. Whether M-01's playthrough wants more held (farms, the ranch, the trading hall) is left to the parent.

Not yet:
- **A long wait at a portal:** the goal's three-minute watchdog can still send a held player off to try elsewhere. It never crosses meanwhile, but it may wander. Untested beyond the fixture's ten seconds.
- **The dragon:** in Co-op it waits for a human to enter the End, and still needs the server's dragon-fight leave. Fighting alongside the human there is the End fight's, not this slice's.
- **The group** is every human in the world, not a team's members.
- **Live:** no live session with a human setting the pace yet (M-01's acceptance).

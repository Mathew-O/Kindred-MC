## Scope and implementation

The owner's notes of 2026-10-23 set the base:
- **Huts are for tests.** The north star is detailed houses, bunkers and builds: multi-floor and, most of all, functional.
- **Three stages in Co-op Survival.** A small base to start the world and give the players a place to store resources. Then bigger and better, so improving the base stays a goal.
- **The same for other builds.** Farms and other functional builds are staged the same way. Mob-spawner farms are rare, so each is a one-off functional build for grinding and resources.

This slice is the groundwork in the build planner and the first three stages of a house.

**The stages** (`Houses`, core, pure). Each is built of a wood and a stone, with its entrance in the middle of the south wall. Every room has a purpose:

| Stage | Plan | Floors | What it holds | Blocks (oak, cobblestone) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cabin | 7×7 | One, gabled roof | A bed, a crafting table, a furnace, two chests, a torch, a door | 222 |
| House | 9×11 | Two, flat roof | Below: a storage wall of five chests, a workshop with a table and two furnaces. A staircase up to a bedroom of two beds. | 553 |
| Manor | 13×15 | Two, flat roof | Below: a storage hall, a workshop, and an enchanting room with the table ringed by fifteen bookshelves. Above: bedrooms (three beds) and a library. | 972 |

`Houses.functions` counts what makes each stage more than walls: chests, tables, furnaces, beds, doors, the enchanting table and its bookshelves. Windows are open; glass comes later.

**What the planner needed for them:**
- **Doors and beds placed whole** (`Placement`: `DOOR`, `BED`). A door is placed by its lower half, a bed by its foot. The other half comes with it, into a cell that must be clear and is never the player's own stance. A door's hinge follows from where it is placed. The bill counts each once.
- **Doors walked through.** The plan treats a wooden door as passable, both for the walk and for the never-seal-itself-in check. In game, the navigator treats a closed wooden door as passable too. The go-to skill opens a door ahead with a use click as a player does, and shuts it behind once through (`useDoor`, the `DOOR` intent).
- **Stairs as footing.** A staircase the build itself places is ground to walk on once each step's head room is clear: that is how the upper floor is reached. A roof's stairs are not footing.
- **Off its own scaffold.** On its own scaffold, the planner prefers placing from where it stands to climbing down.
- **Room to climb.** The stairwell's hole is three cells long, since a step up is modelled as a jump.
- **Roofs.** The two larger stages have flat roofs. A wide gable's middle cannot be reached from below.

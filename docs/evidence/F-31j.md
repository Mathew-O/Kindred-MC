## The question (the lead, from B's audit)

- what will it hold after 30 days with 8 players?

## The 30-day, 8-player estimate

The kept factions8 and e40 logs are on the runner's container, not in the repository, so the rates below come from
the code's caps and the CI suite. Over the 907-test suite (#394's job 133212759665) there were:
- 2 "lets … be a while" set-asides;
- 4 "found … gone from" store looks;
- 2 raid decisions.

A month is 2,160 game days.

| Map | Per player, 30 days | 8 players | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `placeRefusals` | ≤ 65 | ≤ 520 | bounded |
| `placeSetAside` | expired ones pruned when it next places a workstation. With no such step, up to one per set-aside: tens a day at the most, so a few thousand in a month in the worst case | ≤ ~20,000 worst case, very likely tens | grows only where the pruning step never runs |
| `chestsUsed` | ≤ 32 | ≤ 256 | bounded, but full of stale chests once 32 are seen, and then it notes no new one |
| `seenAtStores` | ≤ its stores (a handful), each 5 minutes | ≤ ~50 | bounded |
| `untrustedStores` | its stores robbed in a month: a handful to tens | ≤ a few hundred | grows slowly, never shrinks |
| `recentlyTaken` | the kinds it took in the last 5 minutes: under 20 | ≤ ~160 | bounded |
| `raids` | each opponent's chest it ever decided on: a few a match day. In a month of factions or manhunt, hundreds to a few thousand | up to ~20,000 at most | grows without end in match play |

At about 100 bytes an entry (a boxed `Long` key, a small record, a `HashMap` node), even the worst cases are a few
MB. None is a memory risk at month scale. Three keep what is stale: `raids`, `untrustedStores` and `placeSetAside`
off its pruning path. `chestsUsed`'s cap of 32 stops it noting new chests once it is full of stale ones.

## Proposed bounds (for the owner and the lead; not made)

1. **`raids`:** drop a decision once its `until` has passed and its chest is no longer in `chestsUsed`, and all of a
   match's decisions when the match ends.
2. **`chestsUsed`:** age a chest seen used more than about 12,000 ticks ago (the leave decision's own length), and clear
   it when the match ends. The cap of 32 then holds live chests.
3. **`untrustedStores`:** drop a store once it is no longer among its stores, or once it has been emptied and given up.
4. **`placeSetAside`:** prune expired entries where one is added (:8609) as well as where they are read.

Each changes body behaviour a little (what it remembers of a match, a store), so each waits for the freeze to end and
the owner's word.

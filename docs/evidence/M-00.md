## Scope and implementation

M-00's card: an operator selects a mode per world or match; the playstyle profile maps onto it; a mode declares its rules (survival or creative, edit policy, PvP among opted-in participants, announced mode items, respawn rules, win conditions); matches have a lobby, roles, countdown, timer, end and results; presentation visible without the client mod, with richer screens when it is installed. What this change adds on top of the duels (M-00a), their presentation (M-00b) and the match modes already built (M-02a, M-03a, M-04a):

**The world's mode.**
- `/aiplayer mode world <coop|speedrun|manhunt|builder>` chooses it, operators only. It is saved in the world's `kindred/mode.properties` and read at start.
- `/aiplayer mode world` shows it and its rules; `/aiplayer mode rules <mode or match>` shows any mode's or match's rules.
- Factions is declared but refused: not playable yet (M-06).
- On the K panel (client mod), an operator steps the mode on with one button. The panel shows the match the viewer is in, as its bar reads.
- **It maps onto the playstyle.** Choosing a mode sets every AI player's playstyle to the mode's own: Co-op Survival and Manhunt to `OPTIMAL_SURVIVAL`, Speedrun to `SPEEDRUN_ANY_PERCENT`, Builder to `IDLE_OBSERVING`, since Builder plays only build battles. A resumed player that was idle takes the mode's playstyle. An administrator can still set one player's own with `/aiplayer mode <profile> <player>`.
- **It says which matches may be played.** A world plays its own mode's matches, and duels, which both players opt into. The proposal of any other match is refused with the reason. A world with no mode chosen allows every match, as before modes existed.
- **It is announced.** Everyone online is told the mode and its six rules when it is chosen. Each person joining is told them as well.

**Every mode's and match's rules, declared.** `GameModes` (core) writes out, for each world mode and each match mode, six rules: play (survival or creative), building, fighting, items, deaths and winning. Each person invited to a match is told that match's rules in chat before the [Join], so a rule is known before anyone plays (D6, D7). The rules are enforced where they already live, and nothing new is enforced by the words:
- the leave to strike (`MatchRegistry.mayAttack`);
- the builders' creative plots;
- the hunters' compass;
- the fresh start of a race or hunt;
- each mode's win.

**A duel's own time.** `/aiplayer match duel <first> <second> [seconds]` takes 30 to 1800 seconds, then a draw. The default stays five minutes.

**Presentation (M-00b), completed.**
- A sidebar, sent to each viewer alone (an objective the server's own scoreboard never holds). It shows where the match stands (joined, the countdown, the time left or elapsed, the vote) and each player's standing: joined, out or won, and a racer's splits. A manhunt lists its runners and hunters under their own headings.
- Each player's own part over its hotbar every second ("Duel against Bram - 1:59 left", "Hunter: follow your compass - 12:04"), held off for three seconds after a split is shown there.
- Particles: fireworks round each player at the start, and a totem's burst round each winner.
- **Onlookers.** Players not in the match within 48 blocks of one who is get the bar, the sidebar and the result ("Ada wins").
- **A result for the fallen.** A title for a player who is dead when it comes is held until it respawns. The death screen covers titles, so a human who fell never saw its "Defeat". This was found on an ordinary client, below.

Changed modules/files:
- Core: `GameModes` (new), `MatchRegistry` (`proposeDuel` with its own time), `MatchPresentation` (`sidebar`, `actionBar`).
- Adapter:
  - `MatchPresenter`: the sidebar, action bar, particles, onlookers and held titles.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: the world mode (its load and save, `setWorldMode`, `showWorldMode`, `modeAllows` on every proposal, `playerJoined`, `matchLine`), the rules in each invitation, and the duel's own time.
  - `PlayerCommands` (`mode world`, `mode rules`, `match duel ... [seconds]`) and `KindredMod` (the join hook).
  - The K panel: `KindredPanelService` and `KindredPanelScreen`.
- Tests: `GameModesTest` (new), `MatchRegistryTest`, `MatchPresentationTest`; `NativeMatchFrameworkGameTests` (new, six fixtures), `NativeMatchGameTests` (the held title); `MockPlayers.survival` (a stand-in human).
- Tooling for live checks: `tools/ordinary-client/` (a vanilla 1.21.1 client launcher, an RCON client and a key driver). It is test tooling only, not shipped and not a runtime dependency.

Dependencies satisfied: F-25b/F-25c's native player; M-00a, M-00b; the match modes M-02a, M-03a and M-04a.
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: one new file, `kindred/mode.properties`. When it is absent, no mode is chosen, which is the behaviour from before. No saved key was renamed.

  - **The bug.** On the death screen no title shows. On respawning she was shown "Defeat / Bram won: Mira fell", the held title.
  - Owen, eight blocks off, saw the bar and the sidebar.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player taking a villager over water in a boat, the boat still on the ground with something
between it and the water, first rows the boat aside round it, then into the water and across.

Changed modules/files (adapter, `PlayerRuntime`'s bring only; `NativeRowSkill` and the navigator untouched):
- The ROW step. If the held boat is not touching water and its straight run to the water is blocked, it rows first
  to `boatDetour`'s spot. `boatRunClear` decides "blocked": the boat's box, swept every quarter block at its height,
  meets a block or a hard entity before it is over water. The spot is two to six blocks to one side of the way, the
  nearest from which both the way there and the way on are clear. That spot is kept until the row to it ends. Then
  the crossing proper starts: its start and its row count are taken afresh, so the far bank's test ("ashore": a row
  ended touching water, more than half way over) does not count the rows aside. With no side clear, it rows straight,
  as before. The give-up after four rows counts the crossing's rows.
- The HOLD step. With the boat's spot beyond reach (over 3.5 blocks from its eye), it walks near the villager, as
  before. If it is already within three blocks of the villager, it walks near the spot instead (said once). The spot
  can be on the villager's far side. A walk that ended within two and a half blocks of the villager left the spot
  still out of reach, so the walk was asked for again about 63 times a second, ending at once each time, until the
  runtime's stop. The staged fixture's first runs found this (below).
- `NativeBringGameTests.aLibrarianHeldBehindItsLecternIsRowedRoundIt` (new; the scene's `behind`):
  - the lake scene, the librarian's AI on (the bring takes only such a one by boat), slowed to a stand until it sits in
    a boat;
  - it starts on the bank, where the crossing is planned from, and is moved south of its lectern, in line with it and
    the water, as the bring turns to holding it in a boat (`[HOLD]` in its note), before any boat is put down (as in
    CI, where it strolled off before it was held);
  - a fence either side of the lectern, four blocks across in all, so the boat meets it squarely.

Dependencies satisfied: E-45e's boat crossing.
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: none.

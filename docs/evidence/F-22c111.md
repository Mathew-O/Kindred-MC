A CI census child, test-only, filed by F-22c110 (#442). That PR rims the one fixture whose leak was shown to break another test. This one closes the gap they both come through.

## Scope and implementation

**Cause:**
1. `FixtureGround.restore` puts each block of a kept box back with `NOTIFY_LISTENERS | FORCE_STATE`, without neighbour updates.
2. Fluid that ran out of the box while the test ran is outside it. Nothing tells that fluid its source is gone, so it never drains.
3. It stays standing, or falling, on the ground the next batch is laid on.

**Two tests it broke on CI:**

| 44918551355/1, 43782657628/1 | `NativeBuildBattleGameTests.twoAiBuildersBuildTheThemeInCreativeAndVoteOnEachOther` | The open-water causeway sea's curtain lay over the BuildBattle walkway; the voters' walks to their spots were blocked and "Nobody voted" (F-22c110's evidence) |
| 44973451122/1 (#441) | `NativeBringGameTests`, case 7, `givenUpWithItsBoatInAPit…` | The batch before was `factionOpenWaterCauseway`. Its sea's east spill edge lay at x −12856695; the pit, at x about −12856700 and z 6748022, lay 5 blocks inside it and within the sea's z range. The pit flooded and the librarian floated out of the unbroken boat (y −2.53). Locally 30 of 30 kept it in |

Case 7 is recorded here, at the lead's word: F-22c103b is closed as F-22c110's case, with no open row of its own.

**The change (test support only, `FixtureGround`):**
1. As a box is kept, the cells holding fluid round it are read: out `SHELL` (8) blocks each way, from the world's floor up to the box's top. Unloaded chunks are skipped, and so is any chunk section with no fluid in its palette.
2. As the box is put back, the same shell is read again. Each cell holding fluid now that held none then is cleared: a waterlogged block is dried, anything else set to air, with no updates.
3. Left as they are, as the block put-back already does (F-22c75b):
   - other tests' plots;
   - ground a live test keeps;
   - this test's other kept boxes.
4. Each restore that clears fluid logs "`{test}`: N fluid cells outside its kept ground cleared, the first at … (its box … to …; X ms)".

Depth is unbounded at the lead's word: the open-water sea's curtain fell 27 below its island.

**Nothing granted**, and no check, bound or timeout is loosened. No fixture other than the new one is changed. At the lead's word, no rims are added to the other leaking fixtures: this change covers them.

Changed files:
- `FixtureGround` (the shell and its clearing);
- the new `NativeFixtureGroundGameTests`;
- the gametest `fabric.mod.json` (its entrypoint).

## The fixture

`NativeFixtureGroundGameTests.fluidThatRanOutOfItsKeptBoxIsClearedAsItIsPutBackAndWhatWasThereStays` (batch `fixtureGroundFluid`):
1. It stages water in a stone cup west of the box, within the shell, which cannot run.
2. It simulates the chunks round the box: with no player near, a chunk the test server does not tick runs no fluid (found on #442's CI fixture).
3. It reads the fluid already in the shell. Only fluid not there then is counted, since other tests' ground can stand there.
4. It keeps a 5×4×5 box ten up, lays a stone floor and sets a water source on the floor's east edge, so the water runs out and falls.
5. A hundred ticks on, it checks that water ran out of the box, and then puts the box back.
6. It checks that no new water is left round the box, and that the cup's water still stands.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an AI player speaks first on four occasions, the most pressing first: a warning of a monster
behind a person; an offer of food to someone hungry when it has some to spare, handed over only on their "yes please";
a request for something it needs, once an hour for each thing; and a word now and then when idle together. Every line
but a warning is held to each person's attention budget: one unasked line in three minutes, as their own preference
stretches or shortens it, more for someone who answers and less for someone who never does, both drifting back over a
day. Nothing unasked goes to someone out of earshot, in a countdown or away; nothing but a warning to someone fighting
or building fast. A player whose remarks are off says nothing first.

  - `CompanionSocials`: `unasked` (a line said first, through the lane's checks and typing delay) and `soundingOf`.
  - `PeerRadio`: each player's own last update, for its needs.

## Design changes and what was not built

- The budget governs the new occasions (offers, requests, banter). Greetings and the remarks from before (rain,
  nightfall, a passing word, work done) keep the limits they had, as the design's "as now" has them for greetings and
  goal changes; folding them into the budget would cut today's chatter to one line in three minutes per person.
- A warning is not held by the budget, nor kept from someone fighting (it is about the fight), but has its thirty seconds
  and needs earshot. A player whose remarks are off says nothing first, warnings included.
- With E-53, a player may ask the people about it for what it needs. `NativePeerLedgerGameTests`' teammate fixture checks
  that nothing at all is said of Sol's needs; it now turns Sol's remarks off, so that the team's channel alone is tested
  and that check still holds for every line.
- Not built: a remark on a person's build seen for the first time; a match or outing proposed when idle (E-27); asking
  peers for a need as a record through the team (E-51's turns, and E-40's share-out, are the places); offers of anything
  but food; "the ledger's record of who answers" kept across restarts (the budget is held in memory).

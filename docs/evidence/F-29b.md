# F-29b — A rescue asked within a minute, with a report that says where it can walk and what it can break

Date: 2026-11-08
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/improvising-1108
Build: the commit that adds this file, on main `e1d354a` with the design commit `4df6d1a` merged
Status: in progress. Built; the JUnit and the GameTests pass (below). Open: the evaluation engine's time from a spell's
start to its first ask on ten seeds, which needs a rescue-on arm and a model key (none in this container; the lead runs
the live arms).

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: with the strategist on, a player that has made no progress on its goal for a minute of game time
asks for a way out. Before, it waited for its watchdog's second spell of three minutes. Its own move elsewhere starts at
the same moment and the model is told of it, heading and all. The report now shows the blocks round it as it sees them,
with what it cannot see marked `?`. It says how far it could walk each way and what would stop it, how much room is over
its head, and what is under its feet. It says what its best pickaxe breaks and which blocks within reach it cannot
break, and what it can place.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`:
  - `rescue.RescueSlice` (new, pure): the box of cells round the feet (17 by 17 across, 4 below the feet and 8 above),
    masked to what sight reaches (`perceive`).
  - The walk's block-by-block step (`next`, `walk`): level, up a step with room to jump, or down a drop of one, and
    what stops it.
  - The room overhead (`up`), what is under the feet (`down`), the blocks within reach it cannot break, the grid of
    letters, and what each pickaxe tier harvests.
  - `RescueSliceTest` (9).
- `companion-model`: `RescuePrompt`, which said the player's own ways had "run for minutes and failed". It now says the
  player has been stuck about a minute, that the report shows what it sees, and that its own move stops while the plan
  runs, so the plan should not send it the same way.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `PortalRunCampaign`:
    - `RESCUE_AFTER_TICKS` (1,200) and a quiet clock that a move elsewhere does not reset; `openRescueSpell`;
      `progressMade`.
    - The move begun early (`relocateEarly`), counted as a watchdog spell only when its three minutes pass (`countSpell`).
    - The report (`rescueReport`, `slice`), and the ask's line.
  - `StrategistBridge`: it tells the campaign whether rescues are on.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: `campaignForFixtures`.
- gametest: `NativeRescueGameTests`: one new fixture. Its `rescued` helper now leaves out the ask's lines, which are
  among the rescue lines since this change.
- `tools/eval`:
  - `score.py`: asks counted apart from plans; each seed's median game seconds from the last progress to the ask, and
    every ask pooled.
  - `compare.py`: those, and the plans that got the player out.
- No new dependency; the manifest is unchanged.

Schema/configuration changes: the rescues' lines in the evaluation result now include `rescue asked: spell N, T ticks
after its last progress`. The scorer counts them as asks, not plans; an older result has none. Nothing new is saved in
the world. No setting added.

## What the design said, checked against the code

| Claim | Found |
| --- | --- |
| "F-29 waited 3,600 and then the local relocation" | It waited for the watchdog's second spell (`stuckLevel >= 2`), so at least two spells of 3,600 active ticks with no material progress |
| "a spell is counted once per five minutes as now" | The five minutes are the bridge's `RESCUE_GAP_MS`, between one player's asks, by the wall clock; they are kept |
| The relocation "still runs, in parallel" | While an ask is out the goal holds still (F-29's hold), and a plan runs before the move. The move is begun at the ask, not at the watchdog's three minutes, and goes on once the plan has ended. "In parallel" here means it is begun, told to the model, and not replaced |
| "never proposes deepslate with a wooden pickaxe" | In 1.21.1 a wooden pickaxe harvests deepslate. F-29's three failures at cobbled deepslate were players with no pickaxe at all. The report says what the tier it carries harvests |
| F-29's report was "only what it can see from where it stands" | It read every block of its 9×9×5 slice, the ones behind walls included. It now masks them (`?`). Sight here is the cells reached from its feet and head through see-through cells within the box, so it reaches round corners in open air: looser than a line of sight, stricter than the project's "exposed" rule (any face touching air) |
| Stuck minutes stay comparable | `score.py` counts three minutes for each watchdog spell. Spells are still counted at 3,600 active ticks with no progress: an early move's end does not reset the watchdog's clock, and a spell falls due while the early move is still under way (`countSpell`). With rescue off nothing changes: the trigger needs the bridge's `rescueOn` |

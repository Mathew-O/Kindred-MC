## Scope and implementation

- **A design's fingerprint** (`DesignFingerprint`, core, new). The cells a build fills, whatever fills them. It is aligned on its footprint's corner and compared at each of the four quarter turns.
  - The fingerprint is the same for the build turned, moved or in other materials.
  - Likeness is the cells both fill over the cells either fills, at the best turn. At 0.9 or more, two designs are one (a near copy). Another window or a moved door hardly moves it.
- **Reservations** (`DesignFingerprint.Registry`). Each player's designs are reserved before they are built. A near copy of another player's reservation is refused, naming whose it copies; its own design again is its own. Reservations are kept as text and read back.
- **Bases unique across players** (`PlayerRuntime.unique`).
  - When a player chooses its next base stage, the plan grown from its seed (E-29a) is reserved.
  - If that plan nearly copies another player's base, it takes the next of the stage's variants that does not. The log says so: "its plan 7 would copy Wren's cabin; plan 8 instead".
  - Each player's recorded bases are reserved as its own when its record is read.

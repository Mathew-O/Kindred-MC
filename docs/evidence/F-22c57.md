## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a builder going down its own scaffold column, the block underfoot dug out a step at a time,
stays on the column. That holds even when a bottom slab of the build, a roof's edge, is beside the column at the
level of the block underfoot. Before, it could come down on the slab's top half a block lower. Every walk from
there failed to start ("expanded 1, standing false"), and the cabin stopped short of its roof.

Found: `NativeLifeFirstGameTests`' cabin on #136's CI (run 42826864347), and the house cabin locally. Neither diff
touches builds. On the way down its column, the builder came to rest at y −48.5 on the cabin's bottom-slab roof
beside it.

Two ways onto the slab, both found with the staged fixture below:
1. **Off the column's middle.** The player's box is 0.6 wide. A body more than 0.2 off its cell's middle overlaps the
   next cell. As the block underfoot gives way, the slab in that cell catches it.
2. **The drop chased.** Centred, it fell cleanly. Then, in the break's collect phase, the dirt's drop was still above
   it as it landed: `next.getY() > player.getY() + 0.6`. So it jumped after the drop, drifted about 0.2 sideways in
   the air, and came down on the slab. This came from the per-tick positions of a failing copy.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `NativeGoToSkill`: an optional arrival tolerance, `centredWithin(within)`, from 0 to
  0.4. The default stays 0.4, and no other caller changes. This is the movement change the lead approved under MODES'
  F-35 freeze, option (ii-a).
- `NativePlayerSkills`: `native_goto` takes `centre_within`, building `centredWithin`. Without it, the walk is built
  as before, byte for byte.
- `BuildRunner`: each of its three "Down the scaffold" breaks (the down-first path, the BREAK of its own column, and
  F-22c46's blocked-high path) goes through `downTheScaffold`. More than 0.18 off the column's middle, it first walks
  "Over the middle of its scaffold", with `range` 0 and `centre_within` 0.15, up to three times for a cell, and then
  digs.
- `NativeBreakAction`: for an `underfoot` break only (every underfoot break: down its own scaffold, a shaft dug down, a
  pillar's descent, the dig-down for iron), the collect phase holds still, inputs stopped, while the drop
  falls onto it. A player's pickup reaches a block to either side. Every other break chases its drop as before.

Nothing is granted.

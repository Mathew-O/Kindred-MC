# F-22c8 — The last two blocks of a floor are broken, not walked between; a landing waited out, not a second

Date: 2026-11-05
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/ci-green-1105 (CI fully green, F-22)
Build: on top of `6effd66`; recorded in [validation](../validation.md)
Status: complete (JUnit and native)

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product (`ConstructionPlanner.next`): what is in the way of a build is broken top layer first, the
nearest to the player first.
- **The loop:** the last two blocks of a hideout's floor lay side by side, the player on one. The one underfoot came
  first. The only spot beside it to break it from was on the other block, and there the other block was underfoot and
  came first.
- **What it cost:** it walked from one to the other without end. A labelled hideout run walked between (869, -56, 919)
  and (870, -56, 919) to the end of its ticks. CI run 391's hideout failure ("0/345 placed - In the way of the build",
  its last walks among the floor's last cells at y -55 to -56) has the same shape.

Also, a waste of ticks: `BuildRunner` waits for the body to land (off its scaffold, into what it digs) with a one-second
wait. That came to about 170 waits, 20 ticks each, in a hideout.

Changed files:
- `companion-core` `ConstructionPlanner.next`: of the top layer, a block it can break from where it stands comes first;
  otherwise as before.
- JUnit `ConstructionPlannerTest.theLastTwoBlocksOfAFloorSideBySideAreBrokenNotWalkedBetween`.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `MovementSkills.Landing`, and `native_wait`'s optional `landed`: a wait over once the body is on the ground or in
    water, the seconds given at most;
  - `BuildRunner`'s landing waits use it;
  - `BuildRunner` names the stance a break walks to in its label, and the cell a failed step was at in its stop message
    (diagnostics).

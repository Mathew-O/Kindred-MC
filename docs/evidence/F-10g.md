## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior. A native player on a walk:
- sees a change on the way ahead (a block put down in it, a hole opened, lava come in) and steers round it at a run, rather than walking into it and standing two seconds before re-planning;
- with a wary walk, keeps out of the notice of hostile mobs it does not mean to fight. Their ranges are vanilla's own (a wither skeleton notices a player it can see at 16 blocks, a zombie at 35, a blaze at 48). It passes them at more than that where the ground allows, and plans again as they move;
- fights by choice: the End goal's walks are wary of everything but what its plan wants (blazes while rods are short, endermen while pearls are).

The user asked whether, running down a fortress hallway, it could steer round a three-high pillar put in the middle, or through the one lane left when two thirds were blocked, without stopping; and whether it knows where a fast mob like a wither skeleton would notice it, and can keep out of that.

Changed modules/files:
- `NativeGoToSkill`:
  - every tick it checks the next ten waypoints, and the moment one can no longer be stood on, or has turned hazardous, it plans again from where it is, keeping its inputs; mid-jump, on landing. Re-routes have their own budget (forty, at least three ticks apart).
  - straight runs keep a margin beyond half the body's width, since an eased turn lags its aim and a line that only just cleared a corner left the body caught on it;
  - with no long straight run in sight it steers at a point two and a half blocks along the route, measured from where it is on the route, rather than at the next block's centre;
  - in the air, a waypoint flown over counts as passed, so it no longer swings back toward one it jumped across;
  - wary walks (`avoid`, with the `engage` kinds exempt) price the route by each hostile mob's unease and plan again when one moves three blocks or a new one comes within 24. A piglin counts as hostile while the player wears no gold.
- `NativeNavigator`: `avoiding(dangers)`; a cell's cost gains ten times the sum of the mobs' unease there.
- `MobSenses` (core, new): vanilla 1.21.1's follow ranges, whether sight is needed, what noticing leads to (attack, only when provoked, never), ranged attacks, and whether the mob outpaces a walking player. A sneaking player is noticed at four fifths of the range. Unease is nothing beyond a mob's range plus two and a half blocks, at least half anywhere inside, and one beside it. A mob that cannot see the player counts for a third of its range.
- `PortalRunCampaign.goWary`, `StrongholdLeg.wanted`: the End goal's exploring, travelling and fortress walks are wary.
- `PlayerRuntime.nativeGotoAvoiding`, `NativePlayerSkills` (`avoid`, `engage`).
- `NativeDynamicPathGameTests` (new); `MobSensesTest` (new).

Dependencies satisfied: F-10c, F-10d.
Schema/configuration changes: none.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

Not tested / known failures:
- Wary routes give a berth only where the ground allows. In a hallway there is no way round, and the route goes on through: waiting, sneaking or taking another hallway is not chosen yet.
- It does not sneak past at the edge of a mob's range, which would shrink the range to four fifths.
- Only the End goal's own walks are wary; the Nether goal's walks and the campaign's other walks are not.
- Mobs that are already its target are fought or backed away from by the campaign's fight rules, as before.

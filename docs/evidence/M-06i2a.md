## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (M-06i2's seed comparison, seed 20261030, `bases=true`). Kindred and Sol never held land.
- **Where they started:** all three players spawn at the world spawn, Kindred at (0, 125, 0). That is inside the pack's 500-block spawn exclusion (`packs/factions/config/kindred/factions.properties`, `spawnExclusion=500`).
- **How the first claim was chosen:** with bases on, a founder's first claim is the ground its first base will stand on (`prospectiveSite`, #238, chosen on seen ground since #297). That ground was near the spawn, inside the exclusion, so `FactionPlay.beyondSpawn` pushed the claim some 500 blocks out on its bearing from spawn.
- **Why the target moved:** with every look, the prospective site moved, and the pushed-out target with it.
- **The trace:** a diagnostic run (36,000 ticks, a temporary log line per faction choice, not committed) shows Kindred choosing "Claim the base site" 25 times in nine minutes, at chunks x 29 to 32. It walked from x 67 to 276 and back. On the full run, 64 of its 65 faction walks were refused.

**The change (Factions-only, `PlayerRuntime.prospectiveSite`).** A prospective site whose chunk lies inside the spawn exclusion is no prospect: it is neither the first claim nor the bases' anchor.
- **Then:** the founder takes its first land as without bases, `beyondSpawn` from where it stands, and its base follows on that land (M-06i2's own-claims turn).
- **Unchanged:** a site outside the exclusion is still the first claim, as #238 has it.
- **Also:** `Territory.Chunk.nearestToSpawn` is made public; for fixtures, `PlayerRuntime.basesLoadedForTest` and `baseAnchorForTest`.

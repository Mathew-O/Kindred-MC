## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player below ground at night that wants a bucket of water, with none down there that it knows of, closes its way in and waits for daylight. It no longer climbs out to the surface for it. Water below ground it still goes to. In the morning it climbs up for the water as before.

Found in the live runs on `10a60d5` (seed 8675309, strategist off):
1. At nightfall Sol was climbing toward the surface for water, three blocks under it. Once out, the night's rule dug it in again ("Dig in for the night: step down"). Once in, below ground, the water step climbed it out again ("Climb toward the surface for water: back the way it came").
2. It went up and down a step a second for most of a night. Water took 10% of its time in that run, and the night's digging in another 6%.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign`:
  - the water step (`FILL_WATER`): at night below ground, with no water known down there, `daylightBelow` seals the way in or waits ("Wait for daylight"), as the night's rule for logs does;
  - `fillWater`: at night below ground, only water that is below ground too, with no sky over it;
  - `night`: the night test, with the shelter-early habit, now in one place.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

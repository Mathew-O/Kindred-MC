## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a player taking down its scaffolding, whose last block stands down in a hollow it can drop into but
not walk back out of, goes down and takes the block down when it holds enough blocks to pillar back out. Before, it stood on
the ground above and the build stopped: "Scaffolding at … is out of reach to take down". With too few blocks, it stays out of
the hollow as before.

Found: #121's CI (run 42750254641), `NativeLifeFirstGameTests.hungryWithTooLittleToLastItHuntsFirstThenBuildsItsBase`. #121
does not touch builds. The same message failed #118's CI (run 42744344789, the house cabin), whose diff touches only the
pasture and the ranch.
- From the run's log: the cabin's last scaffold stood in a hollow three below the ground. F-22c40's "Scaffold 2 up to take
  down" got the player up out of it, and it took the higher tops down from up there.
- It ended on the ground at (…622/623, -53, …044). The last block, (…623, -56, …043), was down in the hollow, out of sight
  from above.
- The way down was a drop of three, walkable one way only. `ConstructionPlanner` keeps only spots it can walk back from
  (`returnable`, and the test `neverABreakFromWhereItCouldNotWalkBackToTheBuild`), so no stance was left and the build
  stopped.
- Ten local copies of both cabin fixtures on main passed; the form did not reproduce in the native fixtures. Its end state
  is staged in the planner test below.

Changed files:
- `companion-core` `ConstructionPlanner`:
  - `next` gains an overload taking `spare`, the scaffold blocks the player holds beyond the build's need. The existing
    overloads pass 0.
  - Once built, with a scaffold top out of reach from every spot it can walk back from, the take-down is planned once more
    with a wider set of spots. A spot also counts as one it can come back from when a scaffold of at most `spare + 1`
    blocks under its feet takes it to a step back: the blocks it can spare, and the one the break gives back. The scaffold
    is no higher than `MAX_DROP` (three) and rises only through open cells with head room; water and lava are not
    passable.
  - That way back is worked out on the site with the scaffold tops taken away, so it never climbs out on the block it is
    about to break.
  - `returnable(…, blocks)` and `pillarsBack` carry it. Only the take-down uses it; placing and breaking what is in the way
    are unchanged.
- `companion-server-adapter` `BuildRunner`: `spareScaffold` counts the scaffold blocks held beyond the bill's need, by the
  rule `scaffoldSlot` already uses, and passes it to `next`.

Nothing is granted: the pillar out is of the player's own blocks.

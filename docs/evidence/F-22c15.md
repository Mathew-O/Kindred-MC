## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: [F-22c10](F-22c10.md)'s placement guard catches a builder walking to and fro between two
blocks, each placed from the other's spot. Planned again for the block it set out for two walks ago, it places the one
between from where it stands, and clears its record of the two. But the runtime may ask for the next step again before
that placement starts. Planned afresh, with the record cleared, the step was the first block again, a walk away. The
loop went on: the guard fired every second pass, and its placement never started.

- **CI on PR #25** (`b3f1590`, run 42030233893): `NativeBunkerGameTests` stopped at 266 of 345, walking between two
  cobblestone stances for the rest of its ticks.
- **Reproduced locally**, with temporary logging at the guard (not committed): 1 of 4 copies stopped at 181 of 345.
  - At x43, planned for the block at x43 (to be placed from x41), the guard fired and yielded the block at x41 from
    where it stood.
  - The next pass logged "last null before null", the step at x43 again, and a walk to x41.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `BuildRunner`: the guard's placement is held (`heldPlace`) while the builder
stands where it chose it, on the ground, and the block is still to go. It is let go when any placement task ends,
whether the block went down or not.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/aiplayer goal nether [bot]`, or **Start Nether run** in the K panel, sets a goal the player pursues on its own. Progress appears in the panel and the server log (`<name> goal at x y z: <stage>: <why> - <task>`). `/aiplayer goal none` stops it.

How it works:
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core, pure): from a snapshot of the player's own items and nearby owned workstations, names the next material step of the route (logs, table, wooden pickaxe, cobblestone, stone pickaxe and sword, iron, furnace and smelting, iron pickaxe, bucket, diamonds, diamond pickaxe, water, obsidian, flint, flint and steel, frame, ignition), with upkeep (collect smelted iron, hunt when out of food) first. A lost or worn-out pickaxe with cobblestone at hand goes straight back to a stone pickaxe.
- `WorldKnowledge` (adapter): a bounded incremental sweep (32 blocks across, 24 up and down, 6,000 block reads and 24 line-of-sight checks per tick) that remembers only blocks exposed to open air; ores and fluids also need a line of sight. Lake beds do not count as reachable. Obsidian under the water poured to make it does count.
- `PortalRunCampaign` (adapter): turns one planner step into one bounded skill: `native_goto`, `native_break`, `native_craft`, `native_place`, `native_smelt`, `native_bucket`, `native_swap`, `native_attack`, `native_ignite_portal`, `native_wait`. It digs staircases and two-high tunnels, takes the crafting table and an emptied furnace along when it leaves, clears leaves in the way, skips targets it cannot reach (with growing back-off, and never from the same spot), explores within 192 blocks of home away from open water, makes obsidian by pouring water beside or onto the shore of a lava pool and mining it with water guarding the hole, builds the frame with cobblestone corners, and lights it with its own flint and steel.
- Every task goes through `PlayerRuntime` admission, the paid activity, the durable action ledger and body permission checks, exactly like the commands. Nothing is granted, copied or teleported.
- Safety reflexes: swim to the nearest dry ground from deep water; respawn after death (F-25b4) and try once to recover the drops.

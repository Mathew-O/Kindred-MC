## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/aiplayer goal nether [bot]`, or **Start Nether run** in the K panel, sets a goal the player pursues on its own. Progress appears in the panel and the server log (`<name> goal at x y z: <stage>: <why> - <task>`). `/aiplayer goal none` stops it.

How it works:
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core, pure): from a snapshot of the player's own items and nearby owned workstations, names the next material step of the route (logs, table, wooden pickaxe, cobblestone, stone pickaxe and sword, iron, furnace and smelting, iron pickaxe, bucket, diamonds, diamond pickaxe, water, obsidian, flint, flint and steel, frame, ignition), with upkeep (collect smelted iron, hunt when out of food) first. A lost or worn-out pickaxe with cobblestone at hand goes straight back to a stone pickaxe.
- `WorldKnowledge` (adapter): a bounded incremental sweep (32 blocks across, 24 up and down, 6,000 block reads and 24 line-of-sight checks per tick) that remembers only blocks exposed to open air; ores and fluids also need a line of sight. Lake beds do not count as reachable. Obsidian under the water poured to make it does count. Cave floor it has seen (two blocks of air over stone, sky light 7 or less) is remembered once per 4×4×4 cell as a place to explore.
- `PortalRunCampaign` (adapter): turns one planner step into one bounded skill: `native_goto`, `native_break`, `native_craft`, `native_place`, `native_smelt`, `native_bucket`, `native_swap`, `native_attack`, `native_ignite_portal`, `native_wait`. Ores are found the way players find them: exposed ore in view first, then walking into caves it has seen toward the depth where the ore is common (coal near Y=48, iron near Y=16, diamonds and lava lakes near Y=-58), in strides of about eight blocks to cave cells it has not walked yet, looking around after each stride; only then does it dig, by staircase down and then branch mining (20-block legs, three blocks apart, zigzagging). In the dark underground it crafts torches from coal it can spare and sticks beyond those kept for the iron and diamond pickaxes, and places one wherever it stands in no block light. It digs staircases and two-high tunnels, takes the crafting table and an emptied furnace along when it leaves, clears leaves in the way, skips targets it cannot reach (with growing back-off, and never from the same spot), explores within 192 blocks of home away from open water, makes obsidian by pouring water beside or onto the shore of a lava pool and mining it with water guarding the hole, builds the frame with cobblestone corners, and lights it with its own flint and steel.
- Every task goes through `PlayerRuntime` admission, the paid activity, the durable action ledger and body permission checks, exactly like the commands. Nothing is granted, copied or teleported.
- Progress watchdog: whatever the cause of a loop (pacing, bouncing between spots, nothing feasible), three minutes of game time spent working with no new stage, no new best in a counter the route needs and no new ground makes the player try somewhere else: 24 blocks off in a new heading (walking while that gets closer, then digging), the next heading, then home; the panel shows the attempts. Fights, cover, the night shelter, waiting for daylight and resting do not count as stuck time.
- Safety: swim to the nearest dry ground from deep water; fight a mob that is after the player as one recorded `native_fight` (chase and strike, conservation-checked), never stepping into lava, fire or a drop deeper than three blocks while chasing, and backing away from the edge when that leaves no way to the target; fight a creeper hit and run with a weapon, sprint away from one that starts to hiss, and run from it when unarmed; take cover behind an owned block (built up from the floor where needed) when a skeleton has a clear shot and cannot be fought, fight again any foe that comes within four blocks, back off when badly hurt, rest to heal; dig in on the surface at night, seal the way in with its own dirt or stone while it waits for daylight, and leave surface jobs for daylight; pick up obsidian, diamonds, raw iron or flint that a break left lying; an iron chestplate before the diamond descent, worn as soon as it is made; keep to ore near the tunnel underground; drop surplus stone when the inventory fills; respawn after death (F-25b4) and, unless a mob did it, try once to recover the drops; a fight is broken off once health falls below six, the line below which the goal never starts one, so backing off or taking cover takes over; a block that lands in its head space (gravel falling when a climb breaks the block under it) is broken before anything else, as urgent work that stops whatever else runs; a shield (one iron and six planks, the wood taken while trees are in sight before going down for iron; with that wood in hand the first raw iron is smelted and made into the shield at once, before the rest of the iron is mined; worn in the off hand) is raised while closing in on a shooter and lowered for each swing, and a creeper that starts to hiss nearby is faced behind it instead of run from, so a shielded player takes creepers on down to six health instead of ten; movement while an item is in use (a raised shield, food) is slowed to a fifth with no sprint, as a client does; a break refused only because the player was still moving is tried again rather than set aside as unreachable; a recorded fight is no longer cut short by the runtime's one-block retreat when a creeper lights up nearby, since the fight itself raises the shield toward it or runs; caves and ore within 12 blocks of where a mob killed the player are left alone for one in-game day; the area around a jump-down landing is set aside for a minute, so the last resort is not taken again into the same pool; and no standing spot beside lava at body height or under it, where it could flow in (a hole left by a bucket filled at a pool's edge refills from the sources around it).

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/aiplayer goal nether [bot]`, or **Start Nether run** in the K panel, sets a goal the player pursues on its own. Progress appears in the panel and the server log (`<name> goal at x y z: <stage>: <why> - <task>`). `/aiplayer goal none` stops it.

How it works:
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core, pure): from a snapshot of the player's own items and nearby owned workstations, names the next material step of the route (logs, table, wooden pickaxe, cobblestone, stone pickaxe and sword, iron, furnace and smelting, iron pickaxe, bucket, diamonds, diamond pickaxe, water, obsidian, flint, flint and steel, frame, ignition), with upkeep (collect smelted iron, hunt when out of food) first. A lost or worn-out pickaxe with cobblestone at hand goes straight back to a stone pickaxe.
- `WorldKnowledge` (adapter): a bounded incremental sweep (32 blocks across, 24 up and down, 6,000 block reads and 24 line-of-sight checks per tick) that remembers only blocks exposed to open air; ores and fluids also need a line of sight. Lake beds do not count as reachable. Obsidian under the water poured to make it does count. Cave floor it has seen (two blocks of air over stone, sky light 7 or less) is remembered once per 4×4×4 cell as a place to explore.
- `PortalRunCampaign` (adapter): turns one planner step into one bounded skill: `native_goto`, `native_break`, `native_craft`, `native_place`, `native_smelt`, `native_bucket`, `native_swap`, `native_attack`, `native_ignite_portal`, `native_wait`. Ores are found the way players find them: exposed ore in view first, then walking into caves it has seen toward the depth where the ore is common (coal near Y=48, iron near Y=16, diamonds and lava lakes near Y=-58), in strides of about eight blocks to cave cells it has not walked yet, looking around after each stride; only then does it dig, by staircase down and then branch mining (20-block legs, three blocks apart, zigzagging). In the dark underground it crafts torches from coal it can spare and sticks beyond those kept for the iron and diamond pickaxes, and places one wherever it stands in no block light. It digs staircases and two-high tunnels, takes the crafting table and an emptied furnace along when it leaves, clears leaves in the way, skips targets it cannot reach (with growing back-off, and never from the same spot), explores within 192 blocks of home away from open water, makes obsidian by pouring water beside or onto the shore of a lava pool and mining it with water guarding the hole, builds the frame with cobblestone corners, and lights it with its own flint and steel.
- Every task goes through `PlayerRuntime` admission, the paid activity, the durable action ledger and body permission checks, exactly like the commands. Nothing is granted, copied or teleported.
- Progress watchdog: whatever the cause of a loop (pacing, bouncing between spots, nothing feasible), three minutes of game time spent working with no new stage, no new best in a counter the route needs and no new ground makes the player try somewhere else: 24 blocks off in a new heading (walking while that gets closer, then digging), the next heading, then home; the panel shows the attempts. Fights, cover, the night shelter, waiting for daylight and resting do not count as stuck time.
- Safety: swim to the nearest dry ground from deep water; fight a mob that is after the player as one recorded `native_fight` (chase and strike, conservation-checked), never stepping into lava, fire or a drop deeper than three blocks while chasing, and backing away from the edge when that leaves no way to the target; fight a creeper hit and run with a weapon, sprint away from one that starts to hiss, and run from it when unarmed; take cover behind an owned block (built up from the floor where needed) when a skeleton has a clear shot and cannot be fought, fight again any foe that comes within four blocks, back off when badly hurt, rest to heal; dig in on the surface at night, seal the way in with its own dirt or stone while it waits for daylight, and leave surface jobs for daylight; pick up obsidian, diamonds, raw iron or flint that a break left lying; an iron chestplate before the diamond descent, worn as soon as it is made; keep to ore near the tunnel underground; drop surplus stone when the inventory fills; respawn after death (F-25b4) and, unless a mob did it, try once to recover the drops; a fight is broken off once health falls below six, the line below which the goal never starts one, so backing off or taking cover takes over; a block that lands in its head space (gravel falling when a climb breaks the block under it) is broken before anything else, as urgent work that stops whatever else runs; a shield (one iron and six planks, the wood taken while trees are in sight before going down for iron; with that wood in hand the first raw iron is smelted and made into the shield at once, before the rest of the iron is mined; worn in the off hand) is raised while a shooter it is closing in on draws its bow (and a moment after, for the arrow), so the rest of the way is covered at full pace, and lowered for each swing, and a creeper that starts to hiss nearby is faced behind it instead of run from, so a shielded player takes creepers on down to six health instead of ten, and faces one already hissing within six blocks however hurt it is (that fight is not broken off for low health); movement while an item is in use (a raised shield, food) is slowed to a fifth with no sprint, as a client does; a break refused only because the player was still moving is tried again rather than set aside as unreachable; a recorded fight is no longer cut short by the runtime's one-block retreat when a creeper lights up nearby, since the fight itself raises the shield toward it or runs; caves and ore within 12 blocks of where a mob killed the player are left alone for one in-game day; underground and outmatched for its gear (a creeper, or a shooter while hurt or with company, without a shield; two or more mobs after it without a shield; health below 12, or 8 with a shield), it walls off the ways in where it stands if that is a passage (four openings or fewer), or backs into the last such spot of its staircase and walls itself in there, waits behind the wall until healed (at most two minutes), unless a mob on foot is already within reach (or is one it is already fighting that a hit knocked a few blocks back) or it is being hit, when it fights back where it stands instead, leaves the wall standing and marks the cave dangerous for five minutes, so it digs on elsewhere; in a passage it waits for a mob on foot to come to it (up to ten seconds) rather than chase it out into the open cave; a cave in view that shows mobs it is no match for yet (a shooter or a creeper, or two mobs, without a shield; three with one) is walled off from the passage on that side before any of them comes for it, and marked dangerous for five minutes; the iron pickaxe is made as soon as three iron are in hand (after the shield's), rather than after all the route's iron; without a shield, caves are explored only where no mob can be heard (none within sixteen blocks of the player or of the cave spot, as a player hears zombies and skeletons through rock; endermen aside), otherwise its iron comes from its own staircases and branch mines; when the watchdog cannot tunnel toward a new spot (water or lava on every side) it climbs instead; an opening with a mob standing in it is not one it tries to seal (it fights instead); with no water known for its bucket while underground, it climbs toward the surface (where lakes and rivers are) instead of tunnelling about blind at depth; a staircase step is not dug where it would open onto a drop deeper than the navigator allows, or lead down into a danger zone, and a pit it dug itself is filled with a block so its tunnel can carry on across (no fixture yet: the GameTest plots sit below the depth where the iron staircase starts); without a shield the last-resort jump from a ledge is not taken while mobs are within twelve blocks of the landing; water for a bucket that lies well overhead of an underground player is climbed toward by staircase instead of walked to, and the opportunistic top-up of a water bucket only counts water within 24 blocks and six levels as close; a provoked enderman counts as a mob it is outmatched by without an iron sword, so it walls itself into its two-high passage, where the enderman cannot follow; tunnelling never advances or steps down into water; eight spare sticks are kept beyond those for the pickaxes still to make (four replacements for ones that wear out, a log's worth) and topped up only while trees are in sight or from wood already carried, never by a climb back up from below; the area around a jump-down landing is set aside for a minute, so the last resort is not taken again into the same pool; a step of the watchdog's move elsewhere that fails three times ends that move, as for any plan step (it once retried one step down 516 times); and no standing spot beside lava at body height or under it, where it could flow in (a hole left by a bucket filled at a pool's edge refills from the sources around it); with no sword or axe (just respawned, say) it keeps away from a mob on foot that is not yet within reach and takes cover from a shooter instead of starting a fist fight; and a block that reaches into its eyes from the next cell (standing at the edge of its own) is dug out like one in its own head space.

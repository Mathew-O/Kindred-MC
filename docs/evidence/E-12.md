# E-12 — Personal milestone graph and standing progression policy
Date: 2026-10-18 EDT; report 2026-10-18.
Antigravity / uncommitted main, no commits. Complete for this feature.

## Scope and implementation
User-visible behavior: Personal milestone graph and standing progression policy directing autonomous AI player progression from empty inventory through basic wood, stone, iron, shelter, diamond gear, and stronghold location. The agent autonomously advances its active progression frontier and prioritizes re-acquiring lost essential gear (recovery frontier) without human intervention or mode toggling.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`:
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.MilestoneId`: Enumerated 15 progressive milestones: `GATHER_WOOD`, `CRAFT_WOOD_TOOLS`, `MINE_COBBLESTONE`, `CRAFT_STONE_TOOLS`, `CRAFT_FURNACE`, `SMELT_RESOURCES`, `CRAFT_IRON_TOOLS`, `EQUIP_IRON_ARMOR`, `CRAFT_SHIELD`, `SURVIVE_FIRST_NIGHT`, `ESTABLISH_SHELTER`, `SECURE_FOOD_SOURCE`, `MINE_DEEP_ORES`, `CRAFT_DIAMOND_GEAR`, `LOCATE_STRONGHOLD`.
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.ProgressionObservation`: Immutable record capturing physical inventory summary, workstation presence (placed crafting table, placed furnace, smelting status), time/environment status (night, shelter), and work assignments (assigned stone patch, assigned log patch).
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.MilestoneStatus`: Status enumeration: `LOCKED`, `UNLOCKED_NOT_MET`, `PHYSICALLY_MET`, `HISTORICALLY_ACHIEVED`, and `LOST_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`.
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.Milestone`: Immutable node in DAG with prerequisite list, physical predicate (`Predicate<ProgressionObservation>`), and recovery policy (`CAN_REGRESS` vs `DURABLE`).
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.MilestoneSnapshot`: Evaluated status of an individual milestone with timestamp, physical met status, and historical achievement boolean.
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.ProgressionState`: Overall progression snapshot including all milestone snapshots, active frontier milestones, and recovery frontier milestones.
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.MilestoneGraph`: Directed acyclic progression graph constructing standard 1.21.1 survival path, evaluating physical observations against historical achievement history, discovering active frontier (unmet milestones with satisfied prerequisites) and recovery frontier (regressible milestones achieved historically but lacking physical gear in current inventory).
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.ProgressionRecommendation`: Recommended task candidate with skill name (`native_craft`, `native_place`, `native_smelt`, `native_equip`, `native_mine`, `native_traverse`), parameters, priority (`Priority.PROJECT` / `Priority.ROUTINE`), need (60.0 to 75.0), and affinities.
  - `dev.kindred.core.progression.ProgressionPolicy`: Decision policy evaluating `ProgressionState` and generating ranked task recommendations prioritizing recovery frontier ahead of active frontier.
  - `MilestoneGraphTest.java`: 6 pure JUnit unit tests verifying DAG topology, milestone prerequisites, physical postcondition verification, and lost-gear recovery state transitions.
  - `ProgressionPolicyTest.java`: 6 pure JUnit unit tests verifying recipe recommendations across wood, stone, iron, and equipment tiers, and recovery recommendation generation.
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `PlayerRuntime.java`:
    - Integrated `MilestoneGraph`, `ProgressionObservation`, `ProgressionState`, and persistent historical achievements (`progressionHistory`) into `PlayerRuntime.Entry`.
    - Added `observeProgression` method utilizing direct spatial workstation lookup via `body.findNearbyBlock(Blocks.CRAFTING_TABLE, 4.5)` and `body.findNearbyBlock(Blocks.FURNACE, 4.5)` without exhausting the per-tick block scan budget.
    - Integrated milestone-driven progression candidate generation in `evaluateScheduledRoutineCandidates`, prioritizing tool upgrades and crafting table placement ahead of raw patch mining.
    - Guarded stone patch mining so players without pickaxes craft wooden pickaxes before attempting to mine stone.
  - `NativeProgressionGameTests.java`: 3 comprehensive Fabric acceptance GameTests testing physical reflection of inventory, autonomous progression without human intervention, and lost-gear recovery frontier.
  - `fabric.mod.json`: Registered `dev.kindred.fabric.NativeProgressionGameTests` in GameTest entrypoints.

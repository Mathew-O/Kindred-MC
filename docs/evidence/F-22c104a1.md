# F-22c104a1 — The second-zombie test names each goal step with its health and the zombies' distances

Date: 2026-11-22. Test-only diagnostic. No product code.

## Why

`NativeBackOffFlipGameTests.hurtWithAZombieThatKeepsPaceAndASecondComingUpBehindItFightsBothAndLives` still fails on main with F-22c104 (#430): 2 of 20 on `47dbfbc5`, "Alive, never killed (switches 0: back off @6, second @65, )".

In both failing runs the player:
1. backed off twice;
2. turned to "heal before going on";
3. was slain in "Fight zombie" within the second (ticks 166 and 147), with no eat completed.

In the 18 passing runs one eat completed before the fight.

A fixture that cut the eat short at the heal passed 5 of 5 on main (both zombies killed). So the cut eat may be a symptom of the fight coming while it is lowest. The test's message named only its back-offs and hole-ups, not the step it died on.

## The change (test-only)

`NativeBackOffFlipGameTests.run`, shared by the class's back-off tests, records each change of the goal step and its task on the player's first life:
- the tick since the zombie was set on it;
- the player's health and food;
- each zombie's distance, or "dead".

It goes into the test's log line ("its steps …") and into the "Alive, never killed" failure message, so the next CI failure names the step, the health and the distances at the fight. Its checks, bounds and time limit are unchanged.

# F-25c4r — With no sword or axe, a fight strikes with the pickaxe, not the empty hand

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player with no sword or axe that has to fight strikes with what in its hotbar hits hardest, its pickaxe as a rule, as a player would. Before, it struck with whatever it held, and that could be an empty hand.

Found in the long live run on `a286cc8` (seed 8675309, 240,000 ticks, strategist off):
- Kindred placed a torch at Y=40, then a zombie came at it. Its way back was refused ("No walkable route"), so its goal fought the zombie.
- Kindred had no sword or axe, but it had the iron pickaxe it had made two minutes before (its iron wanted still counted none for another). The fight's record shows nothing in its hand for all 203 of its ticks: the slot it had left selected was empty.
- Nine critical punches, at a point and a half each, did not kill the zombie. Kindred broke off at 7 health, and the zombie killed it at 14:50:12.

A fight given no weapon struck with the item left in hand. Sol's, in the same run, happened to hold its stone pickaxe from digging, and killed its zombie in five blows (F-25c4o).

Changed modules/files:
- `Weapons.hardestInHotbar`: the hotbar slot whose item has vanilla's highest main-hand attack damage, if it beats the hand.
- `NativeFightAction`: given no weapon, it selects that slot when the fight begins. A weapon given is used as before.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

Hunts are left as they were: their strike's record allows no item but the one struck with to change, and that one only to wear, not to break.

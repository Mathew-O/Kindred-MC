# F-22c2 — A slice booked to its period's end is held over it, however early it lapses

Date: 2026-11-05
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/ci-green-1105 (CI fully green, F-22)
Build: on top of `6effd66` (main); recorded in [validation](../validation.md)
Status: complete (native and JUnit).

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: F-11g holds a walk, goto, traverse, retreat or wait whose slice lapsed at its accounting
period's end, then carries it into the next period. The hold asked whether the wall clock had reached the period's end
at the moment of the lapse. A slice booked to the period's end lapses short of it:
- the lapse is seen by the permit's monotonic clock, against a length in whole milliseconds;
- a renewed slice counts from the renewal's request but is sized at its acknowledgement (F-11c), so the write's latency
  comes off its end;
- a renewal that would gain less than `RENEW_GAIN_MIN` (250 ms) is not made (F-11f), so with a slow write each renewal
  is made and loses its latency again.

So it lapsed before midnight by the latency (on a slow write) or by the rounding (otherwise). Then it was not held and
stopped ("Prepaid activity deadline reached"). The proposed `now.plus(RENEW_GAIN_MIN)` margin covers the rounding but
not a write slower than 250 ms, which then still stops the skill.

Changed files:
- `companion-scheduler`:
  - `ActivityPermit.bookedUntil()`: the reservation's wall-clock end.
  - `PaidActivitySession.bookedUntil()`: the latest acknowledged slice's booked end, kept after it lapses or is
    stopped.
- `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime.holdOverPeriodEnd`:
  - A lapse is held when the wall clock is at or past the period's end, as before, or when its slice was booked to the
    period's end.
  - The next period is asked for only once the end has passed, so none of the old period's last moments are booked
    again. `PERIOD_HOLD_LIMIT` (5 s) still bounds the hold.
  - Test seam `delayBudgetWritesForTest`: each budget write held for a set time before it goes to the store, captured
    per session as `store::saveBudget` was.
- JUnit `PaidActivitySessionTest.aSliceRenewedToItsDeadlineLapsesShortOfItByTheWriteAndKeepsWhereItWasBookedTo`.
- gametest `NativeAccountingGameTests.aWalkRunningAtItsPeriodsEndOnASlowStoreCarriesOnIntoTheNext`:
  - staged on the field: a stone way 72 long, the player at its west end;
  - budget writes held 400 ms;
  - midnight 8 s away, at a live server's pace of 50 ms a tick;
  - a goto of 68 blocks.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

With a write slower than 250 ms, a slice near its period's end is renewed again and again, each renewal gaining
nothing (one write per renewal, until the end). This is unchanged and left open.
Updated documentation: none for users (behavior as documented for F-11g).

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:

- A fourth preset, `rough`: swears ("Rain again? Damn it."), makes dark jokes about dying in the game ("That zombie almost
  sent me back to spawn lol."), teases other AI players harder ("Well damn, Sol lives. Thought a creeper got you."). A
  player with no preset is rough when it is playful and combative from 80 up: Sol, of the starters.
- Flirting: a sociable player with some humour (sociability 75, humour 55 up: Kindred) flirts with other AI players
  ("Oh hey, Echo. Looking good in that armor, not gonna lie.") and, in conversations between AI players, when it has no
  news ("Is it hot in here, Echo, or is that just the lava?"). Never sexual.
- Who hears it: a person hears swear words only after `/aiplayer prefs swearing on`, dark jokes only after
  `prefs dark on`, flirting only after `prefs flirting on`; everyone else hears the line softened ("Dang it.") or plain.
  The strictest of everyone in earshot decides a line said to several. The operator may say yes to swearing and dark
  jokes for everyone who said nothing (`KINDRED_ROUGH_AUDIENCE=on`); a person's own `off` still holds, and flirting is
  never the server's to say. Jokes off means none of the three. Some words are never said whatever anyone asked:
  slurs, sexual words, threats to a person ("kys", "go die").
- Chat styles: `proper`, `casual` (lower case, no full stop at the end, `u`, `ur`, `rn`, `idk`, `tbh`, `wanna`),
  `gamer` (lower case, no punctuation, more shorthand, now and then its own word: `ngl`, `fr`, `lol`, `xd`, `lmao`,
  `bruh`), `chaotic` (its word more often, a pleased last word stretched). By personality: Kindred casual, Sol gamer,
  Echo proper; an even personality proper. Names, numbers, coordinates, commands and item ids are never changed.
- The voice (E-20a) is told, per listener, whether it may swear, make dark jokes or flirt, the hard limits, and how the
  player types; its line may swear only to someone who said yes, and every line from every lane passes the same last
  check at delivery.

```text
/aiplayer expression plain|dry|playful|rough|auto [<player>]
/aiplayer chatstyle [proper|casual|gamer|chaotic|auto] [<player>]
/aiplayer prefs swearing|dark on|off|default      /aiplayer prefs flirting on|off
KINDRED_EXPRESSION_CAP=...|rough (default rough)   KINDRED_ROUGH_AUDIENCE=on (default off)
```

## Design changes and what was not built

- Clean by default, as the contract says: a preset never opts anyone in. The owner's "build in" is met by the rough
  preset on by personality (Sol), the cap's default raised to rough, and the operator's one switch for a server of adults.
- A rough line's plain variant for a person who did not say yes is the same line softened, not a different line; a dark
  joke's and a flirt's is the plain line.
- Typos and capitals for excitement are not built; the chat style never changes what a line says.
- Not built: rough or flirty lines in E-53's offers and requests, a flirt answered by a person, a dark line on death or
  respawn.

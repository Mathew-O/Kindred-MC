CI on e689e91c (run 43729280146) failed 1 of 891: `NativeOwnRanchGameTests.withNoSeedsItEarnsThemFromTheGrassThenKeepsAPairOfChickens`.
- The chickens' pen got 23 of 24 blocks. Its oak fence gate was refused "Block placement produced no effect or was obstructed"
  from six spots in about a second. BuildRunner tried what it gave up on once more at once, and "The pen could not be built".
- Working from where it stands, the builder placed every fence from inside the pen, and the gate, set only from outside, was
  left the pen's last gap. In the local passes the gate went in 24th of 24 here, 2nd to 4th on main, with 2 to 3 walks to place
  against 20 to 21.
- The same give-up came on main in #357's CI (run 43722834395 attempt 2, the pigs): the fragility is main's. E-71b1's order,
  which #359 first carried, left the gate the pen's last gap and made it likelier. CI GREEN filed it as F-22c93.

The fix (`BuildRunner`):
- A placement refused with something in its cell names it in the log ("refused with [minecraft:chicken] in the cell"). A
  player there is named, not waited on: its own body, a stance off the middle, is no reason to wait.
- With an animal or another mob in a cell it gave up on, it goes back to it up to 5 rounds (`OBSTRUCTED_ROUNDS`), 2 s apart,
  the spots it was refused from usable again in each, before giving up as before. A first version kept those spots shunned;
  they ran out round the gate in a few rounds, and the plan was "No way to reach" the gate, waited on until the test's end.
- With nothing in the cell, one round, as before.

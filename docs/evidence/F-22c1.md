## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the fixture: `NativeNetherGameTests.vanillaPortalEntryPersistsDimensionAndExactNativeStateAcrossRuntimeRestart`
left vanilla to choose both ends of its trip. The arrival depended on terrain the fixture does not control, and its
assertions on where the arrival was. The product behaved as vanilla does; no product code changed.

- **The facts, from Minecraft 1.21.1's own classes (`javap` of the mapped jar):**
  - `TestServer.runTestBatches` lays every run's tests from a random origin within ±14,999,992 blocks.
  - `TestStructurePlacer` is made with `clearBeforeBatch` false: the grid grows through the run and nothing a test
    built is cleared, lit portals included.
  - `PortalForcer.getPortalPos` links to the portal block nearest the scaled point by full 3D distance, searching a
    square of 16 in the Nether and 128 in the Overworld. The scaled point keeps the traveller's Y.
- **What that did:** at a random origin the Nether end was a portal vanilla made in whatever lay there. The runs below
  show it at the lava sea, 15 blocks from the scaled point. Then the walk out and back failed ("No walk back into the
  portal admitted", "The walk out of the portal did not finish"), or the blaze set 2.5 blocks beyond the player was
  not seen ("No observed-Blaze attack candidate"). The way back could also link to a portal another test left lit
  nearer the scaled point ("Return portal preserves expected 8:1 linked region"); the CI failures carry no
  coordinates to say which.

Changed files:
- gametest `NativeNetherGameTests`:
  - The fixture lifts its Overworld frame and ground to Y=100 over its own structure.
  - It lays the Nether end itself: a closed netherrack room at the scaled point of the walk in, at Y=100, with a frame
    of its own lit there (`NetherPortal.getNewPortal(...).createPortal()`). Both are stated as fixture staging in the
    code.
  - At Y=100 the only portal blocks near either scaled point are the fixture's own. The body still enters and returns
    through vanilla portal collision.
  - The Nether room's chunks are held loaded for the test's length; both portals are put out as it ends.
  - The failures now say where the body arrived and which portals vanilla chose among (`portalsFound`), and every run
    logs it.

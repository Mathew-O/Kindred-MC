## Scope and implementation

**Found:**

  `NativeOwnRanchGameTests.withNoSeedsItEarnsThemFromTheGrassThenKeepsAPairOfChickens` failed "The pair penned and a

**What the failing run shows.** The pen checks now name the pen and where each animal stands from its post (`fromPost`,
test-only). With that, a later run (1 in 6) showed:
- the pen complete: 23 fences, the gate shut;
- both adults outside it, at (−2, 0, 14) and (6, 0, 20);
- as the run ended, the chick standing a block up inside the pen.

No gate, attack or death line falls between the run's end and the check.

**Cause:** to craft the pen's gate, `RanchRunner.craft` sets down the crafting table it carries with
`campaign.placeTable(body)`, which has no keep-off. The wheat plot keeps its own off its plot. Set down within reach, the
table went into the pen's footprint, a step a penned animal hopped the fence by.

**The change:**
- `RanchRunner` sets its table down with `placeTable(body, this::keepOff)`. `keepOff` covers:
  - the pen's footprint, fences and gate included;
  - a block round it;
  - the gate's approach two out, a block either side of the gate's line;
  - from the floor below to two above.
- `PortalRunCampaign`'s last resort, the walk to room for a workstation, honours a keep-off too. Before, it could walk
  back into the excluded spots.

A first version kept the table off the footprint alone. It then stood at the gate's front once and shut the player in:
"No walkable route … [Out of the pen]", 1 class run in 3. The ring and the approach are for that.

**Nothing granted.** The table is the player's own, set down elsewhere. No timeout, bound or check is loosened. The
fixture's own check was widened from the pen to the pen, its ring and its approach, which is a stricter check.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter`: `RanchRunner` and `PortalRunCampaign`.
- Test: `NativeOwnRanchGameTests`. `itsCraftingTableIsNeverSetDownInItsOwnPen` is new (batch `ownRanchTable`), and the
  chicken, wheat-cow and pig pen checks gain the `fromPost` message.

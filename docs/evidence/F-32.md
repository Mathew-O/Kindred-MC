## Design changes

- `match` visibility beside the design's seven, as on the Wire.
- A replaced fact ends (its `until` set) rather than being closed by a correction; only "forget that" sets `closedBy`.
- An opponent hears nothing of the web: E-20a's opponent tier drops every peer line, and the web adds only public facts before it, so the prompt carries nothing beyond what the opponent may know; the design's "public and what it saw" is met by never more, not by adding public lines for opponents.
- A web per player (its owner sees all of it) beside the world's; a query by anyone else filters by their relation, so what a player may *say* to someone follows the fact's visibility.
- Told facts map by key: a key naming a verb, or `<who>_at` as a sighting; others are the message's own business.
- A delivery that cites no event rests, once replayed, on its own `message` event, the Record's id for what was told.
- Bound: 20,000 relations, dropping only lapsed ones; open facts and corrections never.

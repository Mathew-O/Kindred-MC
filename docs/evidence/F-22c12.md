## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: once the librarian is home, the bring (E-45e) picks up what it took up: the rails, the
bridge's blocks, its boat. It walks to each drop's own cell, and one walk that failed left the drop where it lies.
- **CI on PR #25** (`6fe4ed1`, run 42019747225): a rail broken out of the way came down a block up, by the hall's wall.
  The walk to its cell found "No walkable route toward …108, -57, …957", and the rail was left: 62 of 63 back.
- **The fixture then failed:** `NativeBringGameTests.aLibrarianAcrossALakeIsRowedOverAndBroughtHome` waited for the rail
  until the runtime's scheduled stop removed Hal ("Hal still in play").
- **Locally** the drops came down on the ground each time (4 of 4 passed): where a drop lands is vanilla's scatter.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime`: after a failed walk to a drop's cell, the bring walks once more
to anywhere within 1.2 of it, as a player picks up what lies a block off (`Bring.reachTried`). Only then is the drop let
be, as before.

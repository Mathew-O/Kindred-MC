## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player building its Nether portal picks a site for the frame only where it can walk to the spot it would stand on to build it. A site past a wall, across a drop or on ground it cannot get to is passed over for the next.

Found in the closing full suite on `fbdb8ed`: `NativePortalCampaignGameTests.goalCastsAWholePortalFrameFromALavaPoolWithoutDiamonds` failed with "0 portal blocks, 0 obsidian".
1. The fixture lays an arena, walled to four high, with a lava pool in its floor. Beyond its west wall lay an earlier test's floor, at the same height (the test server keeps what each test changed).
2. The player filled its bucket at the pool, then looked for a frame site around where it stood. The first it found stood on that floor, past the wall.
3. Every walk to it was "No walkable route". Dropping the site did not help: from the same spot the same site was chosen again. It tried until its watchdog moved it on after three minutes of no progress.
4. Moved on, it dug through the wall and cast the frame out there, outside the area the fixture counts.

The same choice can be made in any world: a site on the far side of a cave wall, or on a ledge above.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign.choosePortalSite`: a site whose stand is not where the player stands is taken only if a walk finds its way there (the navigator's own search, 600 nodes, twelve blocks round). `PortalRunCampaign` is changed on `modest-faraday` only in its hole-up lines.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: the cast fixture lays the ground beyond its west wall itself, so its failure no longer depends on what an earlier test left there, and puts back everything it laid when it ends (`FixtureGround`). It sets the player down where the suite run's had walked to fill its bucket, by the pool's west end: from where it stood up, the nearest room for a frame is inside, and the fixture passed on the code before.

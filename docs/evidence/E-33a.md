## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a native player waiting (resting to heal, by its furnace, for its sapling, for daylight, between steps) no longer stares
straight ahead. It watches a monster in plain sight, looks at whoever speaks to it, meets the eyes of a player close by who looks at it for a
moment and then goes back to its business, looks at what it waits on, and otherwise looks about slowly, its own way. Its head turns as a
person's does, quickly for a threat and slowly looking about, never in one snap. It never looks an enderman in the eyes. Spoken to by name by
a player it can see, it stops between one task and the next for two seconds to look at them.

Changed modules/files:
- `Gaze` (core, new): the choice of what to look at and the eased step of the head. Pure; it sees only what the adapter says the player sees.
- `NativeWaitSkill` (new): the native wait, its timer as before, with the head eased each tick toward the gaze's choice. What is in view is
  read every five ticks: monsters within sixteen blocks that would come for it (not an enderman, nor a neutral mob not after it), players
  within twelve, each only in plain sight. A step that would meet an enderman's eyes is not taken.
- `NativePlayerBody`: `glance` and the `LOOK` intent, under the wait's own lease. `NativePlayerSkills`: the wait takes an optional point,
  what it waits on.
- `PlayerRuntime`: one gaze for each player, kept from one wait to the next; who last spoke to it by name, from `chat`;
  `attentionCandidate`, the two-second pause to look at whoever spoke to it, among its reflexes and above its work, between tasks only, never
  in a match, riding, swimming or asleep; `nativeWaitAt` for fixtures.
- `PortalRunCampaign`: the furnace, the cobblestone generator and the sapling waits name what they wait on.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

Not tested / known failures: no person has watched it in an ordinary client. A player with nothing to do runs no wait, so it does not look
about then. Walking keeps its look along its way; mining, placing and fighting still turn the head in one step (F-10d). The social lane's
remark on noticing someone (`noticed`) is not yet said when it looks back at a player.
Exact next action: an idle player's look about without a task of its own; the eased turn onto a block before a break (drafted).

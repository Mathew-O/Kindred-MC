## Scope and implementation

The owner asked that hoglins not be attacked unless it is really needed, believing gold keeps them calm. In vanilla 1.21.1 gold armour keeps **piglins** neutral, not hoglins. Hoglins attack any player they see. They keep away from warped fungus, nether portals and respawn anchors. And they cannot reach a player standing two blocks up: a mob's attack box (`MobEntity.getAttackBox`) grows sideways by the attack range but not upward, and a hoglin stands 1.4 blocks high. So with a goal running, a hoglin after the player is dealt with in this order:

1. **Out of reach.** With time to get there (the hoglin more than four blocks off, and not just hit), the player pillars up with its own blocks until its feet are two above the hoglin's, then waits.
2. **Waiting it out.** It stays up while a hoglin near enough to see it is about: hoglins wander close by rather than leave, and one came straight back for the player that climbed down beside it in an early run. After 90 seconds with a hoglin near but not after it, it goes on with its work.
3. **From above.** Kept up there 30 seconds with the hoglin in reach below, it strikes the hoglin from its perch with single swings, never stepping off. The hoglin cannot strike back. This is the one time a hoglin is fought by choice.
4. **Fighting back.** A hoglin already on the player, or one that reaches it while it climbs, is fought with the goal's own fight (the edge and toss rules of E-39e apply). A pillar half built is where a hoglin's toss throws a player off, and an early run died exactly that way, climbing while being hit and never fighting back.

Running work stops for a hoglin as for any attacker. A climb gives way to a fight once the hoglin is on the player.

Without a goal, the runtime's tactical strikes leave a hoglin alone unless it is after the player within three blocks.

Pillar steps planned mid-jump (between two steps of a climb) are now planned for the cell the player is about to land in, and the step waits up to twelve ticks for the landing. Before this, each step cost a one-second pause.

Changed modules/files: `PortalRunCampaign` (`hoglinAfter`, `hoglinNear`, `outOfReach`, the hoglin rules in `defend` and `stopFor`, `combatTask`), `PlayerRuntime` (the tactical strike leaves hoglins alone; `nativeStatusLine` for fixtures), `NativePillarAction` (waits to land), `NativePlayerBody` (`ticksRun`); `NativeEdgeFightGameTests` (two fixtures).

## Scope and implementation

**The failure** (M-06i2c1's trace, Factions seed 20261030). Of 919 faction legs refused for no route, 211 ended in mid-air: `FactionsPlayer.destination` ends a 48-block leg at the player's own height when it sees no ground at the leg's end, and from a summit or a plateau that point is in the air over the slope or the valley below. No route ends there.

**The change (Factions-only, `FactionsPlayer.destination`, `backAlong`).**
- **Only a leg whose end it sees no ground at.** Where it sees ground there (near its own height, or the column's top), the leg is as before.
- **Back along the way:** from the leg's end toward where it stands, 6 blocks at a time (`BACK_STEP`), at most 6 columns, one sight line each (`BACK_LOOKS`), its own budget: the farthest column whose top it sees (`WorldKnowledge.Looks.ground`, F-35m: a top is known only when a sight line reaches it) and could stand on. The leg ends there.
- **Where it sees none along the way,** the leg is as before: asked at its own height and refused, so F-35i's back-off and the way it makes (#120) begin as now. The sky island, basin, no-way and hub walk-out fixtures see today's behaviour.
- **For fixtures:** `PlayerRuntime.factionDestinationForTest`.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player with lava for its cast and no room for a frame, beside lava lakes, climbs a few blocks into the rock over them and makes room for its frame there. A frame site may not lie within three blocks of lava, so none is found along a lake's shore. Before, it tunnelled along the lakes at their level for a site, a block or two from one lake after another.

Found in the live pair on `458d192` (seed 20261030, strategist off):

- From 19:49:08 to 19:54:43 it dug 209 blocks of deepslate looking for room for the frame ("Cast: look for room for the frame"). Its iron pickaxe wore out in it.
- Three or four blocks up, the lakes are out of the site check's reach, which looks two blocks below a frame's foot.

## A site two up, three over the lava (1 November, from CI)

CI's full suite failed this fixture once (run 309), and alone it then failed 1 of 3 on `60cb3a7`, 2 of 3 on
`1f16f1e` and 3 of 3 on `124c501`, before the next levers. Every failure worked a frame site two blocks up, where the
fixture asks for three, and then left it to branch mine for lava.

- `lavaAtOrBelow`, which keeps the climb going, counts lava three blocks below the player.
- `lavaNear`, which refuses a frame site, looked only two below the frame's base, though its doc said three.
- So two up, three over the lava channels, the climb still counted itself in the lava's reach while the site check
  took the site. `lavaNear` now looks three below as well.
- The live run on `64c298d` above had the same shape: Kindred "climbed two steps out of the lava's reach and chose
  frame sites there, then gave them up one after another".
- Its neighbours: `NativePortalCampaignGameTests` alone with the change, 92 of 93. The one failure was F-25c4aj's
  walled-in cell, staged from that same save over a lava lake. Alone it passed 5 of 8 with the change and 5 of 8
  without, failing the same way each time ("Its first frame block cast in the site it chose: 0 there, 3 elsewhere"),
  so it is an intermittent of its own, open, and not this change's.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
- **The problem.** A slice cannot cross its accounting period (`BudgetState`), and a reservation in the next period cannot be made or acknowledged before that period begins (F-11a refuses early acknowledgements). So a skill running at midnight lapsed with its slice and was halted. A long walk out to look for animals, F-11c's motivating case, was cut short every night.
- **A walk, traverse, retreat, goto or wait now holds still there:**
  - It is unpaid and does nothing in the world; the body's gate refuses inputs while unpaid.
  - The next period is opened and its slice acknowledged, through the same `chargeableBudget` choice and hours check as a dispatch.
  - Then it carries on as the same skill (a wait keeps its elapsed ticks).
  - The old period's settlement is acknowledged first, so opening the next period never closes the old one under its own write. The old settlement would otherwise be refused ("Closed accounting periods cannot be updated") and halt the player for reconciliation.
- **What stops as before:**
  - A hold not paid again within five seconds.
  - A hold the next period refuses: no period open, or outside the hours.
  - A recorded action (break, mine, place, craft …). Stalled mid-action its record could be left uncertain, which would block the player for reconciliation; the goal chooses it again in the new period.
  - A lapse at the cap or at the end of the hours, which is not the period's end.

# E-27a — An outing to somewhere it has seen, with whoever says "I'm in"

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an AI player with nothing of its own to do can ask someone near to come and see a place it has seen.
- **When it asks:** someone it may talk with has been near for twenty seconds. It has no goal, ranch, field, build or team
  task, and is not fighting, fleeing or in a match. It is daytime, and it is sociable (sociability 40 or more).
- **Where:** a village or lake it has itself seen (its atlas, F-30), 10 to 96 blocks off.
- **What it says:** "I found a lake not far off. Anyone want to come and see it? Say "I'm in"." At most once in half an
  hour of game time for each player.
- **Who goes:**
  - A person invited says "I'm in" (or "count me in", "I'll come", "let's go") within the minute.
  - Another AI player in sight answers by its own rule: yes only with no goal, nothing in hand, out of danger and of any
    match, and sociability 40 or more. It never goes because it was told to.
- **The trip:**
  - With a yes it says "Off we go." and walks there.
  - At the place it says "Here we are: a lake.", stays a minute, says "Right, heading back." and walks back.
  - An AI player that came walks back to where it set off.
  - The walks are each player's own `native_goto` legs, offered as candidates in its own decision loop and ranked below
    its reflexes. A walk edits nothing, so the operator's switch for AI world edits is not needed.
- **What it remembers:** who was within 16 blocks of it when it arrived (not who said they would come), as an episode in
  the ledger ("went to a lake together") and as friendship (E-24a, +5), on both sides for an AI player.
- **Ending early:**
  - A fight, nightfall or ten game minutes in all ends it: "Let's head back.", or "Never mind, another time." before
    setting off.
  - It ends with nobody's yes in the minute.
  - One that cannot get home is let go five game minutes after it ended.
  - Saying no costs nothing.
- **Log:** `Outing: <host> invited <n> to <place>`, `... is in`, `... set off for ...`, `... got to ...; there with it:
  <n> of <m>`, `... is back from ...`, `... cut short: <why>`, `... dropped: nobody wanted to come`.

  rule).
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `Outings` (new).
  - `PlayerRuntime`: hook lines (the hearing chain; the outing's leg as a decision candidate; what it has seen; whether it
    is free; what it remembers).
- JUnit: `outing.OutingTest` (3).

Found and fixed on the way:
- The invitation was never said: `Outings` dated its lines with the wall clock, but the social lane's clock is the
  runtime's monotonic one.
- The walks never started: dispatched directly, they waited for an idle runner that the player's own decision loop kept
  busy. They are now its own candidates.

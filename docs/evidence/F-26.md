## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a build of Kindred MC can be played unattended on many seeds and scored. `KINDRED_EVAL` in a
server's environment names a scenario: the starters, the goal, the edits switch, the ticks, sprint, the strategist and
the voice, and whether inventories are kept. With it set, the mod plays that scenario with no one at the console,
writes what happened to `logs/eval-result.json` and stops the server. Three Python scripts run a build on many seeds,
score the results with medians and ranges, and compare two builds on the seeds both ran. Without `KINDRED_EVAL`
nothing changes.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`: `eval.EvalScenario` (the setting parsed: unknown keys, values and duplicates refused; the canonical
  starters only; ticks 1 to 10,000,000; earned or staged), `eval.EvalRecord` (stages with their first tick, deaths,
  stuck spells and tokens per player, bounded to 500 deaths and 500 stuck spells each with a count of the rest, written
  as JSON), `eval.Json`; `EvalScenarioTest` (5), `EvalRecordTest` (4).
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `EvalBootstrap`: read by `KindredMod` alone, from the environment when the mod loads. Once the runtime's records
    are loaded, it acts through the runtime methods the operator's commands call. It sets the edits switch and the game
    rules (keep inventory, the daylight cycle), spawns the starters one by one a few blocks apart on the ground at the
    world's spawn, waits for each to be in the world, and sets each one's goal. Then it sprints the ticks. Once a game
    second it looks at each starter's inventory, dimension and run state; at the end it writes the file (a partial
    file moved over it whole) and stops the server.
  - `RunObserver`: told of each death and each ended task beside the log lines for them, by `PlayerRuntime`. It is no
    one by default, and its `runState` gives a player's stuck spells, where it stands, its End leg's stage and its
    tokens today.
  - `PortalRunCampaign.stuckLevel`, `StrategistBridge.tokensToday`.
  - `KindredMod`: the bootstrap read, ticked at the end of each server tick, and told when the server stops.
- `NativeEvalGameTests`: one fixture.
- `tools/eval/run.py`, `score.py`, `compare.py`, `README.md`: Python 3, standard library only.
- No new runtime dependency; nothing in the manifest changes.

Schema/configuration changes: the `KINDRED_EVAL` environment setting, read only by `KindredMod`; nothing saved
changes. The result file is new and lives in the server's `logs/` folder.

## Faults the runs found in the engine (1 November)

Four faults, each the engine's own, found while scoring the E-49 build's ten seeds:

1. **A starter that never came into the world was scored as though it had played.**
   - Seed 404's spawn is in a jungle. Kindred's spot was a trunk's top in its leaves, and Sol's the same. The roster
     spawn refused both ("Make room on solid ground in an already loaded area"), and the run went on with Echo alone.
   - It was so in every ten-seed run of seed 404: before the night fix, the night fix, and the E-49 build.
   - The result said so (`"build": "0.1.0-SNAPSHOT (not every starter came into the world: [Echo])"`), but `score.py`
     did not read it. Each of those summaries, and the night-fix comparison, counted seed 404 as a three-player run.
   - Fixed in two places. The bootstrap now makes each starter on the nearest ground round its spot that the roster
     spawn takes, its own spot first, so a starter whose spot was taken is made where it always was. And `score.py`
     names a run some of whose starters never came, and leaves it out of the medians.
   - `NativeEvalGameTests.aStarterWhoseSpotIsATreesCrownIsMadeOnTheGroundBesideIt`: a six-log trunk in its crown at the
     starter's spot. Before the fix it failed, "not every starter came into the world: []". After it, it passed, with
     the starter on the ground beside the tree.
2. **A run across midnight kept only the end of its log.** The server's `latest.log` starts again at midnight, and
   `run.py` kept only that file, so `RunMetrics` read only the part after midnight (seed 707 of the E-49 build).
   Stages, deaths and stuck spells come from `eval-result.json` and were whole. `score.py` now reads `RunMetrics` from
   `console.txt`, the server's whole console, which `run.py` always kept. For seed 707, Kindred's done and blocked
   tasks went from 2572 and 211 to 3148 and 296.
3. **The fixture had been failing since E-46.** `anEvaluationRunOfTwoHundredTicksWritesItsResultAndEnds` expected the
   scenario as it was written at 093d188. E-46 added `lessons` to the canonical scenario, and F-30 and F-29 added
   `atlas`, `rescue` and `advice`; no run of the class followed. At f6e30f2 it was 0 of 1: "The scenario:
   starters=Kindred; …; keep=none; lessons=on; atlas=on; rescue=on; advice=on". Its expectation is now the canonical
   string, all of it.

4. **A time to a stage was taken over only the seeds that reached it.** The night fix's time to a lit portal was over
   three seeds against three; diamonds, for F-30, are reached on two seeds of nine. A range over two or three seeds is
   not the ten the rule asks for, and two such ranges apart would have been called worth noting. `score.py` and
   `compare.py` now take a time to a stage over every seed, one that never reached it counted as later than any that
   did, so the median or the top of the range can read "not reached"; two ranges are apart only when every seed of one
   reached the stage before any seed of the other. `compare.py` also compares the time to diamonds now.

Also: `run.py` keeps the jar each seed ran in its `run.json`, and `score.py` says when a seed ran on another jar than
its build's.

The class alone, the gametest entrypoints narrowed to it and restored after:
- before the spawn fix, with the expectation brought up to date: 1 of 2 (the tree's crown failing as above);
- after it: 2 of 2 in each of three runs;
- after the fixture found its starter by its own runtime's record, not by name (another fixture's player may share
  the name in a full run): 2 of 2 in each of three runs.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- `RunMetrics`' windows are read from the log's clock, so on a sprinted run they cover more game time than in play;
  sprinted runs are compared with sprinted runs only.
- Stages are seen once a game second; one held for less than that between two looks (a pickaxe made and broken) is
  missed.
- The dragon goal is refused, as in play, unless the dragon fight is turned on; the run does not turn it on.
- A crash before the end leaves no result; the summary names the seeds with none.

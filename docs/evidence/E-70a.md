## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior, per [the design](../proposals/thrifty-travel.md#e-70a-nights-in-bed):
- **In by dusk.** The player knows its walk home: the distance over its pace, from where it stands. It turns for home in
  time to be in its own bed at the first tick a bed can be slept in (12,542 in clear weather). It waits by its bed
  until then.
- **A bed carried.** With home out of reach (no home bed, or a walk over three minutes), a player carrying a bed:
  - digs in for the night as before, and seals its pocket;
  - digs room for the bed beside it if it must, keeping the pocket sealed and leaving air over the bed so vanilla lets
    it lie there;
  - sets the bed down and sleeps (its spawn moves to that bed, as vanilla moves it);
  - takes the bed back up in the morning.

  Back within a short walk of its home bed by day, it uses that bed, which sets its spawn there again.
- **No bed yet.** The hole-up it always had, bounded as before, and a bed wanted first thing.
  - A bed is a standing want, taken as the chance comes: sheep for wool, then a bed crafted at a table.
  - When wanted first thing, it goes for sheep up to 40 blocks off instead of 16.
- **When a person lies down,** every AI player in the Overworld that can goes to bed at weight 96, just under safety.
- **No person's night held up.** An AI player that cannot be in bed (in a fight, no bed, underground mid-task) is left
  out of vanilla's count of sleepers, as a spectator is. One on its way to bed while a person sleeps counts for at most
  `Nights.TO_BED_LIMIT` (600 ticks). The operator's `aiPlayersCountForSleep=true` counts every AI player as vanilla
  counts everyone; default false. People are counted as vanilla counts them.
- **Night work on purpose.** On the stronghold leg's pearl hunt in the Overworld it stays up and says so.
- **Match modes keep their own rules.** Nothing changes in Speedrun, Manhunt, a speedrun playstyle, or for a player
  that only watches. In the Nether and the End there is no night: it carries on.
- **No logging off.**

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- **The engine comparison is not yet run:** ten seeds, `sleep=on` against `off`, portal goal, 144,000 ticks. It
  measures deaths an hour, stuck minutes, the iron pickaxe median and portals, per game hour and per game day, and
  chunk-loads and chunk-minutes per player. LEAD is arranging the cores. E-70a stays open until it is reported.
- **Night work** is declared only on the stronghold leg's pearl hunt in the Overworld. Gunpowder and phantom membranes
  are not declared yet, and no native fixture covers night work (only `NightsTest` does).
- **The home bed** is remembered in memory only. After a restart it is the bed the spawn is at, which after a night
  away with no return yet is the world spawn.
- **Restriction:** a player with no goal and no work of its own beds down only when carrying a bed. Without one, it
  stays where it stands (no hole-up of its own).

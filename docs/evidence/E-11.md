# E-11 — Shared projects and AI-player task assignments
Date: 2026-10-18 EDT; report 2026-10-18.
Antigravity / uncommitted main, no commits. Complete for this feature as recorded then; reopened 2026-10-24 (below).

## Scope and implementation
User-visible behavior: Multi-agent cooperative project coordination and AI-player task assignments. Autonomous AI players coordinate work across shared multi-step projects with strict dependency tracking, single-assignee ownership, material reservations against shared stock, contention handling, automatic revocation upon player death or stop, periodic lease expiration, and durable SQLite persistence (Schema 8) with crash recovery. AI players collaborate on team goals without duplicating assignments or double-spending shared resources.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`:
  - `dev.kindred.core.team.ProjectId`: Strongly-typed project identifier.
  - `dev.kindred.core.team.TaskId`: Strongly-typed task identifier wrapping UUID.
  - `dev.kindred.core.team.StockReservation`: Material reservation record with destination, item, count, and reservation status (`ACTIVE`, `FULFILLED`, `RELEASED`).
  - `dev.kindred.core.team.StockLedger`: Pure material ledger tracking total stock, active reservations, unreserved available counts, and atomic reservation/fulfillment/release.
  - `dev.kindred.core.team.SharedTask`: Task definition record supporting dependencies (`Set<TaskId>`), single-assignee ownership (`UUID assignedPlayerId`), states (`OPEN`, `CLAIMED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `REVOKED`, `FAILED`), material reservation (`Optional<StockReservation>`), lease timing, and completion evidence.
  - `dev.kindred.core.team.SharedProject`: Container record managing task maps, overall project state (`OPEN`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`), and project-level completion checks.
  - `dev.kindred.core.team.TeamCoordinator`: Thread-safe coordination engine providing project registration, dependency satisfaction discovery (`findEligibleTasks`), atomic single-claim assignment with contention rejection (`claimTask`), task startup (`startTask`), task completion with evidence recording and stock fulfillment (`completeTask`), revocation (`revokeTask`), fail-safe death and stop settlement (`handlePlayerDeath`, `handlePlayerStop`), and lease expiry sweep (`handleTickExpiry`).

## Reopened on 2026-10-24

A review ([review](../review-2026-10-24.md), D4) found the feature's acceptance (durable shared tasks and reservations, restart without double spending) unmet in the runtime:
- Nothing in production creates a shared project. They are registered only on loading and by `NativeTeamGameTests`.
- No claim, start, completion or revocation is saved: the store's `saveProject` and `saveReservation` have no callers.
- Startup recovery releases every active reservation and reopens every claimed task, even where the action behind it has just been marked uncertain.
- A completion credits the task's declared output to stock, not what was observed.
- A claim made directly skips the project-state check that discovery makes.

The coordinator's in-memory rules (dependencies, one assignee, contention, revocation on death and stop, lease expiry, a completed task refused a second completion) stand as tested.

Fixed with the reopening (`TeamCoordinator`):
- A direct claim checks the project: a finished or cancelled project's task is refused, as discovery already refused it.
- A completion credits only what was observed of a task's output, up to what it declares: none observed, nothing credited; partial, the part. The completion the runtime makes on a finished skill observes nothing, so it credits nothing.

| Criterion | Check | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nothing, part, or more than declared observed: nothing, the part, the declared count credited; a second completion credits nothing | `TeamCoordinatorTest.completionCreditsOnlyWhatWasObservedNeverTheDeclaredCount` | Pass | JUnit |
| A cancelled project's task neither offered nor claimed directly | `TeamCoordinatorTest.aCancelledOrFinishedProjectsTaskCannotBeClaimedDirectly` | Pass | JUnit |

Then, the same day:
- **Saved as it happens** (`TeamCoordinator.onChange`, `PlayerRuntime`). The coordinator reports every change to a project, with the reservations of the tasks that changed. The runtime saves it through the store's one ordered writer (`saveProject`, `saveTask`, `saveReservation`, each at the revision it replaces), for the projects the store holds: those loaded at startup, and those made through `PlayerRuntime.createSharedProject`, which writes the project before the coordinator knows it. A save that fails is logged.
- **An uncertain action holds its task** (`SqliteCompanionStore.recover`). At startup a claimed or started task of a player with an action marked uncertain stays as it is, its reservation held, with a `TEAM_TASK_HELD` event; every other claimed task is reopened and its reservation released, as before. The coordinator puts a held reservation back in its ledger when it loads the project (`StockLedger.restore`). `/aiplayer recover` frees the player's tasks and releases their reservations, once an operator has looked.
- **What it made, not what the task says** (`PlayerRuntime`). At a claim the runtime notes how much of the task's output the player holds; when its skill completes, it credits what the player holds over that, and the evidence names the action.

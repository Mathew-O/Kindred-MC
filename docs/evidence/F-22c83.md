## Scope and implementation

**The claim, and only this:** an AI player whose faction walk gets where it went weighs its faction play again at once
("There: weigh again at once", `PlayerRuntime.factionCandidates`). That is correct behaviour, and
`NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests.theNextChoiceAfterAPlaceSetAsideAsWaterComesAtTheDecisionInterval` wrongly counted it as
a failure. Nothing about the game changes.

**Found:** the test failed in CI on #330 (run 43662646243, job 132655108039), and once before on 2026-11-15 on an unrelated
PR ("279 ticks"). From #330's log:
- `Paced fixture: walks ended []; choices at [336387, 336987, 337587, 337822, 338422, 339022]`: every choice 600 ticks after
  the last but one, 235 after.
- Just before the early choice: `Ada native_goto COMPLETED [from factionCandidates:10299]`, then `Ada faction: Buying 3
  minecraft:string`. Ada had fallen off its island into the sea and swum to where its faction walk ended.
- "walks ended []": the walk's move was settled on arrival before its `native_goto` ended, so the test's list of ended
  walks (kept only while a move is set) did not have it.

The test is about the decision interval after a place set aside as water it saw (F-35j1). The arrival's re-weigh is another
path, and intended.

**The change (test-only in effect):**
- The runtime records, for the fixtures, the tick of each faction walk whose `native_goto` completed from
  `factionCandidates` (`FactionsPlayer.completedForTest`), and the tick of each place set aside as open water it saw
  (`FactionsPlayer.overWaterForTest`), the last 64 of each, read by `PlayerRuntime.factionCompletedAndOverWaterForTest`. No
  decision reads them.
- The paced test lets an interval between choices be only when a faction walk completed in it and no place was set aside as
  water in it: keyed on those observed events, never on timing or a tick window. Every other interval is held to
  `FactionsPlayer.DECIDE_TICKS`, unchanged, and its message now names the completions and set-asides.
- At least 3 intervals must be checked each run (`CHECKED`), or the test fails.
- No timeout, tick limit or tolerance changed.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter`: `FactionsPlayer` (the two lists), `PlayerRuntime` (the completion recorded where a faction walk
  ends; the accessor).
- Tests: `NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests` (the paced test's check).

## Known limits

- **Not reproduced:** the failure's frequency here is not known. It needs Ada to fall off its island and reach a walk's end
  by water, which no local run did.
- **A completion after its choice:** if a faction walk's `native_goto` were recorded as completed in a later tick than the
  choice it caused, that interval would be checked, and fail as before. #330's log has the completion first.

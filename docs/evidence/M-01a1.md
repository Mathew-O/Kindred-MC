## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: an AI player tending a wheat plot laid where grass grows clears the tuft of grass or flower on
each block first, as a player does, then tills it. Before, its hoe went at the grass block under the tuft, and nothing
happened: "Soil tilling produced no effect", every try, for as long as the plot was worked.

Found under F-35m1. Siting on ground it has seen laid `NativeOwnRanchGameTests.withNoWheatItGrowsItThenKeepsAPairOfCows`'s
plot in that fixture's strip of short grass, and it failed so. The fault is older than F-35m1: on main that plot happened
to land elsewhere. The lead ruled it a child of the ranch work with its own commit.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter:
  - `WheatRunner`: the till step breaks a replaceable, dry block over the soil first ("Clear the plot for tilling").
    The hoe's crafting table is set down off the plot (`onPlot`).
  - `PortalRunCampaign.placeTable`: takes spots to keep off.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: `stagePlotForTest`, a fixture hook (a plot recorded as the player's own). The plot's tending asks
    for a bare cell only as its runner reads one.
- gametest: `NativePlotGrassGameTests` (new), its `fabric.mod.json` line. `NativeOwnRanchGameTests`' wheat failure now
  names each plot cell and what is over it.

### The hoe's table on its own plot (2026-11-14)

Under F-35m1, `NativeOwnRanchGameTests.withNoWheatItGrowsItThenKeepsAPairOfCows` failed in the first run of its classes:
"Its plot sown: … 3 of 4 / Wheat enough: 0", with 17,925 "Its wheat plot tended: a bare cell sown again" lines in the
run.

- It had no hoe. So it crafted a crafting table and set it down beside itself, which was on a cell of its plot.
- It tilled the three other cells. The fourth, its soil under the table, its runner reads as no work, neither soil nor
  farmland.
- The tending decision read that same cell as bare and sent it back to the plot, every tick.

Two changes:
1. The table now goes anywhere but the plot's cells or the cells over them.
2. The tending decision asks for a bare cell only as its runner reads one: tilled soil with nothing over it, or soil
   with nothing but a plant over it.

Result: the fixture failed 0/1 before (the branch without these two changes) and passed 6/6 after. The 6 are the at-risk
classes ×3 and its own class ×3.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a native AI player walking a route (`/aiplayer native goto`, and every walk the Nether goal takes) moves as a player does.
- Across level, clear ground it heads for the furthest of the next six waypoints it can reach in a straight line, instead of stepping from block centre to block centre.
- It sprints on any level stretch when its food allows, and sprint-jumps along a straight clear run of five blocks or more with head room the whole way.
- A sprint-jump, once taken, is held to its landing: no turning back, no dropping the sprint in the air.
- Turns ease: the view closes half the remaining angle each tick (at least 6, at most 45 degrees), the head turns with the body, and the pitch eases toward the ground a few blocks ahead.
- It slows for a sharp turn, and for the last half block before a waypoint only where the route steps up, drops or ends. It eases off to a walk near the goal.
- Tunnels, steps, water and edges are followed waypoint by waypoint, as before.

Changed modules/files:
- `NativeGoToSkill`:
  - Aims at the furthest straight-reachable waypoint. `straight` samples every quarter block across the body's width: standable, no hazard, and head room for a jump.
  - Progress along the route counts through the air, up to 1.4 blocks above the waypoint's level while airborne, not only on the ground.
  - `leapt` holds a sprint-jump to its landing.
  - Sprint and sprint-jump rules use vanilla's food floor for sprinting (7) and 10 for sprint-jumping, which costs twice the hunger.
- `NativePlayerBody`:
  - `walk` adds eased yaw and pitch.
  - `turnLeft`.
  - `move` turns the head with the body.
- `NativeNavigationGameTests.aLongWalkSprintsJumpsAndTurnsSmoothly` (new, batch `playerLikeWalk`).
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests.goalClimbsOutOfAPitBelowItsWayBackWithNoPickaxe`: moved to its own batch (see below).

## Remaining limitations and handoff

Not tested / known failures:
- Not yet seen live. Sprint-jumping costs more food than walking: about 1.3 food points over the 38-block course, where walking costs none. The 10-food floor keeps it from sprint-jumping a hungry player into trouble.

- No strafing, crouching at edges, or bunny-hopping up stairs.

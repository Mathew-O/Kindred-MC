## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/faction capture` takes the chunk the player stands in (an officer or the leader), the same for
people and AI players. Both factions' members online are told. Once a second a faction that has just overextended is
announced vulnerable to everyone online (`faction.vulnerable`), and secure again when it no longer is (`faction.secure`).
Rules (section 4): overextended when claims exceed power, exposed the difference; only an enemy captures; only a chunk on
the border (an edge touching the wilderness or the attacker's own land), so inward only from what it has taken; the
attacker's capacity and 100 coins; a new faction shielded 48 hours from its founder's first founding of any faction (not
reset by founding again); 30 minutes after a faction's last loss; one capture a chunk an hour; never a safe zone or beyond
the border. Before capture a vulnerable chunk is protected exactly as a secure one (`Territory.mayChange` reads only who
owns it); on capture ownership moves in the claims ledger, its chests with it, nothing moved or copied.
Changed modules/files: companion-core `factions/Capture` (rules and the capture ledger), `record/Ledgers` (captures);
companion-server-adapter `FactionsService` (`capture`, `notices`, `tellFaction`, `broadcast`, `clockForTest`),
`PlayerRuntime` (notices once a second), `FactionCommands`.
Schema: none; ClaimLedger's `capture` event with `data.from`, `faction.vulnerable` and `faction.secure` events.

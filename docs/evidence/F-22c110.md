## Scope and implementation

**Found:** the BuildBattle test failed twice on CI:

| Run | Where | Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 43782657628, attempt 1 | `claude/corpus-e40a23-1115`, 11-16 | "Each voted on the other's build: [one vote]" |

The logs show:
1. In both, while Pip's build was shown, Wren cast no vote. Its only walk was the deep-water escape (`evaluateEssentialSurvivalCandidates`' `inDeepWater` shore walk, at lines 3641 and 3574 of those heads), which comes before `voteCandidates`. That walk was "Route … is blocked", to z 8520174 and z 4378686, beside its own gold spot on the walkway.
2. In 44918551355, while Wren's build was shown, Pip's walk to the spot was also blocked twice.

Locally the test passed 6 of 6 copies on main, each in its own batch.

**Cause, in `NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests.island()`:**
1. In every CI run the batch just before `buildBattle` is `factionOpenWaterCauseway`. Its island stands in a sea three deep, ±`SEA` (36) blocks each way and 30 up.
2. The sea had no rim, and its stone floor ended at ±`SEA`.
3. So the water ran out to ±(`SEA`+1), the edge of the box it keeps, and fell below the box.
4. `FixtureGround.restore` puts a kept box back without updating its neighbours. The curtain of falling water outside the box was never told to drain, so it stayed.
5. Where it lands depends on where the sea's centre falls in its chunk, so it moves from run to run. It crossed the BuildBattle field in exactly the two failures:

| Run | Result | The sea's south edge (`SEA`+1), z | BuildBattle's kept field, z |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 44918551355 | fail | 8520173 | 8520150–8520176 |
| 43782657628 | fail | 4378685 | 4378662–4378688 |
| 44955711811 | pass | 11211069 | 11211037–11211063 |
| 44962235063 | pass | 6005421 | 6005387–6005413 |

In both failures, Wren's shore target was the edge + 1. This fits the BuildBattle test's own note on #408, of falling water caught 21 to 24 up over its field.

**The change (test only):** `island()` lays a rim of stone round the sea, at ±(`SEA`+1), from its floor (y −6) to its top (y −3). The rim is inside the box the test already keeps, so it is put back as the test ends. It stops at the water's surface, below the island's top, so nothing the player sees from the island changes.

A new test, `itsSeaKeepsItsWaterWithinTheGroundItKeeps` (batch `factionOpenWaterRim`, STAGED):
1. It lays the same island and sea.
2. 200 ticks on, it checks every cell two blocks out round the box kept, and 20 below it, for fluid.

The BuildBattle test is unchanged.

**The check, made exact (after #442's CI):** on the re-run of head `ead4e53a` (run 44985181724, attempt 2) the new test failed: "No water outside the ground it keeps: 37 cells, the first at -39, -17, 6 from the island's middle". The sea did not leak there:
- the first cell, (−7328691, −47, 1524398), lay inside the plot of `replaygametests.echo-73670_3` (x −7328722 to −7328690, y −59 to −43), a replay scene whose own ground stays standing after it;
- the check counted every fluid cell round the box, so it counted that scene's water too.

Run with the rim taken out to see the check still catch the leak, it passed instead: with no player near, the chunks at the sea's edge often ran no fluid at all, so the rimless sea never spilled. Two changes to the test, none to the rim:
1. the fluid round the box is read before the island is laid, and only fluid not there then is counted. Staged with it: a water source set in a stone cup in the ground read, which must still stand at the end;
2. the island's chunks and those round it are simulated (`FixtureGround.simulated`), so the sea's water runs where it can.

**Nothing granted**, and no check, bound or timeout is loosened.

Changed files: test only, `NativeFactionOpenWaterGameTests` (`island()`'s rim; the new test).

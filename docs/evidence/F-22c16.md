## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: to reach the roof over a cabin's door, `ConstructionPlanner` chose a scaffold column on the
door's step outside. That is the cell its player stood in to put the door in, the cheapest column. While it stood, the
column shut the door. Halfway through taking the scaffolding down, night fell, and the player went to its own bed
indoors (its life routine). Waking indoors, every walk out found no route: the search covered the cabin's inside, 24
cells. The build stopped "8 steps failed one after another (… Scaffolding at … is out of reach to take down … No walkable
route …)".

- **CI on PR #25** (`b3f1590`, run 42030233893): `NativeBaseGameTests.aPlayerWithStoneToolsSitesAndBuildsItsOwnCabin…`
  failed "Built at the first go, never ended short and tried again".
  - The door was at …924, -52, …924. Scaffolding stood at …924, -52, …923 and …922, -52, …923.
  - "No route from …923, -52, …929 … (expanded 24, standing true)".
- **Locally** the night came before the teardown in each copy (9 copies, the fixture's clock moved on in a temporary copy
  only), and every build passed. The state that fails needs the night to fall mid-teardown, which only CI's slower ticks
  gave. The deterministic check is the planner's, below.

Changed files:
- `companion-core` `ConstructionPlanner`: `doorStep`. No column is chosen whose foot is beside a door's lower half, level
  with it or a block below it.
- `ConstructionPlannerTest`: one case.

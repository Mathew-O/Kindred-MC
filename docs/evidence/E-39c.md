## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a crystal whose bars leave no clear arc from anywhere on the island (looked at again at most every 30 seconds) is opened. The player walks to ground beside the pillar's obsidian, pillars up with its own blocks to the cage, breaks the bars in reach on its side without picking them up, and comes back down by breaking the block underfoot a block at a time. `native_break` takes `underfoot` for that, allowed only for the block the player stands on with solid ground below. While up there it does nothing that walks. After a restart, high up with no tower remembered, it finds its own pillar again from the blocks in the world (a column of its blocks at least three high down to end stone), steps back onto it and comes down. A barred crystal with a clear arc through broken bars counts as open, so nothing about a cage needs remembering.
Changed modules/files: `EndFightLeg` (tower, cage, `findTower`, `aloft`), `NativeBreakAction` (`underfoot`), `RoamingEdits` (`underfoot` denial), `NativePlayerSkills`, `PlayerRuntime`; `PortalRunCampaign` (no sky-island pit fallback in the End).

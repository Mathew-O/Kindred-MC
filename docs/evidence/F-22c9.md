# F-22c9 — A block its scaffold's pillar step put down is its scaffold, however the step ended

Date: 2026-11-05

Status: complete (native)

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: `BuildRunner` counted a block of its scaffold only when the pillar step that put it down
ended COMPLETED. A step stopped or planned again after its block went down left that block counted by nobody.
- **The cabin, CI run 392** (`NativeHouseGameTests`): all 222 blocks were placed, then the scaffold could not come down.
  Standing on top of its column, the one known block of the scaffold three below was "out of reach to take down": the
  two blocks between were not known to be scaffold.
- **The hut, the same run** (`NativeBuildGameTests`): "The scaffold taken down: dirt left at …" fits the same loss.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `BuildRunner` (`pillarsAsked`): each cell a pillar step of its scaffold is
asked for is remembered. When it next plans, a solid block there that the blueprint does not want is its scaffold.

Also, a break refused because nothing solid stands there any more is a block already gone, as the step wanted. It is no
longer a failure, and no longer counted as scaffold. Eight such refusals in a row count as failures again. Counted as
failures from the first, a cabin taking its scaffold down stopped short: "stopped, 8 steps failed one after another
(last: Down the scaffold at …, -50, …: Nothing solid to break)", as in CI run 384 and a full local suite on 5 November
(`full-m-1`).

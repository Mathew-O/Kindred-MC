## Scope and implementation

The owner's note of 2026-10-23: in unfriendly, Factions-like worlds a player's base should be a walled-in mini civilization, which it gradually expands as its resource needs grow, over five stages. Farms, cattle, the house and the rest are all enclosed in a walled, restricted, castle-like area. A moat and bridge would come in one of the later stages. Walls should always be lined with something, and entrances should be discreet.

**The five stages, as planned.**
1. **The keep:** the cabin in a walled yard. Built in this slice.
2. **A farm ward:** a second walled ward, sharing a wall with the keep, round a field by water.
3. **A pasture ward:** another ward round a pen for its cattle.
4. **The hall:** the two-floor house in its own ward, with towers at the outer corners.
5. **The moat and bridge:** a moat round the whole compound, with a bridge over it.

Wards are added beside the ones already built, so nothing built is pulled down to grow. The keep's west wall stays an outside wall at every stage, so its way in stays the compound's way in.

**The keep** (`Compounds.keep`, core, pure).
- **The wall.** Stone, from a foundation course in the ground to three blocks above the yard. That is too high to jump, and hides a player standing behind it.
- **Lined.** The wall is lined inside, its whole height, with planks of the base's wood. Torches on the lining light the yard every fourth block.
- **Discreet.** There is no gate. The way in is a stair three steps down beside the west wall, a passage of four under the wall and its lining three below the yard, and three steps up into the yard. Each step is a full block, a step up a jump. The passage is lined with stone (floor, sides, and the course over it under the lining), so that loose ground cannot fall in. Nothing in the wall shows where the way in is.
- **The cabin** is the base's own cabin (M-01b), of its style's wood, plan and variant (E-29a). It stands with room for a scaffold two wide to the north and east, four west of it for the stair up, and three rows south of it for its door and the way in.
- **Sizes.** For the first cabin plan the wall is 17 by 16 and the keep 687 blocks: 345 stone, 260 planks, the cabin's furniture and 13 torches. `/aiplayer build keep <x y z>` builds the first plan in oak and cobblestone.

Changed modules/files:

**The fixture.**
- **The ground:** grass on two of dirt over four of stone.
- **Mason holds** the keep's bill, as though gathered, with stone tools to dig the passage, dirt to scaffold with, and bread.
- **Then:**
  - It builds the keep, starting outside the wall's south side.
  - Every block must be in its exact state.
  - It is moved outside, west of the stair down, and walks to the yard.
  - It must reach the yard with its feet once three below it (the passage), and the keep must still be exact.

**Hand control.**

| Control | Result |
| --- | --- |
| The passage under the wall filled once the keep is built | Failed: "Mason in the world, short of the yard"; its walk found "No walkable route" to the yard, so nothing else in the wall lets a player in |

Found on the way:
- **Walking to and fro, breaking nothing.** In the first four runs the player placed nothing. Digging the passage, the planner put first the block under its feet, to be broken from the far end of the passage. It walked there, a step at a time, and was planned for again at each step. At the far end, the block under its feet came first again, to be broken from the end it had come from.
  - The pure simulation never showed this: there each step is walked and made at once.
  - Now a break it sets out for is walked to the end, up to eight steps, and made there (`BuildRunner`), in every build.
- **Two more in a run of every build class together.** On a scaffold one up beside the cabin, the player was reckoned "up on the build", because the build's lowest block is the passage floor four below the yard. So it waited for a roof stair it could not reach instead of walking round, until its time ran out. The build's floor is now its origin's height where it goes below that. Also, a cabin stopped within a second, its eight route searches all made from mid-fall; it now lands first.
- **Room for the roof.** With the cabin hard against the yard's north lining, the planner could find no stance for its roof in the pure simulation. The yard now leaves two blocks for a scaffold north and east of the cabin.
- **A guard.** When its body is gone, the walk-in step now fails rather than throw. The first run of the control crashed the test server: the fixture's own deadline had stopped the player.
- **Exact stone.** The passage's floor is cobblestone. Where the ground there is stone, it is broken and cobblestone laid, as any build makes every block exact.

## Scope and implementation

The owner's note of 2026-10-23: in unfriendly, Factions-like worlds a player's base should be a walled-in mini civilization, which it gradually expands as its resource needs grow, over five stages. Farms, cattle, the house and the rest are all enclosed in a walled, restricted, castle-like area. A moat and bridge would come in one of the later stages. Walls should always be lined with something, and entrances should be discreet.

**The five stages, as planned.**
1. **The keep:** the cabin in a walled yard. Built in this slice.
2. **A farm ward:** a second walled ward, sharing a wall with the keep, round a field by water.
3. **A pasture ward:** another ward round a pen for its cattle.
4. **The hall:** the two-floor house in its own ward, with towers at the outer corners.
5. **The moat and bridge:** a moat round the whole compound, with a bridge over it.

Wards are added beside the ones already built, so nothing built is pulled down to grow. The keep's west wall stays an outside wall at every stage, so its way in stays the compound's way in.

**The keep** (`Compounds.keep`, core, pure).
- **The wall.** Stone, from a foundation course in the ground to three blocks above the yard. That is too high to jump, and hides a player standing behind it.
- **Lined.** The wall is lined inside, its whole height, with planks of the base's wood. Torches on the lining light the yard every fourth block.
- **Discreet.** There is no gate. The way in is a stair three steps down beside the west wall, a passage of four under the wall and its lining three below the yard, and three steps up into the yard. Each step is a full block, a step up a jump. The passage is lined with stone (floor, sides, and the course over it under the lining), so that loose ground cannot fall in. Nothing in the wall shows where the way in is.
- **The cabin** is the base's own cabin (M-01b), of its style's wood, plan and variant (E-29a). It stands with room for a scaffold two wide to the north and east, four west of it for the stair up, and three rows south of it for its door and the way in.
- **Sizes.** For the first cabin plan the wall is 17 by 16 and the keep 687 blocks: 345 stone, 260 planks, the cabin's furniture and 13 torches. `/aiplayer build keep <x y z>` builds the first plan in oak and cobblestone.

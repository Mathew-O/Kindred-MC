## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: two AI players meeting again after ten minutes or more apart talk, two or three lines in each one's
manner: one asks after what it last knew of the other, the other answers from its own record, the first says a last word.
Only people who can hear the one speaking get the lines; never the whole server, never another AI player. With nobody
to hear, both note the meeting in their ledgers and say nothing. The server's AI players say at most four such lines a
minute (`KINDRED_CHATTER`, or `quiet` for none), a pair talks at most once in two minutes, and not while either is
fighting, backing off, falling, asleep or in a countdown; a fight stops a talk under way. The old fixed peer conference
is gone.

Changed modules/files:

- `companion-core`:
  - `social.Conversation` (new): `Side` (a player as it is to itself, and what its ledger says of the other), `plan` (the
    topic: news to the one asking, ranked by novelty and kind, nothing whose key was spoken in the last thirty minutes;
    the turns from templates in each one's manner, every reply written from its speaker's own record), `Turn.textFor`
    (a tease plainly to anyone who asked for none), and `Chatter` (lines a minute server-wide, the pair's two minutes,
    `KINDRED_CHATTER`).
  - `social.ConversationTest` (new, 4).
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `PeerTalk` (new): a turn a second; pairs in sight of each other; a meeting after ten minutes apart; the talk said a
    turn at a time with speech timing to the people who can hear each speaker, put as the strictest of their
    preferences asks; silence noted in both ledgers; a fight, a flight, a bed, a countdown or losing sight stops a talk.
    Its runtime side is a `Source`, so that a fixture can drive it on its own clock.
  - `PlayerRuntime`: hook lines (the lane and its source, its turn); the fixed peer conference's installation and its
    three methods removed (it whispered to anyone within six blocks, public chat or not); a fixture accessor.
- gametest: `NativeConversationGameTests` (4), a new entrypoint.
- No new dependency; the manifest's versions are unchanged.

## Design changes and what was not built

- Occasions: only meeting again after ten minutes apart. Not built: a find, a need met or unmet, a death witnessed, a
  milestone, a danger seen, a match, idle at base at night, a promise kept or missed.
- Model phrasing is not built: every turn is a local template. The rule that a model phrases a turn only when a person
  can hear, costed as a reply, therefore holds without a model call; phrasing turns through the voice is a follow-up.
- Delivery is the full lines to those who can hear the one speaking; the old `[Nearby]` whisper to anyone within six
  blocks is gone with the fixed conference.
- "Sheltering" is read as a player asleep in a bed (`native_sleep`); a sealed night shelter's dig-in is not a task the lane
  can see, and does not stop a talk.
- Callbacks use the ledger's episodes other than earlier talks; between AI players those are few until gifts, rescues
  and outings are recorded (E-21, E-22, E-27).

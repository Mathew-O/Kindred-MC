## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: where there are caves to go down into, a player smelts its first eight iron and makes an iron chestplate as soon as it has them, after a shield (and, on the mined route, the iron pickaxe) as before. Then it looks for the rest of its route's iron. Before, the chestplate waited until all of the route's iron was in hand, nineteen with a shield, so the iron was mined with no armour on. Death drops the chestplate with everything else, so a death costs the same iron as before; the change is that the player wears the chestplate while it mines the rest.

Found in the live runs from `fb02bc3` to `458d192` (P10 to P16's first seed, strategist off):
- 44 of 52 deaths came with no armour on. All 21 on P16's first seed did.
- Eight deaths came carrying eight iron or more, enough for the chestplate. Four had none on: Sol with 16 on P15's first seed, and Kindred with 12 and 16 and Echo with 12 on P16's. Each time the next life started its iron again from none.
- The other four had a chestplate on: Sol with 14 on each of P13's seeds, and Sol and Echo with 17 on P15's first seed. They had made it with the rest of their iron and were mining more. Armour does not stop skeletons; it only lets a player take more.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core): with caves about, at eight iron in hand the next step is no longer to mine more. The raw iron is smelted and the chestplate made, then the rest is mined. The chestplate's iron is named `CHESTPLATE_IRON`.
- `PortalRunPlanner.fuelPlanks`: the packed fuel counts a plank more for the chestplate's own smelt.
- `PortalRunPlannerTest`: one test.

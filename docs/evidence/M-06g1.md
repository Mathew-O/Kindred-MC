## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a person at a client types bare `/shop` (or `/market`) in a faction world and gets the
market's chest screen, as bare `/ah` gives the auction house's.
- "What the market sells": the buyable raw materials with a price, by id, a page at a time, each with what the market
  pays and asks and its day's change.
- "Sell your stacks": the player's own stacks, each with what it fetches, or that the market does not take it.
- Click an item, then confirm a buy of one, sixteen or sixty-four. Or click a stack, then confirm selling it.
- An AI player, the console or an operator without a body gets the commands' help as before. Every other `/shop`
  command is unchanged.

Changed modules/files:
- `MarketScreenHandler` (new), beside `AuctionScreenHandler`, with the same slot handling:
  - `onSlotClick` never runs vanilla slot logic and resyncs the client on every click;
  - `quickMove` is empty and `canInsertIntoSlot` is false;
  - the stacks shown are display copies.
- Prices come only from the market's `Pricing` and book:
  - the browse view's lines are `MarketDesk.priceLine`;
  - a buy is quoted by `Market.quoteBuy`, a sale by `MarketDesk.estimate`.
- A confirmed click is `MarketDesk.buy` or `MarketDesk.sell`, exactly as `/shop buy` and `/shop sell` call them. So the
  hold, the records (intent, sale, coins; buy, received), the saves and the uncertain report are theirs, rechecked at
  the mutation. The screen makes, copies or moves no item.
- The price is quoted again at the confirming click. If it moved, the screen says so, offers the new price and asks
  for another click; nothing moves.
- A trade settled after the screen has closed is told in chat only; nothing reopens.
- `FactionCommands`: bare `/shop` and `/market` open the screen (`PlayerRuntime.openMarketScreen`).
- Operator guide: the market line.

Dependencies satisfied: M-06g's desk and pricing; M-06h's screen pattern.
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: none. No saved key, event kind or SQL field changed.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

The browse view lists the buyable raw materials alphabetically, 45 to a page; there is no search or category filter. A
made item (planks, say) is bought by `/shop buy` as before, not from the screen.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
- `/companion mode companion|independent [UUID]` (with `together` as an alias for `companion`).
- `/companion scope home|approved [UUID]`.
- `/companion goal list|add|cancel` for durable projects/intentions.
- Mode and scope display in `/companion personality` alongside the nine traits without adding a tenth trait slider.
- Mode switch invalidates old intent authority by advancing `intentRevision` monotonically.
- Switching while stopped preserves stopped state.
- Switching to Independent truthfully explains that personal milestone progression is unavailable until installed, rather than silently substituting owner chores.
- Follow interrupts active tasks without deleting the underlying project/goal.
- Inventory, personality traits, and paid activity allowances are strictly preserved across mode switches.

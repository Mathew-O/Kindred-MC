## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: with Kindred's client mod, a panel on the right: the faction in its leader's chosen dye colour
with the leader marked and its members online; the player's power and progress to the next point; the faction's power,
claims and capacity (green secure, amber when claims come within two of power, red with the count exposed); shield
timers; coins with the last change for ten seconds; the land underfoot (wilderness, safe zone, your land, another
faction's with its relation and exposure); the top five factions by power with claims, members and the hour's arrow, the
player's own row always; the faction's last three notices for a minute; the market's movers and the price of the item in
hand. H cycles full, compact (faction, power, coins, here) and hidden; it hides while a screen is open or the HUD is off;
nothing in it is clickable. The card is the banner's stone grey (`#3C3C3C`) at about 35% opacity (`0x59`) with a
lighter edge (`#8A8A8A`) and its corners cut, a quarter of the screen wide, Minecraft's font. On an ordinary client: a
sidebar with the faction, its power and claims, the player's power, coins and standing, sent to that player alone, and
one action-bar line each time they cross into other land.
Changed modules/files: companion-core `factions/Hud` (state, visibility, layout, codec); companion-server-adapter
`FactionsHud` (sent on change at most once a second, the sidebar by packets to the one player, the action bar),
`KindredHud` (client), `KindredPanelPayloads.Hud`, `KindredClient`, `FactionsService` (notices for the panel),
`PlayerRuntime`, `en_us.json`.

## The panel's client settings (2026-11-08, Claude MODES, `claude/m06l-hud-options-1108`, stacked on #64)

Each player sets these on their own computer, with `/kindredhud`. They're kept in `config/kindred/hud.properties`, and the server never sees them.

- **Opacity:** `opacity 10..100`, default 35, the card's stone grey. The edge stays a little more opaque than the card, as before.
- **Scale:** `scale 0.5..2`, in steps of 0.05, on top of the GUI scale. Default 1.
- **Side:** `side left|right`, default right.
- **During titles:** `titles on|off`, default on: hidden while a title or subtitle shows, such as a match countdown, then back. It reads the title through `InGameHudTitleAccessor`, a read-only client mixin.
- **Other commands:** `reset`, and the bare command, which shows the settings.

Unchanged: the H key's full, compact and hidden cycle, nothing clickable, the vanilla sidebar fallback, the faction's dye colour, and the payload. The settings are pure (`dev.kindred.core.ui.HudOptions`: clamp, text round trip, card colours, placement). `KindredHudOptions` keeps them, one save after another, off the render thread.

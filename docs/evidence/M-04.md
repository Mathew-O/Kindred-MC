## Scope and implementation

M-04's card: a generated arena with a lobby, protected plots, and spectator and voting paths; a random theme announced with a title; a timed build in creative (free blocks in Builder only); voting by human and AI players; results with a podium and fireworks. AI builders turn the theme into a build plan from the local design grammar, reference schematics from M-05, and optionally the model. Acceptance: complete 1v1 rounds against a human and between AIs, with ordinary-client presentation.

M-04a built the round: the plots, the theme, the timed build in creative, AI designs from Kindred's generators, the vote, and fireworks. This change adds:

**The arena** (`BuildArena`, core), laid out with the plots when the round is proposed. It is all set in Builder, where blocks are free.
- **A lobby pad.** Smooth quartz at the walkway's west end. Each human invited is told where it is.
- **The walkway.** It runs along the front of every plot, one block above their floor, with a fence railing on its far side. Onlookers watch from it, and voters walk it to each build.
- **A gold mark** on the walkway in front of each plot's middle, where that build is watched from for the vote.
- **A podium** across the walkway's east end, built at the results: gold, iron and copper steps, first in the middle, each winner named on a sign with its mean vote, facing the walkway.

**The vote as a tour.** The builds are shown one at a time, in plot order, fifteen seconds each.
- Everyone in the round is told whose build is up: a title, the bar ("vote on Cass's build - 0:12") and the sidebar.
- A human is offered [1] to [5] for that build only, with the gold mark's place.
- An AI voter walks to that build's gold mark and scores it there, only with the plot in its line of sight from there. Before, it scored every plot at once by its blocks, unseen.
- A vote counts only on the build being shown, the same for humans and AI players.
- **Walking in adventure.** A body may act in adventure mode only while voting, and then only to walk (`NativePlayerBody.allowAdventure`).

**Designs from the schematic library (M-05).** Schematics the operator put in `config/kindred/schematics` whose names name the theme, and that fit a plot, are read off the game thread as the round is proposed. An AI builder builds one of them, credited to everyone in the round (D8: its creator, license and source); without one, it builds Kindred's own design. Nothing is bundled or fetched.

**Fixed on the way, each found in play or in its fixture:**
- **A human could not build on its own plot.** The server's use hook required the block clicked to be on the player's plot as well as the target, and the plot's floor is not. So every block a human placed on its own plot was refused. Found on an ordinary client. A builder may now place against its own plot's floor (`PlayerRuntime.mayPlace`), and still never on another's plot.
- **An AI voter was taken out of the game as the vote began.** Its game mode changed under an action in flight: a break caught half done, then a pillar step cancelled to make way, then a placement whose block was taken from its hand. Each left an outcome only reconciliation could settle, and the body was blocked; three live rounds showed it.
  - The fix: an AI voter's hands are emptied and its game mode changed only once its last action has settled. Its build is stopped at once, so nothing new begins meanwhile.
  - An operator's stop still takes everything back in its own tick, as M-00 requires.
- **A tied round crashed the server.** A tied vote has no one winner, and an onlooker's result title named the one there was not. Fixture first in `MatchPresentationTest`; it now reads "Hal and Cass tie".
- **A placement finished in adventure.** A placement begun in creative could finish after the vote began. Now nothing but walking runs in adventure.

- **The podium is blocks and signs.** No one is moved onto it.
- **The vote is a fixed tour.** Fifteen seconds a build, in plot order.
- **A changed check.** In the build-battle fixture, "nothing held once the vote began" became "nothing held outside creative once the vote began". An AI voter now keeps its creative stock for the few ticks its last action takes to settle, still in creative. Its hands are emptied as it leaves creative. The end-of-round check is unchanged: every builder leaves with nothing in its own game mode.

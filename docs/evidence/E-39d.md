## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: with the crystals down, or given up on for now, the player holds ground 16 to 32 blocks out from the exit portal's pillar and shoots the dragon in flight when the arc is clear (an arrow on a flying dragon's body does a quarter of its damage, plus one). When the dragon perches it goes to a spot in front of the head where vanilla 1.21.1's wings do not fling a player (each wing's box widened by four and lowered by two, as `EnderDragonEntity` reckons it each tick) and the head's touch does not hurt (its box widened by one, here by 1.3, even at the top of a jump), with the eyes within 2.8 blocks of the head. There it strikes the head in one recorded fight (`native_dragon`) with jumping critical hits, steps back if too close or under a wing, and out of breath at once. With no such spot it shoots the perched head, which takes an arrow's full damage. Every move in the End (out of a breath cloud, out to rest, back to its ground) picks a spot on end stone whose straight walk keeps a block and a half clear of every cloud and away from the perch; hurt, it rests 28 or more blocks out. Nothing in the End is left to the campaign's island fallbacks: with nothing feasible it waits a second and looks again, and tasks the campaign has set aside are skipped. When the dragon dies it says so in chat, walks into the exit portal and, after the credits, is brought back to its spawn as a client's respawn request would bring a player back; the dragon goal is then won and nothing more of the run is done.
Changed modules/files: `EndFightPlanner` (core); `EndFightLeg`, `NativeDragonAction` (new: `strikeSpot`, `wingZones`, `headZone`), `NativeShootAction` (aims at the perched head), `NativePlayerBody` (`strikeDragon`, `returnFromCredits`), `PortalRunCampaign` (`setAside`, the won goal, the End before the generic heal), `PlayerRuntime` (credits return, announcement, a halted entry ends the tick, roster by name), `InventoryEvidence` (wear is no lost item), `NativePlayerSkills`.
Dependencies satisfied: E-39b, E-39c.

Staged dragon runs 5 and 6 (world `dragon2`, seed 8675309, dev server on port 25566): Kindred was given an End kit by command (diamond sword, bow, 64 arrows, 128 cobblestone, iron armour, shield, 32 bread, a water bucket, an iron pickaxe) and teleported to the arrival platform. None of this is earned progress. The fight spanned server restarts as fixes went in:

- Run 5 (builds before the perch rework): all ten crystals down by 02:19:44, then 24 arrows at the dragon in flight took it from 200 to 180, and later sessions to 145. Kindred was slain by the dragon at 02:34:38 walking in under the perched head (the head's touch).

The dragon, at 145, went to 123 and 102 by arrows at the perched head, to 81 and 60 in two perch fights (`dragon fight: 4 hits, 4 on the head, 4 critical`, twice), and to 39 and 17 by arrows.

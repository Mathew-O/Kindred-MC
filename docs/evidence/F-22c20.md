## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: when the planner finds a scaffold block it cannot reach to take down, `BuildRunner` digs
out the stray block under the player ("Off what it stands on", #82) only once two walks round to it have failed. When
that block is the player's own scaffold straight below it, in the column it stands on, no walk round is asked for at
all. It waits instead, and so the escape never comes.
- **Main's CI after the PR #25 merge** (`fc38c94`, run 42114774287) passed 564 of 565.
  `NativeLifeFirstGameTests.itsFirstNightFallingAsItsCabinGoesUpItSleepsThereThenGoesBackTheNextNight` failed.
  - Mason stood at y -49 on its own column, over its scaffold block at -52, with two blocks it had lost count of between.
  - It waited "Scaffolding at … -52 … is out of reach to take down (standing at … -49 …)" 197 times in 42 seconds.
  - Night then took it to bed. The roof ran into a third night, and the test's ticks ran out: three sleeps, against two
    in every earlier CI run of the fixture.
- **Locally** the stall did not come about in 8 copies of the fixture (4 before the fix, 4 after); no stuck wait in any.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `BuildRunner` (`under`). A blocked step whose target is its own scaffold
straight under the player's feet digs out the block underfoot at once, when that block is a scaffold block that is not
the blueprint's (`offPlan`, as before).

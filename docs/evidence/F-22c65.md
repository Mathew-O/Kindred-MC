## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a hurt, hungry player climbing its own stair out of a covered cut keeps climbing until it is
out. Before, two blocks up, with the sky over its shaft, it looked for animals, and the look's walk took it back down to
the cut's floor. There it still counted as up, looked again (north, south, north), and never took the climb again.

Found: `NativeCoverClimbGameTests.itKeepsItsCoverFromAZombieAndClimbsForFoodOnceItIsGone` failed on #194's CI (run
43016464425, merge ref `9256724`): "Climbing on once the zombie is gone: at … −58 …; Iron: hurt and out of food -
Explore north for animals", after 151 explore lines. Alone on main its one test passed 3 of 3, but with 20, 36 and 35 explores a
run against 21 climb steps.

Cause (product), in `PortalRunCampaign`'s hungry branch:
- Once it has had the sky (`hungryUpAt`), the F-25c4ag hysteresis counts it as up anywhere within three blocks under
  there. Up means hunt, fish or look for animals, not climb.
- Two up its own stair, the sky over the shaft, it was up, so it looked. The look's walk led off the stair's step,
  along a ledge of the cut and down to its floor: still within three blocks, still up, so it looked again.

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `PortalRunCampaign`: in that band (within three blocks under where it last had the sky,
  and no higher) and below a rim beside it (`belowRim`), a look for animals is planned first (`lookGoesDown`). The plan
  is one search of the navigator's, as the walk itself searches (its safe drop, doors, node budget and reach). If it
  ends lower than where it last had the sky, the climb is taken instead (`wayUp`) and held (`heldClimb`/`climbHeld`)
  until it is above there or has no step left. A look that ends level is the look's own as before: against a wall it
  cannot get past, as on F-25c4ag's cave floor under a roof, its stalls cut a way through. On top, with no rim, it
  looks as before. The plan is kept for (feet, anchor, the look's target) and logged once with its node counts.
- Test: `NativeCoverClimbGameTests.upOutOfTheCutForFoodItDoesNotWalkBackDownToItsFloor` (its own batch `cutHungryUp`).
  The cover scene with no zombie, at health 4 and food 8 with a stone pickaxe and cobblestone. It fails once the
  player, having climbed two or more, is back on the cut's floor, and passes once it stands on top.
  - Staged: the goal starts once the player's cell reads as covered. Built a tick before, the light not yet worked
    out, one copy of three read the sky over its floor and took the floor for where it last had the sky.

How it got there, against the plan the lead agreed (each a fixture run's finding):
- A look with no route was at first refused too. That broke F-25c4ag (0 of 3), whose looks have no route under the
  roof, so it was dropped.
- `belowRim` is required: without it the held climb looped on top of the stone.
- The plan searches as the walk does: a smaller search said no move where the walk then went down.
- "Lower than there", not "more than a block under": the looks that led it down ended one block under (−57). Every
  one of F-25c4ag's 274 looks across three runs ended level.
- Keyed by the look's target too: from one spot, a look west planned level and refused, and the look north then took
  its answer unplanned and walked down.

Nothing is granted; the climb digs its own stair with its own pickaxe.

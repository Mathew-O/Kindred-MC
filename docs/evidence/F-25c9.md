## Scope and implementation

The owner's note of 2026-10-23: "There should be a general task list with weighted priority levels and ongoing builds should be near the bottom. The player should prioritize getting food when hunger is low, it should get enough to last it, it should prioritize sleeping at night, the home like builds should have beds for spawn setting."

**The weights (`LifeFirst`, core, pure).** A player's choices were already one ranked list (`TaskPreferences`): urgent before project before routine, and by need within each band. What keeps it alive is now weighed into the project band above its work:
- **Sleep at night, 88.** At night or in a thunderstorm, with a bed of its own it can get to.
- **Food to last, 84.** When hunger is at seven shanks or lower and it does not hold enough to last.
- **Its build, ranch or base, 70.** Near the bottom: only routine work is below.
- **Unchanged above all of these:** urgent life (a fall broken, a creeper fled, a wound eaten for). Sleep comes before food: a player does not hunt in the dark, and hunger does not fall while it sleeps.

**Food enough to last.** Enough is a full bar and a reserve of twelve, in its body and its pack together; eating moves food from one to the other. Once it is out getting food it keeps on, past the point that set it off, until it has enough, then says so and goes back to its work.
- **Where it gets food.** Food lying in sight, then animals in sight, hunted as the goal hunts for its stock. While it is penning a pair at its ranch, it never hunts that kind.
- **When there is none in sight,** it walks out to look, up to three walks. Then it says there is no food to be had about, and its work goes on for five minutes before it looks again.

**Sleep in its own bed.** Its bed is one of the beds its base's plan puts in its stages (every stage has one: the cabin a bed, the house and the manor two or three).
- **Which bed.** The nearest that stands, is free, and is within 96 blocks.
- **Getting there.** It walks to it, sleeps, and says why.
- **Its spawn.** Sleeping sets its spawn there.
- **The night.** Its sleep passes the night as any player's does, when every player sleeps. Until then it stays in bed.
- **A sleep that does not take** (mobs about, the bed taken) is tried again after thirty seconds.
- **The sleep action.** It now takes a bed of the player's own base as well as one in an assigned work area.

**Where it applies, for now.** A player with work of its own and no goal running: a build, a ranch, or its base under the operator's leave. A running goal keeps its own rules for food and the night, from its planner. The routine work of a work area keeps its order: fixtures that test eating, mining and the like set hunger low for their own reasons.

## Later, on 2026-10-24: its reflexes apart from the edit switch and the idle playstyle

A review of the project ([review](../review-2026-10-24.md), D1) found that a player kept itself alive only with all of its work admitted. With the server's AI world edits off (the default, D4), or the player idle (the playstyle a new player starts in), it did not eat when starving, step out of fire or off magma, swim to shore or get clear of a hissing creeper. `NativeAutonomyGameTests` asserted the hunger.

**The gate split** (`PlayerRuntime`). Whether it may decide (running, not stopped, nothing of its own in flight or being settled, no hold after a commanded task) is one test. Whether it may edit (the server's switch) is another. Its work is admitted only with both, and not while idle, as before.
- **Its reflexes need only the first:** eating and bringing food to hand, stepping out of a hazard, swimming to shore, getting clear of a creeper, a weapon to hand, a step back or a shield raised, and a strike at a mob that is after it.
- **None of them changes a block,** and every action that does checks the edit policy itself.
- **Still its work, needing both:** a strike at a mob that is not on it; a fall broken with water (it places water); a barricade; the Nether's blazes; an expedition's return.
- **A stopped player does none of it.**
- **The decision loop** is admitted for a reflex when there is one to act on; with none, its explanation is as before.

**The owner's call.** This relaxes a restriction a fixture asserted, so it was put to the owner. They left open calls to whatever makes the players feel less like bots (2026-10-24). A person eats when hungry and steps out of fire whatever the server lets them build, so the change stands as the policy, tested below.

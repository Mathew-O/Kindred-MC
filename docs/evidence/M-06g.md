## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/shop sell hand`, `/shop sell all` (the estimate) and `/shop sell all confirm` (or `/sellall`,
`/sellall confirm`), `/shop buy <item> <count>`, `/shop price [item]`, `/market top`, `/market history <item>`,
`/market baseline <item> <hundredths> <mass|ordinary|scarce> [nobuy]` (operators); AI players through
`/faction as <player> shop ...`. Coins are kept in hundredths.
Changed modules/files: companion-core `market/` (`ItemFacts`, `Canonical`, `MarketTable` and `market.txt`, `Recipe`,
`Pricing`, `MarketBook`, `MarketLedger`, `Market`), `factions/Coins` (hundredths, `show`), `record/Ledgers` (market);
companion-server-adapter `MarketDesk`, `SmithingTransformAccessor`, `PlayerRuntime` (`marketRequest`, `recordThen`,
`reportOpenSales`), `FactionCommands` (`/shop`, `/market`, `/sellall`); JUnit `MarketArbitrageTest` (the adapter's first
JUnit source set).
Schema: none; `market.intent`, `market.sale`, `market.buy`, `market.cancel`, `market.baseline`, `market.uncertain` and
`coins` events.

Design: 158 raw materials priced in `market.txt` (about ten coins a minute of play, from `Worth`'s minutes where it has
them), 42 items counted as a share of one (a block of iron is nine ingots; ratios such as 1/9 exact); everything else
priced by its cheapest recipe from the server's own recipe list. A sale pays the lesser of an item's own price and every
recipe's cost from what it is made of, less 15%; a purchase (raw materials only, and not those marked `nobuy`) asks its
own price plus 15%: any buy, craft, smelt, uncraft or upgrade loop loses the spread. Curve: price × e^(−k·stock), k by
class (mass 0.0004, ordinary 0.002, scarce 0.02), floors 10/20/30% and ceilings 2/3/4× of the baseline, at most 20% from
the price an hour before, stock decaying with half-lives of 12/6/3 hours. A bulk sale walks a unit at a time and rounds
once. A sale is an intent, then the items taken in the tick the intent is confirmed written, then the sale and its coins
as one transaction; the seller gone or the stacks changed cancels it with nothing moved; an intent left open by a crash
is reported (`market.uncertain`) for an operator, never replayed. Not valued: names, map drawings, book text, player
heads' profiles, banner patterns, trims, fireworks, stews' effects (each keyed as the bare item). Pending: unknown data
components, unknown enchantments, potions (no baselines yet), anything unpriced.

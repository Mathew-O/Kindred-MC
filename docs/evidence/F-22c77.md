## Scope and implementation

**User-visible behaviour:** a player putting down an anvil, an enchanting table, a smithing table or a brewing stand puts it where the block will fit whichever way it is turned. A cell its placement is refused at again and again is let be, and another is chosen. Before, it could put the block down at the same cell, refused each time, for thousands of ticks.

**Found:** `NativePetGameTests.itTamesAWolfWithItsOwnBonesNamesItAndSaysWhenItDies` failed on #281's CI (run 43560991650, attempt 1) with "Its body" at 45 s, its limit 8,000 ticks. From that attempt's own report and build log:
- **The masking:** the fixture's own stop (`runAtTick(max(7800, DEFAULT_STOP_AT_TICK = 8000))`) fired before the test was done. The runtime stopped ("Record through event 6561"), its player was logged out, and the check still being retried read the player as gone. The server ran about 180 ticks a second: the fixture's 100-tick line printed 80 times in the 45 s.
- **The real state:** the wolf was tamed in the first second. Then the anvil, for the name tag, was put down 1,087 times in a row and refused each time ("native_place BLOCKED: Block placement produced no effect or was obstructed [from petCandidates]"), from 06:28:07 until it went down at 06:28:45, about 6,800 ticks. The naming did not finish before the stop.
- **Elsewhere:** in each of 12 older CI logs, and on #278's green run on the same day, there were 0 such refusals and the wolf was named.

**Cause (product), in `PlayerRuntime.workstationSpot`:**
1. A cell was accepted when `world.canPlace` passed for the block's default state.
2. An anvil is put down turned a quarter from the way its placer faces (its facing is the player's, turned clockwise). Its top spans the whole cell along its facing axis, and only 10 of 16 pixels across it.
3. A probe on one cell, with a wolf (its AI off) reaching into the cell's west edge:

   | Wolf into the cell | North | East | South | West | Default |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 0.05 | open | blocked | open | blocked | open |
   | 0.1 | open | blocked | open | blocked | open |
   | 0.15 | blocked | blocked | blocked | blocked | blocked |
   | 0.2 | blocked | blocked | blocked | blocked | blocked |

4. So something just inside a cell's edge passes the default anvil's check and blocks the anvil actually put down, whenever its player faces north or south. A tamed wolf sits down where it was tamed, beside its owner and seldom centred in a cell. The same cell was chosen again on every decision, refused every time, until the wolf moved.

**The change:**
- **(a) `placeableEveryWay`:** a workstation cell must take the block in every horizontal facing it has (`HORIZONTAL_FACING` or `FACING`), as well as its default state.
- **(b) The bound, whatever the trigger:** a placement refused at a cell with "no effect or obstructed" is counted in `observeRunner`. On the third refusal in a row (`PLACE_REFUSALS`) the cell is let be for 1,200 ticks (`PLACE_SET_ASIDE`), and `workstationSpot` chooses another. A placement that works clears that cell's count. It logs "… lets x, y, z be a while: its placement there refused 3 times".
- **Workstation placements only:** anvil, enchanting table, smithing table, brewing stand, all through `workstationSpot`. A build's cells are never set aside.
- **Test only, (1) the message:** the pet test's "Its body" checks now say whether its runtime had stopped, and its last state (tamed, named, bones, tag, anvil, last result). So a stop that beats the test names what it was doing.

**Nothing granted.** Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter`:
  - `PlayerRuntime`: `workstationSpot` (it now takes the entry), `placeableEveryWay`, the refusal count in `observeRunner`, and `Entry.placeRefusals`/`placeSetAside`.
  - `NativePlaceAction`: `NO_EFFECT` and `placedCell()`.
- Tests:
  - `NativePetGameTests.itsAnvilGoesDownWhereItFitsWhicheverWayItIsTurned` (batch `petsWolfAtTheEdge`), new.
  - The existing pet test's messages.

**The fixture's staging:**
- Its wolf, tamed to it and sitting (its AI off), in the cell north-east of the player, its box from 0.9 to 1.5 east of the north cell's west face.
- The player facing south, so an anvil on the north cell faces west.

**The gate's question, stated before it runs:** does requiring a workstation cell to fit every facing, and letting a cell be after three refused placements, change any scene's pass rate? Workstations are put down in few corpus scenes. A change in a scene that puts none down would not be this change's.

## Known limits

- **Other placements:** only workstations are covered. A build's placement into a cell something is standing at the edge of is not changed here: a build cell cannot be moved.
- **Other blocks that turn:** blocks with other turning properties (a stair's half, an axis) are not checked every way, as none is put down through `workstationSpot`.

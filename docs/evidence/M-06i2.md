## Scope and implementation

**Why nothing was built (RUNNER 2's finding).** A faction player's candidates came in this order:
1. life first;
2. `factionCandidates`, which returns whenever faction play has a move;
3. deliveries and trades;
4. the goal's campaign, which returns for a Nether portal, a stronghold or the dragon;
5. only then `baseDecision`, which also waits for no goal.

A faction world's standing goal is the End or the dragon, so no faction player's base was ever built.

**The change (Factions-only, `PlayerRuntime`).** Design agreed with the lead (2026-11-12, 2026-11-14).
- **The order** in a faction world: life → DEFEND → its base's due stage → other faction walks → the goal.
  - After `factionCandidates`, `factionBaseDue` begins a due stage through `baseDecision(entry, body, onOwnClaims)`, and its build is then carried on below as any build is, ahead of the goal's campaign.
  - **Due:** bases on, edits allowed, the overworld, no other work under way, its look-again time past, its faction holding land, and either a stage it took up again or the next one `BaseProgression` names (the cabin at stone tools, as in co-op).
- **On its own claims only.** `siteFor`'s existing `fits` parameter is given `onOwnClaims`: every chunk of the footprint must be its faction's. `siteFor`, the ground and the column code are unchanged. No trading hall is begun in that turn.
  - **On seen ground (F-35m1, #297).** Since the merge with main `2fba2aa`, the faction base's site, like every base's, is chosen from `entry.seenGround.ground(world)`: only columns the player has seen. A newly chosen site is checked again at its stand. M-06i2 adds only the claims test on top. The fixture stands 100 ticks looking round before it plays, as `NativeTradingHallGameTests.LOOK_ROUND` does.
  - **Bounded:** with no site there, the stage is looked at again after `BASE_NO_SITE` (1,200 ticks), and the goal goes on meanwhile.
- **Faction walks:**
  - while a stage is due, a newly chosen walk other than DEFEND is not set out on, and is weighed again at the next decision; an act where it stands (a claim, a sale) still goes ahead;
  - while a stage is being built (`factionBaseBegun`), a walk other than DEFEND waits and is kept;
  - a walk under way when a stage falls due goes on to its end first.
- **Earned.** No grants: the build fetches and places its own blocks, every edit still checked by `TerritoryGuard.mayChange`.
- **Outside a faction world** `factionBaseDue` is false, and the co-op base is as before: it waits for no goal (the co-op control below).
- **For fixtures:** `PlayerRuntime.goalForTest`.

**Factions-only, shown.** `PlayerRuntime` is shared by every mode, so here is why the change can't act outside a faction world:
- **`factionBaseDue`** returns false at its first line unless `factionsWorld()`. It is the only way into the new base turn and into the walk hold while a stage is due.
- **The hold while a stage is being built** (`factionBaseBegun`) is inside `factionCandidates`, which returns at once outside a faction world.
- **`baseDecision`'s co-op call** passes no `onOwnClaims` (`baseDecision(entry, body)` → `null`). With `null`, the goal test, the trading hall and `footprint -> true` are exactly as before.
- **Shown by:** the co-op control below, where a player with its goal running begins no base, as before.
- **What the corpus gate should show:** no co-op scene's pass rate changes. Stated before the gate's result.

**The seed comparison's question,** stated before the run: on one Factions seed at `bases=true` against `bases=false`, does goal progress hold (the first wooden tool, iron pickaxe and portal, per player) while each faction gets a cabin on its own land? Both runs are reported.

## The seed comparison

**The question,** stated before the run (above): on Factions seed 20261030, does goal progress hold with `bases=true`, while each faction gets a cabin on its own land? The run: `eb947e0c`'s jar, 144,000 ticks each, `bases=true` and `bases=false` at once, `goal=none; mode=factions`, 3 players. Both exited 0, and the audit was clean on both.

| Player | `bases=false`: wooden tool / stone / iron pickaxe / portal | `bases=true`: wooden tool / stone / iron pickaxe / portal | Faction walks (blocked), false → true |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Echo | 1,340 / 5,600 / 90,060 / lit at 120,324, the Nether at 132,560 | 81,700 / 86,060 / 125,080 / none | 118 (118) → 16 (10) |
| Kindred | 720 / 3,580 / 41,660 / none | 720 / 3,140 / 128,020 / none | 12 (12) → 65 (64) |
| Sol | 1,020 / 3,900 / 24,900 / none | 840 / 5,800 / 28,900 / none; obsidian at 34,710 | 0 → 21 (17) |

**The answer: no on both counts.** No faction got a cabin, and goal progress was slower with `bases=true` (Echo's portal not reached, Kindred's iron pickaxe 41,660 → 128,020). But the base turn this change adds is not what slowed it:
- **Kindred and Sol never held land with `bases=true`.** Three factions were founded, and all 3 claims of the run are Echo's. So `factionBaseDue` (which needs its faction to hold land) was never true for either, and no base turn of theirs ever came. Kindred made 65 faction walks, 64 refused (toward points at y=120); Sol made 21, 17 refused. With `bases=true` a founder's first claim is the ground its first base will stand on (#238), now chosen from seen ground (#297), and here those walks were refused. That path is main's with bases on, not this change's.
- **Echo's base turn came due 49 times,** from its stone tools on, every `BASE_NO_SITE` (1,200 ticks). Each time it found "No flat open ground it has seen for a cabin within 24 blocks" on its three chunks, and the goal went on: one decision each, and new faction walks held only at that decision.
- **Echo's wooden tool at 81,700** came before any stage was due (a cabin needs stone tools). That difference is not the base turn either; one seed's run-to-run spread is wide.

**Whose code the slowdown is.** Kindred's and Sol's refused claim walks come from main's bases-on path, not this change. The founder's first claim is chosen in `factionView` → `prospectiveSite`, gated by `bases && worldEditsEnabled()` with no base built yet (#238, with #297's seen ground). That path is on main as it was, and this branch's diff does not touch `factionView` or `prospectiveSite`. This change's own part acted only for Echo, the one founder with land: 49 looks for a site, each holding back the walk newly chosen at that decision.

So the change does what its fixture shows, and it costs the goal little where it cannot build. But on this seed it builds nothing, for two reasons outside it:
- **Founders who never hold land** because their first-base-site claim is refused.
- **Claims with no seen flat ground** for the cabin.

M-06i2 stays open until a live run builds a cabin on its land.

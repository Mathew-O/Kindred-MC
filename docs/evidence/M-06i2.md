## Scope and implementation

**Why nothing was built (RUNNER 2's finding).** A faction player's candidates came in this order:
1. life first;
2. `factionCandidates`, which returns whenever faction play has a move;
3. deliveries and trades;
4. the goal's campaign, which returns for a Nether portal, a stronghold or the dragon;
5. only then `baseDecision`, which also waits for no goal.

A faction world's standing goal is the End or the dragon, so no faction player's base was ever built.

**The change (Factions-only, `PlayerRuntime`).** Design agreed with the lead (2026-11-12, 2026-11-14).
- **The order** in a faction world: life → DEFEND → its base's due stage → other faction walks → the goal.
  - After `factionCandidates`, `factionBaseDue` begins a due stage through `baseDecision(entry, body, onOwnClaims)`, and its build is then carried on below as any build is, ahead of the goal's campaign.
  - **Due:** bases on, edits allowed, the overworld, no other work under way, its look-again time past, its faction holding land, and either a stage it took up again or the next one `BaseProgression` names (the cabin at stone tools, as in co-op).
- **On its own claims only.** `siteFor`'s existing `fits` parameter is given `onOwnClaims`: every chunk of the footprint must be its faction's. `siteFor`, the ground and the column code are unchanged. No trading hall is begun in that turn.
  - **Bounded:** with no site there, the stage is looked at again after `BASE_NO_SITE` (1,200 ticks), and the goal goes on meanwhile.
- **Faction walks:**
  - while a stage is due, a newly chosen walk other than DEFEND is not set out on, and is weighed again at the next decision; an act where it stands (a claim, a sale) still goes ahead;
  - while a stage is being built (`factionBaseBegun`), a walk other than DEFEND waits and is kept;
  - a walk under way when a stage falls due goes on to its end first.
- **Earned.** No grants: the build fetches and places its own blocks, every edit still checked by `TerritoryGuard.mayChange`.
- **Outside a faction world** `factionBaseDue` is false, and the co-op base is as before: it waits for no goal (the co-op control below).
- **For fixtures:** `PlayerRuntime.goalForTest`.

**Factions-only, shown.** `PlayerRuntime` is shared by every mode, so here is why the change can't act outside a faction world:
- **`factionBaseDue`** returns false at its first line unless `factionsWorld()`. It is the only way into the new base turn and into the walk hold while a stage is due.
- **The hold while a stage is being built** (`factionBaseBegun`) is inside `factionCandidates`, which returns at once outside a faction world.
- **`baseDecision`'s co-op call** passes no `onOwnClaims` (`baseDecision(entry, body)` → `null`). With `null`, the goal test, the trading hall and `footprint -> true` are exactly as before.
- **Shown by:** the co-op control below, where a player with its goal running begins no base, as before.
- **What the corpus gate should show:** no co-op scene's pass rate changes. Stated before the gate's result.

**The seed comparison's question,** stated before the run: on one Factions seed at `bases=true` against `bases=false`, does goal progress hold (the first wooden tool, iron pickaxe and portal, per player) while each faction gets a cabin on its own land? Both runs are reported.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a builder clearing the last two blocks of a floor, side by side, standing on one, now breaks the
other from where it stands and then the one under it from beside. That holds even when more of the build higher up is
still in the way and out of its reach. Before, it walked between the two without end and broke neither.

Found: `NativeCompoundGameTests.withItsKeepStandingAPlayerBuildsItsFarmWardAndWalksInUnderTheSharedWall`. It failed on
#121's run 42798198875 and #135's run 42777662062, and neither diff touches builds. From #121's log:
- At the ward's first course, "3/480 placed - In the way of the build", the builder went between (…444, −55, …762) and
  (…445, −55, …762) 1,961 and 1,960 times. Each walk was "To break" the block under where it stood, from on the other.
- Every walk completed, and no break was made.
- F-22c29's diagnostics: "3840 walks to a break without one; going to null from null (walks 1), feet (…444, −55, …762)
  … last breaks …444 then …445".
- The two blocks are the floor of the ward's way under its wall: a passage along its row, wanted stone either side of it.
- More of the build was still in the way above.

The cause, in `ConstructionPlanner.next`:
- F-22c8's rule breaks first, from where the player stands, a block of the top layer still in the way. It looked only in
  the topmost layer of everything in the way.
- A block higher up with no spot to break it from left that layer with nothing to break, and the rule stopped there.
- The nearest-first choice then took the block underfoot, broken from on the other block. There, the other was underfoot.

Changed file:
- `companion-core` `ConstructionPlanner`: the layer F-22c8's rule looks in is the topmost the player can break any block
  of from a spot it can stand on. Nothing else changes: with a stance for the true top layer, the plan is as before, and
  the nearest-first choice that follows is unchanged.

Nothing is granted.

A native staging of CI's end state was tried first, four ways: the pair on open ground, in a trench two deep, with the
passage's wanted side stones, and with the build asked again every tick through a fixture hook. All passed before any
change, since each had no higher block out of reach. None is committed; the hook was dropped.

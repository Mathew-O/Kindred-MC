# F-25c4dh5 — A back-off or a creeper run takes a way it can walk, when straight away is rock

Date: 2026-11-28
Agent / branch: Claude (MODES on MOVEMENT) / `claude/movement-1123-wayoff-cap` (built on `claude/movement-1123-wayoff`), merged with main `0ca2d9c9`
Status: in progress (the PR's CI)

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: when a hunted player backs off from a mob, or runs from a creeper, and the ground straight away from it can't be walked (rock, a wall, a drop too deep, lava), it backs off along the nearest way it can walk that still takes it farther off. Before, it aimed at a point straight away and walked only as far as the route finder could get nearer that point. In a tunnel that ran across the line to the mob, the walk was refused or ended within two blocks.

How it chooses a way. The pure part is `WayOffHeading` in companion-core `tactics`, with 16 headings evenly round:
- straight away, if it can be walked;
- otherwise the walkable heading with the greatest dot product with straight away, above zero, so a way that gains nothing is never taken;
- a way it turned onto is kept for 100 ticks while it still gains and can be walked, so it doesn't swing between two ways that are nearly as good;
- straight away is never kept, so on open ground nothing changes.

What "walkable" means. `PortalRunCampaign.walkable` reads the first 4 cells along a heading from the player's feet, using the route finder's own reading of each block (`NativeNavigator` over core `Navigator`):
- a stand level with the last cell;
- a step up, with head room;
- or a drop of up to `Navigator.MAX_SAFE_DROP`, clear at both body heights all the way down;
- a diagonal step only with one side of it open.

These checks refuse harms and lava. `passable` refuses them at `Navigator` L462–467, and `standable` refuses lava above, beside or under the stand at L482–492. So a heading across lava is not walkable. Only blocks next to the player are read.

Exceptions and limits:
- With a skeleton or other ranged mob within 16 blocks and after the player (`shooterAfter`), it backs off straight as before. Turning a way off was found to give a bowman a longer shot (scene 173225, first gate).
- A turned back-off aims 6 blocks off with range 1, not 10 blocks off with range 3 (`TURNED_REACH`). In 43893 the uncapped turned walk ran 16–20 blocks with the zombie keeping pace. The straight runs it was compared with went 4 blocks and then dug in.
- The creeper run keeps its 12 blocks.

One line per spell says that it turned: `<name> heading turned for ground: <what> straight away is <deg> degrees, not walkable; takes <deg> degrees`.

Unchanged:
- when a back-off or run is chosen;
- the fight;
- the dig-in;
- everything on open ground.

No model call. The work is one read of at most 16 × 4 local cells, once per back-off decision, never per tick.

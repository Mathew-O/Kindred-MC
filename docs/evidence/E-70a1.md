# E-70a1 — A bed never propped in a floor cell the build set aside

Date: 2026-11-12
Agent / branch: Claude, REFACTOR (`claude/e70a1-bed-prop-1112`)
Build: the PR's final commit; the fix in `6c68411` and `ec47a83`
Status: in progress (the corpus gate and CI on the PR)

## The failure

Two CI runs on 2026-11-12 failed `NativeLifeFirstGameTests` with the same signature: the cabin left incomplete after the
night, "The rest are blocks it cannot place yet", its life note "Night: in its own bed".
- #240, run 43167251070: `ledOffFromTheHerdItGoesBackToWhereItSawItAndHuntsFirst`.
- #239, run 43174614787 (docs only): `hungryWithTooLittleToLastItHuntsFirstThenBuildsItsBase`.

Both re-ran green. LEAD read #240's log:
1. The cabin's floor plank under the bed's foot was refused as obstructed, three times.
2. The plank was set aside; later "Prop for minecraft:red_bed" went in that plank's cell, and the bed on top of it.
3. Dusk, and the player slept in its bed.
4. At the end, the scaffolding in the plank's cell was "out of reach to take down" from six stands, and left standing.
   The plank was never placed.

On main `ec581d5` alone the class passed 6 of 6 in each of 3 runs.

## Cause

`BuildRunner` sets a block aside after three refused placements (`givenUp`): there, an animal of the herd stood in its
cell. It is retried once the rest is built. But a set-aside block was dropped from what the planner owed, so its cell was
free for the planner's own use:
- A bed's foot is set on the top of the block below it, so with that floor plank missing it has no face to be set
  against.
- The planner's prop for it is a scaffold block in the cell under it: the set-aside plank's cell.
- The bed then sits on the prop, and the rest of the floor walls the prop in. No face of it is in sight, so it is out of
  reach to take down from every stand, and the plank can never go in.

**E-70a is not the cause.** The staged fixture below gets the same signature by day, with no night and no sleep. The
night only makes the build long enough for an animal to wander into the floor.

## The fix

- **`ConstructionPlanner.next`** takes the set-aside blocks.
  - They are still owed: never stood in, never filled with a prop or a scaffold column. They are not placed now.
  - A block whose only face to be set against is a set-aside block's waits on it. The step is blocked at that cell, with
    the reason `WAITS_ON_SET_ASIDE`.
- **`BuildRunner`** passes its set-aside blocks to the planner. When a block waits on one, the runner tries that block
  again first, up to `SET_ASIDE_RETRIES` (3) times. The retry once the rest is built is kept as it was.

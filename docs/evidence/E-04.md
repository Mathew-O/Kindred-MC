# E-04 — Registry-based crafting and assigned-furnace smelting

Registry-based crafting and assigned-furnace smelting advance autonomous AI players across the essential survival technological tree in Minecraft Java 1.21.1:
1. **2x2 Inventory Crafting (E-04a):**
   - Authentic player inventory crafting via vanilla `PlayerScreenHandler`.
   - Dynamic resolution of basic 2x2 recipes: oak planks from gathered logs, sticks from planks, crafting table from planks.
   - Conserves exact items; rejects insufficient ingredients without loss.
2. **Crafting Table Placement & 3x3 Workstation Tool Crafting (E-04b):**
   - Authentic block placement into the world (`native_place`) under server edit policy (`EditPolicy.Kind.PLACE`).
   - Discovery and interaction with reachable placed crafting tables (`Blocks.CRAFTING_TABLE`) via `CraftingScreenHandler`.
   - 3x3 workstation crafting of wooden tools (`wooden_pickaxe`, `wooden_axe`, etc.) with authentic recipe grid layout.
   - Rejects 3x3 craft requests when no crafting table is within reach (4.5 blocks).
3. **Stone-Tier Progression & Furnace Crafting (E-04c):**
   - Extended native mining (`native_mine`) to stone and pickaxe-mineable blocks (`BlockTags.PICKAXE_MINEABLE`, `Blocks.STONE`, `Blocks.COBBLESTONE`) with authentic tool wear.
   - 3x3 workstation crafting of stone tools (`stone_pickaxe`, `stone_axe`) from cobblestone and sticks.
   - 3x3 workstation crafting of the furnace (`furnace`) from 8 cobblestone.
   - Authentic furnace block placement into the world under server edit policy.
4. **Assigned-Furnace Smelting & Fuel Accounting (E-04d):**
   - Authentic furnace workstation interaction (`Blocks.FURNACE`, `AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity`) with slot indices: input slot 0, fuel slot 1, output slot 2.
   - Fuel accounting supporting vanilla fuels (coal, charcoal, wood planks, logs) with active combustion handling.
   - Smeltable ingredient loading (raw ores, cobblestone, food, logs).
   - Output product extraction with authentic vanilla XP drops (`dropExperienceForRecipesUsed`).
5. **Integrated Iron-Tier Equipment & Autonomous End-to-End Progression:**
   - Workstation 3x3 crafting of iron tools and equipment: `iron_pickaxe` (3 iron ingots + 2 sticks), `iron_sword` (2 iron ingots + 1 stick), and `shield` (1 iron ingot + 6 planks).
   - Tool tier prioritization in mining candidate selection (prefers netherite > diamond > iron > stone > gold > wooden tools, with automatic hotbar swapping).
   - End-to-end autonomous progression: the decision loop detects raw iron and fuel, loads the furnace, awaits smelting, extracts iron ingots with XP, and crafts an iron pickaxe at the crafting table.
6. **Durable Ledger Integration & 100% Item Conservation:**
   - Single-use `ActionAdmission` tracking across all mutations (`native_craft`, `native_place`, `native_smelt`).
   - Zero item duplication, zero item deletion; exact mathematical conservation verified across all transfers.

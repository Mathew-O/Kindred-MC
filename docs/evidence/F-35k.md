## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player stranded on a ledge underground, every way off it a drop into a cavern or water behind
its wall, no longer stays on it spell after spell. A player already stranded two whole spells over, each ended by a move
elsewhere with nothing gained, that is given its fallback climb "to find another way" again (three looks within three
blocks inside a minute) begins its next move elsewhere at once, rather than at the end of the watchdog's three-minute
spell (counted as a spell only then, as a move begun early for a rescue is). A player that has only now stopped, with
nothing tried elsewhere yet, waits for the spell as before. Leaving the place by more than eight blocks clears its count. Where that
move's walk is refused and neither straight tunnel goes, it makes a way, the planner's own bridge, step, pillar or dig
(`buildRoute`), to somewhere at least eight blocks nearer the new place. It waits to land before planning (its own
pillar's jump no longer ends the move), and holds the way while a mob is within six blocks or a skeleton or stray within
sixteen can see it, leaving the turn to the plan's own fight and cover steps.

Changed modules/files:
- companion-server-adapter: `PortalRunCampaign` (`relocateStep`: the made way after the tunnels, the wait to land, the
  hold for a mob at hand or an archer in sight; the watchdog's early move: `paced`, `pacing`, `PACED` = 3,
  `PACED_AFTER` = 2 spells, `pacedWhere` (cleared beyond eight blocks), `pacedMoves`, reset on a gain).
- docs: this file, `feature-spec.md`, `validation.md`.

## Why

Seed 8675309 in E-40's iteration 20 (`e40a20-79a9aad`): Kindred spent 21 stuck spells, 63 minutes, on a shelf at the
top of a cavern deeper than its replays' box, an aquifer behind the wall. LOOPS's reading (branch
`claude/pocket-evidence-1111`): every try elsewhere failed "No route … expanded 3 … the goal standable false", and four
changes to the plan's own steps (the dead-end floor at the tunnel and the dig down, setting the tunnel and the whole
descent toward lava aside) each failed all 30 runs. Here, on one scene: the walk elsewhere searched three positions, the
straight tunnel turned at every drop, and the climb away stepped up onto the same shelf and back; with ~200 blocks of
cobblestone held, it bridged nothing. A replay does not restore a move elsewhere under way, so a scene's first move came
only at the end of a whole spell, about 2,900 ticks in: with the made way alone, 7 of 10 left the ledge but too late
(out for the last 115 to 454 ticks of the 1,800 needed).

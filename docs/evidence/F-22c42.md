## Scope and implementation

The source is #72's CI, run 42584495579 (job 129050939984),
`NativePortalCampaignGameTests.hurtAndOutOfFoodUpUnderAGapInTheRockItGoesOnLookingForAnimals` (F-25c4ag's fixture),
"Back to the climb: Iron: hurt and out of food - Climb toward the surface for food: back the way it came at -12, 0, -30".

**What the fixture asks.** Hurt and out of food under a roof of rock with one gap in it, the player looks for animals
under the rock and never turns back to the climb for food. The four cows stand 45 blocks out, beyond a wall at 21.

**What happened.** The player cut through the north wall toward a cow and, at (-12, 0, -30) from its start, 32.3 blocks
out, took the climb for food once. Under the gap it is up; within 32 blocks of where it last had open sky it still counts
as up; beyond that it is up only where `underground()` finds open sky. The fixture lays its ground from -1 to 6 blocks
over its origin and nothing above. At that place something stood over the open ground.

**Root cause, in the fixture.** It lays a field 105 blocks across on `EMPTY_STRUCTURE`. The test server spaces its tests
by their structures and runs several at once, so other tests stood inside the field (`FixtureGround`'s own note: a
fixture that lays ground wider than its structure lays it over another running beside it). In the same minute of the log
a builder fixture ("Mason") was working. Whatever one of them left over that ground shut it from the sky.

**The mechanism, shown.** A throwaway copy of the fixture, not kept, with a ring of stone laid 8 blocks over its origin
on all four sides beyond the walls (22 to 40 out), as a neighbouring test could leave: 3 of 3 fail with the CI text
("Back to the climb … back the way it came at -29, 0, 13", "at 29, 1, -13", "at -30, 0, 12"). The same copy with the
stone over the north side only passed 3 of 3: its search went east or south, by its heading, and never under it.

**Fix.** The fixture uses `FixtureGround.WIDE` (120 by 120, 40 high) with sky access, its origin at the structure's
middle, `(60, 2, 60)`: the field lies inside its own structure, which the test server clears before the test and keeps
other tests out of. The staging is unchanged, measured from the new origin.

Changed file: gametest `NativePortalCampaignGameTests` (the hungry-roof fixture's template and origin).

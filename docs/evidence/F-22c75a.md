## Scope

calls `FixtureGround.keep` over the box it lays: from its stone floor up, x −6 to 18, y 1 to 12, z −6 to 14 from its

## Why the ground is put back, not spaced apart

- **Batches run in sequence.** In every run, each batch's `Running test batch` line comes after the batch before it
  completes, and `pitfall` starts in the second after `buildLitematic` ends. `FixtureGround.keep` puts the ground back
  as the test ends, before the next batch starts.
- **Spacing would have to beat 24 blocks.** The pit player takes logs within 24, the plots are 13 apart, and the kiosk
  builds 10 into the plot beside its own. A reserved template about 60 wide would be needed round the pit.

## Scope and implementation

The first slice of Co-op Survival's natural progression (M-01, MODE-02): a ranch with an animal pen and breeding, earned from the player's own materials.

**A ranch.** `/aiplayer ranch <cow|sheep|pig|chicken> <x y z> [player]` asks a native player for a ranch with the pen's north-west post at that spot. Only whoever administers it may ask, and only where the server allows AI world edits. `/aiplayer ranch stop` stops it; what is built stays.
- **The pen.** Seven by seven, a ring of 23 fences with a gate in the middle of the south side (`Generators.pen`). It uses the wood the player has most of, as planks or logs.
- **Its materials.** Crafted at a crafting table from the player's own wood: logs into planks, planks into sticks, then the gate and the fences. The table is one close by, one it carries set down, or one it makes. Nothing is given: with too little wood it says so and stops.
- **Building it.** Through the same build runner as any build (E-08b): every fence and the gate placed from where the player can stand, and checked. The gate is hung from outside the pen.

**Leading a pair in** (`Ranch`, core, pure). The animals follow the food they breed on, as vanilla's own temptation has them: wheat for cows and sheep; carrots, potatoes or beetroot for pigs; seeds for chickens.
- **Walking up and leading back.** With the food in hand, the player walks up to the nearest grown animal of the kind within 32 blocks of the pen. It then leads it back to the gate four blocks at a time, waiting whenever the animal falls behind.
- **Into the pen.** It opens the gate and crosses the pen, and waits for the animal to come in. One that will not come in within fifteen seconds is left, and another fetched.
- **One in, one to fetch.** With one animal in and the next far off, it goes out with the food put away and shuts the gate on the one inside before fetching the next.
- **Shutting them in.** With a pair in, it puts the food away (so they stop following it) and shuts the gate from inside.

**Breeding.** The player walks up to each of the pair and feeds it one of the food, from its hotbar, within the reach the server allows a player's click (`NativeFeedAction`). Each feeding is recorded in the durable ledger: exactly one of the food spent, the animal then in love, nothing else lost. An animal that is young, already in love or cooling down from breeding is never fed. It waits for the young one, up to thirty seconds.

**Leaving.** With the food put away, it waits for any animal standing just inside the gate to move off (up to ten seconds). Then it opens the gate, takes one step out and shuts it behind. It says how the ranch went: "A young cow born in the pen", or why it stopped.

New skills:
- `native_feed`: an animal fed from a hotbar slot.
- `native_gate`: a fence gate opened or shut with a use click on its near side.
- `native_goto` gains `hold`, a hotbar slot held on the way, and `leg`, the blocks walked before it looks back.

## A ranch of its own (2026-11-05, `claude/modes-1105-6`)

The ranch chosen by the player itself, as part of its own progression (M-01's natural survival progression), not asked for.

- **When** (`RanchChoice`, core, new):
  - its first base stands whole, under the operator's bases leave (M-01c);
  - it has nothing more to build yet (the next stage waits, as a house waits for iron or, in Co-op Survival, for the group's pace, M-01e).
- **Which kind:** the first kind it has seen a pair of near it and holds the food to breed: cows, then sheep, pigs, then chickens.
  - "Seen" means grown animals within 32 blocks that the player can see from where it stands.
  - It needs two of their food: one each to breed them.
  - It never keeps a kind it has not seen, and never sets out without the food. Its note says why it waits ("A pair of cows close by; 2 wheat first").
- **Where:** the nearest flat open ground to its base, as its base stages are sited, clear of its other builds. The pen is built from its own wood, through the ranch's own run (M-01a's first slice).
- **Kept with its bases** (`PlayerMemoryStore.Pen`, in its bases file):
  - taken up again after a restart;
  - never begun twice once it has run its course;
  - ended for good by an operator's `/aiplayer ranch stop`.
  - A file from before has none.
- **Strays, found on the way** (the fixture failed 2 of 3 runs before; `Ranch`):
  - One cow wandered 43 blocks off while the pen went up, out of the ranch's reach, and it left with one penned. It now remembers where it saw each animal it might bring in, from its first step and from the ranch's choice.
  - When one short, it walks toward one it can see far off, or to where it last saw one (three spots at most), within twice the ranch's range of the gate.
  - It leads in one it finds near it, though beyond the gate's reach. It still sees only what is about the pen and about itself.
- **A crash, found on the way:** the base decision's caller asked the build runner for the step of a ranch it had just begun. Every run crashed the server on `02c8b64`; the ranch now takes the ranch's own step.

**Sited clear of the others:** a new pen and the wheat plot avoid every pen it has, as they avoid its builds and hall.

**Said right:** "a pair of sheep", not "sheeps" (`Ranch.Kind.plural`).

## Wheat grown for cows and sheep (2026-11-08, `claude/modes-1106-8`)

The cows' and sheep's food earned in play: wheat grown in a plot of its own, as players keep one early on.

**When** (`RanchChoice`): its base stands, a pair of cows or sheep is in sight (or was seen in the last five minutes), and it holds no wheat. It says "A pair of cows close by; 2 wheat first, grown from seeds". Chickens with grass in sight still come first, being quicker to feed.

**The plot** (`WheatPlot`, core, new; `WheatRunner`, adapter, new):
- **Where:** two by two, on the nearest flat open ground to its base, clear of its builds. Kept with its bases (`plot`), and the pen is sited clear of it.
- **The work, one pass at a time:**
  - ripe wheat harvested first;
  - dry soil tilled with a wooden hoe it makes from its own wood at a table;
  - bare farmland sown with seeds broken from the grass in sight.
- **No water needed:** wheat grows on dry farmland, only more slowly, and farmland with a crop stays farmland.
- **While it grows:** it leaves the plot and gets on with other things. Each look at its base takes the plot up again.
- **Ripe:** harvested and sown again with the seeds that came with it.
- **Enough:** it earns the pair's two and two more to lead with (`RanchChoice.EARN`, chickens too), so one that slips out can be led back. Then it keeps the cows.
- **Its limits:** it walks within reach before tilling or sowing, and gives up a visit after 400 steps. Short of wood for a hoe, or of seeds with no grass in sight, it stops and says so.

**Remembered herd:** grown animals it saw about its base are remembered for five minutes and counted. A herd that wandered off while the wheat grew is still the herd it saw, and the ranch starts out knowing where each was last seen.

**Shared fixes, found by the fixture:**
- **The scan finds the near block first** (`NativePlayerBody.findNearbyBlockMatching`). The tick's block scans share a budget of 128. The sweep ran from the bottom layer up and spent it on far cells before the table beside the player, so it set down twelve tables round itself and found none. It now scans nearest first.
- **A refused spot is set aside** (`PortalRunCampaign.setAside`). A ranch's or wheat plot's table placement that failed never reached the campaign, which sets its own failed spots aside, and one spot was refused 1,890 times. It now does. A table the wheat plot sets down is remembered as the ranch's is.

**Not covered:** wheat for cows and sheep is grown, not found, so it is not earned this way. A field of its own, sown with seeds from grass, is next. An operator's `/aiplayer ranch stop` ends the seed gathering too.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behaviour: a player bringing a villager home to its trading hall takes the hall's door off first. When
it comes to the door from beside the hall, it is in reach of the door, but the hall's own wall is between them. It
now steps round to the ground before the door's front and takes the door off from there. Before, it tried to break
the door from where it stood, was refused three times as out of sight, and gave the bring up: "I couldn't bring the
librarian home: door went wrong three times over."

Found: REFACTOR's runs of `NativeBringGameTests` on main 9972e80 (1 of 30). Hal's second try at a strolling librarian
aimed its break of the door from where the hall's own cobblestone stood in the way.

Cause (product): the bring's DOOR step asked only whether the door was in reach (`reaches`, eye to middle within 4.5).
It never asked whether the server would let it be broken from there (`NativePlayerBody.miningEligible`, which looks
for the door past its neighbours). A walk to the door that ends beside the hall, in reach, left it at a spot from
which every break is refused.

Where such a spot is: a probe of the staged scene (each standable cell around the door, its reach and the server's
sight) found every in-reach cell along the hall's front sees the door. The cells in reach that do not see it are at
the hall's sides, a block back from its front corners (x −1 and 7, z 6 and 7).

Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime` (bring, DOOR): in reach but not eligible to be mined from where it
  stands, the player first walks to the ground before the door's front (`b.door.south().down()`, where the OPEN step
  stands too), centred, as "to the door". From there it breaks the door as before. In reach and seen, nothing changes.
- `NativeBringGameTests`:
  - a new `scene` flag, `doorAside`;
  - the fixture `aDoorInReachButBehindTheHallsWallIsTakenOffFromItsFront`.

Nothing is granted.

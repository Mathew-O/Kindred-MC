The planner (`ConstructionPlanner.next`) says "The rest are blocks it cannot place yet" when every block left is in a
cell that is not `placeable()` (AIR or REPLACEABLE), or has no placement rule. So on CI those two cells read as
something else: PASSABLE, SOLID or PROTECTED. Neither log says which. No neighbour's plot or kept box overlaps the
bunker's kept box in either log.

## The diagnostic

`BuildRunner.logCannotPlaceYet` runs when the planner gives up that way. It logs each block not yet in its state:

- the block's position and the state it wants;
- what the site reads in its cell: kind, state, and whether it is floor;
- what the world holds there;
- its placement rule;
- whether it was given up on, refused, or came out wrong.

The line is logged once for each distinct set, not every tick the give-up lasts:

`Build <name>: the rest are blocks it cannot place yet: <pos> wants <spec>: site <kind> <state>, world <state>, rule <rule>[, given up][, refused][, came out wrong]; …`

## The failure

`NativeBunkerGameTests.itDigsOutLinesAndFurnishesItsHideoutUnderTheGround` failed on CI twice with "343/345 … The
rest are blocks it cannot place yet":

- #397, run 43829145998, job 133169957310;
- #417, run 44883669995, job 136784215071.

In both, the mason placed 343 of the hideout's 345 blocks. The two left were the shed roof's last planks, at y −46.
In passing runs those same two planks are the last placed.

The planner (`ConstructionPlanner.next`) says "The rest are blocks it cannot place yet" when every block left is in a
cell that is not `placeable()` (AIR or REPLACEABLE), or has no placement rule. So on CI those two cells read as
something else: PASSABLE, SOLID or PROTECTED. Neither log says which. No neighbour's plot or kept box overlaps the
bunker's kept box in either log.

**Alone, the class passes.** It passed 3 of 3 on main `b2219f3f`, and 1 of 1 with this change.

## The diagnostic

`BuildRunner.logCannotPlaceYet` runs when the planner gives up that way. It logs each block not yet in its state:

- the block's position and the state it wants;
- what the site reads in its cell: kind, state, and whether it is floor;
- what the world holds there;
- its placement rule;
- whether it was given up on, refused, or came out wrong.

The line is logged once for each distinct set, not every tick the give-up lasts:

`Build <name>: the rest are blocks it cannot place yet: <pos> wants <spec>: site <kind> <state>, world <state>, rule <rule>[, given up][, refused][, came out wrong]; …`

## The fix (core: `ConstructionPlanner.cellsOnLine`, `TIE`)
- **On a tie,** two or three faces crossed within `TIE` (1e-6 of the line) of the same point, the line takes every cell either order would pass through, then the cell past the edge.
- **So a break's look** (`clearSight` with outlines) is clear only where the server's is, whichever way it breaks the tie. The spot is dropped, and another spot or a scaffold to break from is planned.
- **Unchanged:**
  - every untied line;
  - placements' sampled line;
  - every other sight rule.
- No world read is added, and nothing is eased.
- **Who it reaches:** `cellsOnLine` and `clearSight` are package-private, called only in `ConstructionPlanner`.
  - `:416` is a placement's look, on the sampled line, unchanged.
  - `:440` is `stanceToBreak`, also reached through `scaffoldToBreak` and `takeDown`.
  - `ConstructionPlanner` is driven only by `BuildRunner`, from `PlayerRuntime` (builds and build work) and `RanchRunner` (a pen and its shut).
  - No mining, cast, `Digger` or `PortalRunCampaign` code calls it. `kindred-world-design` has its own `ConstructionPlanner`.
- **The corpus:** F-25c4dm's three gate logs (about 82,000 lines each) hold 0 `BuildRunner` lines. Their 653 to 730 placements are the replay's own: flint gravel, the cast's mould, cover, torches and shelter seals. So the corpus marker is 0 by construction, and no corpus gate is run.

What the walk found about running the server:
- AI players do not come back by themselves after a restart while their autonomy is off. `aiplayer native list`
  shows each "offline; stopped. ... explicit connect is required", and `aiplayer native connect <UUID>` brings each back
  where it was.
- Vanilla's `/worldborder get` always reads the overworld's border, whatever dimension `execute in` names. So the
  console cannot show the Nether's border, which Kindred keeps at an eighth (1,000) once the hub is set, put right each
  second (`FactionsService.applyBorders`; M-06f's GameTests check it).
- A claimed chunk the asking player stands in shows as `@` on `/faction map`, not `+`.
- AI players left standing with their autonomy off are not looked after: the three died to mobs four times in the
  first night (the Record's `death` events, `cause=mob`). On a live server they play (their autonomy on); here they
  were only the factions' members for the console.

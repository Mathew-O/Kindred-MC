## Scope and implementation

The first slice of Milestone 0 of the owner's combat design ([plan](../combat-plan.md)): the rules written down and the native body held to them.

- **`CombatRuleset`** (core, new) writes down vanilla Java 1.21.1's melee rules. Each was read from the game's own code on the pinned stack; no mod changes them.
  - A swing charges over twenty ticks divided by the attack speed. A sword's next strong hit comes eleven ticks after the last, an iron axe's twenty.
  - A strong hit is a charge above 0.9. A critical hit is strong, falling and in the air, and not climbing, in water, blind, riding or sprinting, for half as much again.
  - A strong hit while sprinting knocks back a level more. A sword's strong hit on the ground, moving slowly, and neither critical nor a sprint's, sweeps.
  - Damage immunity lasts twenty ticks. In the first ten, a hit lands only by what it exceeds the last one by, and not at all if it is no larger.
  - A shield blocks from the front once it has been held up five ticks. An axe's hit on a raised shield puts it out of use for a hundred ticks.
  - A player reaches three blocks from the eyes to an entity's hitbox, and four and a half to a block.
  - The damage numbers stay `DamageModel`'s (E-39e).
- **A client's reach.** Every strike went as far as four blocks between the player's and the target's feet: the recorded attack, the attack skill inside it, the runtime's tactical strikes and the blaze-rod strike. That is more than a client's three to the hitbox. All of them now use vanilla's own check, as the Dragon strike already did.
- **A shield out of use stays down.** The body raised a shield by setting the hand in use directly, which skips vanilla's refusal of an item on cooldown. An AI player could raise a shield an axe had just disabled; a human cannot. It is now refused.

Found on the way:
- **A failing step is retried, not failed.** A game-test step that fails is tried again on the next tick, so a check made every tick inside one passes as soon as any tick is right. The first versions of these fixtures passed their controls that way. Their per-tick checks now keep the first violation and fail at the end. Earlier fixtures checked counts or values that only grow, which this does not affect.
- **The test context's `spawnMob` clears a mob's goals.** A zombie spawned with it stood still and never swung its axe. A fixture that needs a mob to act spawns it by hand.

Changed modules/files: `CombatRuleset`, `CombatRulesetTest` (core, new); `NativePlayerBody` (`attack`'s reach, `block`'s cooldown), `NativeAttackAction`, `NativePlayerSkills` (the attack skill's reach), `PlayerRuntime` (the tactical and blaze strikes' reach); `NativeRulesetGameTests` (new).
Dependencies satisfied: E-07c (attacks and shields), E-39e (the damage model).

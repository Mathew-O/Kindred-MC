## Scope and implementation

The owner's note of 2026-10-23: "A repair feature should be implemented, if a chest breaks it replaces it if something gets blow up or destroyed it fixes it, there should never be a case where we are leaving graveyard builds." This slice covers a player's own base (M-01c).

**Seeing it.** When a player looks to its base (every ten seconds, under the operator's leave, with no goal, build or ranch of its own), it checks each finished stage of its base in the dimension it is in. It counts the blocks of the stage's plan that are gone or out of place. It compares them as a build does, so a door left open, a furnace lit or a bed slept in counts as in place. A stage any of whose blocks are in unloaded ground is not judged. A stage it began and did not finish is taken up first, as before.

**Mending it.** A stage with blocks out of place is built again on its own plan. The build places only what is missing, so the repair is any build: the player builds with what it holds, then fetches the shortfall through its needs in one trip (E-08c). It says what it is doing ("Repairing its cabin, 34 blocks out of place").
- **What it uses.** A chest broken with nothing left of it is crafted again from planks at a table. The blocks a blast threw down are picked up as it works and placed back.
- **When a repair ends short,** it is tried again after five minutes, not at once: what it lacks may come with time. A stage marked finished as far as it went is treated the same way: what it still lacks is a repair, tried later.
- **Which record.** The repair is kept apart from the base's own progress. Mending the cabin does not mark a later stage finished, nor count against the tries a stage gets to be built.

**Working from a hollow beside its build.** The climb back up to a build was meant for a player back from digging for stone, which starts eight blocks off the site. A blast's crater beside the wall had set it off too. The player stepped down into the crater to place a floor plank, "climbed out", stepped down again, and so on until the fixture ran out of time. It now climbs out only from further off than that: a hollow beside the build is where it works from.

Changed modules/files:
- Adapter:
  - `PlayerRuntime` (`baseDecision` checks its bases, `baseDamage`, `baseBuilt` for a repair, `baseRepair` on the player's entry).
  - `BuildRunner` (the climb out only from off the site).
  - Fixtures: `NativeBaseRepairGameTests` (new); `NativeBaseGameTests` (the ground and the exactness check shared).
Dependencies satisfied: M-01c (the player's own base), E-08c (needs).
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: none. A repair is not recorded; the world says what is out of place.

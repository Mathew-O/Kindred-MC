## The cause, read from a diagnostic (`NativeGoToSkill`)

**The diagnostic:**
- One line when a walk ends "Route … is blocked": which check fired, the body against its waypoint, what stood in the waypoint's two cells, and the reason for every replan before it.
- A stall trace at 20 and 30 ticks of "no progress": velocity, ground, collision, sprint, the steer's inputs, food and head room.

**What the two failures it caught showed:**
- Both had spent all 4 replans.
- Every replan was "no progress for 40 ticks", with an open waypoint (air, air) on a complete route, the body 0.8–1.1 short of it.
- The stall trace showed a body moving all the while: velocity 0.153 a tick toward the waypoint, which is what a full sprint of about 0.28 blocks a tick leaves after ground friction. It was on the ground, with no horizontal collision, sprinting at forward 1.0, food 20.
- The stalls came every 12–16 blocks of a straight run: 40 ticks of sprinting.
- Passing told walks of about 51 blocks had 3 to 5 such stalls. A failure was a walk that needed a fifth replan.

**The cause:**
- `bestDistance` was reset only by `plan()`, never when the walk moved on to the next waypoint.
- Progress was "nearer the current waypoint than the best so far, by 0.05". Each waypoint is reached at 0.4 off (`CENTRE_WITHIN`), and the next begins about a block off.
- So a body walking well rarely counted as progressing. Every 40 ticks of a straight run cost a replan out of `MAX_REPLANS = 4`, and a straight walk of more than about 50 blocks ended "Route … is blocked".

## The change (adapter: `NativeGoToSkill`, `PlayerRuntime` for a fixture accessor)

- **F-25c4dm3: moving on to a later waypoint is progress.**
  - `bestDistance` is set to the new waypoint's distance, and `ticksSinceProgress` to 0.
  - `MAX_REPLANS`, `STUCK_TICKS`, the "more than 4 off" and "under the waypoint" checks, and every other check are unchanged.
  - A body that cannot move on to a waypoint gains nothing by this, so a stuck walk still ends within the same bound.
- **F-10d1: the aim on a long straight run.**
  - When steering has chosen a long straight run (the aim is past the next waypoint), it aims at the point `LOOK_AHEAD` (6) blocks along the route (`alongRoute`), if that point is reachable in a straight line.
  - Before, it aimed at the farthest such waypoint itself, which jumped from corner to corner (below).
  - Otherwise the aim is as before, including `PURSUIT`'s carrot near the goal.
  - Sprint, sprint-jump and head-room checks use the same aim waypoint as before.
- **Kept from the diagnostic:** the one line when a walk ends "Route … is blocked", with the replans' reasons.
- **The gate's markers:**
  - **F-25c4dm3's:** "no stall at …, moving on through its waypoints (by the old measure, forty ticks of no progress)". It is logged where the old measure would have called 40 ticks of no progress and the new one did not. A truly stuck body raises both counts together, so it never prints it.
  - **F-10d1's:** "its yaw reversed a third time at …", logged once a walk. A reversal is counted as F-10d's walk test counts it: two successive turns of more than 3° in opposite directions.

## Scope and implementation

The owner's direction (2026-10-25): "A hit dealing more then 3 hearts of damage from another player ai or human should be treated as lethal aggression, the ai player should evade or defend its self if equipped."

**What a hit took.** Measured on the body, as the health and absorption it cost: vanilla's own reckoning of armour, enchantments, a shield and resistance, read rather than redone (`NativePlayerBody`'s damage override, its hit hook). Fabric's `AFTER_DAMAGE` gives a hit before armour, and only to the mod's own runtime, so it is not used. A killing blow is no hit to answer.

**Lethal aggression.** More than three hearts (six points) in one hit from another player, AI or human, however the fight began (`FightOdds.lethal`). Every hit from a player is also noted as the hardest that player has dealt it, which the odds count (E-41h). A lesser hit changes nothing.

**Its answer** (`FightOdds.answer`). It defends itself only where it may strike that player at all, a running match both joined (D7, `MatchRegistry.mayAttack`), when it is equipped (a weapon better than its fist), and when the fight is winnable, or even with no way away (E-41h); otherwise it gets away. Outside a match it never strikes back, whatever its kit.

**At once.** What it was doing gives way, a fall's water-bucket clutch aside: the running skill is cancelled through the one cancellation barrier, and the answer is the most urgent thing it can do after a clutch (`aggressionCandidates`, URGENT 99.9). The interrupt stays owed until the answer is what runs: a task chosen just before the hit and still being admitted (here, a duel's own fight) is cancelled when it starts. A fight already under way with that attacker is its answer when it defends, and goes on.

**Getting away.** At a sprint, to open ground away from the attacker and never toward its home (`awayFrom`, as the manhunt runner's evasion does, M-02c). With no standable way away it is cornered, which only an even fight counts. It is away once far off (24 blocks) and either out of the attacker's sight or unhit for ten seconds; or once the attacker is gone. Then it goes back to what it was doing and does not seek them out. In a duel, the duel's own fight takes up again after it, since the match is a fight it joined.

**What it says.** Who hit it, how hard as it took it, and why it chose what it did, to those who hear it: "Rue hit me hard, 4 hearts. I'm getting away." (outside a match); "Rue hit me hard, 3.8 hearts. I can't win this: they'd outlast me 4.8 to one even at best. I'm getting away."; "Rue hit me hard, 4.5 hearts. I can win this: I'd outlast them 3.0 to one even at worst. I'm fighting back." A stopped player only notes the hit: it decides and says nothing.

**Found and fixed alongside.** A hurt player holding golden apples looped on trying to eat one at full hunger: the runtime counted the apple edible (it is), the eat action refused it. The action and the body now use vanilla's own rule, food that can always be eaten eaten full.

## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: each AI player keeps a record of how it stands with each person and player it knows, worked out
only from what happened between them in the game.

| What happened | Friendship |
| --- | --- |
| A gift it gave that was picked up (E-22a) | +3 |
| A gift it was given | +6 |
| An outing together (E-27a) | +5 |
| A blow from them outside a match both joined (E-49's record) | −12 |

- **What never counts:** anything said in chat, a declined gift, a toss left lying, a chest found short, or a fall after
  coming where asked. Missing items, proximity and asking are not proof of anything.
- **Each thing once:** each event is kept once under its own key (the same gift is never counted twice), with when it
  happened and where the record came from.
- **Bands from the sum:** friend from 30, friendly from 8, cool below 0, wary at −24 and below; neutral otherwise.
- **Effect:** friendship grants and withholds nothing yet (E-25 will let it shape tone and discretionary choices). It never
  changes control, access or budgets, and is never a reason to strike.
- **Inspecting and correcting:** with `/aiplayer rapport [player [peer [strike <n>]]]`, an operator or the player's
  controller sees every standing, or one peer's numbered events. `strike <n>` marks an event wrong. It keeps who did that
  and when, and the friendship is worked out again at once. Nobody else may look.
- **Where it is kept:** `kindred/memory/<uuid>.rapport.json`, beside the ledger. It is saved with it every minute and at a
  stop, and read back at start. It is bounded to 64 peers and their 50 newest events. `/aiplayer memory forget` removes
  someone from it as from the ledger.

## Scope and implementation

The owner's direction (2026-10-25): "We should also add a method that allows the ai player to gauge weather or not a fight is winnable, how much damage is the enemy doing vs its armor/kit."

**The reckoning** (`FightOdds`, core, pure). How long each side would take to bring the other down: the other's health and what it can win back in the fight, over what it deals a second through the other's armour (`DamageModel`, `CombatRuleset.afterArmour`), Protection and a shield held up, no faster than the target's invulnerability allows. The odds are the ratio, at best and at worst: winnable when it outlasts them by a tenth even at worst; losing when it is outlasted by a tenth even at best; even between.

**What it can know.** Itself exactly: its health and absorption, its armour and toughness, its Protection, its best weapon and its Sharpness, its shield, its golden apples (six health each, an enchanted one twenty). An opponent only as seen and believed: the armour it wears and the weapon in its hand, whether either glints with enchantments it cannot read, its health as believed from the hits seen (`HealthBeliefs`), and the hardest hit it has dealt the player. Hidden state is never read.

**What it cannot see**, allowed for at worst: a glint on their armour as eight levels of Protection, a glint on their sword or axe as Sharpness III, and their hits all critical. A hit they were seen to deal counts at least as hard as it was.

**In words**, for when it explains a choice: "I can win this: I'd outlast them 3.0 to one even at worst."; "It's even: ..."; "I can't win this: they'd outlast me 4.8 to one even at best."

**In play.** A hard hit's answer (E-41g) is decided by these odds.

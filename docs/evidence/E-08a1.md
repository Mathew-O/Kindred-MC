## Scope and implementation

`ConstructionPlanner.sealedIn` decides whether a player standing inside the build would be shut in once a block and
every block still wanted were placed. Its search for a way out took any open cell in any of the six directions. So a way
out could go straight up, through the air over a wall, where no player can climb. A ring's last gap, with walls two
high, was refused from inside only by cost: a spot inside the build costs 3 more. Where no spot outside reached the gap,
it was placed from inside, and the player was shut in. Found in E-71b1, whose "from where it stands first" gave the
feet a first claim inside the build.

The search now goes over the build as it will stand: every block still wanted in place (a wooden door still to go is a
way through), and the block being placed.
- Sideways and down through open cells, as before. A drop of any height, or down its own scaffold, is a way out.
- Up only as a player goes up on foot: a step of one onto something to stand on, with head room for the jump.

A first version that took only walking steps, with drops of at most three, was stricter than this. It refused the oak
keep's high slab from every spot (`CompoundsTest.thePlannerBuildsTheWholeKeepAndTakesItsScaffoldDown`: "No way to reach
minecraft:oak_slab"), since a player up there gets out by a longer drop or down its scaffold. It was not kept.

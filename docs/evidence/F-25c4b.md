## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player on the portal run puts up no cover, a block between it and a skeleton or a witch, while a zombie, spider or other mob on foot that is after it stands within three blocks. That mob is past any block it puts up, and holding a block leaves no sword in hand. Too hurt to fight, it still backs away from it; with no way off, it fights it.

Found in B7's second pairs (live, seed 8675309, the three starters on the Nether goal, 144,000 ticks): in the first run, with the strategist off, all nine deaths came in a defensive move, five of them while it held a block for cover.

| Player | Killed by | Doing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kindred | spider | taking cover from a witch, holding dirt |
| Kindred | zombie | taking cover from a skeleton, after a way off failed |
| Kindred | zombie | placing a block, after a way off failed |
| Echo | zombie | taking cover from a skeleton |
| Sol | zombie | taking cover from a skeleton |

The other four: two shot or struck while backing off, one blown up and one struck while holing up.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunCampaign.defend`: a new `onFootInReach` finds the nearest mob on foot after the player, in sight, within three blocks. While there is one:
  - no cover is taken from a shooter, whatever its health;
  - too hurt, or outnumbered and hurt, it backs off as before, but takes no cover; with no way off, it fights that mob;
  - unarmed, it does not duck behind a block either;
  - the fight is with that mob, not with whatever else is nearest.
- `PortalRunCampaign` is changed on `modest-faraday` only in its hole-up lines.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one fixture.

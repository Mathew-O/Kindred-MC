## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: the player throws ender pearls on the arc a pearl really flies. `Ballistics` (core) follows thrown items as well as arrows: a pearl leaves at 1.5 blocks a tick, is slowed by drag 0.99 and pulled down by 0.03 each tick, as vanilla 1.21.1's `ThrownEntity` moves it. `PearlFlight` (adapter) flies a pearl through the real blocks a tick at a time (a point, as vanilla's projectile raycast is), puts the player where vanilla does (where the pearl was when it struck) and finds where the player then comes to rest. A throw is chosen by flying each candidate (the high arc and the low, nudged by up to 1.6 degrees) sixteen more times as vanilla's spread would throw it (`ProjectileEntity.calculateVelocity`: a triangular amount of up to 0.0172 added to each part of the unit direction before it is scaled, so the speed varies too; the sixteen are drawn once from a fixed seed). It throws only when fourteen of the sixteen land on the ground meant, and never when any sample, or any of the spread's eight extremes, could strike an End crystal (vanilla blows a crystal up at any hit, a pearl's too, beside the player it brings), leave it over the void, or leave it a drop that would take it below four health. `native_pearl` throws one owned pearl from the hotbar under the durable ledger, waits out vanilla's cooldown, follows the pearl until it lands, verifies that exactly one pearl was spent and reports where the player landed. In the End, a player that finds itself high on a pillar's top or a cage that is not its own tower pearls down to end stone.

Tried and dropped, a pearl onto a caged crystal's roof. A roof of iron bars is mostly gap to a pearl: a pane's arms are an eighth of a block wide. A simulation of the cage as vanilla builds it (`EndSpikeFeature`), with the pane shapes and the crystal's box widened by 0.3 for a projectile's hit, found no throw from 8 to 40 blocks off that lands on the roof more than about three times in five. The steep throws drop through onto the crystal 30 to 60 per cent of the time, and every throw had some chance of a harmful miss. In the native fixture the leg's own search chose a throw from 34 blocks off that 8 of its 16 samples landed and none flagged as harmful. Thrown for real, it fell through and blew the crystal up. Sixteen samples cannot rule out a rare harm, so the spread's extremes are now flown too, and the roof way is gone: caged crystals are shot through their bars or pillared to (E-39c).

Changed modules/files: `Ballistics` (core: `Projectile` with `ARROW` and `PEARL`, the high arc), `BallisticsTest`; `PearlFlight` (new), `NativePearlAction` (new), `NativePlayerBody.throwHeld`, `NativePlayerSkills` (`native_pearl`), `PlayerRuntime` (`nativePearl`), `EndFightLeg` (the stranded pearl down, `downThrow`); `NativeEndGameTests` (`aPearlIsThrownUpOntoALedgeAndLandsThere`, `strandedOnAPillarItPearlsDownClearOfTheCrystals`).

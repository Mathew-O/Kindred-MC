## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior:
Autonomous AI players can verify, ignite, and transit Nether portals, establish arrival waypoints, and conserve inventory and state across dimensions:
- Geometric portal frame validation (`PortalFrame.isValidObsidianFrame`): validates standard 4x5 obsidian portal frames (minimal 10 obsidian blocks up to 14 blocks full frame with 2x3 air interior opening) across both X and Z alignments.
- Authentic portal ignition (`native_ignite_portal`): executes durable checkpointed ignition using owned flint and steel or fire charge under single-use `ActionAdmission`, incurring authentic 1 durability wear on the ignition tool, creating `Blocks.NETHER_PORTAL` in the 2x3 interior. Rejects obstructed frames or non-ignition tools without consuming durability.
- Qualified cross-dimension transit: `NativePlayerBody.canUsePortals` is gated on server authorization via `portalTravelAuthorized` (default false preserves unqualified barrier per F-25b boundaries). When authorized, players traverse into portal blocks and transfer between Overworld (`minecraft:overworld`) and Nether (`minecraft:the_nether`) with authentic 8:1 coordinate scaling (`toNetherCoordinates` / `toOverworldCoordinates`).
- Nether expedition anchoring and progression milestones: on dimension transit detected in `PlayerRuntime.beforeNativeTick`, updates companion dimension in memory and persistence (`withDimension`), anchors arrival portal as `WaypointKind.NETHER_PORTAL` in `expeditionState`, and advances milestones (`MilestoneId.ENTER_NETHER`, `GATHER_BLAZE_RODS`, `GATHER_NETHER_WART`) via `MilestoneGraph` and `ProgressionPolicy`.
- Exact state and inventory conservation: bit-for-bit conservation of all 36 inventory slots, equipment slots (head, chest, legs, feet, offhand), health, and durable action accounting across dimension transfers and SQLite persistence (`SqliteCompanionStore`).

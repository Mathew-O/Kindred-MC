## Scope and implementation

**Found:** `NativeObsidianPickupGameTests.anUntakenStepToTakeTheWaterBackLeavesNoObsidianBehind` failed in CI on #355, #357
(run 43722834395, attempt 1) and #360: "Went exploring with its obsidian left in the water". The obsidian it had mined
lay in the hole its lava left, under the shore, while the water it had poured was taken back and the last of the flow
drained over it.

**Cause, in `PortalRunCampaign` (shown by the staged fixture's logs, each failure on main the same):**
1. `pickUpLoose` saw the drop, and `intoTheDropsCell` found no way into its cell while the water still ran there. It set
   the drop aside for a minute ("no way into its cell", `DROP_NO_WAY`), and nothing went back for it after: the next
   decisions climbed toward the surface for logs.
2. **F-22c94a:** once the water drained, the drop, in the hole under the shore, was out of the player's sight, and
   `pickUpLoose` took only drops in sight. It was never seen again and was left behind.

**What "in use" means for an obsidian site, before and after:**
- **Before** (`siteStillUseful`, unchanged, still used where it was): its lava, or its obsidian, or **any** water at
  its water cell, flowing or still.
- **After** (`siteInUse`, new, used by the two guards below): its lava, or its obsidian, or its water cell holding
  water that **is still a source**. The last of the flow, draining once the source is back in the bucket, is not in
  use: held for it, a decision went exploring while the drop lay in it.

**The change:**
- `intoTheDropsCell(body, drop, at, label)`: with no way into the drop's cell, the drop is not set aside while the
  site is in use; the site's own steps come first, as for a drop in the water (F-25c4cs). If the cell is wet, the drop's
  own settle wait (`dropWaits`, 100 ticks, one second at a time) comes first. Only with still no way in after that is it
  set aside, as before.
- `pickUpLoose`'s settle guard for a drop still in the water uses `siteInUse` in place of `siteStillUseful`.
- **F-22c94a:** a drop of the player's own break counts out of sight too (`player.canSee(drop) || body.ownDrop(id)`),
  never another's. `NativeBreakAction` records the drops of each of its breaks on the body (`knowOwnDrop`).
  `NativePlayerBody` keeps the last 64 such ids in memory only. They are never saved, and `respawn()` clears them, so a
  new life starts with none.

**Nothing granted.** No item is made, moved or copied by the change; the drop is still walked to and picked up.
Changed files:
- `companion-server-adapter`: `PortalRunCampaign` (`siteInUse`, `intoTheDropsCell`, `pickUpLoose`), `NativeBreakAction`,
  `NativePlayerBody`.
- Test: `NativeObsidianPickupGameTests.theWaterTakenBackItsLastFlowStillDrainingTheObsidianIsPickedUpFirst` (batch
  `obsidianPickupDrain`, new).

The untaken-step scene, with one staging: from the obsidian being mined until its water is back in the bucket, the player

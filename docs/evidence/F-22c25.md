## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product: bringing a villager home by rail, a player pushes the cart into it and the cart rolls home
with it aboard. While it rolls, the player went back behind the line's start, so that a cart running out of way short of
the cell would meet it there and be pushed on again. Pushed from the line's start, the cart has the whole line ahead of
it again: the turn and the climb into the hall took the way from every push, and it rolled back to the start each time.
After eight pushes the player gave up with the villager aboard.

Now, while the cart rolls, the player follows it along the line, two rails behind the furthest rail it has got to, on the
rails level with the line's start; up the climb into the hall there is no room to stand. A cart that runs out of way and
rolls back meets the player there and stops against it. It is pushed on from the rail behind it, near the climb, and gets
up it.

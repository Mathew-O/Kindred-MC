# F-22c19 — A bring's cart is home only once at rest on its cell's floor for a second

Date: 2026-11-05
Agent / branch: Claude / claude/ci-green-1105 (CI fully green, F-22)
Build: on top of `bbe5ae8`; recorded in [validation](../validation.md)
Status: complete (native), not reproduced deterministically

## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. In a bring's ROLL phase (E-45e), the cart counted as home once it was on its cell's floor
and still. Still meant a speed under 0.01 at that moment, or not moved for two seconds. A cart that runs out of way at
the top of the climb into its cell stops there for a tick before it rolls back, and that tick read as home.
- **CI on PR #25** (`bbe5ae8`, run 42091545108): 563 of 564; `NativeBringGameTests.theRailsAndTheCartAreMadeFromItsOwnIron`
  failed.
  - After a second push (0.15), the bring went on to shut the cell: its front rail broken, a shutter placed.
  - Then it went to break the cart in the cell: "The cart is out of reach".
  - The cart, the librarian in it, was back at the line's start, off its first rail ("riding Minecart … y=-58.00").
  - The walk to the cart's cell's front went on without end until the test's ticks ran out.
- **Locally**, on a 25 ms store, 8 copies of this and the off-the-line fixture passed before the fix: where the cart
  stops depends on its speed off the push.

Changed file: `companion-server-adapter` `PlayerRuntime` (`Bring.floorSince`). The cart is home only after resting on
its cell's floor for 20 ticks. Until then the bring waits for it.

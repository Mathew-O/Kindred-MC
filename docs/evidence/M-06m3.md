## Scope and implementation

**What a player does differently:** a player far out in water, with no dry ground in reach where it is, swims back to the dry ground it last stood on, as a player swims back the way it came.

**Changed:**
- **companion-core `route/DryMemory` (new):** the last dry cell and its dimension.
  - `swimBack(dimension, feet)` offers the cell only in its own dimension, within `BOUND` (128) blocks, and not within 3 (there already).
  - It does not offer a cell given up. A cell is given up by `swimFailed()`, or by a swim back that set out no nearer than the last.
  - `in(dimension)` forgets a cell of another dimension.
  - `forget()` clears it at a death and a respawn.
- **companion-server-adapter `PortalRunCampaign`:**
  - **`surface()`:** once both dry-ground searches find nothing, it asks `swimBack`. A cell offered becomes `go(cell, 1.0, partial)`, labelled "Get out of the water: back to dry ground it stood on". Otherwise it does as before.
  - **`noteDry(player)`:** records the cell where the body's own feet stand on a solid top, out of the water.
  - **`forgetDry()`.**
  - **`taskFinished`:** a failed swim back gives the cell up.
- **companion-server-adapter `PlayerRuntime`:**
  - **The tick:** every 20 ticks, for each player with a campaign and a connected, live body, it calls `noteDry`, whatever task drives the body. That is the lead's condition, and "a campaign" means the campaign object exists, not a goal running.
  - **Death and respawn:** `onNativeDeath` and `respawnNative` call `forgetDry`.
- **Nothing is read of the world** but the body's own feet. No scan, no look at the remembered cell.

**Why the per-second record:** the first version recorded the cell only when the goal planned. In the shipped pair on `1328f544`, faction walks carried Eli from land about 220 blocks off its goal's last plan out to sea at (−516, 61, −141). The cell it remembered was beyond 128, and its 12-minute spell had no swim back.

**Tests:**

- `NativeSwimBackGameTests` (new), registered after the dry-miss class.

**No schema or configuration change.**

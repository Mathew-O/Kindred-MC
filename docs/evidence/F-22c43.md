## Scope and implementation

Root cause, in the product. The source is #98's CI, run 42574813864 (job 129020033339),
`NativeCompoundGameTests.aPlayerBringsAPairInByThePasturesDoorwayBreedsThemAndShutsItBehindThem`, "Both cows penned: 0".
- Wren led the first cow in by the doorway into the pen, then went "Out for another cow" with the pen's gate open. The
  ranch shuts the gate on those inside only when the next is far off.
- The first cow walked out of the open gate, across the pasture, and down the stair of the pasture's own way in, the
  passage one wide under its east wall (`Compounds.passage`, `DEEP` 3).
- Wren went for it and led it through that passage, the second cow following. It ended under the wall with a cow each
  side of it, (6239226, −56) and (6239229, −56). Every walk was "Route … is blocked", both cows were given up, and the
  ranch left with neither in the pen.

The design choice was the lead's: (b) a way in that cattle cannot enter, and (c) the gate kept shut. A first attempt was
dropped: on a walk refused down there, putting the food away to let the cows wander off.
- Across 13 copies of a fixture staging CI's end state, it passed 8.
- A debug line showed why. The player stood behind the cow it was leading, which stood pressed to it in the one-wide
  way between it and the gate. No waiting clears that.

Changed files: companion-core `Compounds`, `Ranch`.
- **`Compounds.pastureWard` (b):** an oak door, two high, on the yard just before the way in's stair inside
  (`wayInDoor`), and oak fences along the stair's open side and the door's, at the yard's level. The stair's other
  side is the ward's lining.
  - A player's walk opens the door and shuts it behind, as walks through doors already do (M-01b). Cattle neither open
    it nor jump it.
  - A first form put the door on the stair's head, its top a block over the yard: the fixture's cow jumped onto it and
    walked off into the stair.
  - The door and fences are blocks of the ward, built from what the player holds, gathers or crafts like any other.
  - A pasture ward built before keeps its way in as built until it is built again or repaired to its plan.
- **`Ranch` (c):** outside the pen, with one or more inside and the gate open, the gate is shut before anything else
  until the next animal is lined up at the gate's front. Before, it was shut only when the next was more than
  `FOLLOWING` off.
- M-06a's description (the tracker row and the operator guide) says so.

Changed tests:
- **`CompoundsTest`:** the pasture ward's "no door: the way in is under the wall" now checks for one door, the way in's,
  and none elsewhere. This is the design change, approved, not a loosened check.
- **New fixture `NativeCompoundGameTests.aCowLooseInThePastureDoesNotGetIntoItsWayIn`** (batch `compoundWayInDoor`):
  the three wards as they stand; a cow just inside the way in, on the yard; a person beyond the door down in the passage
  with wheat in hand, which the cow follows. For 600 ticks the cow must never be past the door.

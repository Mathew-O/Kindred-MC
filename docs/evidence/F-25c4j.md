## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player on the cast route goes to lava it has seen, however far off, a leg of 96 blocks at a time, instead of digging down to the deep lava. With both buckets made, an iron pickaxe that wears out is not made again before the cast; stone pickaxes made from the stone round it dig what is left.

Found in the live runs on `10a60d5` (seed 20261030, strategist off):
1. Kindred knew of lava, but more than 160 blocks off. "Cast: walk to the lava" was refused as one walk six times ("Goto target is too far for one route"). The source was set aside, and Kindred dug a staircase from Y=62 down to Y=-55 and branch-mined there for lava.
2. Kindred's iron pickaxe wore out by Y=-55. Its plan counted 3 iron for another one, which made 4 with the flint and steel, and it held 2 ("Mine iron ore (2/4)"). It mined one iron ore at Y=-54, then climbed from Y=-55 to Y=5 for coal to smelt it.
3. Over the eight player-runs on `fb02bc3` and `10a60d5`, players made up to four iron pickaxes each, 12 iron, against 6 for both buckets.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core): `ironPickaxeWanted` takes the snapshot. When the frame is cast, the iron pickaxe is wanted only while a bucket is still to make. Before that it comes first as ever, and on the mined route it is always wanted. The same rule counts the pickaxe's iron in `ironShortfall`, its sticks in `sticksKept` and the stage.
- `PortalRunCampaign.castPortal`: lava further than 96 blocks is walked toward in legs ("Cast: walk toward the lava at …", a partial walk to `legToward`). Two legs in a row that get it no nearer set that lava aside, and the search for other lava runs as before.
- `PlayerRuntime.campaignForTest`: a fixture accessor that makes the goal's campaign as its first plan would, so a fixture can stage what the player saw before.
- `PortalRunPlannerTest`, `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: one case and one fixture.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- Legs follow the straight line to the lava. Water, a ravine or a mountain between is crossed as far as each partial walk gets; two legs with no gain give up on that lava.
- Lava nobody has seen is still looked for in caves and then at Y=-55.
- Before the buckets, iron pickaxes that wear out are still made again. Iron is still a third to a half of each player's time (19 to 49% in those runs).

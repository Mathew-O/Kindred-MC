## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: with `KINDRED_STRATEGIST=on` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in the server's environment, an AI player with the Nether goal consults a Claude model (default Claude Haiku 5.5) when something changes. The model picks how careful the player should be for the next five minutes and may name places to avoid. The local plan still plays every moment. Each player keeps a memory file for each world. Without the switch, or on any failure, play is unchanged.

Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core/.../survival/StrategyAdvice.java`: the advice contract and its validation. A stance from a fixed set (`PRESS_ON`, `PLAY_SAFE`, `SURFACE_AND_RECOVER`) or no advice at all; at most three places to avoid, each within 24 blocks of a place the player reported; reason and note trimmed to one plain line of 240 characters.
- `companion-model/.../ClaudeStrategist.java`: transport through the official Anthropic Java SDK (`com.anthropic:anthropic-java:2.75.0`, pinned in the manifest).
  - The reply is constrained by structured output to the `Reply` record (stance enum, reason, note, places).
  - Planning-lane bounds: a fifteen-second deadline, no retries and 1,000 output tokens.
  - It runs on its own daemon thread.
  - The key is read from the environment only; it is never stored or logged.
- `companion-server-adapter/.../StrategistBridge.java`: when to ask and what it may cost.
  - Triggers: the goal starting, a death, a no-progress spell, a stage change, or five minutes with changed progress. At least 20 s apart per player.
  - Concurrency: one request in flight per player, one at a time in all.
  - Rates: six a minute per player, twelve in all.
  - Daily token budgets per player and in all: 50,000 and 100,000 by default, set by `KINDRED_STRATEGIST_PLAYER_DAILY_TOKENS` and `KINDRED_STRATEGIST_DAILY_TOKENS`.
  - Reservation: the conservative input estimate plus the maximum output is reserved before a request and settled to the reported usage. A failure without usage keeps its reservation.
  - The reply is applied on the game thread, and only to the same goal that asked.
- `companion-server-adapter/.../ModelContextStore.java`: one JSON file per player per world at `<world>/kindred/model-context/<player UUID>.json`. It holds the last 20 notes, deaths (place, cause and stage) and pieces of advice, plus consultation and token totals. It is read and written on its own thread, and each write replaces the file whole through a temporary file.
- `companion-server-adapter/.../PortalRunCampaign.java`: the strategist section.
  - An event log of deaths, watchdog moves, stage changes and new danger zones, and the death history.
  - `strategyReport`: the compact report the model sees.
  - The stance in force for five minutes, and its effects:
    - `PLAY_SAFE` skips caves and is outmatched four health points sooner.
    - `SURFACE_AND_RECOVER` climbs out to daylight (walking back or a staircase), heals to 18 there, then turns into `PLAY_SAFE` for the rest of the five minutes, so it does not climb out again.
    - Advised places become danger zones for a quarter of a day.
- `companion-server-adapter/.../PlayerRuntime.java`: constructs the bridge, ticks it after each goal step, passes the death message on as the cause, closes it on stop, and adds `adviseGoal` (used by fixtures).
- Build: `companion-model` depends on the SDK. The adapter depends on and includes `companion-model`. The release jar does not yet carry the SDK; with the switch on there, a `LinkageError` is caught and play goes on without the strategist.

Dependencies satisfied: F-19a (companion-model module), F-25c4 (the goal it advises).
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: no SQLite or NBT change. The context files are new, per world, and may be deleted. New environment switches are listed above.
Requirements and AT portions covered: AI-01 (optional, operator key, off by default), AI-02 (no code, permissions, items or completion; model text is data), AI-03 (strict schema and validation; the reply is applied only to the goal that asked), AI-04 (meaningful changes only; local work continues while a reply is pending), AI-05 (planning lane deadline, rates, concurrency, reserved budgets). The reply is a stance, not a one-to-six-step plan, so there are no steps to revalidate at dispatch.

### Live runs

The runs below are development runs on seed 8675309, one AI player from an empty inventory. They are not a release gate.

- Run 60 had no strategist. It ran on `ffbb4c4` for 32 minutes and is the baseline.
  - Five deaths: a zombie, drowning, a creeper, a skeleton and a pillager.
  - Gear: an iron pickaxe within three minutes, then two buckets, a chestplate and a shield.
  - It was still at the iron stage when stopped. Six iron pickaxes were made, worn out or lost in deaths.
  - The drowning came while every goal step was cancelled by the urgent retreat, underwater.
- Run 61 had the strategist on.
  - The first three consultations, at the goal start and at the stone and iron stages, said `PRESS_ON`. Each was 1,199 to 1,429 input and about 100 output tokens.
  - The player's context file was written after the first reply.
  - Status at commit time, six and a half minutes in:
    - No deaths. One iron pickaxe, made at about two minutes.
    - Seven consultations, all `PRESS_ON`, at the goal start, the stone and iron stages, three no-progress spells and a one-step fall back to the wood stage when its pickaxe wore out.
    - 10,788 input and 1,276 output tokens: about a fifth of a cent at Haiku 5.5 prices.
    - Three notes kept.
    - Replies came back within a few seconds of each request; play did not wait for any of them.

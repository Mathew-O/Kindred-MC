## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a player casting its frame block by block, a bucket of lava at a time, sometimes has a block to dig out before the next cast: the cell over a side's top block, where the frame is cut into rock. If that block is out of reach from where it stands, back at the lava with a full bucket say, it now goes back to the frame and digs it from there. Before, it gave up the frame on the spot, with what it had cast in it, and began another.

- With no room for a frame there, it tunnelled through the deepslate for 209 more blocks. Its iron pickaxe wore out; it made a stone one from what it carried.
- From 19:55:50 to 19:56:11 it cast six of the frame's ten blocks at -3, -55, -90 to -87, going back to the pool for each bucket.
- Back at the pool at 19:56:12, the cell over the next side's top block, deepslate at -3, -51, -90, was out of reach, and the frame was given up. The world save shows the six blocks still there, and four more in a second frame begun at x=-6.

## Scope and implementation
User-visible behavior: Pure tactical priority hierarchy, read-only threat/weapon evaluation, and bounded combat prediction foundation for autonomous AI players. Shadow evaluation runs diagnostics in `beforeNativeTick` without issuing body inputs, modifying inventory, or dispatching unearned actions.
Changed modules/files:
- `companion-core`: `dev.kindred.core.tactics.*` (`TacticalObservation`, `WeaponProfile`, `StandardCombatPredictor`, `CombatEstimateModel`, `TacticalPriorityPolicy`, `TacticalOption`, `TacticalDecision`, `TacticalSequence`, `TacticalObjective`, `SurvivalClass`, `DamageInterval`), `TacticalPriorityPolicyTest`, `CombatEstimateModelTest`.
- `companion-server-adapter`: `NativeTacticalObserver`, `PlayerRuntime` (shadow evaluation in `beforeNativeTick`, diagnostics accessors `nativeTacticalDiagnostics`, `nativeTacticalObservation`), `NativeTacticalGameTests`.
Dependencies satisfied: Java 21, Fabric Loader 0.19.5, Yarn 1.21.1+build.3:v2, SQLite schema 7.
Schema/configuration changes and migration coverage: Zero schema additions. Strictly read-only observation and pure calculations; zero inventory mutations, zero RNG consumption, zero direct entity damage cheats.
Requirements and AT portions covered: Pure survival-first hierarchy (`SURVIVABLE > EMERGENCY_ONLY > UNKNOWN > INELIGIBLE`), lexicographic objective ranking (`RESOLVE_IMMEDIATE_SERIOUS_HARM > PREVENT_IMMINENT_IRREVERSIBLE_MATERIAL_LOSS > END_ENCOUNTER_SAFELY > FULFIL_SURVIVAL_OR_TEAM_TASKS > WAIT_FOR_MEANINGFUL_CHANGE`), authentic attack cooldown charge curve ($0.2 + 0.8c^2$), critical strike 1.5x on base portion only, target-sensitive enchantments (Sharpness, Smite, Bane of Arthropods), target armor mitigation formula, item switch charge resets, 10-tick i-frame non-additivity (`Math.max(0, current - prev)`), unknown enchantments yielding `SurvivalClass.UNKNOWN`, creeper fuse with forced ignition, multi-enemy accumulated self-damage, read-only observation preserving player privacy, and shadow diagnostic execution without mutations or body inputs.

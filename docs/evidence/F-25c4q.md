## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: on the cast route (both frame buckets, no diamonds), a player keeps its first three iron for the rest of the route's iron. It no longer makes them into an iron pickaxe at once. It digs for the iron with stone pickaxes, and once all the route's iron is in hand it makes the iron pickaxe with the buckets and the rest, for the way down to the lava. Where the frame is mined, the iron pickaxe still comes first: the diamonds need it.

Found in the long live run on `a286cc8` (seed 8675309, 240,000 ticks, strategist off):
- Kindred made four iron pickaxes, at 14:42, 14:47, 14:57 and 15:05, and never a bucket. It spent 62% of its time mining iron and never left the iron stage.
- The goal gathers all of a route's iron before it makes the buckets (19 in all, the chestplate's eight among them). But it made the iron pickaxe from its first three.
- Each iron pickaxe wore out before Kindred had found the rest: "Mine iron ore (4/15)" became "(1/15)" when the next one was made. The next three iron then went to another. Found three at a time, as Kindred found them, the iron never added up.

Changed modules/files:
- `PortalRunPlanner` (core): the iron pickaxe first only where the frame is not cast.
- `PortalRunPlannerTest`: one case. F-25c4j's case no longer checks the iron pickaxe first on the cast route; the new case checks it first on the mined route.
- `NativePortalCampaignGameTests`: two fixtures staged three raw iron and relied on the pickaxe first to smelt them at once. Their stock now includes both buckets and a chestplate, so the three are more than the route still wants and are smelted at once as before.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

- The route's iron is still all gathered before the buckets are made, and the chestplate's eight are the most of it. A death before then loses it all.
- Stone pickaxes dig all the iron now, so more of them are made, and more sticks. The stick reserve already counts the iron's digging as done with stone.

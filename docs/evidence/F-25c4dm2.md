## The change (adapter: `PortalRunCampaign`, `PlayerRuntime`)

- **`toldLava`, within 6 and inside the 200 ticks, never returns empty:**
  - first a walk onto the told cell itself, range 1.5, labelled "…, onto the place told";
  - once that walk has ended, a look, a one-second `native_wait`: "Cast: look for the lava … told it of at …";
  - then, at 200 ticks, it is set aside as before ("there, and no lava in sight"), and the hunt goes on.
- **The walk onto the cell is known by its own label.** Asked for while the walk toward the place was still going, the walk that then ended was the earlier one. In a first try a flag took it for the walk onto the cell, and nothing walked it on round the corner. Now `lavaTaskFinished` marks it ended only when a walk with that label ends.
- **A hazard retreat is not a refusal.**
  - **What happened:** beside the pool, the body's own reflex cancelled the walk onto the cell ("Hazard/threat detected: urgent retreat required"). It was counted as refused, the place was set aside, and the hunt dug before sight had the pool.
  - **How it is known:** from the runtime, not the message's text. `PlayerRuntime.campaignTaskFinished` calls `campaign.cutForSafety(task)` when `entry.hazardInterrupted` is set, before the task's end is reported.
  - **What it means:** such a cut counts as the walk ending where it got to.
  - **Bounded once a told place:** the second safety cut on the same place is a refusal ("cut short for its safety twice on the way"), so the place is set aside. A place it cannot safely go to is never walked into again and again.
- **Unchanged:**
  - Nothing reads lava it has not seen, and nothing digs toward the told place.
  - `lavaForCast`'s chain, `descendOrTunnel`, the 200 ticks and the other set-asides are as they were.
  - The fixtures' "mined blind" check is the same.

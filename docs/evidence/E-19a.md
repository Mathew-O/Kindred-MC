## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: a person sets, for themselves only, how AI players speak to them:

```text
/aiplayer prefs                           what you asked for
/aiplayer prefs unasked fewer|usual|more  the pause before you are spoken to unasked again: 60, 30 or 15 seconds
/aiplayer prefs teasing on|off            off: no teasing between AI players while you can hear it
/aiplayer prefs jokes on|off              off: no jokes to you or near you; the plain line instead
/aiplayer prefs forget                    delete what you set
```

Nobody else can set them, the console and operators included, and they give nobody control of a player. A line said to
several people at once is put as the strictest of them asked. A reply to one person follows that person's own, and the
voice is told in one line how to speak to them. They are kept per person, not per player, and survive a restart.

## Design changes and what was not built

- "Strictest in earshot" is taken as everyone the line will be said to: each line is sent to each hearer, so someone
  whose own pause has not run out does not hear it and does not count.
- Preferences say nothing of what a person may ask (E-52) or how many lines an AI player may say in all (E-53's attention
  budget reads `unasked` when it is built).
- The model's own lines to one person follow that person's preferences; nobody else hears them.

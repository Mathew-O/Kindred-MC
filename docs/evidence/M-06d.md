## Scope and implementation

User-visible behavior: `/faction claim`, `unclaim`, `map`, `coins`. In a faction world only a claim's own faction's
members break, place, open or use anything on it (doors, buttons, chests, levers, frames, stands, storage carts; both
halves of a double chest); a safe zone only operators. Explosions and fire break nothing on claimed land; fluids,
pistons, hoppers, dispensers and droppers reach nothing across a border into another owner's land; a projectile by
an outsider (or by nobody) sets nothing off there. An AI player's change is checked at admission
(`RoamingEdits.common`) and again at dispatch (`PlayerRuntime.territoryPermits`), the same rule as a person's.
Changed modules/files: companion-core `factions/Territory`, `factions/Coins` (the coin ledger: 500 at an account's
first sight, every change an event with its balance after), `record/Ledgers` (coins); companion-server-adapter
`TerritoryGuard`, mixins `ExplosionTerritoryMixin`, `FluidTerritoryMixin`, `PistonTerritoryMixin`, `HopperTerritoryMixin`,
`DispenserTerritoryMixin`, `DropperTerritoryMixin`, `ProjectileTerritoryMixin`, `SmallFireballTerritoryMixin`,
`FireTerritoryMixin`; `FactionsService`, `FactionCommands`, `PlayerRuntime`, `RoamingEdits`, `KindredMod`.
Schema: none; claims (`claim`, `unclaim`) and `coins` are World Record events.

Fixture notes: claims are written to the Record directly (test positions lie far beyond an 8,000 border; the claim
rules are JUnit's); both sides of the border are kept simulated (`FixtureGround.simulated`); the water's pool is walled,
as water makes only for the nearest drop. Found while building: Fabric's transfer API pulls through its own storage
lookup when vanilla's input is empty, so the hopper rule is at `extract`/`insert` themselves, ahead of it; a runtime
from an earlier fixture may still be registered, so every registered faction world must allow a change.

Design changes: the coin ledger starts here (claims cost coins; M-06g adds the market to it); claims overworld only for
the first run (the lead, 2026-11-08). Claim flags for outsiders' public use (doors, buttons) are not built: outsiders use
nothing, the stricter reading.

## Remaining limitations and handoff

Not blocked: mobs other than by explosions (an enderman lifting a block, a ravager trampling, a wither's body) on
claimed land; a later slice. Safe zones are empty until M-06f sets the hub. Vulnerable and captured land: M-06e
(`mayChange` already treats a vulnerable chunk exactly as a secure one).

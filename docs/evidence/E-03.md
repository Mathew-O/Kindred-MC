## Scope and implementation

**The patch worked out** (`PlayerRuntime.patchMineCandidates`). A player given a patch of ore, stone or logs mines every block of it with a face open to the air, nearest first: ore and stone with a pickaxe it holds that harvests them (the first such, from its hotbar or its main rows, swapped to hand), logs with an axe. Each block is walked to until it is in sight and reach; one it cannot get in sight of after three walks is set aside. What the patch drops (worked out from the block's own loot with that tool) is picked up before the next block. Once nothing of the patch is open to the air it says "I mined out the patch: 6 iron ore." or "I cut the patch down: 8 oak logs." With no pickaxe that harvests the patch it mines nothing and says "None of my pickaxes can mine iron ore." The chore ranks ahead of its own progression's steps. Each mine is the recorded `native_mine` under the patch's own edit decision.

**A tool that cannot harvest** (`NativeMiningAction`). A mine ordered with a tool that cannot harvest the block (a block that needs the right tool to drop anything) is refused before anything is done, as nothing would drop.

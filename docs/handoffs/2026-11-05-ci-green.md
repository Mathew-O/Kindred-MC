## Block 1 (claude/ci-green-1105)

**What the failures were.** The suite's flakes come from a handful of causes, each a fixture or the product racing time
or terrain. Concurrency within a batch is not one of them. Of 287 batches, 271 hold one test, and nearly every flaky
fixture has a batch of its own.

**Root causes and fixes, by ID:**
- **F-22c1 (fixture):** the Nether portal restart fixture left both ends of its trip to vanilla, at a random test origin.
  Its Nether portal was made in whatever ground lay there, and the way back could link to another test's lit portal.
  Both ends are now staged at Y=100.
- **F-22c2 (product):** a slice booked to its accounting period's end lapses short of it by its monotonic clock and the
  renewal's write latency. It was not held over midnight. Now it is, by where it was booked to, not by the wall clock
  at the lapse.
- **F-22c3 (fixture):** the hungry climb's plain reached past the simulated chunks; a cow struck across a chunk's edge
  left its beef unticked.
- **F-22c4 (product):** a reroll's lectern put down where its spot was taken meanwhile was lost to the reroll.
- **F-22c5 (test server, systemic):**
  - the unpaced test server ran game time about ten times faster than a live server, so a durable write cost up to
    ten ticks;
  - every fixture counting a reaction or a budget in ticks raced the CI runner's disk: the fall clutches, the hoglin
    edges, the stair's pace, the hideout's tick limit, the tree in water;
  - a tick now lasts until the stores' queued work is done, up to a live tick's 50 ms.
- **F-22c6 (fixture):** a replay's creation cell was chosen before its structure had settled.
- **F-22c7, F-22c8, F-22c9 (product):** the build loops the new pacing exposed, CI's hideout, cabin and house failures:
  - a walk ending while the body still moved;
  - two last floor blocks walked between;
  - a one-second landing wait;
  - pillar blocks not counted as scaffold.
